## Important normalization discrepancy, not silently repaired

The immutable EllipticCurves Layer 6 prose specifies the full logarithmic x-height limit. Pinned `Point.canonicalHeight` instead defines
`lim_n h(2^n P)/(2 * 4^n)`. Pinned `neronTatePairing_apply` is the **halved polar form**, and `neronTatePairing_self` returns this canonical height. Thus neither the height name nor the regulator name establishes agreement with the roadmap's prose or a paper's convention.

GZ.0 explicitly owns change-of-convention maps, including scaling a rank-r Gram determinant by `c^r`; it already instructs the implementer to inspect the actual pairing definition. The explicit GZ.0 → BSD.1/5/9 links make that contract load-bearing. Compare the actual pairing, then the regulator and arithmetic quotient, before applying a source formula. Do not discard a power of two in a 2-primary formula, and do not insert a second infinity-component factor when the period is already the full real period. This report does **not** silently change the anchor or assert that its displayed BSD quotient already matches the pinned Lean implementation. The convention bridge is a named remaining mathematical obligation, not an assumed equality.

A second distinction matters: the inspected regulator file proves **basis-change invariance**, not the finite-index theorem for an arbitrary proper sublattice. Nor does the anchor's Layer 6 explicitly promise every quadratic/Heegner index formula. Accordingly this decision does **not** move a missing finite-index calculation out of BSD.1/5 under the name “regulator reuse.” Those specializations, and any elementary determinant/index lemma still needed for them, remain work of the continuation using the existing lattice and pairing.
