## 2. Why two roadmaps remain

Write **C** for `AutomorphicCongruences`, **I** for
`ModularIwasawaMainConjectures`, and **K** for `KatoEulerSystems`.

C constructs the arithmetic proof inputs: congruences and classes on several
different unitary groups, universal zeta morphisms, integral descent and
source-specific endpoint arguments. I constructs the common formulations and
proves how qualified endpoint theorems interact with ordinary, cohomological,
signed and family interfaces. These purposes are complementary. Neither
roadmap is a thin remainder after its imports are made explicit.

The phrase “main conjecture” does not identify a unique mathematical output.
One must specify the lattice, local conditions, coefficient ring, selected
class or analytic element, and which direction of divisibility is proved.
Nor does an equality of ideals up to a unit identify arbitrarily normalized
periods or zeta elements. The restructuring therefore assigns precise proof
outputs rather than merging stages because they mention the same theorem.

The important distinctions are:

- C.L1 supplies an ordinary reverse bound. I.L1 combines the independent
  bounds on matched objects and proves the ordinary equality/interface.
- C.L4 already states the FW pointwise and family endpoints. I.L2 and the FW
  part of I.L4 import them. Their representation, determinant and consequence
  comparisons remain here, including the full signed part of I.L4.
- C.L5b explicitly supplies the BCS cyclotomic endpoint. I.L3 imports its
  rational and integral branches, but still owes the elliptic-curve/modular-form,
  period and lattice identifications. C.L5 is only the aggregate of its
  children; assigning another proof to that parent would count it twice.

No title change is proposed. The main-conjecture interface is not merely
“more automorphic congruences”, and its signed/control work should not be
hidden under a continuation title for a geometric construction roadmap.
