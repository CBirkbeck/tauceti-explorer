## 1. Decision

Keep three mathematical projects, but make AnalyticNumberTheory an extension of the existing Arithmetic Dirichlet series and Tauberian methods roadmap. Its proposed title is **Arithmetic Dirichlet series and Tauberian methods, Part II: analytic number theory and zeta functions**. Remove AN.0 and AN.1 as duplicate construction stages, and AN.6 as an export/register stage. Their mathematics and downstream requirements remain assigned below. The surviving analytic roadmap starts from the anchor's summation and conditional prime-number-theorem transfer, rather than rebuilding those foundations.

ArithmeticStatistics remains the owner of arithmetic-family parametrizations, uniform counts and distribution theorems. SieveMethodsAndPrimePatterns remains the owner of sieve inequalities and prime-pattern endpoints. Neither is merely a renamed analytic-number-theory chapter. The two Tau Ceti anchors remain unchanged. Of the 22 family stages, 7 are kept, 12 narrowed and 3 dropped; the JSON records 31 target-level ownership entries and 57 supplier-to-consumer links.

### Contracts that must not be weakened

**ADS:** norm-regrouping does not preserve pointwise multiplication of ideal weights, and nonnegative grouped coefficients do not imply termwise nonnegativity on individual ideals. Perron's finite-height value at a jump is not exactly one half: the anchor fixes `arctan(T/c)/pi` at the kernel endpoint and obtains one half only in the limit. A named boundary remainder, its continuity and the pole subtraction must actually be constructed before applying the conditional PNT theorem. Generic Dirichlet density remains the anchor's ratio/normalization API.

**EC:** EC.8 counts isomorphism classes via integral short models minimal as a pair; it does not say all rational equations of bounded displayed height form a finite set. The explicit rank inequality inspected in Tau Ceti requires finite generation of the point group and finiteness of the chosen subgroup containing the descent image. It does not independently establish Selmer finiteness, let alone Sha finiteness. EC.7's general Selmer sequence and EC.6's explicit 2-descent have different carriers; ST.1 supplies the required geometric comparison.

**Sieve:** the existing `SelbergSieve.level` truncates weights. It is not a proof of `sum_{d<=D} |R_d|` control. The existing upper-Mobius lambda-squared theorem assumes weight at 1 equals 1. BV must preserve all quantifiers and loss factors; neither it nor bounded gaps implies the twin-prime conjecture.

**Special zeta:** with the standard series `Phi(z,s,a) = sum_{n>=0} z^n/(n+a)^s`, the exponential-zeta series is `z*Phi(z,s,1)`, not `Phi(z,s,1)`. Establish the comparison first on a common convergence domain and then continue with the correct branch data. The existing circle-valued Hurwitz parameter does not supply an arbitrary complex parameter.

**Tate admissibility is not silently deleted.** The packet uses a class Z defined by conditions on both f and its Fourier transform: continuity/L1, uniform lattice-sum convergence on the stated compact-idele/translation domains, and weighted multiplicative integrability. AL.0–1's existing Schwartz–Bruhat language is not a proof that arbitrary Z data are equivalent to Schwartz–Bruhat functions. The migration must keep the Z-admissible extension and prove the comparison/specialization actually used. Likewise, the multiplicative fundamental-domain volume is kappa, whereas the normalized additive covolume is 1. The two possible residue contributions are `-kappa*f(0)` and `+kappa*fhat(0)`; a vanishing residue may remove a pole.

Retarget the existing dependency chain admissible class -> Lemma A -> global FE -> ideal-series comparison, the local FE -> comparison edge, comparison -> Artin interface, and zero-free region -> explicit formula. Preserve source IDs, hashes, locators, excerpts and review corrections; do not mark provenance records as new unfinished duplicate proofs.
