## Decision and scope boundary

Make `ShimuraCompactifications` an extension entitled **Analytic toric geometry,
Part II: arithmetic toroidal compactifications**. Keep the roadmap id and all
nine stage ids. Narrow C0, C2 and C3; keep C1, C2.general, C3.general and C4–C6.
There is no merger, retirement or dropped target.

The member already says to consume the common toric supplier. The proposal turns
that prose into named owners and prerequisite edges. The first prerequisite is
`AnalyticToricGeometry`, not a new parallel cone/fan implementation. An extension
of the common API must recover the existing finite-fan objects and maps by named
compatibility isomorphisms; it must not impose `finite_cones` on arithmetic fans
or introduce an unrelated `ShimuraFan` dialect.

The dividing line is **finite regular complex toric geometry versus the additional
arithmetic, relative and integral geometry**. Finite generation of an individual
cone, finitely many cones in a fan, and finitely many cone orbits under an
arithmetic group are three different conditions. Neither the anchor's
quasi-compactness arguments nor its properness criterion can be applied simply
by replacing finite with finite modulo a group.

### Three non-transferable obligations

**Refinement existence.** L0 defines subdivisions and L5 treats their maps. Neither
therefore supplies existence of sufficiently fine common, smooth or projective
arithmetic refinements. C0 keeps that whole target, including the needed finite
special cases not already stated by the anchor. Projectivity stays conditional
on the projective admissible fan; it is not deduced from properness.

**Quotients and boundary.** C2 cannot infer arithmetic separation, normality,
reflex-field descent or projectivity from L3. Regular finite toric charts give a
local simple-normal-crossings model; any descent statement must verify the
actual stabilizers, chart transitions and possible component identifications.
The original normal-crossings conclusion is retained, not silently strengthened
to global simple normal crossings. Likewise, any nonregular-cone local geometry
needed for the original normality/properness targets remains C2 work: the smooth
anchor does not prove it. Generic pure Shimura data never acquire a universal
abelian scheme by this restructuring.

**Refinement cohomology.** L5 proves toric map/properness results, not
structure-sheaf pushforward or higher-direct-image vanishing. C3 keeps these as
separate source-specific theorems. B3 proves the coefficient extension and
comparison using them. Properness, or equality in degree zero, is not treated as
a substitute for the higher-degree statement a particular coefficient requires.
