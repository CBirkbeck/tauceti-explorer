## Results and corrected scope

The Newton strata partition `G` (Theorem 3), and give direct-sum decompositions of its test functions and cocenter (Theorem 10). Theorem 11 supplies congruence-level Iwahori–Matsumoto generators. Howe finiteness is retained over a field of characteristic different from `p` (`F3–F4`), with the compact core supplied by `C17/N20` in place of the source's false unrestricted finite-minima assertion. Theorem 23's rigid generators-and-relations presentation is retained as the proposed direct repair `R16`, with its proof obligations explicitly routed.

The coefficient ring for the underlying test algebra and cocenter is a commutative `Z[1/p]`-algebra, with `μ(I′)=1`. The quotient is an R-module, not a quotient algebra. Invariant distributions are algebraic R-linear functionals; families of component restrictions form products. Generic R-duals need not separate points or preserve finite generation. Even a field does not repair the source's finite θ-stable support splitting (`E14`).

## Group Kottwitz quotient and support

`A10` constructs `κ_G:G→Ω` from `A5/A7`: the subgroup `G0` generated by parahorics is normal and θ-stable, `G=G0N`, and the quotient is `N/N0≃Ω`. The map is surjective with kernel `G0`, is constant on `I wdot I` with value `pr_Ω(w)`, and is θ-equivariant. Therefore

`κ_G(hgθ(h)⁻¹)=κ_G(g)+(1−θ)κ_G(h)`.

Only the composite to `Ω_θ` is twisted-conjugacy invariant. `N8`, `R2` and `R16` now import this construction; no arithmetic `B(G)` theorem is needed to construct it. Support in `κ_G⁻¹(S)` is invariant exactly when `S+(1−θ)Ω=S`. Merely requiring θ-stability of S is insufficient.

For the norm-one torus of an unramified quadratic extension, `X_*(T)=Z`, inertia acts trivially and Frobenius acts by −1. Thus `T(F)/T(F)_0=0=(X_*(T)_I)^σ`, whereas full Galois coinvariants are `Z/2`. Every torus is quasi-split, so adding quasi-splitness does not fix `E8`. Keep the intrinsic `Z(F)/Z0`, the real translation image of `A8` and the finite kernel of `A9` distinct. The real apartment cannot detect this torsion discrepancy. For a general reductive group the minimal Levi `Z_G(A)` need not even be a torus. Haines–Rapoport's strictly henselian Proposition 13 and its Remark 9 descent are different inputs.

## Reusing the pinned Hecke product

At the pins, Mathlib already defines `HeckeCosetModule`, `HeckeRing` and `IsHeckeTriple`; Tau Ceti provides `HeckeCosetModule.mul`, `structureConstants`, basis-product theorems and semiring/ring instances. `H6` is the missing compact-level comparison, not a new double-coset product.

For compact open K, prove `IsHeckeTriple (⊤:Submonoid G) K K` from finite index of the open intersections inside K. Define the R-linear equivalence Φ taking `1_KgK` to the existing basis element. The exact formula is

`Φ(f*g)=μ_G(K) • HeckeCosetModule.mul R (Φ(f)) (Φ(g))`.

The existing `DecompQuotient` indexes left cosets `σ_i g K`. Evaluating convolution at d counts those i for which `g⁻¹σ_i⁻¹d∈KhK`; the unique corresponding j yields `σ_i g τ_j h K=dK`, exactly the existing multiplicity fibre. Each coset contributes `μ_G(K)`. This proves the comparison by bilinearity. The scalar cannot be dropped: in the C2 counting model `1_K*1_K=2·1_K`, and it becomes zero over F2 although the abstract Hecke basis squares to itself. When μ(K) is a unit, μ(K)Φ gives the normalized algebra comparison.

For `L10` (Proposition 13), the root counts and contracted-product bijection prove the hypotheses of `mul_single_single_of_mulMap_eq`: the multiplication map has the single target double coset and multiplicity at most one. Apply that theorem, then `H6` to insert `μ(I_n)`. The local geometric count remains required. SR.1 explicitly requests this reuse.

For **E14**, use `G=PGL3(F)`, the anti-diagonal transpose-inverse twist, `ω=1`, `R=C`, and standard `I⊂P=PGL3(O_F)`. The kernel vector `(1_I,−1_I)` in the `(I,0)` and `(P,0)` summands has `Ω′=Ω0={0}`. Here `Ω=Z/3` and θ acts by −1. A length-zero τ with κ(τ)=1 takes `1_I` to support label 2. The two functions have the same nonzero cocenter class, witnessed by Haar integration. Thus the alleged two cocenter summands intersect. This refutes the splitting used in the proof, not the presentation theorem.

For **E15**, over a field let M be the direct sum of local test modules modulo local commutators and W the span of all graph relations. If E⊂M is finite dimensional, a basis of `E∩W` uses only finitely many relation generators. Collect their five-tuples in A; then `E∩W_A=E∩W`. Extending algebraic linear functionals gives `W_A^⊥|E=W^⊥|E`. The corrected product version of Theorem 22 identifies `W^⊥` with compatible families. This supplies the missing finite-A argument after a valid support reduction; it fixes neither E14 nor general-ring duality.

For **E16**, equal `Ω_θ` labels fix the θ-averaged central component of the Newton vectors; W0 acts trivially on that component. Their nonzero difference then has zero central projection and hence positive integral translation length. The existing `N19` gives a simpler alternative: an infinite sequence of translation identities has the same pair of finite affine errors at two exponents, whose subtraction forces equality up to W0. Keep that proof, which also handles central directions.
