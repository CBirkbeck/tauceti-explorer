## 1. What the paper proves

Let F be a totally real field of degree n, H/F a finite abelian extension which is a CM field, G = Gal(H/F), S a finite set of places of F containing the archimedean places and the primes ramified in H, and T a finite set of primes of F disjoint from S such that no nontrivial root of unity of H is congruent to 1 modulo every prime of H above T. The Stickelberger element Θ_{S,T} ∈ ℤ[G] is the group-ring element whose value at every character χ is the S-depleted, T-smoothed L-value L_{S,T}(χ^{-1}, 0); its integrality is the theorem of Deligne–Ribet and Cassou-Noguès. Cl^T(H) is the ray class group of H of conductor the product of the primes above T. Write M^- for the minus part of a ℤ[½][G]-module under the complex conjugation of H, M^∨ for the Pontryagin dual with the contragredient action, and x ↦ x^# for the involution of ℤ[G] induced by g ↦ g^{-1}.

The main results are:

- **Theorem 1.2 (Brumer–Stark away from 2).** Θ_{S,T} ∈ Ann_{ℤ[G]}(Cl^T(H)) ⊗ ℤ[½]. This is Tate's Conjecture IV.6.2 after inverting 2.
- **Theorem 1.3 (Strong Brumer–Stark, Kurihara).** Θ^#_{S,T} ∈ Fitt_{ℤ[G]^-}(Cl^T(H)^{∨,-}), the zeroth Fitting ideal of the minus part of the Pontryagin dual.
- **Theorem 1.4 = Theorem 3.5 (Kurihara's conjecture).** Fitt_{ℤ[G]^-}(Cl^T(H)^{∨,-}) = SK^T(H/F)^-, where SK^T(H/F) = (Θ^#_{S_∞,T}) ∏_{v ∈ S_ram} (N I_v, 1 − σ_v e_v) is the Sinnott–Kurihara ideal (I_v the inertia group, e_v its idempotent, σ_v a Frobenius). Kurihara's Proposition 3.1 (Lemma 3.4) shows SK^T(H/F) ⊆ ℤ[G]; Greither had proved the formula under the equivariant Tamagawa number conjecture.
- **Theorem 1.6 (Rubin's conjecture away from 2).** For r primes v_1, …, v_r of F splitting completely in H, the Rubin–Brumer–Stark element u_RBS ∈ ⋀^r_{ℚ[G]} ℚU_{S,T}^-, characterised by ord_G(u_RBS) = Θ_{S,T}, lies in Rubin's lattice 𝓛 ⊗ ℤ[½], where 𝓛 is the intersection of the minus part with the r-th exterior power bidual ⋂^r_{ℤ[G]} U_{S,T}.
- **Theorem 1.7 = Theorem 3.3 (keystone).** For every odd prime p, with Σ = S_∞ ∪ {v ∈ S_ram : v | p} and Σ' = T ∪ {v ∈ S_ram : v ∤ p}, the ℤ_p[G]^--module Sel_Σ^{Σ'}(H)_p^- (minus part of the p-completed Burns–Kurihara–Sano Selmer module) is quadratically presented and Fitt_{ℤ_p[G]^-}(Sel_Σ^{Σ'}(H)_p^-) = (Θ^#_{Σ,Σ'}).

Everything else is deduced from Theorem 3.3: Theorem 3.7 computes Fitt(Sel_Σ^T(H)_p^-) = SK_p^T(H/F)^- by removing the primes of S_ram not above p from the smoothing set (a Fitting-ideal calculation with the multiplicativity lemmas 2.6–2.7); Lemma 3.1 gives a surjection Sel_Σ^T(H)^- ↠ Cl^T(H)^{∨,-}, whence Strong Brumer–Stark (Corollary 3.8) and Brumer–Stark; §3.4 deduces Rubin's conjecture from Strong Brumer–Stark by a lemma on Fitting ideals and exterior powers (Lemma 3.9, proved with compound matrices); and Appendix B recovers Kurihara's full formula from Theorem 3.7 by a functoriality argument for the Ritter–Weiss module under change of field (Lemmas B.1–B.7, Theorem B.8).

## 2. How the keystone theorem is proved

The proof is a generalisation of Ribet's method, in the tradition of Wiles's proof of the main conjecture for totally real fields, with three new ingredients.

1. **Divisibility implies equality (§4–5).** After extending scalars to a finite extension O of ℤ_p and projecting to a connected component R = O[G_p]_χ of O[G]^- (a character group ring, §2.2), Theorem 5.1 shows that if Fitt_R(Sel_Σ^{Σ'}(H)_R) ⊆ (Θ^#_{Σ,Σ'}) holds in every situation, then equality holds. The proof descends to the field cut out by χ (§4), then argues by the T-smoothed analytic class number formula for the minus part (Lemma 2.1, from the Dedekind class number formula at s = 0) and a size count of quadratically presented modules over finite-index subrings of products of DVRs (Lemmas 2.4–2.5), with a delicate treatment of the characters with trivial zeroes at primes above p (Lemmas 5.4–5.7).
2. **The smoothed Ritter–Weiss module ∇_Σ^{Σ'}(H) (§6, Appendix A).** Ritter and Weiss's Tate sequence for S-units is rebuilt with a smoothing set Σ': from the local modules V_w, W_w, the global Tate extension of Δ G by the idele class group and the map θ of Ritter–Weiss, one gets a module ∇ with (P1) an extension 0 → Cl_Σ^{Σ'}(H) → ∇ → X_{H,Σ} → 0, (P2) minus extension class equal to the tuple of class-field-theory classes (λ_v)_{v ∈ Σ} in ⊕_v H^1(G_v, Cl_Σ^{Σ'}(H)^-), (P3) a Jannsen transpose isomorphic to Sel_Σ^{Σ'}(H) after p-completion, and (P4) a quadratic presentation after p-completion (Lemmas A.3–A.9). Hence Fitt_R(Sel_R) = Fitt_{R^#}(∇_{R^#})^# (Corollary 6.2), and it suffices to bound the Fitting ideal of a quotient of ∇.
3. **Group ring valued Hilbert modular forms and extra congruences (§7–8).** For the trivial-zero-free quotient R_Ψ of R (Lemma 7.1), the paper works with Hilbert modular forms over the group ring, M_k(𝔫, R, 𝛙) in the sense of Wiles and Silliman, whose specialisations at the characters ψ of G are classical forms of nebentypus ψ. Using the constant terms of the Eisenstein series E_k(ψ_𝔓, 1) at all cusps (Dasgupta–Kakde, Proposition 8.4), a modified Eisenstein series W_k, Hida's level-one form V_k ≡ 1 (Theorem 8.8) and Silliman's theorems on group ring forms with prescribed constant terms (Theorems 8.9, 8.15), it constructs a cuspidal p-ordinary group ring family F_k(𝛙) congruent to an Eisenstein series modulo xΘ^# (Theorems 8.17–8.18, Corollaries 8.19, 8.21), where x = Θ_{S_∞}(1−k)/Θ_{S_∞}(0) is a non-zerodivisor congruent to ∏_{𝔭 | p} (1 − χ(𝔭)^{-1}) (Lemma 8.16) that encodes the mod p trivial zeroes. The congruence yields a surjective Eisenstein homomorphism φ from the Hecke algebra 𝕋̃_𝔐 of the ordinary cuspidal family onto an R/xΘ^#-algebra W (Theorem 8.23).
4. **Galois representations and the Fitting ideal bound (§9).** The Galois representations of the ordinary cuspidal eigenforms (Hida, Wiles, Carayol, Taylor), with their ordinary local shape at primes above p, are assembled into ρ: G_F → GL_2(K) over the total quotient ring of 𝕋_𝔐. A choice of τ moving every local eigenspace (Proposition 9.3, using Lemma 9.1 of Ribet type and Lemma 9.2 for CM forms) gives a global basis whose off-diagonal entry b defines a cohomology class κ with values in a quotient B̄_p of the 𝕋_𝔐-module B generated by the b(σ) and the local data A_𝔭/C_𝔭. Proposition 9.5 shows κ is unramified outside Σ' and tamely ramified there, so class field theory and property (P2) give an R^#-surjection β: ∇_Σ^{Σ'}(H)_{R^#} ↠ B̄_p(𝛙^{-1}) (Corollary 9.6, Proposition 9.7, Theorem 9.8). The new Fitting-ideal calculation Theorem 9.10 gives Fitt_R(B̄_p) ⊆ (Θ^#) by cancelling the non-zerodivisor x, whence Fitt_R(Sel_Σ^{Σ'}(H)_R) ⊆ (Θ^#) (Corollary 9.11), which is the divisibility that Theorem 5.1 upgrades to equality.
