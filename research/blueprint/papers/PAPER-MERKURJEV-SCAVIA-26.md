## What the paper proves

For finite H, finite H-module A, and a field F containing a primitive root of order e(A)e(H), Theorem 1.3 identifies the subgroup of H²(H,A) that vanishes after pullback along **every** continuous Γ_K→H, for **every** extension K/F. It is generated by

`cor_H′^H(a cup c)`, with `H′≤H`, `a∈A^H′`, and `c∈H²(H′,Z)`.

This describes universal weak solvability of the associated abelian-kernel embedding problem. The lift need not be surjective. Galois algebras in this argument can be disconnected; replacing all of them by field extensions would lose the actual quantifiers.

Theorem 5.1 says that, for every odd prime p, n≥3 and field F of characteristic different from p, both the GL_n and upper-triangular B_n reduction extensions modulo p² are non-negligible. The theorem has **no** roots hypothesis. The proof may extend F to contain μ_p² because negligibility is monotone under base extension.

Theorem 1.4 then gives a specific field and representation: if μ_p⊂F and p is odd, `F(x₁,…,x_p)` admits a continuous n-dimensional residual representation with no modulo-p² lift, for every n≥3. The number of independent variables is p, independent of n. Chu–Kang supplies rationality of the invariant field of a faithful p-dimensional Heisenberg representation; stable rationality alone would not prove this exact statement. Padding the three-dimensional witness with a trivial summand preserves nonlifting by a pointwise block-projection argument. The same witness is upper triangular, so it also disproves the proposed flag-lifting statement, even over fields containing all roots of unity.

## Corrections that matter to the proof

**The unrestricted Lemma 4.1 fails (`/64`).** For H=C₂ acting by t↦−t on A¹_R, B=Z/2, x=(t) and f_x=t, the stabilizer is all of H. The claimed norm generator is therefore the square class of t itself. Its translate differs by the square class of −1, which is nontrivial in R(t), so it is not invariant. More generally, tensor exactness followed by invariants is only left exact; it does not establish surjectivity onto divisor invariants.

The repair needed in Theorem 1.3 is available: assume nB=0, e(H)|e and μ_ne⊂F. The stabilizer ratio h(f_x)/f_x is a character with values in μ_e, and every such value is an nth power in F×. Hence b⊗f_x is invariant under H_x, its relative norm is defined and invariant, and the orbit generators lift. This proves the restricted lemma used in the main theorem. The report leaves the unrestricted printed statement unpromoted.

Other manuscript corrections are recorded at their consuming items:

- In (2.3), the inflation target is **H²(K,A)**, not H²(L,A). The displayed segment is exact at its middle, not a short exact sequence with unnamed zero ends.
- The last computation in Lemma 2.5 swaps u and its restriction u′. The typed identity is `−cor(res u cup x)=−u cup N(x)`.
- Proposition 3.3 applies Lemma 3.4 to **(3.3)**, the trivial-action root sequence, not (3.2), whose L× term usually has nontrivial action.
- Lemma 5.3’s map is the **top-left block**. An element of the inverse image can have a nonzero p-divisible lower-left block and need not preserve the lifted summand. Products of both off-diagonal blocks vanish modulo p², which proves the block map is a homomorphism. The final proof also needs its pointwise lifting implication.
- In Claim 5.7 the character of H²(Z,Z) is **τ_31**, the inverse of τ_13. The p=3 coincidence of inverse characters does not justify the printed formula for every p.
- The E_12 input to φ_U in that proof must be its relative norm; E_12 itself is not U-invariant. The source’s overbar on the generated subgroup must be retained throughout Claims 5.6–5.7.

These changes repair typed intermediate steps; they are not a claim that either headline nonlifting theorem fails.
