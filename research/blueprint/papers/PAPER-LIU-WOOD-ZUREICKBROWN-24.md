## What the paper proves

Let Γ be a finite group and K a totally real Γ-extension of Q or F_q(t). Let K^# be the maximal unramified extension of K that is split completely above ∞ and whose finite subextensions have degree prime to b, where b = 2|Γ| over Q and q(q−1)|Γ| over F_q(t). The paper models Gal(K^#/K), a profinite group with a Γ-action, by random groups.

**Galois-side facts (§2).**
- Gal(K^#/K) is *admissible*: it is generated by the elements g^{−1}γ(g) and has order prime to |Γ| (Proposition 2.2).
- It has *Property E*: surjections lift along non-split central Z/p extensions (Theorem 2.5, via central embedding problems and a global character, Lemmas 2.6–2.7).

**The random-group model (§§3–7).**
- 𝓕_n is the free admissible Γ-group. The random groups are 𝓕_n/[Y(S)], quotients by n+u Haar-random relations of the form Y(r). Proposition 3.13 shows these are exactly the Property-E quotients.
- As n → ∞ they converge weakly to a probability measure μ_Γ (Theorem 1.2), with an explicit formula on basic opens (5.13).
- The proof computes the finite-n probabilities through the fundamental short exact sequence (Theorem 4.12). It controls the level tower with Γ-chief factor pairs (Corollary 5.9, Lemma 5.10) and proves countable additivity (Theorem 5.12).
- The moments are E|Sur_Γ(X, H)| = [H : H^Γ]^{−u} (Theorem 6.2).
- Abelianization gives the Cohen–Lenstra–Martinet distribution (§7.1). For Γ = Z/2, pro-p completion gives Boston–Bush–Hajir (Theorems 7.4–7.5).

**Conjecture and function-field evidence.**
- **Conjecture 1.3:** Gal(K^#/K) is equidistributed according to μ_Γ, with fields ordered by radical discriminant.
- **Theorem 1.4:** over F_q(t), every moment has the predicted value [H : H^Γ]^{−1} in the limit q → ∞. Corollary 1.5 is the class-group case.
- The proof of Theorem 1.4 turns the count into F_q-points of Hurwitz schemes 𝓗ur^n_{G,c} with G = H⋊Γ (Lemmas 9.3 and 10.2). It then compares cohomology with the complex fiber (Lemma 10.3), applies Grothendieck–Lefschetz and Deligne's bound, and counts Frobenius-fixed components with the Ellenberg–Venkatesh–Westerland/Wood lifting invariant (Theorem 10.4, via Proposition 12.7 and Lemma 12.10).

## Source issues (`sourceIssues` E1–E4)

All four are in arXiv v2. The published text could not be checked, and the Publisher Correction does not touch any of them.

- **E1 (misprint, proof of Theorem 4.12, p. 25).**
  - **Printed:** "by the definition of the fundamental exact sequence (7.1)".
  - **Correction:** (4.1).
  - **Why:** (7.1) is the p-covering-group sequence of §7.2.
- **E2 (misprint, proof of Theorem 1.4, p. 41).**
  - **Printed:** "N(E, Γ, q^n, F_q(t))" in three displays.
  - **Correction:** N(H, Γ, q^n, F_q(t)), the count of Lemma 9.3. The first display of the same proof has it right.
- **E3 (misprint, §7.1, p. 34).**
  - **Printed:** "(𝓕_n)^𝒞_p ≅ (Z_p[Γ])^n".
  - **Correction:** (Z_p[Γ]/(Σ_g g))^n, by Corollary 3.10. The printed module has Γ-invariants Z_p^n, while 𝓕_n^ab has none.
  - The argument (finite-index sublattices over the hereditary order Z_p[Γ] are isomorphic) is unaffected.
- **E4 (gap, Theorem 5.12, p. 30).**
  - **Problem:** Theorem 5.12 proves countable additivity of μ_u only for "u ≥ 0". Theorem 5.15, Lemma 6.1, Theorem 6.2 and Theorems 7.4–7.5 use μ_u as a Borel measure for every integer u.
  - **Fix:** the proof never uses u ≥ 0. The bound g_n ≤ g in Lemma 5.10 holds for all u, because |Sur_Γ(𝓕_n, H)| ≤ |Y(H)|^n (Corollary 3.8). Corollary 5.9 needs only u > −n. So the theorem holds for every integer u.
  - The main Theorems 1.2 and 1.4 use u = 1 and are unaffected.

The items use the corrected statements. The slip γ_d^{−1}(g) for γ_d(g)^{−1} in the proof of Lemma 3.6(3), not recorded by the extraction, is now E20. E5–E29 are described in the last section.

## Independent review corrections (REV-PAPER-LIU-WOOD-ZUREICKBROWN-24, 23 September 2026)

The review read the whole of arXiv v2 from the same TeX source and PDF. The published article is still closed: Unpaywall lists no open copy. Three read-only helper agents split the paper (§§1–4, §§5–7, §§8–12). They recomputed the formulas on small cases: exhaustive enumeration of random quotients, Monte Carlo, braid-orbit counts, lattice-point counts and PARI. The reviewer checked every finding at the page image or in the text.

All seven routes are accepted, and the random-Γ-groups Part II brief is amended (below). E1–E4 are confirmed; E4's correction is sharpened in place. Twenty-five new source issues are added.

**The main new finding (E5).** The explicit limit formula is false for groups without Property E. This covers (4.14) in Theorem 4.12, (5.13) (the formula of Theorem 1.2) and Theorem 5.15.
- 𝒜_H contains Z/p with trivial action, for which |Y(G)| = 1. For such G, step (4.17) fails: h^m = 1 + λ, with m constant in n.
- The finite-n formula (4.13) correctly gives 0, as Proposition 3.13 requires. The limit formula gives a non-zero, sometimes negative, value.
- Example: Γ = Z/2, p = 3, H = F_3² with σ = −1, at level 3-class ≤ 2. The printed formula gives 2.70·10^{−3}, the true value is 0, and the paper's own Theorem 7.4 gives 0.
- The corrected statements assume Property E at level 𝒞 and give measure 0 otherwise. The moments, Theorem 5.12, Theorem 6.2, §7.1 and Theorems 7.4–7.5 are unaffected.

**Other substantive findings.**
- **E6.** The inner limit in q of Theorem 1.4 and Corollary 1.5 is not shown to exist. The proof bounds every limit point within O_G(1/b), so the theorem is read with lim sup and lim inf.
- **E7.** With E_Γ(D, Q) counting isomorphism classes, Lemma 9.3 and the p. 40 display are off by [Γ : Z(Γ)]. Example: one S_3-field of radical discriminant 229, but N = 6. The factor cancels in every moment ratio.
- **E8.** Lemma 12.8 needs positive weights a_i; with a zero weight the count is infinite.

**Proof gaps, repaired:**
- E10: Lemma 5.10 needs positivity of f_n to be monotone in n. It is in both applications.
- E11: the function-field case of Lemma 3.17 uses O_K^× = F_{q^r}^×, which is false for totally real K. The complete curve repairs it.
- E12: Corollary 3.8, the Y ↔ sections paragraph and Remark 3.2(3) need pro-|Γ|′ groups.
