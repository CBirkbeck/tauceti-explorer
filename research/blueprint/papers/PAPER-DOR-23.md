## What the paper proves

The paper turns the classical equality of the Godement–Jacquet and Jacquet–Langlands (Hecke) L-functions of GL(2) into algebraic structure.

**Part I: local.** Let F be non-archimedean (char ≠ 2) and G = GL_2(F).

1. **A hidden action.** M_2(F) is a Lagrangian in M_2(F) ⊗ W (W symplectic of dimension 2). The group S_3 ⋉ G^{3,det=1} maps to Sp(M_2(F) ⊗ W), permuting three commuting copies of GL_2 symmetrically, and it has a unique metaplectic lift for which the transposition acts by g ↦ g^T (Proposition 2.12).
   - Compact induction then gives Y = S(M_2(F) × F^×) a third, "middle" G-action commuting with left and right multiplication (Construction 2.13).
2. **Spectral decomposition of Y (Theorem 2.2).** For generic irreducible V, Ṽ ⊗_G Y ⊗_G V is canonically the (twisted) Kirillov model of V. The isomorphism intertwines Godement–Jacquet and Jacquet–Langlands zeta integrals (Proposition 2.5), which categorifies the equality of the two L-functions.
3. **A new tensor product.** Reading Y as a bifunctor gives V ⊛ V' = V ⊗_G Y ⊗_G V' (transposition fixes the left/right convention). Its unit is the Whittaker space 1 = cInd_U^G θ, and it is a unital weak symmetric monoidal structure (no associator yet; Claim 3.18 by Gelfand–Kazhdan, Corollary 3.21).
4. **Two tractable subcategories.**
   - On degeneracy-free representations (those generated by 1), ⊛ is the tensor product over the quasi-unital ring Z of Bernstein centres (Theorem 3.26).
   - On spherical representations (generated by E = cInd_{SL_2(O)}^G 1, an idempotent coalgebra through the distinguished test function 1_{M_2(O)}), ⊛ is ⊗_{Z_sph} (Claim 3.49, Theorem 3.42).
5. **The full structure (Theorem 3.52).** Using Gurevich–Hadani's idempotent theorem, ⊛ extends uniquely to a symmetric monoidal structure on all of Mod(G). In it, generic irreducible representations are idempotent commutative algebras (Example 3.55), and Mod(G) is a commutative Frobenius algebra in presentable categories (Remark 3.54).

**Part II: global.** Let F be a global function field of characteristic ≠ 2.

1. The local structures glue to ⊛ on Mod(GL_2(A)).
2. The theta functional μ(Ψ) = Σ_{M_2(F)×F^×} Ψ is invariant under S_3 ⋉ GL_2(F)³ (Proposition 4.9, by Poisson summation). It defines a multiplication m on automorphic functions, a kind of theta lift, compatible with the zeta integrals.
3. The unit is given by Poincaré series (Construction 4.15). It satisfies a Petersson-pairing identity (Proposition 4.16) and is surjective onto ℐ, the automorphic functions orthogonal to characters (Proposition 4.19). So ℐ is a unital commutative ⊛-algebra (Corollary 4.22).
4. The ℐ-modules form a full subcategory of Mod(GL_2(A)), the **abstractly automorphic representations** (Claim 4.28, Definition 4.29). It is closed under subquotients and contragredients and contains every automorphic representation (Proposition 4.32, Remark 4.33).
5. The paper announces, with proofs in a sequel, that its irreducible objects are exactly the automorphic representations and that it decomposes into Eisenstein and cuspidal parts.

**Appendix A.** An unramified spin structure (a square root of the canonical bundle) turns the spherical part of ℐ into a commutative convolution algebra, a "spectral Fourier transform".

## Routes

**One new roadmap: "Exotic symmetric monoidal structures and abstractly automorphic representations for GL(2)"** (46 items). Its brief states the final theorems (2.2, 2.5, 3.26, 3.42, 3.52, 3.55, 4.19, 4.22, 4.28–4.32, A.7) and covers Part I, Part II and the appendix in order.

- **Imports:**
  - MetaplecticAutomorphicForms MP.0–MP.2 and MP.4–MP.5, extended to function fields;
  - AutomorphicLFunctionsAndLocalFactors AL.0 and AL.2;
  - SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.0–SR.3 and SR.5;
  - GL2AutomorphicRepresentationsAndTransfer R16.2 and R16.5;
  - Mathlib's monoidal category API.
- **Tests:**
  - the Steinberg representation, and 1_G ⊛ 1_G = 1_G^St;
  - unramified principal series against 1_{M_2(O)};
  - the explicit middle action;
  - the commutative analogue V ⊗_H V ≅ V.

**Why new rather than a Part II.** MetaplecticAutomorphicForms is the closest roadmap (MP.3 plans dual pairs and local theta modules), and the Weil representation is the paper's main tool. But its outputs are categorical statements about Mod(GL_2) and about automorphic representations. They need representation-theoretic and categorical layers (the Bernstein centre, degeneracy-free subcategories, idempotent coalgebras, module categories) that no existing roadmap owns in this direction. A Part II of the metaplectic roadmap would mix directions, and nothing else goes this way.
