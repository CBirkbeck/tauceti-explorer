## Local refinements, moved-down notions and limits of the suppliers

KTheoryLowDegrees is outside the 94-roadmap Caraiani–Newton tier list. I did not invent a tier or claim a bundle membership. No `FoundationsAndLibraryIntegration` or `UPSTREAM:` placeholder is left in the package. Bookkeeping references were replaced by the actual declarations or owners above. Mathematics for which a cited owner supplies too little is stated explicitly instead of silently treated as an import.

1. **Integral character comparison (§§4.1–4.6).** The reductive-group dictionary and complex character theory do not prove integral reduction or all-characteristic character injectivity. This package keeps the underlying-module exact structure, resolution/Euler bridge, reduction at residue fields, torus grading, integral GL-product characters, and associated-projective argument. Later general representation roadmaps should import this interface rather than re-plan it.
2. **SL-to-SO comparison (§8.4).** The pinned library search did not find the retraction needed by the circle proof. The explicit QR/polar deformation is a target here on the native continuous matrix carriers, with source Morris §7.1, pp. 152–154. The covering and path are already supplied by Spin. Nonvanishing alone is claimed, not a full computation of the circle's SK₁.
3. **Arithmetic reciprocity refinements (§11.1).** The supplied class-field interfaces do not include the entire degree-m Artin/symbol comparison and the ramification-dependent higher-unit calculation used by BMS. Named targets `localPowerSymbol_artin` and `localPowerSymbol_higherUnits` state those comparisons on the existing reciprocity/symbol objects. Their hypotheses include the roots of unity, arithmetic Frobenius convention, the actual ramification index and the degree-change power. BMS Appendix A13–A21, pp. 85–90, was read for this input. This mathematics is a local refinement here, not a second definition of class field theory.
4. **Arithmetic finite generation (§11.4).** `arithmeticSL2_finitelyGenerated` states finite generation of SL₂(O_{F,S}) and its finite-index subgroups for every finite S. Serre §1.2, p. 490, explicitly states it, citing O'Meara. Only Serre's public statement was read; I did not consult an uncleared copy of O'Meara. The roadmap gives the reduction-theory/prime-local-tree route and Schreier step. Positive unit rank enters centrality, not finite generation. The accepted packet had recorded this as an unresolved arithmetic input; it is now an explicit theorem target, not an assumption that the relative defects are already finite.
5. **Relative SL₂ extension and metaplectic computation (§11.5).** These are not part of ProfiniteArithmetic's outer-action programme. The relative universal property, local covering classification, restricted-product passage, rational splitting and the finite-to-profinite coefficient argument are targets here, with BMS, Serre and Moore locators. The real local fundamental kernel is ℤ. Merely knowing μ₂ or abelian local reciprocity would not prove the SL₂ covering theorem. Moore's local and relative theorems were read as additional public primary sources.
6. **Four based refinements (§13.2).** The accepted U.5 continuation is conditional on `R-St-quotient`, `R-fibre-interchange`, `R-plus-based`, `R-ring-model`. Their complete mathematical contracts are retained: kernel as the required normal closure in a Steinberg quotient; a based iterated-fibre comparison with its signed prism; coherent functorial plus squares and relative loops; and a based projective-nerve/plus ring model compatible with π₂, naturality and relative fibres. These remain refinements of K2SymbolsBrauer T.1/T.6, StableHomotopyKTheory H.2/H.3 and GeneralAlgebraicKTheory K.2/K.5. They are not certified as already supplied by current upstream. Independent review must inspect the exact contracts before treating the relative comparison as closed.
7. **Curve determinant coherence (§§3.1, 3.5, 14.4).** A determinant object and its core functor are insufficient for the canonical determinant comparison for a nonsplit exact sequence. The exterior extension filtration and its graded pieces, then `determinant_exactSequence` and filtration coherence, supply that missing target. SchemeAndStackFoundations §5.7's determinant-on-conflations input should point to this contract; the object-level exterior determinant stays in AlgebraicVectorBundles.
8. **Regular-perfection Witt input (§14.5).** The full regular-noetherian claim needs more than the smooth finite-type lifting argument. The package explicitly requests supported-category filtered continuity, regular-pair dévissage compatible with the actual α-map, and the Popescu passage. Infinite Witt vectors do not commute with arbitrary filtered colimits. These are refinements of SchemeKTheoryOperations' support interface, not claims that its currently stated targets already prove them. The supported determinant extension can use the smooth case with alterations while the full regular-noetherian theorem remains a separate target.

The mathematics remains conditional on the stated supplier theorem targets, as a roadmap should. No unsupported supplier conclusion was promoted to a proved theorem or replaced by a weaker tautological Lean statement. The four based refinements and the Witt continuity refinement should be carried forward when the related roadmaps are next reconciled. No upstream files or accepted packet data were changed by this package job.
