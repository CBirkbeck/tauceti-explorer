The primary proof has not been read and the book is not cleared. - **G6: Analytification integration identity.** ComplexComparisonPartII:C0 supplies the correct planned carrier, but its claimed upstream input is an open PR, not an accepted layer in current read-only main. Reconcile that exact carrier and accepted layer when available; neither Tau Ceti at the pin nor current main was found to supply a ready-to-use analytification functor. Do not introduce an incompatible replacement carrier here.
- **G7: Strict functorial models and automorphism actions.** The BHH/IC construction gives a homotopy-coherent completion and materialization.
