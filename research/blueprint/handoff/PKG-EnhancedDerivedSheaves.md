## Structural decisions (for the reviewer)

1. **Build order.** The layer ids are kept (other roadmaps cite them), but the README presents them in an acyclic build order: E0, E1, E3, E2:towers, E5:abstract, E5:presentability, E5:descent, E5:animation, E2, E4, E5:cotangent-export, E5:spectra-comparison, E5. This carries out the packets' structural proposals 4, 5 and 9: the sub-layer E2:towers holds `derivedTowerLimit` (E2/coherent-towers-and-roos) and `IsProZero` (E2/pro-towers); the sub-layer E5:descent holds descendable algebras, module descent and the uniform-index theorem; formal tensor inversion moved to E5:presentability; E3 precedes E2. E5:animation now also precedes E2, because E2's descendability of perfect h-covers uses discreteness of perfections of animated 𝔽_p-algebras (BS17 Proposition 11.6); for this, the E2 prerequisite of E5:animation/bounded-totalization-colimits was dropped (its statement is proved by the finite matching-object tower and does not use E2).
2. **Upward citations moved down.** The plan cited DerivedDeRhamCohomology DD.0 (tier 9) for the cotangent complex. Following WORKERS.md, the notion now lives here: Layer E5:cotangent-export states the cotangent complex of animated rings and its properties (square-zero extensions and derivations, L_{B/A}, the universal property, the naive comparison with Mathlib's `Algebra.H1Cotangent`, transitivity, base change, localization and colimits, smooth and étale maps, regular quotients, lci amplitude, derived exterior powers), restated from DD.0's nodes with Stacks locators checked. **DerivedDeRhamCohomology's plan and package must import these targets instead of planning them in DD.0** (`DerivedDeRhamCohomology:DD.0/cotangent-complex`, `derived-derivations`, `derivations-cotangent-comparison`, `cotangent-naive-comparison`, `cotangent-transitivity`, `cotangent-base-change`, `cotangent-localization-colimits`, `smooth-cotangent`, `regular-quotient-cotangent`, `lci-amplitude`, `derived-exterior-powers`). The lci target states only the forward direction (lci implies perfect of amplitude [−1, 0], Stacks 08SL); DD.0's converse for flat finitely presented maps had no verified locator and was left with DD.
3. **Citations of StableHomotopyKTheory (not among the 94 roadmaps) removed.** The E∞ comparison is stated with commutative algebra objects of D(R) (HA 7.1.4, transported along HA 7.1.2.13). The spectral comparison is restated abstractly: Sp is defined here as the stabilization of pointed spaces (new target `spectra`, HA 1.4.2), and `moduleSpectra_equiv_derived` gives Mod_{HR}(Sp) ≃ D(R) (HA 7.1.2.13). Comparing a concrete point-set model of spectra with Sp is left to StableHomotopyKTheory, which can apply `monoidalModelLocalization` (README, Scope and ownership).
4. **Gaps of the plan made into targets.** `SheafOfModules.sliceFree` (slice-free module sheaves; replaces the request to DiamondsAndVStacks D0 for site-module generators); `derivedTensor_symmetricMonoidal` (the coherent symmetric monoidal structure on D∞(O) by NS Proposition A.5, which needs no enough-points hypothesis; this closes the plan's point-free tensor gap and is placed in E5:abstract to avoid an E1–E3 cycle); `towerLimit_lim1_mul_eq_zero` and `derivedLimitFiltration` (the multiplicative derived-limit filtration of BS17 Lemma 11.22, gap G5.2); and the ordinary geometric inputs of h-descent (gap G0.6): `properPushforward_coherent`, `flatBaseChange` (Stacks 02O5, 02KH), `properSurjective_inductiveLevel` (BS17 Definition 2.5 and Lemma 2.6), `descendableCover_zariski`/`_fppf`/`_nilpotent`, `descendable_pushforward` (BS17 Lemma 11.23) and `descendable_hCover_noetherian` (BS17 Proposition 11.25). Their scheme-theoretic inputs are cited from SchemeAndStackFoundations SF.0, SF.2b and SF.4c (merged into TauCetiRoadmap on 2026-10-14, so cited as `TauCetiRoadmap.SchemeAndStackFoundations`) instead of DiamondsAndVStacks D0.
5. **Merged pairs** (as the reader asked). E0's `IsPresentable` is the definition; E5:presentability/presentable-categories became the PrL/PrR target. E3's presentable limits are stated for nerves of small ordinary categories (`presentable_limit_restricted`) and E5's for arbitrary small ∞-categories with stability (`presentable_limit`). E5's compact-objects target reuses E0's `IsKappaCompact`, and the Neeman criterion in E5:presentability is the κ-accessible form, with the compactly generated case cited from E3. E0's restricted straightening covers nerves of small ordinary categories (N(Fin_∗) and posets were missing from the packet's list and are used by E5:abstract and E3).
6. **Renamings.** Packet API `IsLeftKanExtension` became `IsEnhancedLeftKanExtension` (Mathlib has `CategoryTheory.Functor.IsLeftKanExtension`); `descendable`/`descendabilityIndex` became `IsDescendable`/`descendabilityIndex_le`; `detection` became `enhancedDerived.detection`; `coefficient_system_reconstruction` became `coefficientSystemReconstruction`. Theorem nodes without API names received Lean names (for example `dgNerve_quasicategory`, `IsReplete.leftComplete`, `hypercoverDescent`).
7. **Gorenstein index bound** is stated under "Noetherian of finite self-injective dimension d", using Mathlib's `HasInjectiveDimensionLE`, as BS17 Example 11.19 argues.
