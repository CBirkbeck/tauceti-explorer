## Remaining proof/source comparisons

These are not closed just because a more explicit target is now written.

- **Soluble-image fixed-prime ordinary seed (§4.7).** Taylor's reference [RT] is **Rogawski–Tunnell**, not Ribet–Taylor. Read the scans of Proposition 2.8 and Theorem 2.9/proof, pp.25–27. The theorem provides regular-weight congruences only outside a finite set of coefficient primes depending on the form. It cannot by itself prove the claimed refinement at an arbitrary fixed l. Certify `solubleResidualRegularOrdinarySeed`, including the chosen ordinary quotient/tame character, from the weight-optimization owner or give a fixed-prime Hasse/control proof with all integral-model hypotheses. A strong Artin weight-one lift alone is insufficient.
- **Exact-completion conjugacy (§2.20).** The necessary local compatibility must concern all ℚ-conjugate embeddings, not just unrelated local Galois extensions. For ℚ(i) at 5, degrees one and two at the two places cannot occur in an extension Galois over ℚ. The README now makes that restriction explicit. Compare the source's G_(ℚ_l)-action/embedding notation with the accepted packet and supplier IG.2.6 before declaring that the original contract has been reproduced. The scheme-Chebotarev supplier also needs its degree-one prime comparison; ordinary number-field Chebotarev alone does not give the residue point on T.
- **Geometric source leaves (§§2.4–2.7).** Altman–Kleiman supplies the newly expanded compactification route, but this run did not independently close all EGA/Jouanolou/Murre/Bertini interpolation and relative Abel-map prerequisites. Compare the exact SchemeAndStackFoundations and AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry exports, including coherent Quot, dualizing sheaves, cohomology/base change and quotient representability. The compactification announcement is not a license to identify an ordinary Picard object with the compactification.
- **Original monodromy sources (§§1.17–1.20).** Larsen–Pink and Bruhat–Tits were inspected; Sen Theorem 1, Larsen Theorem 3.17 and §§1.12–1.13, and Bogomolov pp.701–703 were not. Retain Savitt's corrected equal-character boundary and extra lattice. The prime-three source plan does not certify those separate inputs.

EGPZ arXiv:2606.25485v1 is a prescribed-lift input, not a potential-modularity theorem. Dieulefait 2004 is the small odd crystalline theorem, not the unrestricted DP endpoint.
