## Mathematical changes and limits retained honestly

The local theorem retains p>3, unramified finite étale fields, completed K₃ and the actual p² lattice. The finite-residue argument proves spanning of differences as well as values, so it works for products without using forbidden zero/one symbol components. A root class is m⁻¹⊗m[ζ] over ℤₚ, not necessarily an integral bare Bloch symbol. The regulator-to-rational-cohomology arrow can kill torsion and is not an inclusion.

The small-weight comparison truncates both sides and keeps divided exactness separate from the undivided p-power error. The normalized syntomic exponential is p⁻ʳ times the raw undivided boundary, with an explicit weight-two p=5 witness. For a good-reduction curve over an unramified degree-f field, the p-Frobenius/q-Frobenius change includes N_f; only the normalized regulator commutes directly with unramified base change. Semistable coordinates retain both β and ρ, and the branch-change law includes the quadratic monodromy term. A pairing against ker N determines only H/im N. The general semistable comparison remains available, while the explicit split-model symbol formula keeps the component and node hypotheses actually read in its source.

The analytic branch retains arbitrary crystalline representations on the meromorphic and big-exponential domains, and the general de Rham theorem. Intrinsic Wach statements use nonnegative weights and no trivial quotient. NC and a noncritical refinement are imposed exactly where the image/determinant argument needs them. The bounded-image proof now explicitly uses Weierstrass unit reflection and the actual finite free Wach image; analytic scalar extension alone does not descend an image equality. The integral finite-index statement requires the named integralWachLowerInclusion; the available LLZWach proof has its slope and p≥k−1 restrictions. The general version remains an additional target, never an inference from a rational image theorem.

The following are mathematical proof/comparison obligations, not claimed established by this package:
