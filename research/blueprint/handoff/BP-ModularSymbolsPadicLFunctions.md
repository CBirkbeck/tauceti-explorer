## Mathematical changes and sources

1. **Higher-weight rational splitting.** Read Shokurov’s published English translation, *Shimura integrals of cusp forms*, Math. USSR Izvestija 16 (1981), 603–646, especially §3.3–3.4, pp.634–635, and §4.2–4.5, pp.636–641. Theorem 4.3 and Corollary 4.4 identify the higher-weight rational separation route. Choose a sufficiently large ℓ≡1 mod N, compare the boundary scalar 1+ℓ^(k+1) with the parabolic Hecke spectrum, and use Cayley–Hamilton and coprimality to build the rational Bézout projector. The coefficient/arithmetic transport remains explicit below. Source: https://www.maths.ed.ac.uk/~icheltso/shokurov/pdf/cusp.pdf ; SHA-256 `355169cc8f428ee787020cf4870c837aff3d353795603193ea72654fca9f3012`. PDF page 2 is printed p.603.
2. **Half-logarithm correction E10.** Read Sprung, *On pairs of p-adic L-functions for weight-two modular forms*, ANT 11 (2017), §4, Proposition 4.25 and Lemma 4.26, pp.907–908. For Pollack’s original products, inversion multiplies the plus logarithm by (1+T)^(p/(p+1)), and the minus logarithm by (1+T)^(1/(p+1)). The constant coefficients are 1/p; the linear coefficients are −1/(2(p+1)) and −1/(2p(p+1)). The products are not invariant under inversion. The explicit nonzero coefficients discriminate this error. Source: https://msp.org/ant/2017/11-4/ant-v11-n4-p04-s.pdf ; SHA-256 `96c02f56980a65f7bbbae12841cc5cb0eff9efbafdcdbeeafcd566dc75774c50`. PDF page 2 is printed p.885.
