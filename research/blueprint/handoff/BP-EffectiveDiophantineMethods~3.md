### Details that distinguish the new contracts

For R1, with last row `W_last*r_j` and `W_last*p^m`, the target is `−W_last*r0`. A congruent bounded integer vector gives a lattice vector with `Aa=Wb+y`. When the target is zero, positivity of `W_last` forces `r0=0`; the explicit nonzero-vector hypothesis then permits the distance bound. Soundness only uses the separately certified p-adic residue identities and the pinned integer/p-adic divisibility comparison. The raw data itself contains no p-adic values or proof fields.

For R2, the raw search checks a complete finite box including nonsolutions, verifies every reduction step and exception, and compares its accepted output with the claimed solution set. The Thue–Mahler check includes all power-basis coordinates, integrality, division by the leading coefficient, normalization/coprimality and the final exact equation. The S-unit check includes numerator/denominator prime-stripping and excludes the zero value of `1−x`. Zero logarithm terms are certified and removed before invoking a lower bound; the remaining form must be nonzero. The actual five-case source table uses the displayed algebraic generators, not merely a list of length five. Direct arithmetic gives `F(399,302)=−199290375=−3^13*5^3`; certifying its `P52^2*P53` ideal part and exclusion after removing Case III remains a precisely named test obligation.

For R4, an effective divisor is a Galois-stable multiset of geometric points, or a sum of closed points with residue-field degrees. Integration uses traces and multiplicities. A doubled point and a conjugate quadratic pair cannot be represented by an ordinary finite set of Q_p-points. The full disc checker requires restricted interpreted series, coefficient congruences, a strict tail certificate, all p children at each internal ball, and exact distinct zero identities. Isolating a p-adic analytic root alone does not establish algebraicity or rationality. Only the finite coefficient fragment is prototyped.

For R5/R6, an actual geometric Tate tensor must satisfy the crystalline Frobenius condition; a rational tensor with the elementary matrix conditions need not be an algebraic correspondence. Height use additionally requires the actual nice correspondence. The base-change contract transports the geometric data and the Chabauty–Kim locus; it does not assert equality of every auxiliary scalar height for different base points. Rank over Q remains an explicit application gap even when GZ.8 and HE.7 supply admissible conclusions over a quadratic field.
