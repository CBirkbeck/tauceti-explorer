## Deliverables and mathematical scope

The package contains the six layers AL.0–AL.5, all 124 accepted targets, the 137 API items and 118 tests of its 31 definitions/constructions, and the additional six API items and ten tests attached to other targets. Each definition/construction has at least three tests. Exact hypotheses, local measure choices, Fourier signs, continuation domains, parameter conditions and source locators are retained. The README groups related targets into mathematical subsections and gives their finer construction order: local AL.2/AL.3 before the newform application; the global analytic AL.3 prefix before AL.2 growth; the AL.4 determinant interface before the function-field suffix; rational periods after AF.4/ALS.5 rational structures. The layer numbering is unchanged.

The README uses the structure of the current upstream ArithmeticDirichletSeries and RepresentationTheory/CompactGroups roadmaps, both read completely. Its bibliography identifies the actual editions. There are no source excerpts or source-by-source chapter summaries. `metadata.toml` is exactly `topic = "math.NT"` followed by a newline.

`Suggested.lean` joins the original suggested file with one standard header and import block. It elaborates native formula interfaces, API lemmas and discriminating examples. Its named omission manifest preserves the full statements and tests that require unavailable representation, completed tensor, quotient, curve, lisse-sheaf or cohomology carriers. These omissions are explicit; an omitted full signature is not replaced by a dummy `Prop`. The two implemented predicates, character triviality on a subgroup and centered order-one growth, have actual mathematical bodies.

The prototype changes of substance are:

- Dirac evaluation is an alias of native `TemperedDistribution.delta`, rather than another sorried definition.
- Centered entire growth, its multiplication/translation/polynomial API, paired Hadamard products including local uniform convergence, and the corrected non-polynomial strictness conclusions have native signatures and examples.
- Complex stabilization has the actual coefficient formula, prime-to-p and multiple-of-p API, the absolutely convergent native L-series reindexing identity, and three tests.
- The continued-period regression uses an actual entire constant quotient agreeing with `s/s` away from zero. It evaluates that continuation at zero as 1. The scalar analytic identity retains analytic hypotheses and is explicitly separate from the unavailable representation-level period theorem.
- The critical-value scalar comparison retains an actual algebraicity theorem as an input. Dividing two complex scalars is never credited with proving algebraicity.

## Downward ownership changes required by WORKERS.md

The current tier rule supersedes upward citations in the accepted packet. The package's prerequisites cite libraries, current Tau Ceti roadmaps, its own targets and lower-tier roadmaps only. These changes must be reflected in the later owners' plans; this job is not authorized to edit those plans.

1. **AnalyticNumberTheory AN.2 → AL.2:** centered entire order-at-most-one growth, the zero-count/Jensen route, and the paired Hadamard theorem required for superpositivity. The theorem retains multiplicities, the zero at the origin, parity and eventual real positivity. Strict derivatives require a non-polynomial function; only coefficients in degrees `m+2j` are positive. This is the precise entire-function input, not the rest of analytic number theory.
2. **FunctionFieldArithmetic FA.2 → AL.3:** the adapter from existing curve completions/residues/Riemann–Roch to completion-valued adeles, trace-residue characters, conductor degree, topological diagonal and Poisson. Basic curve arithmetic stays in current upstream **AlgebraicCurves** layers 0, 4, 5, 9 and 12. Its K-valued repartitions are distinguished from completed adeles. No second completion, residue theorem, Riemann–Roch or canonical-divisor theorem is planned.
3. **GlobalShtukasAndFunctionFieldLanglands GS.6 → AL.3:** precisely the everywhere-unramified pure realization needed for the polynomial targets. Its named inputs are rank-one unramified reciprocity; two-leg no-level GL_n shtukas; central degree quotient; sufficiently convex Harder–Narasimhan truncations and the particular proper smooth compactifications over the Frobenius-diagonal-avoiding leg locus; stack cohomological descent; lower-rank negligible boundary; proper unstable-open fixed-point comparison and local stabilization; the unramified truncated fixed-point count; essential middle cohomology; and rank induction. There is no general ramified shtuka compactification or arbitrary-character continuous Galois assertion. Finite-order central character gives the pure lisse realization; general unitary degree twists are restored as Weil-sheaf twists. The later broader correspondence imports this base case.
4. **FunctionFieldArithmetic FA.5 → AL.3:** the tensor-lisse cohomological determinant ratio, duality, Euler characteristic, purity and cyclic self-twist H^0/H^2 factors. Finite-Galois Artin factors alone do not establish these results. The geometry above proves the needed realization before these determinant consequences, avoiding an assumption of the polynomial/RH conclusion in its own construction.
5. **ModularSymbolsPadicLFunctions L1/L2 → AL.5:** the complex character/sign/Gauss/period dictionary and coefficient-level stabilization comparison needed by the Euler correction. The accepted critical-value target was conditional on a supplied algebraicity theorem and explicitly proved no new algebraicity; that scope is preserved. There is no p-adic measure or general-number-field algebraicity target here. The ordinary interpretation adds a p-adic unit hypothesis to the refinement, while the complex coefficient identity only needs a nonzero refinement.
6. **AutomorphicGaloisRepresentations R19.4 → AL.4:** conditional classical/Hilbert normalization adapters, with actual compatible arithmetic realizations as inputs. The classical comparison uses the dual Galois representation with geometric Frobenius and the `(k−1)/2` shift; the cited Hilbert convention has the `−1/2` automorphic twist. This does not move the construction of those Galois representations, prove general local Langlands, or extend the characteristic-zero R01.2 epsilon input to equal characteristic.

The rank-one curve comparison reuses **JacobianChallenge** A and D–F for Picard schemes, Jacobians and Abel maps. It uses Pic^1 even without a rational point, and proves only the Jacobian finite-étale Lang-isogeny/cover adapter. Generic Lang theory remains with ReductiveGroupsPartII. The bundle foundations are imported from current **AlgebraicVectorBundles**; scheme/stacks, moduli, scheme duality and weights come from SF, R09, EDC and DWP. The no-level construction does not use R09.7's characteristic-zero resolution of singularities.
