## Main mathematical repairs and convention witnesses

All retained target contracts and the corresponding API/check statements were audited. The detailed pass table below gives the instances per target; these are mathematical checks, not proofs supplied by an admitted build.

- Classifying spaces of empty categories stay empty. Product topology distinguishes finite-CW factors from the CGWH product. Category/path composition follows `f ≫ g = g*f` in `SingleObj`; the bar comparison inverts each entry and does not reverse the tuple. Its noncommuting permutation witness computes both orders.
- Homotopy fibres use paths from f(a) to b. Hurewicz fibrations have full HLP; relative Serre lifting uses the actual disk-pair lifting problem, without silently assuming fibre pairs are NDR pairs. Twisted cellular/Serre comparisons retain the action. Quasifibration cover and filtration locators use May's Corollaries 2.3–2.4, Lemma 2.5 and Theorem 2.6 at their actual pages.
- Plus requires a connected CW-type domain and a perfect normal subgroup. Its local coefficient condition is relative to the quotient. The two/three-cell relative group-ring differential is the identity on the attachment index module; ordinary homology alone is not substituted. General plus extension uses twisted obstructions, while the typed abelian-target extension has its explicit weaker scope.
- Group completion is an H-map between homotopy associative/commutative H-spaces, with the actual induced maps on components/homology; unrelated abstract group or module isomorphisms do not suffice. Swapping (2,1) gives ranks +1 and −1. The nonexistent strict inverse transformation is `id → id ⊗ swap`, not `id → swap`. Finite-rank E₂(𝔽₂) is excluded from stable perfectness.
- Symmetric spectrum iterated structure maps are tied to the first structure map, with zero/one/successor equations. The sphere is the smash unit and degree-one sphere is identified with the simplicial circle. Ring/module products agree with the actual pairing and existing addition. The two degree-one sphere factors give the Koszul minus sign; degrees zero/one give plus. Matrix units distinguish the right-module order: e₁₂e₂₁=e₁₁ and e₂₁e₁₂=e₂₂.
- Rotating a stable triangle uses minus the suspended first map. The identity-sphere triangle pins that sign. Cochain degree 2 corresponds to π₋₂; cochain shift [1] moves it to π₋₁. The fibre-to-cone compatibility has the minus target inclusion. Finite biproduct API states all four matrix entries.
- Both sequential hocolimit and tower holimit triangles use the actual `1−shift`, with injections/projections and comparison-map equations. Multiplication-by-p on the sphere gives π₋₁=ℤₚ/ℤ; the rational telescope starts with multipliers 1 and 2. Completion uses prime p, compatible residues and both finite-generation hypotheses where required.
- The two Bockstein outer maps differ. At p=2 the inclusion 1 mod 2 maps to 2 mod 4, whereas reduction 1 mod 4 maps to 1 mod 2. The positive Moore homology statement excludes m=0; its extended zero cone has an extra degree-one ℤ. Moore-space versus Moore-spectrum indexing uses the shift n−1. Quotient spectra have the bound p^(2r); π₂(S/2)=ℤ/4 refutes the bound p^r. CRT inclusions for moduli 2 and 3 send 1 to 3 and 2 modulo 6.
- Exact-couple exactness does not imply i²=0: the tuple example has i²(1,0)=(1,0). Filtered-spectrum replacements are coherent and cofibres derived. Convergence distinguishes exhaustiveness, completeness, conditional convergence, RE∞, and Boardman's whole-plane W. Burklund's 2-primary integer bound is 2q≥3(n+1); S/8 passes E₁ and S/32 passes E₂, with no blanket uniqueness assertion.

The convention witnesses are named reader checks and documented Lean examples. Elementary permutation, matrix, tuple and residue computations use actual finite arithmetic where available; homotopy-theoretic identifications are admitted theorem/test statements. No warning-only build is counted as a truth proof.
