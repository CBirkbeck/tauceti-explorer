## Six unresolved contracts revised

1. **IG.0/unramified-local-pel-datum.** The Lean carrier now records finite unramified field factors through finite étale integer orders and fraction fields, the matrix decomposition and maximal integral order, and the actual scalar-extended B-action and pairing. The Hodge projector is B-linear with isotropic image and complement, and `(1−P)+tP` has similitude factor t. Rational B-action and symmetric quasi-polarization belong to the framing object; integral O_B-action, determinant condition and principal polarization belong to a separate object. `IntegralSlopeRepresentative` records the structured quasi-isogeny and complete slope divisibility. Its nonemptiness is requested precisely from R07.2, and the Newton-map/slope-filtration consumers use that adapter. Rational Dieudonné–Manin classification is not claimed to supply a principal polarization. The ordinary specialization test now explicitly assumes splitting in F.
2. **IG.2/ekedahl-oort-stratification.** Replaced the unsupported universal mixed-Newton test by the disjointness of distinct G-zip fibres, with a matching Lean example. Removed the obsolete witness gap. No universal mixed-Newton claim or fabricated concrete witness remains.
3. **IG.4/finite-level-formal-models.** Separated raw O_C/p models from their residue-field reductions, recorded the cartesian squares and finite-presentation factorization, and stated integrality at sufficiently large level. The target k-model is finite type; the integral source need not be. Replaced the arbitrary-scheme perverse t-structure by explicit geometric costalk/stalk bound predicates. The transition signature includes both raw and residue-field p-level maps and the explicitly imported auxiliary-level/boundary comparison predicate. Integral pushforward and uniform continuity are exact EDC.5 requests; torsion nearby cycles and level/boundary naturality are LPV.6 requests.
4. **IG.4/semiperversity.** Reconciled the proof with these models, algebraized open/finite minimal-level charts and the two-level colimit. The ℓ-power tame tower uses geometric continuous étale Cartan–Leray and left t-exact derived invariants. Exact pro-p invariants are used only at the geometric prime p≠ℓ. SF.2 supplies the geometric comparison; the general derived invariant interface is explicitly requested from SR.0:derived-extension, beyond the early abelian SR.0 export. Compact perversity separately requests both stalk and costalk continuity and full nearby-cycle t-exactness.
5. **IG.7/koshikawa-local-vanishing.** Added the source-named Lemma 3.1 application as `koshikawa-parameter-irrelevance`. Its proof lifts supercuspidal support, including the inner-form lift, and uses reduction/parabolic compatibility and characteristic-zero LLC. It does not assert that every irreducible representation lifts or that a lift of a residual unramified parameter is unramified. Added typed integral spectral/Satake and reduction signatures, the q^(1/2) coefficient extension, and the Hecke inversion required by the opposite endomorphism algebra. The integral finite-generation/localization step is a precise SR.6 request, not vector-space Nakayama.
6. **IG.7/koshikawa-generic-vanishing.** Added `koshikawa-ordinary-costalk-bound` (Corollary 8.2) and `koshikawa-ordinary-costalk-stalk` (Proposition 1.7). The former uses finite closed specialization sets, the correct right adjunction and a derived inverse limit. The latter uses localization at the local spherical ideal at p, smooth base change, the ordinary dualizing object κ⁻¹[−2d], the common equivariant Tate character, opposite-action tensor/Hom duality and spectral support. No self-duality of local compact cohomology is asserted. V_b is the bounded admissible object; the whole local compact-cohomology complex is not assigned that property. An explicit VS4 Bun-stratum/smooth-representation comparison is requested, retaining the connected Banach–Colmez kernel before proving the coefficient comparison. The global endpoint consumes these three application nodes and the product formula, independently of the away-p Pink/trace route.

The three added nodes are key source theorems, not a decomposition into small proof lemmas. Original planets are unchanged. Supplier mathematics remains with its owner; opaque supplier interfaces in the prototype are not new IG ownership claims.
