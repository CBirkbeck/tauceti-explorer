Positive ordinary moments determine an integral unit measure. After pushing
to Z, finite polynomial expansion kills every polynomial with zero constant
coefficient. Descending Pochhammer evaluation is n! times the nth Mahler
function. For n>0 cancel the nonzero factorial in the characteristic-zero
domain Z; it need not be a unit. The ambient Amice transform is therefore
constant. Actual unit support puts it in ker ψ; ψ fixes constants, so it
vanishes. The existing unit-measure Amice equivalence gives injectivity.

Convolution multiplies moments. Nonvanishing of every positive moment thus
implies regularity. The unit a represented by p+1 has no positive power equal
to one, so δ_a−1 is regular. Native norm and unit criteria give a, and
characteristic zero proves the power condition. This choice works for p=2.
An arbitrary a≠1 is insufficient, as the inherited correction E3 records.

The existing admissible-evaluation construction now yields actual positive
pseudomoments with values in ℚ_p, using the algebra structure induced by the
power-character integral. The value is the corresponding integral numerator
moment divided by a^k−1. For any g, cross multiplication gives the numerator
identity with factor g^k−1, even when that factor is zero. Vanishing of every
positive pseudomoment kills the numerator for a=p+1. Its regular clearing
factor becomes a unit in Q, so the pseudomeasure vanishes.

Total mass kills this same regular factor, so it cannot extend to a ring
homomorphism Q→Z. This specializes the existing generic obstruction and
preserves E4. It adds no source finding. The full completed-group-algebra
comparison and general finite-extension coefficient lattices remain gaps.
