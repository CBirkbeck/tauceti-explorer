## Ownership decisions and source restrictions

**R1:** specialized inputs move into this roadmap, while the existing general
construction remains with its supplier. This is not an extension of another
package. In particular:

- Quadratic induction/epsilon comparison and the η_v(−1) correction belong to
  0.7. AL.4 supplies the parameters, not the comparison being proved.
- AL.0 supplies Mellin/gamma operations. The precise V, p and q kernels and
  their continuation, values and decay now belong to 6.2.
- A6 supplies Hom rigidity and degree. Actual integral CM isogeny-diagram Hom,
  positive-degree fibres, sign quotient and stabilizers belong to 7.3.
- Rational JL does not imply integral JL. Section 9.6 selects the intersection
  lattice in its rational differential line and proves ratio algebraicity and
  integrality as separate targets; it promises no lattice-preserving JL map.
- Cotangent saturation is 9.1, using BLR §7.5 Theorem 4(iii), with good reduction,
  connected kernel and e<p−1. Integral dualizing differentials are 3.1, using
  Česnavičius–Neururer–Saha Theorem 5.15 and Remark 5.16, rather than an R14.6 assertion.
- HE.2 supplies reduction geometry. Ordinary CM charts, nonsplit canonical and
  quasi-canonical lifts, Artinian Hom and the resulting intersection counts are
  separate targets here. Section 0.5 constructs the CM cyclic pair including
  discriminants −3 and −4 with its actual automorphisms.
- Mixed-kernel derivatives and majorants belong to 6.24; weight-two projection
  belongs to 6.1. Complex Green PDEs import compact elliptic Hodge theory from
  ComplexComparisonPartII C1.2/C5.3, not AS.4.
- CM.5's maximal-versus-Eichler special-fibre comparison is kept, while lift
  orders and lengths are owned by 7.1–7.3. R18.5 uniformization is not treated
  as an existing theorem on CM fixed-section multiplicities.
- AN.4 supplies genus characters and norm counts. Section 6.16 supplies the
  norm/congruence bridge; r_A(l)>0, l>0 and Nn+l≡0 mod D remain essential.
- SR.2 supplies smooth representations, not the inverse-character toric Hom
  definition; 4.1 supplies it and the central-character obstruction.
- The ordinary moduli chart and CM moment descent are 9.4. P4 supplies the
  fixed-CM Poincaré trivialization only. The p∥N alternative is restricted to
  the canonical connected-subgroup ordinary component; annular continuation
  is the separate semistable target 9.5.
- Mathlib already supplies general complete-local-ring Weierstrass preparation
  at the pin. Section 9.3 imports it and owns the valuation/analytic-zero
  consequences and the R-coefficient inverse Amice construction. It does not
  duplicate preparation or invoke L4's finite-residue-field specialization.
- BSW's algebraic-point subgroup input is 9.2, not DT.3. F-stability is obtained
  by the sum of endomorphism translates, not by an intersection that could
  lose the algebraic point.

**R2:** ArakelovGeometryAndAbelianHeights now exists as a package. Section 2.1
uses its actual §§1.8–1.10 and 1.15, including `ArithmeticDivisor` and
`ArithmeticChow1.pairing`; no invented `ArithmeticChow1.c1` name is cited.
The squared Green function is G=2g because its ddᶜ convention is i/(2π).
Prasanna Theorem 2.3(ii), p. 910, and Theorem 2.4, p. 912, supply the routes
for the named algebraicity and integrality targets, under his squarefree-level
standing assumptions (p. 907). At weight two integrality assumes p>3 and
p∤∏_{ℓ|N}ℓ(ℓ−1)(ℓ+1); its conclusion is v_λ(α)≥0, not a unit.
Brooks's exact CM identity in 9.9 is proved from the Layer 5 period formula and
explicit local constants; Prasanna Theorem 3.2, p. 940, gives a comparison only
up to a p-unit and is not credited with Brooks's exact scalar.

Two further closure corrections were made during the pass. The Maass
coefficient-norm identity in 5.2 is owned here, in addition to MP.7's eigenline
bijection, with DIT (5.17), p. 966, the 12π coefficient and the two signed gamma
factors. The split Shimizu contraction in 5.1 is now stated via Waldspurger II.1
Theorem 1 and II.2 Proposition 3, pp. 183–187, including its double-Whittaker
product and unramified value. A ternary second-term identity modulo residual
forms is not used to supply that exact contraction.

The toric-root normalization target 9.14 is placed before the central-value
constructor. It owns the ramified squarefree-level (Heeg′),(ST) specialization,
not supplied by the all-split L3h target; the all-split case remains an import.
Its unit is constructed by symbolic cancellation and the period dictionary,
never division by a possibly vanishing L-value. The character-dependent ideal
factor is group-like. The valuation ledger includes the Fricke phase (integral
operator and inverse at p∤N), rather than inferring p-adic unit status from
complex absolute value one. The constructor in 9.15 and its uniqueness use the
root and coefficient-series targets without a dependency cycle.
