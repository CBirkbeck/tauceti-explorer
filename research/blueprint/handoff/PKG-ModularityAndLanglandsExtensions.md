## Corrections and clarifications relative to the accepted input

1. **Sym² elliptic real factor.** The accepted `gamma_two` Check assigned complex conjugation −1 on the middle (1,1) line. In the basis e²,eē,ē² it exchanges the outer basis elements and fixes the middle one, so trace is +1 and determinant −1. The real factor is still Γℝ(s)Γℂ(s), now justified by the correct sign relative to (−1)^h. The Lean matrix example proves these two values. Martin–Watkins §4.2, author PDF p. 7, supplies the factor convention.
2. **Odd-rank gamma conversion.** The accepted E16 correction is preserved: the determinant formula includes (−1)^((n−1)/2). BLGGT v4 §5, p. 64 prints the formula without that rank factor. The rank-three weight-two Sym² witness distinguishes the formulas. Full gamma factors require coherent real-sign data; weak compatibility alone is not promoted to all-place compatibility.
3. **Newton–Thorne reduction.** The accepted summary of Proposition 6.1 ended with potential-diagonalizability conditions and omitted conditions (6)–(8). The package restores potential unramifiedness away from pv₁, both large dihedral auxiliary primes, the four distinct residue/order primes, and both ray-class/exponent-two splitting conditions. It defines E(S) from §5, p. 38 and identifies a₀(p) by Lemma 2.3, p. 11. Source: NT Hilbert arXiv v2, pp. 45–46. This is an input-plan correction, not an edit to the packet.
4. **Clozel–Thorne reductions.** Theorem 7.1 is an implication from the universal RAESDC premise to the essentially-square-integrable conclusion, with finite-place comparisons. Lemma 7.4’s twisting character is continuous and need not itself be algebraic in the mixed-parity case; its parity and conjugate-self-duality equations are now stated. Proposition 7.6 retains its odd residual extension and auxiliary-prime restrictions. Source: Cambridge accepted manuscript, pp. 46–49.
5. **Weight names.** The algebraic purity parameter w_alg in a_{τ,i}+a_{τc,n+1−i}=w_alg differs from motivic weight by w_mot=w_alg+n−1. Polarization signs use the former, Frobenius magnitudes and gamma factors the latter. The full convention dictionary now makes this explicit.
6. **Elliptic realizations.** The seed consistently uses V=T_ℓ(E)⊗ℚ_ℓ and H=V∨=H¹_et. Thus det H=ε⁻¹ and HT(H)={0,1}. Changing Frobenius does not perform this duality. ACC+ Corollary 7.2.4 is translated as Sym^m H; Qian’s seed uses the same H. This resolves notation ambiguity rather than changing those sources’ assertions.
7. **Bad factors and degree zero.** Omitting a ramified factor need not universally destroy every possible functional equation; it fails to guarantee the stated equation with the original conductor/epsilon data. The corresponding Check says this precisely. Degree zero is a boundary convention giving the completed zeta function, outside the positive-degree entireness conclusions.
8. **Bianchi bad set and Artin scope.** The BCGNT Sato–Tate assertion excludes ramified places, preserving the accepted correction of the printed bad-set label. The finite-image strong Artin conjecture and its regular-geometric refinement are distinguished; a weak BLGGT/NT comparison is not silently upgraded to the finite-image all-place assertion.

All older accepted source corrections remain reflected in the statements: Qian’s ℓ>n bound and auxiliary-prime congruence, the local indexing in BLGGT lifts, source-version locators, real/complex reciprocity direction, canonical bad/good parameter decomposition, nongeneric KMSW local inputs, finite packet multiplicities, and the scope of the Mœglin–Renard criteria. Source-error history lives here and in the accepted reader, not in the package’s mathematical narrative.
