## Mathematical and source corrections made in the package

- A Kummer fibre `[2]⁻¹(a)` is a finite J[2]-torsor. A two-covering of a J-torsor is a positive-dimensional J-torsor with a degree `2^(2g)` morphism. These are distinguished, including at g=0. The Poonen Selmer-set comparison uses a chosen J[2]-torsor Y₀→I, not the J-torsor I itself.
- The unmarked fibre over W[2] in Sel₄(J) is the quotient of the two-covering Selmer set by `δ₂(J[2](K))`. A chosen identification of the intermediate cover gives the marked comparison; the unquotiented statement requires this subgroup to vanish. For E:y²=x³−x over ℚ, Sel₂(E) has four elements, while the unmarked Sel₄→Sel₂ fibre over zero has one. This is a scope refinement of the introductory BGW comparison, proved from the kernel sequence `0→J[2]→J[4]→J[2]→0`; the existence/divisibility argument in §8, pp.21–22, is retained. It does not change BGW's density theorem.
- Brauer notation is additive throughout, via the additive wrapper of the native multiplicative field carrier. `cyclicAlgebraClass_eq_one_iff` becomes `cyclicAlgebraClass_eq_zero_iff`, and `finite_eval_ne_one` becomes `finite_eval_ne_zero`. Names of the original tests are retained; their expected classes are zero, sums and negatives. Hamilton's real invariant is still 1/2.
- Cyclic-algebra parameters P_i(t_v) must be nonzero. Product norm assembly keeps both column and row conditions. Added explicit counterexamples: 3 and 7 over ℚ(i) with S={2,∞} satisfy row sums but fail at exceptional primes; −1 has column sum zero on S but fails at the real place. Reciprocity eliminates exceptional entries, not every individual entry in S.
- The Ueno degree argument uses components of finite intersections of at most dim X translates; it does not equate the entire infinite intersection with an arbitrary finite selection. A controlled Poincaré complement requires X to generate its ambient abelian variety, or replacement of the ambient variety by that generated by X−X.
- The worked μ₂-covers of y²=(x²+1)(x⁴+1) are smooth projective normalizations of the displayed function-field extensions. The affine equations alone are not torsor presentations at zeroes of x⁴+1 or at infinity. The even divisor gives an étale normalized cover.
- Parshin's implication retains auxiliary Shafarevich finiteness as a hypothesis. The upward Faltings supplier is removed. There is one unconditional rational-point finiteness theorem, proved by the Vojta–Bombieri route. Conjectures are never asserted unconditionally; finite Ш remains an explicit assumption of genus-one Brauer–Manin sufficiency.
- The complete-intersection descent consequence also requires geometric étale simple connectedness and the proper étale–Brauer/descent comparison. Brauer vanishing alone is insufficient for this conclusion.
- Number-field projective Northcott imports bounded-degree scalar Northcott, rather than replanning its minimal-polynomial proof. Finite rank uses a finitely generated subgroup contained in Γ, with Γ in its division hull; the Lean statement matches this direction and does not assume Γ finitely generated.
- Corrected Poonen Proposition 8.2.9's page to printed pp.230–231. The `yuan-26` input bibliography misidentifies arXiv:2108.05625v4: the file is Xinyi Yuan's *Arithmetic bigness and a uniform Bogomolov-type result*, dated April/May 2024, not a Yuan–Zhang paper. Its cited height normalization in §4.3.1, pp.70–72, remains applicable. Added the distinct Yuan–Zhang arXiv:2105.13587v9, Theorem A.6.3, pp.248–249, for the adelic equidistribution contract.
- Added Stoll's public author manuscript, Corollary 7.4 and Lemma 7.5, pp.30–31, to source conditional genus-one sufficiency and its descent/Brauer comparison. This is separate from torus Poitou–Tate duality. Source and supplier citations replace process identifiers in the README.
