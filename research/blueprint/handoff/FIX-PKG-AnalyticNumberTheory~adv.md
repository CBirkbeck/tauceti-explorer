## Residue and where to resume

The layer-end **Remaining analytic proof obligations** paragraphs are retained as named targets, not completion certificates. The independent reviewer should start with these still-open leaves:

2. **Classical prime analysis:** higher-genus original good-circle proof and some canonical-product/Mellin adapters; sharp Riemann–von Mangoldt phase; full unsubtracted/imprimitive Dirichlet formulas; quantitative pole-cleared zeta and line-one reciprocal bounds; full character/family density. The primitive subtracted half-interval formula and public Goldfeld-Siegel route added here should not be replaced by their old missing-source descriptions.
3. **Arithmetic periods and effective number-field counts:** ramified local Artin determinant/induction comparisons, canonical Hecke/Tate identification and original number-field density/smoothed split-prime analysis; full genus classification, quadratic regulator signs, oriented Nielsen boundary/cusp and Gross–Zagier normalization adapters. 4. **Multiplicative estimates:** both polynomial-modulus q≤T⁴ coprime Möbius estimates, positive-truncation bilinear cancellation, inverse-totient O(x), proved second/fourth-moment internal proofs, Dickman uniform-delay analysis and inherited Wintner/Euler-tail adapters. General moments remain explicitly conjectural. The norm-family intersection, all-fixed-A Sathe extraction, Shiu rough sieve and Beurling smooth Tauberian contracts added here should be reviewed in their full stated ranges.
5. **Binary cubic:** obtain and read Igusa, *Some results on p-adic complex powers* (1984), Theorem1, pp.1013–1032, for arbitrary ramified Schwartz distributions, including residue characteristics2,3.
