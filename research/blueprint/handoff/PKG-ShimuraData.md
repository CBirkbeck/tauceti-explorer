## Mathematical closures and corrections

- **Mumford–Tate:** retain the largest Hopf ideal inside the rational coordinate kernel, not the kernel itself. The native arbitrary join supplies its construction and universal property. Add the explicit finite-type quotient argument from the determinant-localized polynomial algebra. The quartic-CM kernel counterexample is retained. The elliptic test is justified by the local auxiliary `ellipticMumfordTateClassification`: calculate Hodge endomorphisms from `cτ²+(a−d)τ−b=0`, then use connected reductive irreducible subgroups with scalars. The torus/non-CM GL₂ alternatives do not assume an unproved CM criterion or use an End=ℤ shortcut. Genericity under arbitrary isogeny explicitly compares polarizations via the invariant alternating-form line when MT is full GSp.
- **Variations:** use the actual holomorphic suppliers above, not the native local coefficient system alone. Keep every requested Hodge projection, marked chart, filtered flat bundle, kernel, image, subvariation, direct sum and polarized orthogonal splitting. Native polarization is integral, not directly a rational-form predicate: the rational orthogonal argument chooses a fibre lattice and clears denominators before applying it, then glues the flat orthogonal complement. Automatic transversality is restricted to types {(1,0),(0,1)}; a Tate twist of the nonhorizontal weight-three example shows why weight one alone is insufficient.
- **Compact duals:** the filtration convention gives P(μ⁻¹). Add `parabolicTypeVariety` with the actual smooth proper/relatively ample contract and affine-base projectivity, not unrestricted projectivity over every base. Conrad's self-normalizing-quotient construction and parabolic representability give descent of the whole type variety and universal family, without a rational representative or point. The quaternion-conic negative test remains. General parabolic analytification uses ComplexComparisonPartII §C−1.1; LieGroups Layer 8's full flag case alone would not suffice.
- **Integral flags:** ReductiveGroups Layer 7 is field theory and Layer 9 supplies the pinned group/root subgroups, not all requested integral geometry. State local auxiliaries `integralParabolicQuotient`, `integralSchubertBaseChange` and the Richardson incidence proof in §3.17, with Conrad/Jantzen/Brion–Lakshmibai proof inputs, integral cells, Bott–Samelson maps, cohomological/coordinate-algebra base change and geometric-fibre incidence. Flatness alone is explicitly not the proof of closure/base-change compatibility. Over a field, pass to a central cover and use inversion to transfer the cited Richardson theorem to the left quotient convention. Empty geometric fibres imply an empty incidence scheme over ℤ.
- **Kostant:** import ALS.4.5's root-definition, supply the native Coxeter length formulation and comparison/factorization. Rename the duplicated `kostantKostantIndex` API to `kostantIndexComparison`. The two longest elements have different roles; they coincide when P=G, which the opposition check now allows.
- **SV5:** add `torusArithmeticAnisotropicFiniteIndex`. A basis of the rational character lattice, not all geometric characters, maps a compact-open subgroup to integral adelic units; rational values are ±1, and the common kernel has finitely many components. This closes the finite-index input before using the anisotropic compact quotient. The split-Hilbert-centre failure is retained.
- **Lifts:** add `galoisLatticeResolution` and `centralExtensionWithDerivedCover`, using Milne–Shih 3.1–3.2 rather than asserting that a simply connected z-extension has an arbitrary prescribed derived cover. The proof uses cocharacter lattices and a pullback of a free Galois-module presentation. Killing `w=−μ−ιμ=(1+ι)μ₀` requires replacing μ by **μ+μ₀**; the printed minus in Milne's proof does not give zero weight. This is corrected locally, with no separate errata job taken.
- **Distinguished data:** all six Lovering properties, the neutral fibre-product comparison and B₁→B diagram remain. Read Deligne 2.5.6 directly, rather than merely following a secondary reference. Centre/Levi identities use the ambient `Z_s(G)_E`; the split centre of a newly isolated Levi is not substituted. Tests retain totally real and CM reflex extensions and non-product behaviour.
- **Hilbert flags:** `hilbertFlagWeilRestriction` compares represented functors of line summands. The product of projective lines and its Segre polarization descend over a finite splitting extension; affine Weil restriction does not prove this. Full and scalar-determinant groups remain distinct, while the common-determinant PEL datum itself is imported from PELModuli. Real components require exactly equal determinants. The adelic scalar subspace is proved closed using a ℚ-basis beginning with 1. Extending compact opens uses the finite compact-group orbit of a small ambient open subgroup and its common core.
- **Siegel conventions:** for first-g-coordinate L₀ with F⁰=L₀ in the homology convention, use μ=diag(I_g,zI_g). Add `siegelRootDatumComparison` for the explicit coordinate table rather than attributing that table to ReductiveGroups Layer 7. Keep the central coordinate, parity and half-integral ρ. Genus-one open-cell vectors vary with the cell coordinate; only the Weyl representative has the fixed vector e₁.
- **Genus two:** keep all CG roots/coroots, four Kostant actions, chamber reversal, S/J identities, unitary block map, compact Cartan character parity versus the torus itself, exponential kernel, Cayley inverse/columns/eigenlines and both coordinate changes. The Cayley pullback sends `(a,b;c)` to `(-a,-b;a+b+2c)`; the positive-root dictionary follows it by the longest Weyl element. Negative multiplier and wrong-sign Cayley tests remain.
- **Neatness:** use all places above one absolutely unramified rational ℓ>5. Handle roots of unity of mixed order via “order not an ℓ-power”, not just prime-to-ℓ order. Keep the ℓ=5 and one-place-only failures, including F=ℚ(√2), u=7+5√2 with conjugate product −1. `primeToPNeatInsert` concludes full adelic neatness after insertion, not merely its original prime-to-p assumption. The rational faithful representation has dimension 4[F:ℚ].
- **Small degeneracies:** MT of a nonempty Tate direct sum is G_m, whereas the zero direct sum is trivial; the rational grading includes the zero object. No convention silently deletes these tests.
