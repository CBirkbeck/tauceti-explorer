## Mathematical checks to retain

- Carayol's 1986 Compositio theorem is at a **split finite place**. At a division place the totally-real theorem is Boutot–Zink, with maximal division local level and its specified other p-adic factors. Boutot–Carayol's arithmetic Part III is the rational global case; its local Drinfeld theory works over general K.
- The auxiliary regular PEL representation has abelian dimension 4[F:Q], its Morita reduction 2[F:Q]. Relative quaternionic formal height is 4; absolute p-height is 4[F_v:Q_p]. The determinant line is det W tensor det W^t, lying in omega squared, with the division-prime factor **pi times omega squared**. At xy=pi both x and y can be nonunits: use the smooth/generic locus and codimension-two extension rather than a false pointwise unit claim.
- The torus bridge tensor is over O_E,p, with the inverse diagonal central action. Pointwise p-divisible extension needs absence of a weight minus-two component and the dyadic Barsotti–Tate classification; it does not construct a global universal family. Raw ramified tau-quotients are not exact. Ramified base change of a node loses regularity.
- AF.5's current discrete-centre hypothesis does not supply definite forms with positive-rank O_F units. Effective stabilisers are (UZ intersect t-inverse D-times t)/F-times. Finite stabilisers alone do not justify averaging. Taylor's automatic prime-to-p neatness has p>3 and unramified F; the factorial coefficient pairing requires the stated weight range or an independently perfect lattice pairing.
- KW geometric Lemmas 7.1–7.4, Corollary 7.5 and Proposition 7.6 belong to R18.3; R22 chooses primes and patches. The auxiliary group is Delta-prime modulo its **N-torsion**, not modulo Nth powers. Freeness is over O[Delta], with the invariant coefficient hypotheses visible. Localised distinct-root control needs the early R19 local–global compatibility; the geometric orbit/freeness theorem does not. The R19 request excludes importing that same control theorem, R22 or R23 back into its proof.
- R18.4 integral freeness and reduction require both H-zero residual vanishings. Rational Poincare duality pairs the coefficient with its dual Tate twist. Definite and indefinite JL compare matching cuspidal eigensystems, not entire form and cohomology spaces. The elliptic-family purity node in R34.5 needs a genuine higher-dimensional PEL projector extension for the quaternionic constituent.
- Drinfeld's model is the union of formal opens in successive smooth-point blow-ups. The half-plane is a rigid analytic open, not a Zariski complement. BC's framing arrow is the inverse of the BZ convention. Height shifts and normalized projective action are distinct from the right Pi-inverse arithmetic descent. CDN's all-level theorem is rigid generic-fibre uniformisation for n>=1, not an assertion of semistability at every integral level. Dual graphs retain loops/repeated edges; monodromy weights edges by thickness and is supplied generically by R11.4.
