## What changed

This is the existing mathematical predicate, which includes symmetry, the adjoint identity and the local nilpotence required to state it. A tensor-form theorem and conditional instance supply the no-ghost product form from the corresponding base-form hypothesis. The lattice-form construction itself still has its explicit proof gap. The twisted group algebra now retains the cocycle in its carrier type instead of reducing definitionally to a cocycle-independent group algebra.

The fifth-order aggregate contract is explicit: both six-component vectors, both completions, the coordinate correction cancellation, the half-argument permutation in the T matrix, the S matrix, real analyticity, harmonicity and local boundedness towards rational points are stated natively. The sum's modularity consists of holomorphy and the two generator laws; no unspecified congruence kernel is asserted. The packet keeps Zwegers' eight corrected double sums and a separate gap for identification with Ramanujan's Eulerian series. The f0 coefficient test is the precise expansion `1+q−q²+q³+O(q⁵)`. A second test distinguishes ψ0's zero constant term from the unit constant term of `1+2ψ0`. The former scope-only test and documentary `andrews_corrections` API item moved to acceptance metadata; source-error comparison remains documented rather than becoming an opaque proposition.

The index-13 example now contains the characteristic-theta quotient, the period-integral coefficient formula, the explicit T/S laws, the conjugate unary-theta shadow, real analyticity and the harmonicity convention. Its tests include the residue, 26 components, the phase at index one and the failure of harmonicity after multiplication by E4. The latter is a particular mathematical non-example, not a universal necessity claim about all nonconstant residues. The residue decomposition retains its off-lattice domain.

Native examples were added for odd-weight level-one principal parts, the irregular infinity cusp in the preimage of the cyclic subgroup generated by −T modulo four, and exclusion of the zero-dimensional VOA from CFT type. Two leftover packet proof slips were repaired: the final boundary factor in the Bruinier–Funke pairing uses geometric width w throughout, and the beta/incomplete-gamma proof separates x>0 from the complete-gamma endpoint at zero.

The reader was rebuilt around the current layer contracts. It includes every node statement, hypothesis, API item, test, source locator, prerequisite, acceptance check, gap, supplier request, baseline record and source finding. It also incorporates the four reader corrections identified by the latest bounded topology review: the ninth scalar cocycle export, the inverse eta multiplier, the scaled Glaisher coefficient convention and the current QM.5/QT.7 ownership split.
