## Residue for the next fix or the maintainer

1. **Dress–Wall–Oliver induction.** The induction theorems are Oliver, *Whitehead groups of finite groups*, Theorems 11.2 and 11.9, and Dress, LNM 342 (1973). They are used by `dressWall_induction` (§6.4). Through Chinburg–Pappas–Taylor Theorem 1.7 they are also used by `sk1_vanish_abelianSylow` (§6.5), because CPT's proof passes from ℚ_p-p-elementary groups to arbitrary finite groups by Oliver 11.9. The restatements in CPT II Theorems 4.4, 4.6 and 4.7 (pp. 31–32) are accessible; no freely readable proof was found. Lam's 1968 ENS paper (Numdam) treats global orders up to torsion exponents and does not give the needed p-adic K′₁ computability. A direct proof of SK₁(ℤ_p[A⋊P])=1, with A an abelian p-group and |P| prime to p, would remove the second use.
2. **J-adic K₁ continuity**, `k1_continuity`, is Fukaya–Kato Proposition 1.5.1. Surjectivity is proved in §3.11, step 1. Injectivity is the closedness of E_m(Λ(G)) in GL_m(Λ(G)). CPT II Theorem 2.4 proves this p-adically for finite group rings over p-adically complete noetherian domains, which gives the finite-level statement but not the T-adic direction of Λ(G).

Both are classical published theorems, cited by exact number. Oliver's book and Fukaya–Kato are the books to obtain if the maintainer wants these closed from the originals.
