## All-field comparison and its dependency boundary

The central pushout Gplus=(G times T)/Z has centre T, quotient C and the same adjoint group. This weaker all-field construction needs no centre flat-H1 bijection or rational factorization of all Gplus(E). Its scheme/root-data interfaces are requested from proposed RG2.6.
2. The rational image H=i(G(E))T(E) is closed and normal with compact abelian quotient in Gplus(E). Henniart–Vignéras, *Representations of SL₂(F)*, Lemma 3.1 and Proposition 3.3 with proof, printed pp. 238–240, establish this over every nonarchimedean local field. The map G(E) times T(E) onto H is open by the locally compact sigma-compact open-mapping argument; H itself can be nonopen. The plan gives this topology argument explicitly.
3. Extend omega_pi from the closed subgroup Z(E) to a smooth omega_T: kill its restriction on a compact open U, extend the character on the discrete quotient using divisible L-times, then inflate. The representation tau(t i(g))=omega_T(t)pi(g) of H is well-defined, smooth, irreducible and admissible. The compact-open invariant calculation is specified.
4. Use only Henniart–Vignéras section 2.2.4(2), p. 236. Smooth induction from H is admissible because the quotient is compact. A nonzero finitely generated admissible subrepresentation has finite length, hence contains an irreducible Pi. Right-adjoint Frobenius reciprocity gives a surjection Pi restricted to H onto tau, and therefore Pi restricted to G(E) onto pi. The finite-length input is separately documented in Vignéras, *Representations of p-adic groups over commutative rings*, section 5, printed p. 340. Full torus reciprocity turns it into a smooth character of C(E). Torus naturality identifies the difference on T(E) with pullback of that character, which is trivial on Z(E).
