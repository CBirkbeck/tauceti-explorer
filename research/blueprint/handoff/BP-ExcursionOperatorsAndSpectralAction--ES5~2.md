## Exact unfinished target and where to resume

The target is compatibility with the actual rational central scalar character for every connected reductive G over every nonarchimedean local E. Disconnected Z(G) must not be treated as a torus with an ordinary torus parameter. The current conditional comparison requires an injective comparison into a group with torus centre, a torus quotient, a full centre flat-H1 bijection, rational central factorization, open quotient maps and compatible common refinements. Kaletha supplies this package over p-adic fields. These hypotheses have not been supplied over all E.

There is a concrete obstruction to supplying that particular package uniformly. For E=F_q((t)), flat Kummer cohomology gives H1(E,mu_p)=E-times/(E-times)^p, an infinite group. No open subgroup is contained in the pth powers: every principal-unit neighbourhood contains 1+t^n with p not dividing n. Finite separable norm images are open by Conrad Lemma 4.1.2(i), p. 19. Torus H1 is finite by Conrad Proposition 4.1.7(i), p. 22. Consequently no torus can have the required full flat-H1 bijection with this centre. For SL_(p r), with r greater than one prime to p, the centre has both a nonsmooth mu_p part and a disconnected mu_r part. This obstruction concerns the attempted construction; mu_p(E) is trivial, so it does **not** disprove central-character compatibility.

Resume at the packet's `z-embedding-central-character-comparison`, Gap 6 and the RG2.5 comparison request. A different all-field argument must specify the actual rational-centre character statement and handle nonsmooth centres without requesting this impossible flat-H1 bijection. Read FS IX.6's final paragraph, p. 333, Kaletha's *Rigid inner forms vs isocrystals*, section 5.1, pp. 16–20, and Conrad's *Finiteness theorems for algebraic groups over function fields*, Remark 1.2.1, pp. 3–4 and the two results above. Do not substitute a surjective z-extension: an induced-torus kernel and simply connected derived group do not imply a connected centre. Do not set status complete until the all-field target has a valid proof plan. The previous review expressly permits a precise partial checkpoint instead of an unsupported replacement.
