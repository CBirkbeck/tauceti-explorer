## The two remaining mathematical obstructions

### Complete flatness and p-torsion: §1.24

ALB23 Lemma 5.1.6, p.76 in arXiv:1907.10525v4, asserts the general result
and calls the first Čech term p-completely flat over A. BS22 Proposition
3.13, pp.34–35, constructs a (p,I)-completely flat prismatic envelope.
Changing the completion ideal is the missing step. The current author copy
of ALB has the same argument at p.80; its different pagination is not used
for the package's ALB23 locators.

For a generator d of I, the proof works when ((A/d^n)[p]) is pro-zero,
equivalently when the d-torsion of A/p is bounded in the relevant tower.
This covers crystalline, transversal, perfect and noetherian prisms.
Bounded p-power torsion of A/d alone is insufficient:

- Let T=Z_p[[d]], with Frobenius d↦d^p+p. Set J_n=(p,d^n) and
  M=∏_(n≥1)J_n. Put A=T⊕M with M²=0 and Frobenius (a,m)↦(φ_T(a),0).
  Its delta operation is (δ_T(a),−a^(p−1)m).
- Both p and d are nonzerodivisors. The ring is (p,d)-complete and d is
  distinguished. The module J_n/dJ_n is Z_p·p⊕F_p·d^n, so the p-power
  torsion of A/d is uniformly killed by p. This gives a p-torsion-free
  bounded prism, with derived completeness checked by the Koszul tower.
- In contrast J_n/pJ_n has a summand F_p[[d]]/(d^n) generated by p. The
  transition from d^m-torsion to d^k-torsion multiplies by d^(m−k);
  its n=m component is nonzero for every m≥k. The system is not pro-zero.

Do not turn this into a counterexample to the syntomic H⁰ theorem or to
p-torsion-freeness of its particular envelopes. A special envelope proof
may still work. One possible route is an integral normal form for finite
delta-envelope stages and their completion. Merely proving p-flatness
before (p,d)-completion leaves a further step.

Čoupek, *Crystalline condition for A_inf-cohomology and ramification
bounds*, Lemma 2.1, Corollary 2.2 and Lemma 2.3, printed pp.146–148,
give useful regular-sequence controls in the transversal case. They do not
establish this general case. Guo's note on prismatic sites, Propositions
3.9–3.16, pp.7–9, provides relative QRSP discreteness and flatness modulo
I; it does not supply p-flatness over the arbitrary prism either. Neither
source was silently substituted for the missing assertion.

### Proper log crystalline comparison: §8.77(2)

KY Proposition 8.8, p.89, follows from the log crystalline comparison,
base change and the Frobenius isogeny. CR.6/hk-finiteness gives finite
W(k)-cohomology and rational Frobenius invertibility for the special
fibre. Neither gives Proposition 8.9's scalar comparison over A_crys.

Read KY Proposition 8.9 and its proof on pp.89–90. Finite presentation
and finite charts let a sufficiently high Frobenius kill the finitely
many nilpotent coefficients on a truncated characteristic-p base. This
does not construct the final direct map between the displayed log PD
bases. For N=N with its generator mapping to [π^♭] in A_crys, the hollow
W(k) chart maps that generator to zero. The proposed target chart after
n iterations maps it to [π^♭]^(p^n), which θ sends to π^(p^n)≠0.
There is no chart-compatible map with the stated monoid map.

This identifies a missing argument, not a disproof of the comparison.
A possible replacement uses a common proper log PD deformation and
Frobenius contraction. The estimate
t^(p^n)=(p^n)!γ_(p^n)(t), with valuation (p^n−1)/(p−1), is available
for an element of a PD ideal, and dominates a linear isogeny bound.
The estimate alone does not construct the common complex or prove its
effectivity, independence and descent. BMS1 Proposition 13.21, p.116,
has trivial logs and does not resolve monoid compatibility.

## Main mathematical changes

### Derived self-intersection: §2.14

The reduction calculation now proves survival through completion. For
B=A/I≠0 and R=B⊗̂ᴸ_(B⟨x⟩)B, the conjugate pieces are R{−i}. Each
extension splits R-linearly because its obstruction group is H¹ of
the preceding stage. Compatible splittings retain R as a retract;
uniform bounded p-power torsion controls completion.

The accepted assertion that the unreduced complex is always nondiscrete
is false. Over (Z_p,(p)), the complex is
[P̂·ε —u→ P̂] in degrees [−1,0], where P is the divided power
polynomial algebra in u. Multiplication sends u^[n] to
(n+1)u^[n+1] and is injective on restricted p-adic coefficient
sequences. The cokernel is discrete but has the nonzero p-torsion
class u^[p]. Its derived reduction is nondiscrete. The source is
Bhatt's derived de Rham paper, Lemma 8.3, Theorem 8.4 and the proof
of Proposition 8.5, pp.31–32; the zero relation is treated by derived
base change, not by applying the regular-quotient conclusion to zero.
Warning 8.9, p.33, fixes the completion convention.

This corrects the plan without editing the authoritative packets.
No theorem of unreduced nondiscreteness should be restored from its
negative derived reduction.

### Integral log q-model and merging: §8.40

Arbitrary integral charts use free prelog presentations, exactification,
q-PD envelopes and derived totalization. No absent ambient coordinate
is declared an integral log form. The 2N⊂Z example at p=2 is retained
with its intrinsic generator Y and coefficient 1 in D(Y)=Y.

For an ordered coordinate map S→T, the proposed merge operator on a
monomial with exponents a_s gives e_s the factor
q^(Σ_(r<s,f(r)=f(s))a_r). Telescoping gives the chain equation;
operator products of the γ_r define it on ungraded envelopes. The
two-coordinate order change has an explicit degree-two homotopy.
Semicosimplicial Čech diagrams avoid invalid q-codegeneracies. Their
mod-(q−1) positive rows contract, completed Nakayama removes them,
and the two projections give the comparison zigzag. Slotwise merge
operators commute with cofaces, identifying the derived map with site
sections. Iterated unions supply presentation coherence.

The model and comparison have nine API entries and eight named Checks.
K1 Constructions 7.15–7.16, Theorem 7.17 and Remark 7.18,
pp.43–45, were read, as were the classical contraction inputs of
Bhatt–de Jong Lemmas 2.15 and 2.17 and Example 2.16, pp.5–6.
The new resolved and weighted constructions still merit an independent
coherence check; they are not implemented in Lean.
