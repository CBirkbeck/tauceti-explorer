## Mathematical checks and boundaries

The local theorem uses derived p-completed K-theory, not an unjustified tensor
product of ordinary K-groups. The root-class domain excludes 1 in every residue
component. The finite-value function is defined on F_q^× minus {1}; the total
Lean extension at 1 is not a regulator value. Both values and differences of
admissible values are needed for product-algebra spanning. Integrality, the
completed extension, equal rank and residue spanning are independent inputs.

The reader distinguishes representation vectors from canonical de Rham vectors,
raw syntomic boundaries from normalized ones, and p-Frobenius from
q-Frobenius. The accepted D.5 norm-chain-map specification is retained in full.
For Iwasawa theory it distinguishes integral derived inverse limits from their
rationalization, singular Euler relations from inverses, bounded from analytic
images, and the meromorphic general-weight codomain from proved analytic
cancellation ranges. The general Robba module is not assumed etale merely
because the representation-level comparison is available.

The cubic calculation works in (Z/5^N)[a]/(a³−a²+1), an unramified product
with residue degrees 2 and 1. It uses the depleted dilogarithm's rational series
and residue-disc integration, independently of the printed regulator digits.
Working precisions 16 and 20 gave identical 12-digit values after division by
25. The root-value matrix modulo 5 has determinant 3. Reflection and inversion
were also checked modulo 5^14. The second root in GSWZ (273) is ζ², not ζ⁵.
This is finite-precision acceptance evidence, not a formal proof or a proof of
global injectivity. The reader records the algorithm and all retained data;
the temporary computation files are not needed by the next worker.
