sat in T.5, `T.2/k2-finite-field` could not use them: it feeds `T.2/milnor-examples`, which feeds
`T.5/real-sign-symbol`, so the result would be a T.2 ↔ T.5 stage cycle. The T.3 part's requests name T.2:symbols as the
owner. Accepted packets already place nodes in another stage of their own roadmap (for example RD.2 → RD.0 and
R27.3 → R27.1). The nodes have no planets, because T.2:symbols already shows ten across its packets.

The new nodes close G-monomial-kernel. Through the routed rows they also close G-finite-classical, for K₂(𝔽_q) and
for the finite-field case of Matsumoto's theorem. They need no new request and add no gap.

## Source issue

`K2SymbolsBrauer/E-T5-division-ring-attribution` (new, gap). Weibel III.5.10.5 (p. 43 of the Chapter III author file)
credits Milnor §9 with generation of K₂ by symbols for division rings. Milnor proves it for fields only: Theorem 9.11
and Lemma 9.7 assume commutativity, and Corollary 9.13 states fields. For skew fields §9 gives only ker φ ⊆ W
(Theorem 9.12). Weibel's errata list (Wayback snapshot, read in full) is silent. The VI.6 mis-citation of III.7.2(a)
is already EllipticKTheory/E1 and is not repeated.
