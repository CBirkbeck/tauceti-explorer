## Additional signature corrections

The final audit inspected elaborated signatures, including the section-variable dependencies, rather than relying on what a nearby `variable` command appeared to assume.

- `finite_bounded_integral_endomorphisms` now has explicit finite-generation and freeness hypotheses. Positivity alone on an infinite direct sum cannot give finite endomorphism enumeration.
- `SignedHermitianSpace` retains finite dimension; its adjoint is now accompanied by named identity, uniqueness, composition and involution targets. On the countable rational direct sum, the map taking every basis vector to the first one has no dot-pairing adjoint: the required image would have infinitely many nonzero coordinates.
- `signedHermitianTransfer` retains the finite extension and `IsScalarTower` hypotheses. Its two module structures must describe the same restriction of scalars.
- `meanZeroL2` retains finite measure in its definition, not just in a later theorem. For infinite counting measure, the harmonic sequence and its negative plus the first-point indicator are L² and have totalized integrals zero; their sum has integral one. An unrestricted integral-zero carrier would fail to be a submodule.
- The finite-dimensional domains of `GoodOn` and `latticeGaussianSum`, and the finite-measure domain of `finitePoissonConfiguration`, are explicit. Definitions' assumptions agree with their README domains.
- `c2Difference` and its evaluation docstring both say `1−σ`. The code and characteristic-two tests already used that sign.
- Orthogonal-order polynomials are interpreted as group orders only for the stated positive rank and field cardinality. The rank-zero even polynomial and `q=1` are negative controls; zero local denominator lies outside the positive-ratio theorem.
- All named Lean declarations, including instances, have mathematical docstrings. No test, hypothesis or locator was removed to reduce the package's size.
