## Changes in this run

1. **Corrected the tensor unit over a noncomplete base.** `PR.5/formal-affine-complete-qcoh` extends to an arbitrary ring R with an ideal J generated by a finite regular sequence. Its unit is Λ_J R. The preceding statement incorrectly said it had the same unit as the complete-prism case. The API, proof and reader now distinguish the two. The new `FormalAffineQCoh.noncompleteBaseUnit` check uses R=Z and J=(p): its unit is Z_p, while Z is not even an object of the derived p-complete category. Bounded prisms still have unit A. The empty regular sequence recovers the ordinary affine comparison.
2. **Added `PR.1/laurent-relative-frobenius-cosimplicial-lemma`.** BS22 Lemma 5.4 is polynomial, while R6 row 20's logarithmic consumer uses k[G] for a free abelian group G. The extension is now a separate target with an explicit proof. Pointed residue representatives for G/pG split the relative Frobenius semicosimplicially; the augmented Čech nerve of k→k[G/pG] is split, and its homotopy survives tensoring with arbitrary cosimplicial coefficients. The proof treats arbitrary-rank G directly, without reducing an arbitrary coefficient module to finitely many variables. Four checks distinguish the empty group, negative exponents, failure of codegeneracy compatibility, and nonflat coefficients. The polynomial target is unchanged. R6 row 20 and both reader references now use the extension; the log Cartier-type and grading arguments remain the consumer's own.
3. **Made finite-coordinate approximation precise.** In `PR.6/framed-algebras-over-flat-bases`, descend an étale presentation and enlarge its finite coordinate set to include the representative of the particular element f. Only then does uniqueness of étale lifting show that all other γ_s fix f modulo I^(n+1). The preceding text descended the presentation without explicitly including those element coordinates. The older summability target now acknowledges the flat-base theorem's proved range while retaining the q-PD-envelope and monoid-chart gaps.
4. **Synchronized coverage, provenance and reader.** The reader has the effective inventory, the new theorem and corrected APIs/tests, and says explicitly that the part-prerequisite edits remain unapplied. Source metadata records the exact fresh readings below. The Lean file adds only a reader-target comment: the missing enhanced carriers are not fabricated at the pins.
