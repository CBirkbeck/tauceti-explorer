### R1: why the requested triple cannot be preserved

For a smooth projective geometrically integral variety over a real closed field, an invariant line bundle has a descent obstruction represented by the square of a conjugate-linear isomorphism. Global units are scalars. After rescaling, that square is either plus or minus one. In the negative case its action on each coherent cohomology group has square minus one. Writing it as `v ↦ M v̄`, the determinant identity forces every such group's dimension to be even. Thus its Euler characteristic is even. Riemann–Roch makes the Euler characteristic of a torsion line bundle equal to that of the structure sheaf. Odd structure-sheaf Euler characteristic therefore forces descent. Hilbert 90 gives injectivity of Picard restriction, including preservation of the torsion order. This works for all torsion, all dimensions and all real closed fields under the stated smooth projective geometric-integrality hypotheses; no real-point assumption is used.

Vanishing higher coherent cohomology gives Euler characteristic one. Consequently a different construction cannot satisfy all three requirements in R1. The issue expressly permits deleting false statements. The correction is stronger than the existing calculation excluding just the printed Campedelli construction; the two arguments are kept distinct. This is an elementary deduction explained in the README, not a result attributed to Benoist–Wittenberg Example 4.4.

For a valid obstruction, the displayed intersection of two diagonal quadrics in projective three-space is an empty-real smooth geometrically integral genus-one curve `B`. Distinct diagonal coefficients prove smoothness; the complete-intersection resolution and adjunction establish connectedness and genus. Benoist–Wittenberg I, Lemma 3.7, p.50, provides a non-descending invariant two-torsion class. Its proof explicitly constructs order two, rather than merely unspecified two-primary order. Pullback to `B × ℙ¹` retains this failure because the projection has a real section. The surface has `q=1`, `p_g=0`, Euler characteristic zero. Corollary 3.17(ii), p.56, and Corollary 3.11, p.52, give the even-genus obstruction and intermediate index two. This supplies actual defining equations and the necessary proof route, with no vanishing claim that contradicts descent.

### R2: what was verified

Künnemann 1996, §5, equations (15)–(17), pp.786–787, gives the orthogonal-lift domain and Conjecture 5.2(i)–(ii). The paired codimensions are `p` and `d+1-p`. The positivity statement has sign `(-1)^p`, height argument `L^(d+1-2p)x`, and primitive condition `L^(d+2-2p)x=0`. In middle dimension `d=2r-1`, `p=r`, this is precisely the kernel of `L`. The curve normalization in Theorem 6.1, p.788, divides raw intersection height by the number-field degree and gives the negative canonical Jacobian–dual-Jacobian pairing after the displayed principal polarization.

Künnemann 2001, §1, pp.505–507, independently supplies the regular-model and orthogonal-lift hypotheses, finite-extension multiplier and independence argument, and the unconditional abelian-variety construction after finite extension. The README's general construction is conditional on the model arithmetic-intersection pairing and orthogonal-lift surjectivity. It does not deduce higher arithmetic intersections from ordinary Chow intersections or arithmetic degrees of line bundles. The abelian theorem does not remove these conditions for arbitrary varieties.

Strict positivity on rational vectors does not imply strict positivity after complexification for a real-valued rational bilinear form. The README's explicit form on `ℚ²`, obtained by squaring `u-√2v`, has no nonzero rational zero but has a nonzero real radical vector. The complex predicate therefore remains separately formulated. This also avoids silently turning Li–Liu Remark 1.10's weak inequality into a strict positivity assertion.

Read Li–Liu Notation 2.5(C3), p.14, equation (6.1), pp.28–29, Proposition 6.10 and Definition 6.11 with footnote 10, p.32, and Remark 1.10, p.8. Their local vanishing condition is stronger than geometric homological triviality. The factor two at archimedean places, the positive level-volume factor, and the initial `ℂ ⊗ℚ ℚ_ℓ` target are retained. Its complex-valued interpretation in the computed case is a conclusion of the height formula; equality with the absolute model normalization would require a separate comparison. No such equality is asserted.
