## Changes in this run

Added three concrete fixtures to the package Suggested file and explained
their construction in the README. These cover five previously absent test
names, with additional characteristic equations and inverse checks:

- `ReducibilitySL2Checks`: SL₂ acts on K² through Mathlib's determinant-one
  matrices and `toLin'`. Parabolics are SL₂ itself and every line stabilizer;
  a proper parabolic's Levis stabilize that line and a complement. For
  algebraically closed K, `cr_torus` tests complete reducibility of the
  diagonal torus and failure of irreducibility. `cr_unipotent` tests the upper
  root subgroup's containment in the standard Borel and failure of containment
  in **every** Levi of that Borel. The cyclic image generated by the upper
  unipotent is tested separately over a characteristic-zero K; it is not
  silently replaced by the entire root subgroup.
- `CoarseSL2TupleChecks`: the regular coordinate algebra of SL₂ⁿ is the
  polynomial quotient by the determinant-one relation in each factor.
  Conjugation uses one universal SL₂ matrix in the acting tensor factor and
  all n parameter matrices in the other factor. Evaluation and conjugation
  have coordinate characteristic equations. The curve with upper entries
  kᵢt² contracts the powers of the standard unipotent simultaneously.
  `projected_unipotent` asserts equality of **all** regular invariant
  evaluations in **every** tuple arity with those of the trivial parameter.
  `semisimple_unipotent` combines this with failure of the containing-Levi
  criterion and failure of conjugacy of the lifts. This is the explicit
  Q=1, characteristic-zero specialization; it does not export a replacement
  general IHG pseudocharacter carrier.
- `RankOneTupleChecks`: use the actual torus coordinate algebra R[ℤⁿ].
  Reindexing pushes weights forward by summing over fibres; multiplication
  pulls them back. `projected_rank_one` evaluates every Laurent monomial at a
  character Γ→Aˣ over any commutative R and R-algebra A. The tuple and component
  relations have signatures. Positive and negative weights recover the unit
  and its inverse. `recoverCharacter` and `characterEquiv` record the inverse
  construction, with whole-family injectivity and an inverse test.
