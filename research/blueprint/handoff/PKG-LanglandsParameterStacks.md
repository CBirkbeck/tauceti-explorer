## New affine construction and where to resume

`IntegralCocycleScheme` now denotes the genuine spectrum of an explicit
coordinate-algebra quotient, rather than an arbitrary supplied family of
coordinates. Its inputs are a commutative Hopf R-algebra C, finite generators
I, a finite relator set in `FreeGroup I`, and an algebraic action of the
presented group through the opposite group of units of bialgebra endomorphisms
of C. This imports the existing convolution group of points; it does not
redefine the reductive-group model or IHG invariant coordinates.

The new block begins with `section FreeCrossed`, followed by
`namespace IntegralCocycleScheme`, before the continuous-cocycle section.
It provides:

- `CrossedCocycle.fromGenerators` and its generator equivalence;
- `pointAction`, its coordinate-pullback formula and coefficient naturality;
- the commutative-algebra coproduct `tupleCoordinates`, universal generator
  points and tuple evaluation;
- `freeCocycle` and the explicit ideal generated by c(r)(a)−1(a), retaining
  nilpotents;
- `coordinateRing`, its `scheme`, `universalCocycle`, natural `pointsEquiv`
  and the universal evaluation formula;
- the finite-generator description of the relator ideal and finite
  presentation when C is finitely presented over R;
- a concrete gauge coaction constructed from the universal cocycle,
  evaluation against H-points, counit and coassociativity signatures;
- `baseChange` as the spectrum of S⊗[R]coordinateRing, with its point
  equivalence constructed using Mathlib's tensor adjunction;
- `presentationEquiv` with an evaluation formula transporting the action
  along an isomorphism of presented groups;
- free-group, trivial-group and tame-torus examples, plus algebraic
  inversion and twisted-gauge tests. The q=3 characteristic-two torus
  fibre explicitly requires t−1≠0 and (t−1)²=0.

The torus uses the imported Laurent-polynomial Hopf algebra. Its two-coordinate
ring is divided by t^(q−1)−1, without taking a radical. The relator uses
geometric Frobenius σ⁻¹τσ=τ^q. Presentation independence here concerns two
presentations of the **same abstract group**, not arbitrary choices of dense
Weil subgroup over Z[1/p]. The latter comparison still needs the continuous
extension theorem over Z_l.

The accepted `scheme_l_adic` example is still missing from Lean: it must use
the actual relatively discrete condensed coefficient functor, not merely an
ordinary tensor or continuous-map shadow. Specializing these affine signatures
to the supplied dual-group coordinate algebra and actual finite-wild Weil
presentation is also still required. No generic finite-presentation theorem
asserts the Weil-specific dimension, flatness or local-complete-intersection
results.

The README explains these coordinate equations in LP1.1. Its upstream links
now use the directory layout of current TauCetiRoadmap main: roadmap folders
are at the repository root, without an extra `TauCetiRoadmap/` component.
