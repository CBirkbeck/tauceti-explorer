Each is an *Assembly note* in the document; none changes a packet.

1. **The H4 packet's request for H3:smooth-duality is partly answered by the H0 packet.** The H4 planning job and its review read the H0 packet at its 192-node checkpoint. The H0 packet's last pass added 29 general-dimension trace and duality nodes to H3 — the nodes the H0 review proposes to move to H3:smooth-duality. Of the request: (a) Hub96 5.7.2 is planned only for curves (`H3/algebraic-curve-comparison`); (b) the trace in every dimension is `H3/smooth-relative-trace` for partially proper rigid morphisms, with composition, the d = 0 counit, base change and the d = 1 curve normalisation, and duality is `H3/berkovich-derived-duality-interface` on the Berkovich side with adic transports for proper f; (c) Berkovich 7.4.9 is `H3/smooth-proper-relative-local-system-duality` for proper f and 𝔽_ℓ.
