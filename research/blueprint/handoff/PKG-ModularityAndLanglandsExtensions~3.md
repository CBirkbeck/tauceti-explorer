Three source inconsistencies matter directly to these constructions. Kim's pp.156–157 assertion about simultaneous inert primes fails for the three quadratic subextensions of a biquadratic extension. The revised proof instead constructs infinitely many degree-p extensions for each fixed good split place, with fresh auxiliary ramification, and uses the lexicographically maximal local complexity/first prime. Thus the finite exceptional set in descent cannot remove that good place's entire subsequence. Henniart 2009's printed exponent two for the exterior-square central character fails the eigenvalue calculation: the exponent is three. Wang's p.5 value +6 at −I for the dimension-six representation conflicts with its tensor description: its central sign is negative. These are local negative controls in this package, not a separate errata job.

The solvable projective-image branch of §4.13 first enlarges the scalar centre to include determinant square roots and then decomposes on the binary-polyhedral central product. Restricting the resulting decomposition handles central extensions which were not themselves binary groups. Its only Artin modularity input beyond characters is the solvable **two-dimensional** theorem; dimension three/four constituents use explicit Sym²/Sym³ transfers. No general higher-rank solvable Artin theorem is assumed.
