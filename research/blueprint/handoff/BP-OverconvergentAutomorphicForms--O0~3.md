  scalar core. These are prerequisites, not the analytic coefficient node.

  formula. It reduces each finite-level ring by the ideal generated by p^m,
  forms the directed ring colimit at that precision, then takes the subring

  restriction from compatible maps of ring towers, localization at p and a
  constant-tower equivalence with Mathlib `AdicCompletion`. Four examples
  check precision compatibility, representative independence in level,
  identity restriction and the constant-tower comparison. No level/precision
  interchange is made. These affine algebraic objects do not supply the
  ordinary completed structural sheaf or its analytic comparison.
