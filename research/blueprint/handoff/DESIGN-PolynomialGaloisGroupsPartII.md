## What the design supplies

* Bounded equal-part merging preserves occurrences, includes fixed points, and is not declared transitive. The cycle-over-fibres proof supplies a genuine merging witness.
* The ramified bridge uses the existing arithmetic Frobenius theorem without an unramified-prime hypothesis. Integral reduction fibres count root multiplicities, and the maximum counts normalized irreducible factors rather than repeated degrees.
* Higher irreducibility uses actual polynomial quotients over fields generated by ordered distinct root tuples. It retains separability and positive degree, rejects zero-fold and over-degree predicates, imports the existing k=2 adapter, and uses normal-extension restriction onto the quotient Galois group.
* The arbitrary partition law has three quantified measure bounds, including the pair diagonal and occurrence-sensitive lower tails. Every probability theorem has a separate probability-measure hypothesis.
* The primitive minimum-degree chain includes the graph proof for the non-doubly-transitive case and the commutator-counting proof for the doubly-transitive case. The latter uses the read source's n≥38 restriction and handles smaller primitive degrees through explicit small-support recognition.
* The imprimitive proof uses five middle-range block orbits and four large-range block orbits. This avoids all possible companion cycles longer than n/4 before applying the small-part gcd event. The strengthened four-part event has the required probability estimate.
* The final criterion is uniform in the partition law and merging exponent. Concrete coefficient distributions are consumers in ArithmeticStatisticsPartIIRandomPolynomials, never inputs to this criterion.
