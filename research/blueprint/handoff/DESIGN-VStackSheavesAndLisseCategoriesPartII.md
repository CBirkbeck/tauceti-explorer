## Decisions a reviewer should check

1. **Proper means proper nice.** Scholze's proper maps of v-stacks are 0-truncated (Sch17 Definition 10.7(iii), Remark 10.8, Definition 18.1), so classifying-stack maps are never proper. HKW Theorem 4.1.8 claims Rf_! ≅ Rf_* for every proper fine map, but GHW (arXiv v1, the only public version) does not state it, and representability of proper fine maps in locally spatial diamonds is not established (0-truncated decent v-sheaves need not be diamonds, e.g. Spd Z_p; GHW Proposition 4.3(i) leaves the related claim as an exercise; Sch17 Proposition 13.4(v) reduces it to representability in diamonds). The plan takes proper 2-morphisms and proper pushforwards to be proper nice (proper, representable in locally spatial diamonds, locally of finite dim.trg), which covers every use in HKW; the general case is gap `VS-PII/G-lsep-representability` and source issue E2.
2. **The generic trace prefix is owned here** (VS9/dualizable-object-and-trace, trace-functoriality, symmetric-monoidal-grothendieck-construction). The HKW brief and the Abe/Yang–Zhao briefs want one home shared with `EtaleDualityAndPerverseSheavesPartIIRelativeTraces`, but that continuation does not exist (PAPER-ABE-25 and PAPER-YANG-ZHAO-25 are both at verdict `revise`). Restructure proposal 1 asks that continuation to import these nodes.
3. **Liu–Zheng's DESCENT machinery has no owner.** EnhancedDerivedSheaves E3 owns only Liu–Zheng §2. Gap `VS-PII/G-liu-zheng` and restructure proposal 2 (an EnhancedDerivedSheaves Part II) record it; VS7/enhanced-operations-for-qcqs-v-sheaves and VS7/fine-shriek-functors rest on it.
4. **GHW numbering.** HKW cite GHW numbers (Definitions 4.1, 4.3, Propositions 4.10, 4.11, 4.17) that do not exist in arXiv:2202.12467v1; the plan cites v1's numbering throughout (source issue E3).
5. **SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups** is now a Tau Ceti roadmap (TauCetiRoadmap main, commit 0d144d04). It is cited through the node identifiers of its atlas plan (SR.0:derived-extension/derived-smooth-dual, compact-generation, derived-invariants, averaging-projector; SR.1/locally-constant-compact-support, a-valued-haar-measure, integration, convolution-algebra; SR.2/smooth-induction, compact-induction, frobenius-reciprocity, modulus-character), which match the upstream README's SR.0, SR.0d, SR.1 and SR.2 targets of the same names; nothing it plans is re-planned.
