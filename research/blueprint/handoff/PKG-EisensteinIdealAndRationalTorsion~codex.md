## Source reading and corrections

The substantive primary-source passages were read directly: Mazur 1977 II §§5, 9–18 and III §§1, 3–5, the component calculation of Mazur–Rapoport's appendix; Mazur 1978 §§2–4; Mazur–Tate 1973 §§2–4; and Mazur–Wiles 1984 §8.I, printed pp. 187–188. The public level-13 model data and Bruin–Derickx–Stoll §3, p. 3, were read for the model and cusps, not for a circular rank-zero argument. No unread Ogg or Masley table is used to justify the numerical inputs. All statements in the repository are in our own words.

Corrections relative to the input and source-sensitive choices:

- The generic quotient is an actual connected quotient by the kernel of adic completion; `J/IJ` would be zero because I contains n and [n] is surjective. The full order and its cuspidal image ideal are kept distinct.
- Mazur's global α counts geometric residue-field points, not rational residue-field points. The latter can shrink under an unramified twist and breaks the cohomology calculation. δ likewise uses the geometric P-fibre.
- Mazur–Wiles supplies the missing ordinary Weil-duality argument in the proof of Mazur II Corollary 16.3, replaces the invalid divisor-class manipulation in the quadratic modular-unit argument by a Siegel-product square identity, and corrects the marked-generator/diamond contribution to the X₁ Hecke formula. All three changes are reflected in the relevant targets; the invalid argument is not repeated.
- The width-one cusp ∞ is the μ_P identity direction and the width-P cusp 0 is the component direction for the fixed forgetful map. Mazur III §5 Step 3 uses opposite labels to II §1, p. 64; the package consistently uses the latter/moduli convention. This is recorded as a checked convention discrepancy, not attributed to the published Mazur–Wiles errata.
- A finite set of j-values does not give finitely many ℚ-isomorphism classes because of twists. The isogeny contradiction now has a direct local invariant: at 3 the Tate parameter is raised to its P-th power at each μ_P quotient, so its pole order strictly increases.
- Formal immersion retains arbitrary level N over ℤ[1/m], m² the largest square dividing N, including p∥N. The potential-good-reduction corollary retains squarefree N and arbitrary number field K, with e_v<q−1. It is not artificially restricted to prime level or good fibres.
- Mazur II 5.10 is a lemma, 14.2 is a proposition; the Mazur–Tate model/endomorphism calculation is §3, pp. 43–45, and its kernel/descent is §4, pp. 45–48. The modular-curve supplier's level-13 split-Cartan curve is not X₁(13).
- The winding invariant-to-coinvariant map is multiplication by two. The normalized map is defined integrally through the two-term group-ring basis, including at 2; no coefficient 1/2 is inserted into ℤ₂.
