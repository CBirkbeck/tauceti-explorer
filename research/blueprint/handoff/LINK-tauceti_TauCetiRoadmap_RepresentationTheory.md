## A concrete source error affecting the projective route

**Source:** InductionRestriction, Layer 7, “Factor-set classes and the second
cohomology”; also “What is missing (build here)”.

The text says that projective representations are projectively equivalent iff
their factor sets differ by a coboundary. The following paragraph correctly says
that H² classifies factor-set classes, not projective representations; the two
claims must be reconciled in favour of the latter.

Here is a counterexample with the same coefficient field and the same dimension.
Let G=C₂={1,g}, V=ℂ², and use the two normalized genuine linear representations
ρ₀(g)=I₂ and ρ₁(g)=diag(1,−1). In both cases ρ(g)²=I₂ and the factor set is
identically 1, so their factor sets are equal, not merely cohomologous. But their
maps G→PGL₂(ℂ) have kernels G and {1}: diag(1,−1) is not a scalar matrix.
Conjugacy of projective representations preserves the kernel. They are therefore
not equivalent.

**Required correction:** rescaling a chosen lift changes its factor set by a
coboundary; H² classifies factor-set classes and appropriate central extensions.
A fixed factor-set class has its twisted-group-algebra module category, not one
projective representation. Correct the earlier summary that also says H²
classifies projective representations. This is an authored mathematical
counterexample checked directly, **not** a claimed independent review of the whole
child roadmap or a change to another worker's files.
