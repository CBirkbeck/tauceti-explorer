## Scope boundaries and unsupported generalizations

There is no outstanding source-access blocker within the stated scope. The following exclusions are deliberate and visible in Scope and ownership, rather than hidden assumptions:

- Restricted-ramification finiteness for arbitrary connected groups over global function fields is excluded. The package proves the connected statement over number fields; over function fields it needs only proper finite-étale torsor finiteness, including wild degree, with bounded different. No pseudo-reductive classification, exotic characteristic-two case or uncleared Conrad–Gabber–Prasad book is used.
- Function-field Mordell is for smooth curves over arbitrary constants, regular curves over finite constants, and regular curves of geometric genus at least two over arbitrary constants. Nonsmooth geometric-genus-zero-or-one curves over arbitrary infinite constants are excluded. Finite-dimensional Riemann–Roch spaces are never treated as finite sets over infinite constants.
- The owned Gabber extension is for quasi-projective schemes with an ample bundle over an infinite field. The purity proof is smooth characteristic zero. Arbitrary arithmetic ample schemes and general mixed-characteristic absolute purity are outside this proof route.
- Compact local evaluation partitions establish existence. They do not promise a terminating Brauer solver, certified enumeration, an effective Mordell height bound or an effective threshold for arithmetic Nakai. Conjectures remain propositions, not theorems.
