PR #2746) says those are mis-assigned, and an earlier worker declined the job on that ground rather
than choose between the two records.

I delivered both nodes, as the job requires, and recorded the conflict in the packet's `restructure`
list with the evidence: `Polylogarithms:P.1` plans exactly this function and this identity, in its own
words, while V.3's stage text plans nothing analytic and mentions the dilogarithm only in a warning.

five-term identity from P.1 rather than rebuilding them; what they add is the descent to `P(ℂ)` that
V.3 actually needs, together with the statement that the descended map kills torsion. Neither is
marked as a planet of V.3. If the maintainers accept the restructure entry, the two nodes move to
`Polylogarithms:P.1` with their content intact and V.3 cites them.
