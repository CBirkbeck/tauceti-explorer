## Changes that alter what the roadmap plans

1. **ι-ordinarity is the parent's.** PotentialAutomorphyInfrastructure PA.2 owns the ι-ordinary automorphic representation, its Steinberg criterion and its behaviour under soluble base change, and PA.5 owns soluble base change and descent for GL_n (the parent's packet says so in its `restructure`). The definition node `PL.0/iota-ordinary` is therefore removed (PROTOCOL.md section 15) and replaced by the theorem `PL.0/iota-ordinary-principal-series` (Thorne 2015 Lemma 2.3; Clozel–Thorne Lemma 2.5; Geraghty Lemmas 5.1.1, 5.1.3), which imports the parent's definition. `PL.0/soluble-descent`, `PL.0/steinberg-weight-zero-iota-ordinary` and the two ordinarity comparisons import the PA.2 and PA.5 nodes. No other packet referred to `PL.0/iota-ordinary`.
2. **Hida classicality** (`PL.2/hida-classicality`): Geraghty's Lemma 2.6.4 is a statement about Hecke algebras after tensoring with the residue field of an arithmetic prime, onto the Hecke algebra of the isotypic part for the finite-order character, with nilpotent kernel. The earlier statement (the whole level-c space, integrally) was wrong.
3. **The definite unitary group** (`PL.2/definite-unitary-group`): the condition 4 | n[L⁺ : ℚ] is automatic for n even and unnecessary for n odd once L/L⁺ is unramified; the division algebra variant with S(B) ≠ ∅ used by Thorne 2015 and Allen–Newton–Thorne is now described.
4. **Primitivity** (`PL.6/primitive-representation`, `PL.6/genericity-under-restriction`): the proof of Thorne 2015 Proposition 5.3 uses that the residual representation is not the *semisimplification* of a properly induced representation, which is more than primitivity. The plan adds `IsStronglyPrimitive` and states the proposition with it; a gap records what this means for the theorems of PL.7.
5. **Reducibility ideals** (`PL.6/reducibility-ideal`): confirmed that Allen–Newton–Thorne define the determinant reducibility ideal as a product over two-block partitions inside P_𝒮, distinct from Thorne's split ideal in R^univ.
6. **Characteristic-zero lifts** (`PL.4/characteristic-zero-lifts`, `PL.7/global-lifts-schur`): BLGGT14 Proposition 1.5.1 needs H⁰(G_{F⁺,S}, ad r̄(1)) = 0, which holds for r̄|G_{F(ζ_l)} absolutely irreducible and ζ_l ∉ F; Bellovin–Gee Corollary 5.1.1 was read and its use under the Schur property is justified, with the hypotheses p > 2 and F₀ ⊄ F₀⁺(ζ_p) added.
7. **Relaxed adequacy** (`PL.4/relaxed-adequacy`): every use of adequacy in the proofs of Thorne 2017 Propositions 2.21, 7.1, 7.2, Theorems 4.2, 5.1 and Corollaries 4.3, 7.3 was listed; none needs more than H¹(H, k) = 0, H¹(H, ad) = 0 and the trace condition. The former gap is closed.
8. **Geraghty's Lemma 5.9** (published numbering) is, by the cross-citations, Lemma 5.2.1 of the preprint; it assumes level prime to l. `PL.0/ordinary-implies-iota-ordinary` keeps the hypothesis 'level potentially prime to l', and the former gap is closed.
9. Smaller corrections of statements and proofs in about seventy nodes (multiplier conventions, residual versus characteristic-zero hypotheses, which Selmer group and which ring in PL.8, the Ihara-avoidance step in the ordinary R = T theorem, the standing assumptions of Liu–Tian–Xiao–Zhang–Zhu and Le–Le Hung–Levin–Morra). Every citation now gives section, numbered statement and page of the version read, and says in the plan's own words what the place states.
