## Mathematical addition

The namespace is `TauCeti.QuantumTopology.GenericQuantum`.

- `word_append` and `wordDegree_append` give multiplication of concrete quotient
  words and of their degrees. `component_one_mem`, `component_generator_mem`
  and `component_mul_mem` prove unit, generator and product membership in the
  actual word-span components. Multiplication uses binary span induction.
- `evenCarrier A d` is the ℂ(q)-linear span of words whose parity degrees lie
  in `QuantumParityGroup.evenSubgroup (gram A d)`, the subgroup generated by
  the central sign and the e-degrees. It is neither an arbitrary supplied
  subspace nor the root-weight-zero component.
- `evenCarrier_one_mem` and `evenCarrier_mul_mem` prove closure. `evenAlgebra`
  bundles this as an actual `Subalgebra QuantumRationalFunctions (Algebra A d)`;
  its scalar inclusion follows from scalar multiplication of the unit.
- `evenAlgebra_toSubmodule` proves equality with the supremum of the homogeneous
  components indexed by that subgroup. This word-span equality and closure
  require no quantum PBW theorem or assumed direct-sum decomposition.
- `evenAlgebra_E_mem`, `evenAlgebra_FK_mem` and `evenAlgebra_K_square_mem` prove
  the three generator tests. FK has degree e⁻¹ because K̇²=1. E has degree
  v̇^d ė and can have nonzero root weight.
- `rankOne_K_not_even` states the complementary rank-one exclusion. It is the
  sole new `sorry`: it needs the parity/PBW comparison and is a suggested
  theorem, not an implemented result. It distinguishes the even algebra from
  the incorrect full-algebra definition. The membership tests have proofs.
