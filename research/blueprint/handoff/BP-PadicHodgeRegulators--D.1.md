### Mathematical decisions a reviewer should check first

1. **GSWZ Theorem 9 (D.3).** The proof's gap (GSWZ E39: "the image is contained in p²O_{K_p}" asserted without proof, and D_p not defined on the completed group) is repaired: D_p := ε·log_BK ∘ c_{2,1} on K₃(L; Z_p); its image is exactly p²O_L by the integral Bloch–Kato logarithm `L1/integral-logarithm-unramified` (log_BK H¹(L, Z_p(r)) = (r−1)!·p^r·O_L·e_r for L unramified, p odd, 2 ≤ r ≤ p−2), derived from Benois–Nguyen Quang Do (Ann. ENS 2002, open on numdam; index form verbatim in Lemme 1.3.2 + Théorème 2.1, lattice form from §2.3.2 and Proposition 2.2.4 — recorded as a gap because no open source states the lattice form in one line). Generation by roots of unity then follows from the spanning theorem; for products the spanning needs the difference argument (`D.3/residue-spanning` (b)–(c); new source issue E102).
2. **D_p versus the dilogarithm (new source issue E101).** GSWZ (19) says Coleman's D_p "coincides with the p-adic regulator map as shown in Besser–de Jeu [7, Thm.1.6(2)]". BdJ prove this only on classes presented by special units of the valuation ring (Theorems 1.6(2), 1.10) and on cyclotomic elements (1.12); the general statement is their Conjecture 1.14. The packet therefore defines D_p as the regulator and uses the dilogarithm formula only on special-unit presentations at p, which covers GSWZ's uses (Lemma 3.1, Example 4.3: all z_i and 1 − z_i there are global units of norm ±1 — checked).
3. **Normalisations.** Besser's syntomic regulator η∘c^syn = log_BK ∘ Soulé's regulator with no constant (Huber–Kings Prop. 2.3.4, Tamme Cor. 5.19/5.21, NN Props. 4.13, 5.7; Besser 2000 itself was not readable); BdJ's Definition 4.6 identification agrees with HK's η; Gros = (1 − σ/p²)·Besser, so Gros values at roots of unity are units while D_p(ζ) ∈ p²O. For curves (D.5), regP = Θ∘reg_syn = log_BK∘r^ét and regSynCan = (1 − φ/q²)·regP; the Coleman-integral symbol formula holds for regP.
