## What this packet is

The roadmap had a **reviewed integrated decomposition** of Scholze's *Perfectoid spaces*, *Étale cohomology of
diamonds* and Gabber–Ramero's *Almost ring theory*. All 33 node identifiers are kept with their statements,
hypotheses, proof steps, acceptance tests and verified locators; what is added is prerequisites resolved to the
pinned libraries or to a named supplier stage, an API outline with unit tests for each of the five definitions
and six constructions, 20 planets, and a coverage record per layer. RS-05 is accepted and binding, and the
layers are planned only as far as it keeps.

## The thing a reviewer should check first: there is no library audit, so the boundary was drawn by hand

`data/library-coverage.json` has **no entry for any layer of PerfectoidSpaces**. The protocol says to plan only
what the pinned libraries do not have, so the declaration index was read directly for this packet. What is
already there is not negligible:

- **Mathlib**: `PreTilt` (the inverse limit along Frobenius of the quotient by `p`), `PreTilt.untilt`,
  `WittVector.fontaineTheta` as a ring homomorphism, **`surjective_fontaineTheta`**, and `BDeRhamPlus`.
- **Tau Ceti**: `TauCeti.Huber.Pair`, `TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.spa`, `TauCeti.ValuationSpectrum.rationalSubset`
  with a full basis / covering / refinement / perturbation theory, and the localization with its universal
  property.

Two consequences, both recorded in the packet:

1. The node on Fontaine's `θ` asserts two things — surjectivity, and that the kernel is generated by a primitive
   element of degree one. **The first is already proved in Mathlib.** The node's baseline citation says so, and
   what it plans is the kernel half.
2. The rational-localization and tilting-homeomorphism nodes build on Tau Ceti's adic-space development rather
   than rebuilding adic geometry; the baseline notes say which object each one uses.

A reviewed audit would put this on a firmer footing, and it is recorded as a gap. It is worth scheduling before
the P8 part of this roadmap is planned.
