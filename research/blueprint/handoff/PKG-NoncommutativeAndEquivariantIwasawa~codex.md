## What changed

1. **Completed coefficients and subgroup compatibility.** Current PadicMeasuresIwasawaAlgebras §1.7 already supplies finite free continuous coefficient extension for a profinite group with a countable quotient tower, including ramified coefficient DVRs. The previous procyclic-only description was stale. The boundary now consumes that general comparison. NE.0.3 adds its naturality for the subgroup inclusion and the finite coset basis, proved using finite quotient Dirac formulas. Its residual finite-field comparison needs no countability assumption. The map is covariant on completed group algebras; it is not restriction of a measure to an arbitrary closed subset. Checks include index one, the trivial group, ramified coefficients and residual extension, including p=2.
2. **Arithmetic is not supplied by the measure package.** PadicMeasures §§3.1, 5.1 and 5.2 give abstract commutative pseudomeasures, commutative determinant lines and compact inverse-limit exactness. They do not construct normalized arithmetic pseudomeasures, Selmer complexes or derived arithmetic base change. Those falsely attributed imports are removed. IntegralIwasawaTheory I.6/I.9 and the cochain comparisons remain explicit requirements.
3. **Odd-primary weak Leopoldt.** NE.4.4 now states the vanishing with trivial discrete ℚ_p/ℤ_p coefficients for every number field F and every algebraic E between F_cyc and F_Σ, with p odd and Σ containing p and infinity. It includes cyclotomic descent and the passage to arbitrary overfields by continuous cocycles and restriction. Total reality, analytic Galois group and μ=0 are not hypotheses of this theorem. Wingberg Proposition 5.1, pp.348–349, and Venjakob Remark 4.3, preprint p.26, replace the incorrect inference from a cohomological-dimension bound. NE.4.5 separates H²=0 from higher-degree vanishing. This is a proof plan, not a formalization. The added proof treats odd p; it does not certify Wingberg's two-primary generality or replace the real-place comparison of NE.4.8.
4. **Filtered transfer hypotheses.** NE.0.5 separates noetherian/domain transfer, the numerical global-dimension comparison, and Auslander regularity. It now records the finite weighted-ideal closure condition of Venjakob Definition 3.19 and Theorem 3.21, pp.16–17. Complete does not mean compact, and finite graded global dimension alone does not imply the Auslander condition. Schneider–Teitelbaum Proposition 1.1, pp.4–5, and Lemma 1.4, p.6, give accessible primary statements for noetherian lifting and strict ideal generators. The original filtered-resolution proof for the general numerical bound remains an obligation; it is not silently attributed to Venjakob Theorem 3.21. Björk's *Filtered Noetherian Rings* is the 1987 work cited there.
5. **Uniform smoothing remains a gap.** NE.6.25 now puts the smoothing element in the abelian group G_S=Gal(K_S/K) used by Ritter–Weiss. Its quantifiers are explicit: one g with nontrivial cyclotomic image for the whole measure identity, hence the same g in all coefficient tests. Two proved Lean examples give a negative control for the proposed compactness argument: all finite intersections of pⁿℤ_p contain a nonzero element, but the full intersection is zero. This does not refute the published Ritter–Weiss theorem. It shows why conductor-dependent choices and compactness do not finish this proof route.
6. **Actual numerical K-theory imports.** The boundary now identifies GeneralAlgebraicKTheory §§4.6, 4.15–4.17, 6.19 and 10.6, with their precise scopes. Their resolution, additive triple sequence, Gillet–Waldhausen and Morita results do not provide the missing determinant and Ore-fibre comparisons.
7. **Suggested file.** Retained all four light definitions and all seven named theorems. Added only the two proved compactness controls. Converted layer comments to ordinary block comments so that there is one module docstring, and removed an errata-process reference from a declaration's documentation. Heavy carriers remain in the README and its one closing inventory comment.
