## Exact work required for closure

Begin with the reader’s dependency boundaries and the packet’s `requests` and `gaps`; do not replace enhanced objects by ordinary inverse limits of homotopy categories.

- **G-enhancement**: The native libraries have ordinary DerivedCategory and scheme sites, but not the required enhanced stable/presentable v-sheaf, derived-completion or coherent coefficient-system carriers. E0–E4 declarations read are planning evidence, not implemented APIs. The suggested file gives concrete native Scheme prototypes and an ordinary categorical mate prototype; enhanced signatures, their API lemmas and examples are omitted with a per-name inventory, as Protocol §13 requires when their conditions cannot be stated. A follow-up must replace the inventory with genuine supplied types and elaborate every omitted signature without dummy Prop fields. The P¹ base-field test is represented only by a generic base-change proper/lfp signature; its concrete chart identification needs the projective-space supplier.
- **G-support**: A complete proof must verify the finite relative cohomological bound on the proper/fp models, its local uniformity under base change, exact tensor/scalar-change comparisons for larger torsion coefficients, left-completed Postnikov extension and enhanced coherence of choice-independence. The source sketches these reductions; classical noetherian support cannot simply be asserted on every unbounded complex.
- **G-local**: ECD 27.6 says the local complexes can be computed and sketches a dimension induction. Supply the explicit Kummer/cycle-class comparison on all strata, twists and residue maps, smooth-chart induction, and proof that the remaining cone is supported on finitely many closed points. The proof cannot be inferred from an isomorphism of abstract cohomology ranks.
- **G-trait**: Identify the analytic/diamond closed stalk with RΓ(IK,M), with residual Gk action, and prove that the natural comparison equals the scheme-side cocycle map. H0 continuous group cohomology alone supplies neither this geometric identification nor its compatibility.
- **G-test**: Fresh reading confirms ECD 24.4 assumes analytic source and target, whereas Spa(O,O) is nonanalytic. 27.5 asserts the relative test-space smoothness by that citation. The precise extension is requested from S5, with a direct proof as an alternative; it is not treated as an established consequence of 24.4.
- **G-proper-descent**: A proper alteration is not an étale-site covering map. SF.2 must export geometric proper hypercover cohomological descent for these invertible torsion coefficients, and E2 then supplies the enhanced cartesian totalisation. No division by the alteration degree is allowed.
- **G-rational**: The defined scalar-localised integral lattice category exists once its enhancement is supplied. BS 6.8.14 identifies it with constructible rational sheaves on its topologically noetherian schemes. No source read establishes the analogous all-constructible-Qℓ identification for arbitrary small v-stacks; request a separate lattice/constructibility proof before making that assertion.
- **G-owners**: RT-AREA-algebraicgeometry/16 and RT-AREA-etale/21 prescribe opposite owners. Accepted RS-25 keeps source-scoped alterations in SF.4, and Protocol §15 forbids duplicate owners. This packet therefore imports SF.4 and proposes rescoped L5 tasks 4–5. Maintainer decision is required to supersede RS-25 if a new alterations stage is preferred; both prescriptions cannot be applied simultaneously.

The scheme-support extension must use the existing SF.2 carrier: descend finite data to noetherian models, establish higher-cohomology continuity and uniform support bounds, then extend coherently to coefficient towers and unbounded complexes. Exactness of finite-level functors alone is insufficient. The Gm continuity formula is a colimit of the system of Gm sheaves, not an identification of each individual inverse image with Gm on the limit. Abe’s stronger Hom formula uses F bounded above constructible and G bounded below; the category equivalence is for bounded constructible complexes with noetherian coefficients.

For the Boxer–Pilloni refinements, use the ambient compactification’s boundary ideal for the blow-up. For bundle extension, split the finitely many constant-rank pieces, take their disjoint schematic closures, extend coherently and apply Tag 0ESN with schematic density and constant rank. This avoids an L2→L5 flattening cycle. Parallel-arrow equalisation is an explicit cofilteredness obligation; the fp version is constructed after noetherian descent to avoid a non-fp equaliser over a noncoherent base.
