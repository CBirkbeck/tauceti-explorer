## Nine-stage input/output audit

**Layer 0.** Start with a preadditive category and a two-sided additive morphism ideal. The quotient has universal properties before any Frobenius assumption. The ideal generated by factorizations is its additive closure; zero, finite-biproduct and isomorphism closure are needed to combine an ideal element into one factorization. Exact structures enter separately. Outputs include relative predicates, Frobenius equality/enoughness, stable quotients and the generic finite-dimensional Frobenius-functional/self-injectivity interface. R-RELATIVE identifies the foundational ownership ambiguity; no arbitrary ideal quotient is declared triangulated.

**Layer 1.** Frobenius exactness and admissible pushout/pullback calculus produce suspension, syzygy, choice independence and Happel triangles. A conflation-exact functor descends as a triangle functor only with preservation of projective-injectives. The new GEF input supplies the exact-functor calculus, not this additional preservation hypothesis.

**Layer 2.** Appropriate finite-dimensional self-injectivity or two-sided noetherian/Iwanaga-Gorenstein conditions support the stable-module, totally acyclic and singularity comparisons. Every resolving term has its own finite-generation condition. The consumer proves closure of Gorenstein-projectives before applying the newly recorded induced-exact-structure input. General `D^b(E)` requires exact-acyclic localization owned here, not the narrower GEF K0 comparison. Finite resolutions do not supply bi-infinite total acyclicity.

**Layer 3.** Additive categories support degreewise-split homotopy constructions; the abelian/exact hypotheses needed for homology and localization remain explicit. Outputs are positive-period cyclic complexes, differential modules and their homotopy/derived categories. Parity forgetting has no canonical inverse. Contractibility, homological acyclicity and vanishing after localization are not interchangeable.

**Layer 4.** Boundedness makes folding sums finite. The orbit category has actual direct-sum Hom groups and coherent composition. Its DG-hull comparison imports DGAInfinity's pretriangulated envelope under finite-dimensional/finite-global-dimension conditions. Generation under shifts and cones precedes idempotent completion: `tria` cannot become `Perf`. Projectivity of an underlying module is not automatically homotopical projectivity, and is not the same use of "relative" as R-RELATIVE.

**Layer 5.** Signed graded-algebra and DG-category interfaces support the right-curvature equations `d_A²(a)=aw-wa`, `d_M²(m)=mw`, with opposite curvature `-op(w)`. Common-curvature cancellation makes the Hom differential square-zero; it does not make each curved module an ordinary DG module. The consumer proves shifts, cones, connection-change signs and duplex comparisons, with tensor potentials and handedness visible.

**Layer 6.** The curved homotopy category plus required products/coproducts produces absolute, co- and contra-acyclic closures and their Verdier quotients. Coalgebraic Koszul second-kind categories are distinct instantiations. No canonical co-to-contra comparison or unrestricted agreement with ordinary acyclicity is inferred; graded-dimension and degree restrictions remain.

**Layer 7.** Finite-projective duplexes and separately stated hypersurface assumptions support the matrix-factorization category and Eisenbud/Buchweitz/Orlov comparisons. Distinguish regular-local/nonzerodivisor assumptions from the affine regular/flat-superpotential theorem. Keep zero potential, potential-changing duality and idempotent completion separate.

**Layer 8.** Reconciliation uses constructed functors, kernels/images, structure preservation, hypotheses and coherent equivalences. No pair of different quotients becomes definitionally identical.

GEF Layer 0 specifies exact structures without explicitly packaging those predicates, while GEF Layer 3 already assumes enough projectives for comparison and horseshoe calculus. GEF's boundary forbids dependence on the stable/periodic/curved area. This is an unresolved source-contract inconsistency, not a licence to add a reverse dependency that creates circular ownership.

**Proposal:** GEF Layer 0 owns relative projective/injective predicates on a specified exact structure, enough-relative classes, opposite and exact-equivalence transport, and comparisons with split and canonical-abelian structures. GEF Layer 3 and SPC Layer 0 consume that foundation. SPC retains Frobenius equality, factoring ideals, stable quotients, module examples and triangulation. No source file has been edited to enact this proposal.

The acceptance contract is explicit: a relative projective lifts maps across every admissible deflation; a relative injective extends maps across every admissible inflation. Enoughness produces the appropriate conflations. Opposites exchange the predicates, and exact equivalences preserve them. Every object is relative projective/injective for the split structure; canonical-abelian comparisons recover the ordinary categorical predicates. These checks must use the chosen exact structure, not an unrelated ambient one. Equality of the two predicates and existence of enough objects are separate Frobenius conditions.

The other three overlaps remain **keep**: generic Frobenius functionality versus graph-specific trace calculations; general exact-derived localization versus GEF's explicitly narrower bounded comparisons; and curved-module closures versus coalgebraic second-kind Koszul constructions.
