### Source errata and nontrivial controls

**ALB23 Lemma 5.1.6, p.76 (§1.24).** Boundedness of A/d does not imply the pro-zero condition on ((A/dⁿ)[p]). The preserved square-zero construction starts with T₀=Z_p[[d]], J_n=(p,dⁿ) and M=∏J_n; t acts by the d-weighted right shift. Complete Z_p[d,t] at (p,d), and form its square-zero extension by M, with φ(d)=d^p+p, φ(t)=t^p, φ(M)=0 and δ(a,m)=(δ(a),−a^(p−1)m). The resulting bounded prism has p regular and (A/d)[p^∞] killed by p. After inverting t, the classes z_n=t^(1−n)e_n satisfy dz_(n+1)=z_n and survive modulo p. The degree −1 Milnor term gives nonzero p-torsion in the d-completion B although B/d is étale over A/d. The extra A/p torsion hypothesis in the positive theorem is essential. §1.24 retains the full calculation and its hypotheses in 2,484 bytes. This is an errata candidate for the maintainer; no separate errata job or source file was edited.

**ALB23 Example A.4, p.95 (§0.89).** The square-zero presentation must kill every product x_ij x_kl, not only the squares. Without those relations a mixed product survives and the advertised nonzerodivisor conclusion fails. The annihilation relation has p^(i−1)f rather than p^if. The roadmap retains the corrected ring and negative controls in its own words.

**Proper log parameter control (§§8.76a–8.77).** Work on the PD thickening D(P) for every fs P, not just a single parameter. Positive heights give contraction of the augmentation PD ideal: φⁿJ⊂p^eJ, e=(pⁿ−1)/(p−1). At p=2 the initial factorial valuations are 1 and 3. Together with a uniform Frobenius-inverse denominator bound this contracts the mapping complex, giving a unique higher section. For effectivity, a cone killed by p^m and factoring through p^e with e>m gives an inverse to 1−p^(e−m)u. A genuine PD map D(P)→A_crys handles nonzero parameters; there is no hollow-log W(k) map sending a zero section to a nonzero section. Finite coefficient nilpotents are killed by one common Frobenius iterate. Proper Cartier-type crystalline base change and HK Proposition 2.24 then supply the required comparison. These calculations include nonfree fs cones, two independent parameters and the zero/rank-one extremes.
