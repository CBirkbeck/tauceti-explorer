## R07.1 (closed)

New in this checkpoint:

**Formal Lie groups and Tate's theorems**

- **formal-lie-group:** commutative formal Lie groups, their dimension, p-divisibility and height (Stix §10.2–10.3).
- **serre-tate-connected-p-divisible:** connected p-divisible groups are exactly the p-divisible formal Lie groups (Tate Proposition 1, Stix Theorem 70).
- **p-divisible-dimension:** the dimension of a p-divisible group.
  - **dimension-plus-dual-dimension:** dim G + dim G^D = h (Tate Proposition 3).
  - **p-divisible-discriminant:** the discriminant of A_v is generated by p^{n v p^{hv}} (Tate Proposition 2).
- **hodge-tate-p-divisible:** Tate's Theorem 3, and the Hodge–Tate decomposition with weights 0 and 1 (Stix Theorem 91, Corollaries 92–93).
- **closure-of-generic-p-divisible-subgroups:** Tate's Proposition 12. The closures become p-divisible only after a shift, and φ need not be a closed immersion (Serre's example).
- **tate-generic-fibre-theorem:** Tate's Theorem 4 with Corollaries 1–2, over an integrally closed noetherian domain.
- **raynaud-extension-of-generic-p-divisible:** Raynaud's Proposition 2.3.1.

**Groups over henselian bases and rings of S-integers**

- **finite-part-of-quasi-finite-group:** Stacks Lemma 37.41.5 over a henselian base.
- **etale-groups-as-galois-modules:** Stix Theorem 33 and construction 7.3.1. Over ℤ[1/N] the π₁-modules are the finite G_ℚ-modules unramified outside N∞, and the twisted constant schemes V(ρ) are included.
- **gluing-equivalence:** Schoof's Proposition 2.3, via the Stacks Beauville–Laszlo theorem 15.92.16 and Lemma 15.92.19.
- **mayer-vietoris-hom-ext:** Schoof's Corollary 2.4.
- **katz-mazur-groups:** T_ε, from Schoof 2003 §2 and Schoof 2005 §2.4.
