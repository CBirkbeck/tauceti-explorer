## The coefficient field of primitivity

The PL.6 part found (source issue E76) that Allen–Newton–Thorne Theorem 4.1 needs weak primitivity over the residue field of the normalization of R/𝔭, which can be larger than k, and that primitivity over k does not pass to it. The two-dimensional S₃ representation is primitive over 𝔽₅ and induced over 𝔽₂₅. The same example is strongly primitive over 𝔽₅, since C₃ has no nontrivial 𝔽₅-character, so the main packet's strong route has the same condition. Its proof wrote the normalization as k⟦T⟧ and enlarged k without justification. This is recorded as the rewritten gap 5, needed by `PL.6/generic-prime-r-equals-t` and the finite-coefficient theorems resting on it (`PL.7/ordinary-steinberg-finiteness`, `PL.7/two-constituent-automorphy-lifting`). Theorems with ℚ̄_l coefficients test primitivity over F̄_l and are unaffected, and so are the character applications (the character-ratio criterion holds over every field). The document's conventions state the convention that avoids the gap: impose primitivity over k̄, or take k a splitting field for every subgroup of the residual image. **Question for the re-review:** NT21 Proposition 5.8 (`PL.7/prescribed-type-lifts`) assumes ρ̄|G_F primitive over the finite residue field and then applies the ℚ̄_l lifting theorem, which tests primitivity over F̄_l. This assembly did not read that proof and did not mark the declaration; it should be checked.
