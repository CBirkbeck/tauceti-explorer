## Mathematical boundaries to retain

Use the native fixed-lattice, fixed-form period-point carrier. The compact dual omits opposedness and positivity. The represented connected Mumford–Tate orbit retains its faithful representation and component; it can be a point inside a positive-dimensional ambient domain. Full ambient equality is confined to the full-isometry/component specialization. Compact isotropy belongs to the effective normalized real isometry action, not the full noncompact scalar centralizer. Keep all integer Hodge indices and the pinned positivity factor.

The horizontal tangent is the image of F^(−1)g in g/F⁰g, rather than all negative grades. The five-dimensional weight-two test detects a nonhorizontal grade −2 tangent. Exponential coordinates are locally invertible only after the actual derivative/complement and complex inverse-function inputs.

The logarithmic curve derivative retains proper connected fibres, disjoint markings and unitary coefficients. Its trace target has one divisor twist, ω²(D). Its cotangent factorization is κ dual composed with trace multiplication; a constant family has zero derivative even when multiplication is nonzero. The classifying-map version imports the existing pointed curve moduli supplier and its deformation dictionary. The row-period normalization is A inverse B with right-acting cycle changes; do not mix it with the column convention.
