## The RefinedTraceMethods supplier obligation

RefinedTraceMethods consumes §§13.8–13.11 and 13.13 (its handoff, "Maintainer requests and exact restart", item 1). They now give the following.

- **Keune's Theorem 15**, §13.9, is a named target with its exact hypotheses: commutative A, I⊆Jac(A). Generators have an entry in I. D1 and D2 apply when every displayed pair is relative, so D2 needs r∈I or s,t∈I. D3 applies when one input lies in I. Both literature forms of the D3 guard are shown equivalent, and every step has Checks. The **generation half is proved** from an obtained source, Dennis–Stein 1973 Theorem 2.1, p.287, proof pp.287–290. Only the injectivity half rests on the unretrieved Maazen–Stienstra paper. Suslin–Tulenbaev stability is not needed: Dennis–Stein's theorem holds in every rank.
- **Fibre comparison, §13.8**: proved for split radical pairs, which covers dual numbers, truncated polynomial rings and TrivSqZeroExt. The boundary maps vanish there, which settles RefinedTraceMethods' "boundary maps … compared" requirement for its test rings.
- **Dual numbers, §13.13**: now states van der Kallen's theorem for every commutative ring, K₂(R[ε],(ε))≅M_R, with the explicit module and generator map N(a,b)=D(−b,aε)+D(−abε,abε). Its proof route is generation by normal forms plus a semidirect-product inverse. It uses neither Maazen–Stienstra nor double excision. **"2 invertible" is retained in one place only**, the identification M_R≅Ω¹_{R/ℤ}, which needs F=0, a consequence of F(2a)=0 when 2 is a unit. New Checks: M_ℤ≅ℤ/2, generated by D(ε,ε); M_{𝔽₂}=0; the 𝔽₂(t) basis Dt, F(t) shows that M_R is larger than Ω¹ in characteristic 2. §13.11 says explicitly that no hypothesis on 2 is needed there.
- §§13.10–13.11: δ is checked to respect every guarded relation on D(A,I). Its transport to K₂(A,I), and ker δ=S for a general square-zero ideal, use Keune's theorem. For k[ε] they are independent of it via §13.13.
