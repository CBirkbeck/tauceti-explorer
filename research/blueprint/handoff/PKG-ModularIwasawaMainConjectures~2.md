## Remaining points (not blockers of this revision)

- `KatoPeriodDefectUnit` in general weight is a named hypothesis outside the detection range and the elliptic case. Fouquet–Wan call p-integrality of p-adic periods the delicate point (p.9). No general unit theorem is asserted.
- `pollackMultiplierUnit` is named rather than proved. Kobayashi's paper, needed to read his Theorem 6.2's integral images directly, is not freely available.
- The p=3 control input of §1.6 is taken from EPW Theorem 2.1.2, which states Hida's theorem for every odd p and cites Hida. PadicFamilies plans only p≥5; if it later adds p=3, §1.6 should cite it instead.
