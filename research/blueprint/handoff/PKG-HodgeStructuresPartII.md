## Concrete acceptance witnesses for the owner extensions

These are resumption criteria for the contracts already requested by H.0,
not new package targets or permission to edit their owners here.

1. **Ordinary connection.** On O=Q[x], take E=O and D=d. The identified
   parameter-one operator must send x to dx, while its exterior square is zero.
   Multiplication by x is not horizontal: D(x·1)=dx but xD(1)=0. The carrier
   must therefore admit additive connections which are not O-linear and
   enforce horizontality as a separate equation. The same identifications
   must commute with restriction and agree after gluing local trivializations.
2. **A nontrivial split Rees fibre.** Over O=Q[x], let E have basis e₁,e₂,
   F^p=E for p≤0, F^1=Oe₁ and F^p=0 for p≥2. Set ∇e₁=e₂ dx and ∇e₂=0.
   This is an integrable Griffiths-transverse connection. Its Rees sheaf is
   free over O[t] on u₁=t^(-1)e₁ and u₂=e₂. With relative dt=0,
   D=t∇ satisfies D(u₁)=u₂ dx and D(u₂)=0, and
   D(xu₁)=(x u₂+t u₁)dx. The t=1 fibre recovers ∇;
   the t=0 fibre has nonzero Higgs field taking the degree-one class to the
   degree-zero class times dx; localization recovers E[t,t^(-1)] and t∇.
   Merely replacing the zero fibre by a zero operator fails this example.
   The zero module and the one-step filtration must also recover their
   ordinary fibres, and the maps must respect restriction and tensor products.
3. **Changing the Rees splitting.** In the same chart take e₁′=e₁ and
   e₂′=e₂+xe₁. Then u₂′=u₂+xtu₁ and the new-basis column matrix is
   B=((1,xt),(0,1)). With A=E21 the component matrix is
   B⁻¹AB+tB⁻¹δ(B)=((−xt,t²(1−x²)),(1,xt)). At t=0 this is still E21;
   at t=1 it becomes ((−x,1−x²),(1,x)). At x=0,t=1 the upper-right
   entry is 1; pure conjugation gives 0. An owner specialization that
   only agrees on graded objects or ranks misses this discrepancy.
   The maps and operator comparison must respect this transition after
   restriction, so the local constructions agree on overlaps. The five
   new Lean examples prove these matrix identities without admissions.
