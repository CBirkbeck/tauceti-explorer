## Effect on the main results

The Main Theorem 9.2.2 constructs a measure `L_Eis` on `X_p × T_H(Z_p)` interpolating the critical values
of the standard `L`-function of a cuspidal antiholomorphic ordinary `π` on a unitary group, under the
Gorenstein, Global Multiplicity One and Minimality hypotheses. **No finding refutes it.** What the findings
do is: pin down normalizations it states loosely, repair intermediate steps, and — in one case — leave a
real question open. Ordered by how far they reach:

**The one that cannot be settled from this paper alone.**

- **E52 (error, reaches a stated result; §4.3.6, pp. 58–59).** The Schwartz function (58) defining the
  section at `p` is given in the polarization coordinates `W = V_d ⊕ V^d` as `Φ₁(−X₁)Φ₂(2X₂)`, but is
  evaluated on p. 59 as `Φ₁(Y₁)Φ₂(Y₂)` in the coordinates `W = V ⊕ V`. In those coordinates it is actually
  `Φ₁((Y₂ − Y₁)/2)Φ₂(Y₁ + Y₂)`, because `ι_d(a) + ι^d(b) = (a + b, b − a)`. So Theorem 4.3.10 computes the
  zeta integral of a **translate** of the section the paper defines. In a ramified case with `n = 1` the
  section as defined has zeta integral `0` while the theorem gives a nonzero `ε`-factor; in an unramified
  case the two differ by an extra pole (checked by exact enumeration mod `3⁵`). v1 put the change-of-basis
  matrix into the translation explicitly; v2 dropped it. **Whether the Main Theorem's `p`-adic factor is
  affected depends on which section the Eisenstein measure of [Eis15] is built from, and the paper does not
  say.** Confirmed present in the published version.

**Normalizations in the statement of the Main Theorem.**

- **E73 (misprint, a stated result).** The `p`-adic factor should be `L_p(s + 1/2, ord, π, χ)`, not
  `L_p(s, ord, π, χ)`, in Theorem 4.6.1 and in the Main Theorem: (83) itself gives
  `L(s + 1/2, ord, π_w, χ_w)`. Both readings are in print.
- **E155 (gap, a stated result).** The Main Theorem omits the conditions under which it is proved:
  `m ≥ n`, `κ¹` sufficiently regular, and `(χ, ψρ^υ) ∈ Y^class_H`.
- **E58 (error, a stated result).** (83) counts the local pairing `⟨φ_w, φ̃_w⟩` twice: by (51),
  `I_w = Z_w/⟨φ_w, φ̃_w⟩`. This carries into Theorem 4.6.1 and Corollaries 9.1.8 and 9.2.1, which as printed
  depend on the auxiliary level `r`. The Main Theorem itself has the right form.
- **E57, E60, E41 (errors).** A missing sign `ω_{π_a⊗χ₁}(−1)` in `I₂`; Remark 4.3.12's volume identity is
  not independent of `r`; the unramified identity holds with `χ_v^{−1}` under the paper's own normalization
  of `I(χ, s)`.
- **E85 (error, a stated result).** (113) drops the archimedean scalar `∏_σ B(χ_σ, κ_σ)`; either put it in
  or impose `B(χ_σ, κ_σ) = 1` in Definition 5.3.2.

**Hecke algebras, families and the measure.**

- **E130 (gap, a stated result).** Theorem 8.2.2, the Eisenstein measure, is not proved here: the paper
  points to a "similar" result of Eischen–Fintzen–Mantovan–Varma, and the sections at `p` are not matched
  with it. With E52 this is the weakest link in the chain.
- **E106, E110, E115, E117, E119, E103 (errors and a gap).** Theorem 6.6.9 needs `π_p` irreducible and `κ`
  satisfying (166); the pairing of Lemma 6.6.12(iii) is not perfect, its image being the congruence ideal;
  Theorem 7.1.1 omits "sufficiently regular"; the control theorems 7.2.1(ii) and 7.3.1(ii) are misstated at
  level `r > 1`; Proposition 7.3.5 is proved only for regular weights; Lemma 6.6.5(ii) confuses eigenspace
  and generalized eigenspace.
- **E132, E82, E113, E149, E112 (gaps and misprints).** Corollary 8.2.4's proof checks only part of
  Definition 5.3.2; normalizing by the Euler factors at `S` can give a pseudo-measure; the independence of
  `C(π, χ)` from `χ` is asserted without proof; §6.7 drops the superscript `0` in the volumes, changing the
  periods by a power of `p`; Lemma 6.7.6 loses the twist by `χ`.

**Foundations.**

- **E9 (gap, a stated result).** The positivity condition on `δ` that makes the unitary PEL datum of §2.3 a
  PEL datum is never stated; `δ` has to be chosen after the CM type `Σ`.
- **E22, E31, E89 (errors).** `t⁺` contracts `N⁺`, it does not normalize the Iwahori; "Frac(O⁺) is a number
  field" is false as defined; the canonical-bundle characters of §6.1.3 have exponents `±2a_σ` where `±a_σ`
  is right.
- **E32 (gap).** `p` must be odd in §2.10.2.

## Method

The 160 findings were converted from the extraction unchanged: every `id`, `kind`, `affects`, `locator`,
`printed`, `correction` and `reason` is byte-identical, and the existing `searched` entries are kept in
order. What this job adds is (i) one further `searched` entry on each finding, recording the published
version, its SHA-256 and the outcome of checking that finding there, and (ii) the new `known` value for
E123.

The comparison was made on the text of the PDF content streams of both files, not on a text layer: the
Cambridge Core watermark (one block per page, in a shifted encoding) was stripped, the `\NNN` ligature
escapes of the published file were removed, and both texts were normalised by deleting the `ffi`, `ff`,
`fi` and `fl` ligatures, which the arXiv file drops and the published file keeps. A finding counts as
located when a probe of at least twenty characters, drawn from its own `printed` quotation, occurs in the
published text; eleven further findings whose probes are shorter (E22, E28, E42, E44, E58, E73, E85, E94,
E105, E125, E134) were located by hand at their statements, and E123 was checked by hand in both
directions. The remaining 31 are quotations of displayed formulas: the text layer renders those as
unpunctuated glyph runs, so a probe drawn from a quotation using unicode subscripts cannot match, and no
conclusion either way should be drawn from that. Nothing in the earlier work was edited.

`python3 scripts/check_errata.py research/blueprint/errata/PAPER-EISCHEN-HARRIS-LI-ETAL-20.json` reports no
errors. Only the two named deliverables change. No Lean file is part of an errata job and no formalisation
is claimed.
