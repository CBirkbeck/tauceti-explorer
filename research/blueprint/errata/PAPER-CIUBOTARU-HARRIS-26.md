## E1. Conjecture 9.5, not "9.3" (misprint)

The main Theorem 1.11 and the paragraph before it assume "Conjectures 9.1 and 9.3". (9.3) is a displayed decomposition; the numbered conjecture asserting it is Conjecture 9.5 (Lafforgue).

## E2. Even, not odd, in the symplectic case of Theorem 8.5 (misprint)

The proof leaves the points ½ω^j_{⌊r_j/2⌋} "when j is odd and r_j ≥ 3", just after showing ν_i = 0 for odd i. The statement, and the orthogonal factors of the centralizer, have j even.

## E3. n_u, not n_v, in three comparisons (misprint)

In the list of cases where Property A fails in the proof of Theorem 10.3, three comparisons print n_v on both sides:
- "n_v(9) = n_v(9) = 0" for F₄, B₃;
- "n_v(9) = 1 > 0 = n_v(9)" for E₇;
- "n_v(16) = 2 > 1 = n_v(16)" for E₈, D₆.

The right-hand sides should be n_u, as in the other cases.
