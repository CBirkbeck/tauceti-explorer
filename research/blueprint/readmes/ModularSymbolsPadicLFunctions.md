# Modular symbols and analytic p-adic L-functions of modular forms — blueprint

This blueprint covers stages L0–L4. This first checkpoint plans the algebraic core of **L0** from Pollack–Stevens,
*Overconvergent modular symbols and p-adic L-functions*, Ann. Sci. ÉNS (4) 44 (2011), §2.1–2.2 and §3.4, which is
open access on Numdam. Stages L1–L4 are not yet read.

## Purpose

Modular symbols are the algebraic carrier of the periods and critical L-values of modular forms, and later of their
p-adic L-functions. L0 supplies:
- the Steinberg module and modular symbols with values in an honest representation;
- Manin's presentation of the Steinberg module;
- the coefficient modules V_k;
- the Hecke operators and the involution at infinity.

L1 builds periods and critical values on these; L2 and L3 build the distribution-valued symbols of the control theorem.

## What the libraries supply

AUDIT-26 records L0 as not built.
- **Mathlib has:** P¹(ℚ) as `OnePoint ℚ` with the `GL₂(ℚ)` action by linear fractional transformations, the congruence
  subgroups Γ₀(N) and Γ₁(N), finitely supported divisors, representations, monoid algebras, and homogeneous polynomial
  modules.
- **Tau Ceti has:** the Γ₀(N) Hecke ring of double cosets with its commutative ring structure
  (`HeckeRing.GL2.commRingHeckeRingGamma0`).
- **Missing:** every modular-symbol notion below.

## Conventions

- Δ₀ = Div⁰(P¹(ℚ)).
- Pollack–Stevens' right action v|γ is recorded as the left representation v ↦ v|γ^{-1}.
- The unimodular path [γ] for γ = (a b; c d) runs from a/c to b/d, and d(γ) = {γ0} − {γ∞}.
- σ = (0 −1; 1 0) and τ = (0 −1; 1 −1).

## Milestones

Library modules: `TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularSymbols/{Basic, Manin, Hecke}`, namespace `TauCeti.ModularSymbol`.

### Milestone 1: symbols

**Definition: the Steinberg module** (`Delta0`, `steinbergRep`; node `steinberg-module`; §2.1). Δ₀ is the degree-zero
divisors on P¹(ℚ) with the permutation action of GL₂(ℚ). It is generated by the divisors {b} − {a} (`divisorOf`,
`span_divisorOf`).

*Unit tests.*
- {∞} ∉ Δ₀.
- ({b} − {a}) + ({c} − {b}) = {c} − {a}.
- {a} − {a} = 0.

**Definition: modular symbols** (`symbols`; node `modular-symbols`; planet "Modular symbols"; §2.1). The Γ-equivariant
additive maps Δ₀ → V.

*API.* `symbols_ext` (determined on unimodular divisors).

*Unit tests.*
- Symb_{SL₂(ℤ)}(ℤ) = 0.
- Symb_{Γ₀(11)}(ℤ) has rank 3.
- The zero symbol.

### Milestone 2: Manin's presentation

**Construction: the unimodular map** (`unimodularMap`; node `unimodular-map`). d(γ) = {γ0} − {γ∞}.

*API.* `unimodularMap_of`; `unimodularMap_of_mul` (d(gγ) = g·d(γ)).

*Unit tests.*
- d(1 + σ) = 0.
- d(1 + τ + τ²) = 0.
- d(−γ) = d(γ).

**Lemma: surjectivity** (node `manin-surjectivity`). By continued fractions, every divisor is a sum of unimodular
divisors.

**Theorem: the Manin relations** (`ker_unimodularMap`; node `manin-relations`; planet). ker d is generated by 1 + σ,
1 + τ + τ² and 1 − (−1) over SL₂(ℤ); this is Manin's ℤ[G](1 + σ) + ℤ[G](1 + τ + τ²) over PSL₂(ℤ).

*Proof.* Use the Farey tessellation and cellular homology of H* relative to P¹(ℚ). Pollack–Stevens cite Manin
without proof.

**Lemma: the coset basis** (`free_monoidAlgebra`; node `coset-basis`). ℤ[SL₂(ℤ)] is free over ℤ[Γ] on right coset
representatives.

### Milestone 3: coefficients and Hecke operators

**Definition: V_k** (`Vk`, `slashVk`; node `polynomial-coefficients`; §3.4). Degree-k homogeneous polynomials, with
(P|γ)(X, Y) = P(dX − cY, −bX + aY). This is a right action of integral matrices, through the adjugate.

*Unit tests.*
- rank V_k = k + 1.
- A scalar c·1 acts by c^k.
- V_0 is trivial.

**Construction: Σ₀(N)** (`Sigma0`; node `hecke-monoid`). Integral matrices with nonzero determinant, N | c and
(a, N) = 1. It contains Γ₀(N).

**Construction: Hecke operators** (`slash`, `heckeT`, `heckeU`; node `hecke-operators`; planet; §2.1).
- φ|T_ℓ = φ|(ℓ 0; 0 1) + Σ_a φ|(1 a; 0 ℓ) for ℓ ∤ N.
- φ|U_q = Σ_a φ|(1 a; 0 q) for q | N.

They preserve symbols and commute, through the commutative Γ₀(N) Hecke ring.

*Unit tests.* On Symb_{Γ₀(11)}(ℤ):
- T_2 has characteristic polynomial (x − 3)(x + 2)²;
- T_3 has characteristic polynomial (x − 4)(x + 1)².

**Construction: the involution at infinity** (`iota`; node `involution-at-infinity`). ι = (−1 0; 0 1) acts by x ↦ −x and
normalises Γ₀(N).

**Lemma: the plus/minus decomposition** (node `plus-minus-decomposition`). If 2 is invertible,
Symb = Symb^+ ⊕ Symb^−.
