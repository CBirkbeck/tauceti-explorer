# Explicit K₂: the Milnor to Quillen graded comparison

This part covers **T.2:graded-map**. For a field \(F\), its target is the natural
unital graded ring homomorphism
\[
  \Phi_F:K^M_*(F)\longrightarrow K_*(F),\qquad
  \{a_1,\ldots,a_n\}\longmapsto [a_1]\cdots[a_n].
\]
Here \([a]\) is the degree-one class of the unit matrix \((a)\), transported
from classical \(K_1\) to the chosen Quillen model. The order of the entries is
part of the formula. Degree two agrees with the classical Matsumoto
isomorphism followed by the classical-to-Quillen comparison. Degree three is
exported to **K3BlochGroups V.2**.

The construction and its degree-three component already belong to
`K2SymbolsBrauer:T.2/graded-map` and
`K2SymbolsBrauer:T.2/graded-map-degree-three` in
[K2SymbolsBrauer--T.1.json](../packets/K2SymbolsBrauer--T.1.json).
They retain those identities. This continuation specifies their interfaces
and separates the equality of the degree-two homomorphisms as
`K2SymbolsBrauer:T.2/graded-map-degree-two-identification`. It does not supply
a second Milnor ring, a second higher K-theory product, or a second degree-three
construction.

## Objects and conventions

All fields are commutative. Field homomorphisms preserve one. All tensor
products used to define Milnor K-theory are over \(\mathbb Z\). Write
\(U_F=\operatorname{Additive}(F^\times)\); the multiplicative identity in
\(F^\times\) becomes zero in this additive group. Its integer module structure
is the canonical one.

The imported `K2SymbolsBrauer:T.2/milnor-k-theory` is the graded ring
\[
 T_{\mathbb Z}(U_F)/\langle\ell(a)\ell(1-a):a\ne0,1\rangle.
\]
The brackets denote the two-sided ideal generated by the displayed elements.
Each generator has degree two. The grading, degree decomposition, quotient
universal property and natural maps are responsibilities of **T.2:symbols**.
In particular, an ungraded ring quotient does not itself establish the
homogeneous components. The group \(K_n^M(F)\) is the degree-\(n\) component;
\(K_0^M(F)=\mathbb Z\), \(K_1^M(F)=U_F\), and products of symbols concatenate
the ordered tuples. The empty symbol is the ring unit.

The target is the nonnegative graded ring formed from the Quillen groups,
with addition in each homogeneous component and multiplication
\(K_p(F)\times K_q(F)\to K_{p+q}(F)\). **GeneralAlgebraicKTheory K.7** owns the
pairing produced by the biexact tensor-product functor, its associativity,
unitality, naturality and symmetry coherence. Its unit is the class \([F]\)
in degree zero. For homogeneous classes, the symmetry law is
\(xy=(-1)^{pq}yx\). The total ring is used as an integer algebra; it must not
be assumed to be an ordinary commutative ring. In particular, swapping two
degree-one factors changes a sign. A square of a degree-one class need only
be killed by two; this construction does not impose its vanishing.

Write \(\theta_{1,F}:K_1^{\mathrm{cl}}(F)\simeq K_1(F)\) for the natural
comparison supplied by
`KTheoryLowDegrees:U.6/pi1-plus-construction`, including its identification
of the loop of a matrix with that matrix's quotient class.
`KTheoryLowDegrees:U.3/units-to-K1` supplies the unit matrix class, and
`KTheoryLowDegrees:U.3/SK1-field` identifies its inverse with determinant.
Thus
\[
 \iota_F(a)=\theta_{1,F}(K_1.\mathrm{ofUnits}(a))
\]
is a homomorphism from the multiplicative unit group to additive \(K_1(F)\).
In particular \(\iota_F(1)=0\) and
\(\iota_F(ab)=\iota_F(a)+\iota_F(b)\).

Write \(\theta_{2,F}:K_2^{\mathrm{cl}}(F)\simeq K_2(F)\) for the natural
comparison owned by `K2SymbolsBrauer:T.1/k2-pi2`. Classical \(K_2\) is the
kernel of the stable Steinberg extension; it can be written multiplicatively.
Quillen \(K_2\) is written additively. The intended product comparison is
\[
 \theta_{2,F}(\{a,b\}_{\mathrm{cl}})
       =\iota_F(a)\iota_F(b).                    \tag{1}
\]
The symbol on the left is the parent's fixed classical symbol. Equation (1)
requires agreement with that comparison's orientation; it is not a license
to replace \(\theta_2\) by its negative without verifying the existing maps.

## The product-symbol input

The existence of bilinear products alone does not give (1) or the Steinberg
relation for their degree-one values. The required supplier statement is
map-level: identify the actual K.7 tensor pairing with the plus-model product
and compute its value on the unit-matrix loops under the chosen classical
\(K_2\) comparison.

Weibel's author copy, **IV.1.10–1.10.1, printed p.266 / PDF p.274**, states the
natural associative product, its graded symmetry and agreement in degree two
with the classical matrix-symbol product. **III.5.12–5.12.2, printed
pp.227–228 / PDF pp.235–236**, supplies that classical product and its
unit-matrix specialization. The two-source comparison must preserve these
normalizations as well as the abstract groups.

Loday's published paper gives a concrete proof to use for this supplier
contract. His **§2.1, Proposition 2.1.8 and Theorems 2.1.11–2.1.12, printed
pp.334–335 / PDF pp.27–28**, gives the natural stabilized pairing and its
product laws. For unit matrices over a commutative field, the calculation in
**§2.2, Proposition 2.2.3, printed pp.337–338 / PDF pp.30–31**, uses the
commuting elementary matrices
\[
 D_a=\operatorname{diag}(a,a^{-1},1),\qquad
 D'_b=\operatorname{diag}(b,1,b^{-1}).
\]
Their lifts have a central commutator. The reduced tensor pairing on the two
unit loops is represented by their torus map into the elementary-group plus
construction. The central-extension connecting map identifies its class
with the **inverse** of the commutator in Loday's convention. His proof uses
the torus fundamental class and the Hurewicz comparison of Lemma 2.2.1,
printed p.336 / PDF p.29. This is why citing the product's bilinearity cannot
settle the orientation.

The K.7 request therefore includes the comparison between its biexact
pairing and this stabilized plus pairing, the unit-loop computation, and the
sign relative to the imported \(\theta_2\). The H.3 request specifies the
natural elementary-group plus cover and its oriented degree-two Hurewicz
interface needed by T.1's comparison. These contracts preserve the owners:
K.7 constructs and compares products, H.3 supplies the homotopical input,
and this part applies them to the Milnor source. Reading Loday removes the
unread-source portion of the parent's product-symbol gap, but does not prove
that the existing K.7 plan already contains the model comparison.

As a source correction, the combined K-book author copy assigns rank
\(m+n\) to \(R^m\otimes R^n\) in the introduction to III.5.12 and repeats
that rank in III.5.12.1's proof on printed p.227. The correct rank is \(mn\),
with endomorphism ring \(M_{mn}(R)\). Already \(m=n=1\) distinguishes them.
The packet records this as a misprint against the inspected author copy,
without asserting anything about the published edition. Its corrected
Kronecker matrices have product rank.

## The graded map

Import the target `K2SymbolsBrauer:T.2/graded-map`. Send
\(U_F\) into the degree-one component of the total target ring by \(\iota_F\).
This is an integer-linear map. **Mathlib's `TensorAlgebra.lift` already
extends it uniquely to an integer-algebra homomorphism from the tensor
algebra**; there is no new tensor-algebra universal property to develop.

For \(a\ne0,1\), equation (1) and the classical Steinberg identity give
\(\iota_F(a)\iota_F(1-a)=0\). Hence the extension kills each generator of
the two-sided Steinberg ideal and descends through the source quotient.
Mathlib's `RingQuot.liftAlgHom` supplies the ungraded relation-quotient
factorization, with `liftAlgHom_mkAlgHom_apply` and `liftAlgHom_unique` fixing
its evaluation and uniqueness. The Milnor owner's homogeneous quotient
interface establishes that the descended map preserves degree. The fact
that the degree-one generators land in degree one then determines its
restriction \(\Phi_{F,n}:K_n^M(F)\to K_n(F)\).

For a unital field map \(f:F\to E\), both composites around the naturality
square send \(\{a\}\) to \(\iota_E(f(a))\). The imported Milnor universal
property proves their equality. The same argument gives uniqueness of
\(\Phi_F\) among unital graded maps with this degree-one generator formula.
Unitality fixes degree zero: \(n\mapsto n[F]\).

The nearest pinned degree-zero carrier is
`TauCeti.SplitK0 (FGModuleCat F)`.
`TauCeti.SplitK0.finrankEquiv` identifies this split Grothendieck group with
\(\mathbb Z\), under its stated division-ring and universe-smallness
hypotheses; `finrankEquiv_of` sends the class of a line to one. After the
canonical K.1/K.2 degree-zero model comparison, \(\Phi_{F,0}\) is the identity
of \(\mathbb Z\). The pinned split-group computation is reused; it is not
itself a theorem identifying this group with the higher K-theory space.

## Degree two and the degree-three export

The new comparison target is
`K2SymbolsBrauer:T.2/graded-map-degree-two-identification`.
Let \(m_F:K_2^M(F)\simeq K_2^{\mathrm{cl}}(F)\) be the symbol-preserving
Matsumoto equivalence imported from `K2SymbolsBrauer:T.2/matsumoto`. For
every field \(F\), with equation (1) supplied in the stated orientation,
\[
 \Phi_{F,2}=\theta_{2,F}\circ m_F.                 \tag{2}
\]
Both sides agree on every two-symbol; those symbols generate the degree-two
additive group. Therefore the homomorphisms agree. The right side is a
composition of natural equivalences, so the degree-two component is a
natural equivalence. Checking the Steinberg relations proves descent, not
injectivity: the converse presentation assertion of Matsumoto remains an
input. This comparison identifies the map already constructed; it is not an
input to that construction, so there is no circular descent argument.

The imported target `K2SymbolsBrauer:T.2/graded-map-degree-three` is simply
\(\Phi_{F,3}\) and its ordered triple-symbol formula. The producer needs
neither its image nor its cokernel to construct it.
**K3BlochGroups V.2** imports this component and owns the decomposable
subgroup as its actual image, the indecomposable quotient as its cokernel,
the quotient maps and exactness, and the injectivity theorem. The current
`K3BlochGroups:V.2/milnor-to-quillen-degree-three` node already imports this
stage. The dependency goes from **T.2:graded-map to V.2**. No reverse edge
or independent degree-three product construction is introduced. This is
the ownership resolution of **RT-AREA-ktheory-2/20**.

## Checks and worked examples

The eight checks below belong to the imported graded construction, whose API
and tests are expanded in the continuation packet. Arithmetic and residue
checks use computations owned by their stated consumers; they do not add
incoming dependencies to construct the map.

1. **Empty symbol.** The empty tensor is one, so its image is \([F]\).
   The pinned dimension equivalence sends the class of this line to one.
   This rejects the zero map and a map omitting the degree-zero unit.
2. **An identity entry.** In \(U_F\), the unit \(1\) is zero. Consequently
   \(\iota_F(1)=0\), and each positive-degree symbol with an entry one maps
   to zero. Treating the source group identity as the total ring's one fails
   this check.
3. **A rational unit.** For \(F=\mathbb Q\), the degree-one image of \(\{2\}\)
   has classical determinant two. A trivial map or an inverted generator
   map fails. The determinant comparison comes from U.3, not from a new
   definition of \(K_1\).
4. **Steinberg descent.** For \(a\ne0,1\), the product
   \(\iota_F(a)\iota_F(1-a)\) vanishes. Multiplying this relation by any
   symbols on either side still gives zero. Killing only an additive
   span of relations, without their two-sided products, fails the descent
   contract.
5. **An orientation-sensitive two-symbol.** In \(\mathbb Q(t)\) at \(t=0\),
   apply \(\theta_2^{-1}\) to \(\Phi_2\{2,t\}\) and use **T.3's** tame-symbol
   convention \(\partial\{u,t\}=\bar u\). The result is two. Reversing the
   product, or negating the comparison, gives \(1/2\). Weibel III.6.3,
   printed p.234 / PDF p.242, uses the inverse residue convention; its
   formula must be inverted before this check. A check on \(\{-1,-1\}\)
   alone cannot detect the error because inversion fixes its two-torsion.
6. **Naturality.** Under \(\mathbb Q\to\mathbb Q(t)\), the image of the
   degree-one class of two agrees with the class of the mapped unit. The
   same square commutes on \(\{2,3\}\). Identity and composite field maps
   obey the source and target functor laws; checking only an identity map
   would not test the field-map adapter.
7. **Triple-symbol degeneracies.** The ordered product formula gives
   \(\Phi_3\{a,b,1\}=0\) and \(\Phi_3\{a,1-a,b\}=0\) whenever all entries
   exist. These follow from the identity-entry and Steinberg checks,
   without using the V.2 injectivity theorem.
8. **No general degree-three surjectivity.** The external computation for
   \(\mathbb Q\) gives \(K_3^M(\mathbb Q)=\mathbb Z/2\) and
   \(K_3(\mathbb Q)=\mathbb Z/48\). The canonical map is injective by the
   theorem owned in V.2, so it cannot be onto. The source locators are
   Weibel VI.4.3.2, printed p.482 / PDF p.490, and VI.5.2.1, printed p.488 /
   PDF p.496. These are external acceptance results, not a proof of the
   graded map or new arithmetic targets of this part.

## Dependencies and coverage

The input chain is **T.2:symbols** for the Milnor quotient and classical
symbol presentation, **T.1:plus** for the classical-to-Quillen \(K_2\)
comparison, **KTheoryLowDegrees U.3/U.6** for the unit inclusion, determinant
and its plus-model comparison, and **GeneralAlgebraicKTheory K.7** for
products and their requested unit-symbol calculation. **StableHomotopyKTheory
H.3** supplies the natural cover/Hurewicz interface used by T.1. Its existing
plus-construction targets are imported, not rebuilt. The higher K-theory
space and its model comparisons remain with **K.1/K.2:plus**.

The scoped stage is **planned**. Its two existing targets are reused and its
degree-two comparison is explicit. Four precise boundaries prevent a claim
of closure: the imported homogeneous quotient interface; the imported
Matsumoto converse proof; the natural cover/Hurewicz interface; and the
product-model/sign comparison. The first two belong to the earlier symbol
and classical plans. The final two are the explicit H.3 and K.7 requests in
this packet. Independent review checks the statements, sources, orientation
and ownership contracts. The atlas retains the existing planet **Milnor to
Quillen graded map**; this continuation proposes no duplicate planet.
