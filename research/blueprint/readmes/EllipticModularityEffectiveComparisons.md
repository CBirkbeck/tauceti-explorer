### Prime-power inversion of the divisor multiplicity

Declaration: `TauCeti.EffectiveEllipticComparison.prime_power_convolution` (lemma). Node: `EllipticModularityEffectiveComparisons:EC.0/prime-power-convolution`.

For prime p and e≥0, the Dirichlet-convolution square λ=μ*μ of the native Möbius arithmetic function has λ(p^0)=1, λ(p)=−2, λ(p²)=1, and λ(p^e)=0 for e≥3. Consequently λ*τ is the convolution identity, where τ counts positive divisors.

Construction or proof:

1. Use μ(p^0)=1, μ(p)=−1 and μ(p^e)=0 for e≥2; evaluate the prime-power convolution sum.
2. Use τ=1*1 and μ*1=δ; associativity gives (μ*μ)*(1*1)=δ.

For E/Q with full rational two-torsion, prime ell≥5, irreducible E[ell] of Serre weight2 and full prime-to-ell Artin conductor N, prove ell>H(N) produces modular F/Q of conductor exactly N with full two-torsion and F[ell]≅E[ell]. Decompose exact lifting, coefficient rationality, norm contradiction, elliptic quotient, mod-four filters and ideal-power Sturm, finite point-count witness and two-isogeny selection. Supply the qualified M0=N local-weight adapter.

Kraus first produces a weight-two trivial-character newform at the exact prime-to-ell residual conductor N. This requires exact Serre-level and weight optimization, a coefficient prime, irreducibility and Serre weight two. If its field is not Q, characteristic-zero Sturm recognition gives a noninteger coefficient at a prime q≤I(N)/6. Hecke recurrence converts prime coefficients into the entire array needed by Sturm. At q|N a primitive weight-two coefficient is±1 or0, so the witness is good for the newform.

The witness is below F(N), hence different from ell when ell>F(N). At E it is good or a deleted multiplicative prime, by the full residual conductor comparison. Its elliptic trace or signed q+1 is congruent to a_q(f). The nonzero integral norm of their difference is at most(√q+1)^(2[K_f:Q])≤F(N), contradicting ell>F(N). Rational integral coefficients give a one-dimensional modular quotient C/Q of conductor exactly N and C[ell]≅E[ell]. This precedes the full-two-torsion step.

Compare C with the odd Eisenstein series at level four. The two local polynomial filters remove discrepant Euler factors. Kraus Appendix II supplies finite-prime recognition modulo a prime-power ideal; complex Sturm equality does not supply integral congruence. Modulo four, any failure of4|#C(F_q) at a good odd prime has a witness q≤I(lcm(N,4))/6<G(N). Full two-torsion on E, residual congruence and the rational norm bound exclude it when ell>G(N).

Point counts divisible by four at all good odd primes imply a rational two-point through the finite E[2] image and Chebotarev. In y²=x(x²+ax+b), the quadratic compositum and halving criterion force b or a²−4b to be square. If the discriminant is square C already has full two-torsion. If b is square its quotient by(0,0), y²=x(x²−2ax+(a²−4b)), has quadratic discriminant16b and full two-torsion. A degree-one-or-two isogeny preserves conductor and E[ell] for odd ell.

The M0=N adapter compares all Artin exponents away from ell, including wild ones. At ell it requires good reduction or multiplicative reduction with ell|v_ell(Δ_min). Serre weight two must establish precisely this alternative in each application. The final output uses ell≥5, irreducibility, Serre weight two, full rational two-torsion and ell>H(N), and has conductor exactly N.
