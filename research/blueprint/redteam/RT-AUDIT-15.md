## Findings

### RT-AUDIT-15/1 — library-claim (medium)

**Where:** research/blueprint/audit/AUDIT-15.result.json; OverconvergentAutomorphicForms:O0 targets[4].note; MetaplecticAutomorphicForms:MP.3 targets[3].note

O0 says no smooth representation theory of p-adic groups exists and restricts the existing theory to finite, Lie, compact and algebraic groups. MP.3 similarly says the only representation theory is of finite groups, Lie algebras and algebraic groups. These blanket statements omit the existing general topological-group smooth-discrete API, which MP.3 itself already cites in targets[2].

At Tau Ceti f790474, TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/SmoothDiscrete.lean:254 defines TauCeti.IsSmoothDiscrete on TopRep R G by a discrete coefficient module and open point stabilizers. Its section allows any topological monoid G; :268 proves IsSmoothDiscrete.res along a continuous homomorphism. For a topological group, :324 proves isSmoothDiscrete_iff_continuousSMul. The discrete-module constructor is :226 and equivariant linear morphisms are supplied by ofDiscreteModuleMap at :376. There is no finiteness or compactness assumption on G. This includes p-adic topological groups with the specified discrete coefficients; it does not establish locally analytic representations, admissibility or a theta correspondence. https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/f790474821cf4256814db967cb154e7af3d0c369/TauCeti/RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/SmoothDiscrete.lean#L245

**Fix:** Replace both categorical absence statements with the narrower missing results: locally analytic coefficient/induced modules, admissibility, Jacquet/theta and family constructions remain missing, while smooth discrete TopRep objects, restriction and the discrete-module/morphism dictionary are available. Add related citations at O0 and make MP.3 internally consistent. Retain the absent status for these complete targets and the not-built layer verdicts; do not infer admissibility or locally analytic theory from discrete smoothness.

### RT-AUDIT-15/2 — library-claim (medium)

**Where:** research/blueprint/audit/AUDIT-15.result.json; OverconvergentAutomorphicForms:O0 targets[2].note

The assertion that there is no locally analytic function theory in either library is too broad. Searching only the phrase locally analytic missed the generic convergent-power-series API, which applies over p-adic fields. The uniform weight-family radius theorem is still missing.

At Mathlib 082e2d3, Mathlib/Analysis/Analytic/Basic.lean:58 assumes NontriviallyNormedField k and normed k-spaces, not RCLike. HasFPowerSeriesOnBall at :74 requires a positive radius and actual HasSum convergence. HasFPowerSeriesAt at :95, AnalyticAt at :110 and AnalyticOnNhd at :120 express analyticity on neighbourhoods. Mathlib/Analysis/Analytic/Linear.lean:61 proves ContinuousLinearMap.analyticAt and Composition.lean:859 proves AnalyticAt.comp under the general normed-field hypotheses. Mathlib/NumberTheory/Padics/PadicNumbers.lean:872 supplies NontriviallyNormedField Q_p for prime p. Thus these are usable p-adic local analytic foundations, although neither affinoid families nor their uniform radius estimates follow. https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/082e2d37e8b0463410cdb532e111cd43d5a66174/Mathlib/Analysis/Analytic/Basic.lean#L58 ; https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/082e2d37e8b0463410cdb532e111cd43d5a66174/Mathlib/Analysis/Analytic/Composition.lean#L859 ; https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/082e2d37e8b0463410cdb532e111cd43d5a66174/Mathlib/NumberTheory/Padics/PadicNumbers.lean#L872

**Fix:** Credit HasFPowerSeriesOnBall, AnalyticAt and AnalyticOnNhd as related existing foundations, with their general normed-field hypotheses. Say that the particular locally uniform radius for the bounded Hilbert weight family, locally analytic distribution spaces and rigid/affinoid family comparison are not supplied. Keep the complete target absent and O0 not built; do not turn elementary analytic composition into the missing family theorem.
