## /1 (high, library-claim): K(S,n) is defined by valuations, and it is not the S-unit power classes

**Where.** `SelmerIwasawaCohomology:L0`, the target "Integral Kummer comparison H^1(F, Z_p(1)) = lim_m F^x/(F^x)^{p^m}", last sentence of the note.

**Checked.**
- **Mathlib's definition.** `IsDedekindDomain.selmerGroup` (`Mathlib/RingTheory/DedekindDomain/SelmerGroup.lean:163`) is the subgroup of K^×/(K^×)^n with carrier {x | ∀ v ∉ S, v.valuationOfNeZeroMod n x = 1}. It is a valuation condition on power classes. Mathlib's `fromUnit` and `fromUnit_ker` (lines 193, 201) embed unit power classes as a subgroup, the case S = ∅.
- **Tau Ceti's exact sequence.** `TauCeti/RingTheory/DedekindDomain/SInteger/SelmerGroup/Basic.lean`, in namespace `IsDedekindDomain.selmerGroup`, proves 1 → 𝒪_S^×/(𝒪_S^×)^n → K(S,n) → Cl(𝒪_S)[n] → 1:
  - `fromSUnitLift_injective` (line 186), whose domain is `S.unit K` modulo n-th powers;
  - `ker_toClassGroup` (line 269): the kernel of `toClassGroup` is the range of `fromSUnit`;
  - `range_toClassGroup` (line 296), under `[NeZero n]`: its range is the n-torsion of the S-class group.
- **The Kummer map's direction.** `TauCeti.kummerClassMap` (`TauCeti/FieldTheory/GaloisCohomology/Kummer.lean:279`) maps `powerClassQuotient Kˣ n` into `Multiplicative (H1 (AbsoluteGaloisGroup K) (KummerCoeff K n))`. Its target is H¹, not K(S,n).

**Change.** The final sentence is replaced. The note now says:
- K(S,n) is the group of power classes whose valuation is divisible by n outside S, defined by that condition rather than by an S-unit representative.
- Tau Ceti proves the exact sequence, exact on the right for n ≠ 0. So K(S,n) equals the S-unit power classes exactly when Cl(𝒪_S)[n] is trivial.
- These are algebraic statements. `kummerClassMap` goes from K^×/(K^×)^n into H¹(G_K, μ_n), and no comparison of K(S,n) with an S-restricted H¹ is stated.

The three Tau Ceti declarations are added to the target's `declarations` with fit `related`.

**Kept.**
- The `partial` classification.
- The finite-level Kummer injection and kernel evidence (`kummerClassMap_injective`, `ker_kummerMap`, `kummerShortExact`).
- The statement that the p-adic limit and the Z_p(1) comparison are missing.
- The exact sequence is reused, not planned again. No S-restricted cohomological comparison is claimed from it.
