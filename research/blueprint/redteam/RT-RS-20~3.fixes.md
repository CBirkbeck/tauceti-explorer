`A⁺₀ = (W_OE(R⁺) ⊗̂_OE O_E∞)[s_m : m ≥ 0]^∧_[ϖ]`.

Thus the roots are of `π/[ϖ]`, as displayed in FS II.1.1, printed p. 48. The tilt coordinate satisfies `t₁♯ = π/[ϖ]`. The following are separate acceptance checks, not one slogan about adjoining roots:

- **Coherence:** `s_m^p = s_(m−1)` for `m ≥ 1`. For the wrong generator `π_m/[ϖ]`, its p-th power has denominator `[ϖ]^p`, so it does not equal the previous generator.
