## 1. Integral essential-vector export is missing

`RT-RS-21/1` — missing, medium.

AutomorphicCongruences L3 explicitly imports “derivatives/co-Whittaker/essential vectors from SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups” and needs Fouquet–Wan Appendix A compatibility. RS-21 assigns the import to SR.5, whose unchanged contract covers derivative functors, top derivatives, co-Whittaker families, center action and projective interpolation. It does not state an essential-vector/invariant-line comparison.

The freshly read [Fouquet–Wan v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.13726v3), Appendix A §6.2, Propositions 6.8–6.9, printed pp.77–78, makes this a concrete specialization interface. Finding 1 contains its precise locator, short quotation and hypothesis boundary.

R16.2 does own the local GL₂ conductor/newvector theorem. That is not a reason to reconstruct it at SR.5, but its retained text does not specify the extra coefficient-qualified family comparison either. Top-derivative base change is not congruence-invariant base change: these are different functors.

Give the integral comparison one canonical smooth-local owner and an actual export to L3. A later comparison substage can consume SR.5 and R16.2's field-valued special case. It must not add R16.2 → SR.5: the existing SR.5 → R16.2 path would make a cycle. Its blueprint needs the invariant-line map, allowed coefficient changes and tests; L3 then verifies the arithmetic application. Source coefficient wording must be transcribed and reconciled, not silently generalized. No assertion is made that arbitrary co-Whittaker families satisfy this comparison.

This strengthens the accepted review's §5(3) observation with an inspected primary-source interface and distinguishes it from an ordinary newvector-dimension claim.

## 2. General global Fourier–Whittaker expansion has no explicit owner contract

`RT-RS-21/2` — missing, medium.

R16.5 keeps the GL₂ global Whittaker expansion and calls the expansion and converse theorem new. AL.3 supplies general GLₙ×GLₘ integrals and “the global unfolding for cuspidal data”. SR.5 supplies local nonarchimedean functors, not a global adelic expansion. The owner ledger contains no general global-expansion entry.

The source dependency is visible in [Cogdell's author PCMI notes](https://people.math.osu.edu/cogdell.1/pcmi-www.pdf), §1.1 Theorem 1.1 and §2.2.2, printed pp.7 and 20–21. The expansion is used in the unfolding; finding 2 records the exact locator and short quotation.

Two intended interpretations are possible, neither resolved by the proposal:

- If AL.3's global unfolding includes its general prerequisite by backward chaining, it owns a theorem whose GL₂ specialization R16.5 must import and compare, not rebuild as new.
- Otherwise the general-rank unfolding has an unassigned supplier. R16.5 supplies only rank two and lies downstream.

This is an ownership/interface omission, not a claim that a finished AL.3 blueprint already omitted a named node. Resolve it by assigning a lower AL.3/global-automorphic component the general theorem and retaining R16.5's integral-model/classical-normalization comparison and distinct converse theorem. AL.3 → R16.5 is the correct existing direction. Its reversal is not a permissible repair. This substantiates the review's §5(6) observation, without demanding Whittaker genericity for arbitrary reductive groups.
