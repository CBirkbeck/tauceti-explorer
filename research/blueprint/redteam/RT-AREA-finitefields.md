### 5. error, medium: `FiniteFieldsAndCharacterSums:FF.2`

**Claim.** FF.2's acceptance test names 'multiplicative perfect powers' as the degenerate case of multiplicative character sums. As stated this is false in both directions. For chi of order d, the degenerate polynomials are c*g^d: a constant times a d-th power, with g over the algebraic closure. A perfect power g^k with d not dividing k is not degenerate. For example, with chi of order 3 and f = x^2, sum_x chi(x^2) = sum_x chi^2(x) = 0. Conversely, c*g^d with c not a d-th power is degenerate but is not a perfect power. A test written from this text would assert a false degeneracy and miss a true one.

**Evidence.** Roadmap text, FF.2 (content/campaign/FiniteFieldsAndCharacterSums/README.md l.45): 'Artin-Schreier-trivial functions and multiplicative perfect powers are tested as degenerate cases rather than receiving a false square-root bound.' Mathlib 082e2d3, MulChar.sum_eq_zero_of_ne_one (Mathlib/NumberTheory/MulChar/Basic.lean:607): '[IsDomain R'] {chi : MulChar R R'} (hchi : chi != 1) : sum a, chi a = 0'. For chi of order 3, chi^2 != 1, so sum_x chi(x^2) = sum_x chi^2(x) = 0, within the square-root bound. For f = c*g^d one has chi(f(x)) = chi(c) at every x with g(x) != 0, so the sum is chi(c)(q - #{x : g(x) = 0}), of size q.

**Fix.** Restate FF.2's multiplicative bound and its acceptance test. For chi of order d > 1 and f in F_q[x] with m distinct roots in the algebraic closure, if f is not of the form c*g(x)^d, then |sum_x chi(f(x))| <= (m-1) q^{1/2}. The degenerate tests are f = c*g^d, including c a non-d-th power. Add the non-degenerate control test f = x^2 with chi cubic. State the mixed sum sum chi(f)psi(g) with its own combined condition.
