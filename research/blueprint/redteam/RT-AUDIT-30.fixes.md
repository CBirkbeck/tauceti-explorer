## /2 (medium, error): valuation divisibility is the unramified condition only away from n

**Where.** `MotivicEtaleKTheory:M.8`, `targets[6]`, note.

**Checked.**
- `IsDedekindDomain.selmerGroup` (Mathlib `082e2d3`, `Mathlib/RingTheory/DedekindDomain/SelmerGroup.lean:163`) is defined by `valuationOfNeZeroMod n x = 1` for every v ∉ S. There is no condition relating n to the residue characteristics.
- **Counterexample.** Over ℚ with S = ∅ and n = 2, the class of −1 lies in K(∅, 2), since every finite valuation of −1 is 0. Its Kummer class cuts out ℚ(i), which is ramified at 2.

**Change.** The note now:
- describes the declaration as the valuation-defined classical Selmer group;
- says that under Kummer theory the valuation condition is the unramified condition for μ_n only at primes not dividing n. For a number field, K(S, n) is the unramified-outside-S Selmer group only when S contains the primes above n, and the counterexample is recorded;
- says that no Galois-cohomological comparison is stated for it.

**Kept.**
- The `partial` label and the three cited declarations (`IsDedekindDomain.selmerGroup`, `IsDedekindDomain.selmerGroup.fromSUnit`, `WeierstrassCurve.Affine.selmerGroup₂`).
- The statement that there is no Selmer complex, no crystalline or semistable local condition, no determinant line and no regulator map. The crystalline and semistable conditions at p stay separate and missing.
- M.8's `not built` verdict.
