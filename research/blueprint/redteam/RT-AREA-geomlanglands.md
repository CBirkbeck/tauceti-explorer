## Summary

Red team of the Geometrisation of local Langlands area: six roadmaps and 57 stages (ExcursionOperatorsAndSpectralAction 17, GeometricSatakeAndFusion 14, LanglandsParameterStacks 8, BunGAndNewtonStrata 7, VStackSheavesAndLisseCategories 6, HeckeStacksAndLocalShtukas 5), with four integrated decompositions, read against Fargues–Scholze (arXiv 2102.13459v4), Scholze–Weinstein's Berkeley lectures and the neighbouring roadmaps. There are 39 findings: 4 high, 28 medium and 7 low. The four high findings are these. (1) The atlas orders fusion before Satake closure (GS3:fusion → GS2:Satake-closure), but Fargues–Scholze prove closure under convolution and dualizability first (VI.8.1(iii), VI.8.2), and the fusion product uses them (VI.9.4–9.5); the edge should be reversed. (2) No stage that can prove it owns the comparison of the classical Rapoport–Zink, Lubin–Tate and Drinfeld towers with the minuscule local-shtuka spaces (Berkeley 24.2.5, 24.3.5), which ES7 imports; HS2 sits upstream of the towers and HS3 disclaims it. (3) LP2 rests on geometric invariant theory that no layer plans and neither library has: Haboush, finite generation of invariants over Z_ℓ, the closed-orbit description of the quotient, and Hilbert–Mumford–Kempf. (4) LP1's flatness theorem (FS VIII.1.3) needs the Prasad–Yu reductivity of fixed points, which is owned by LP3, downstream of LP1, and finiteness of unipotent classes, which is planned nowhere. The medium findings fall into three groups. Missing inputs: the Bernstein centre over Z_ℓ[√q]-algebras, admissibility and Schur's lemma, z-extensions, the quasi-split reduction of FS IX.7.2, Prasad–Yu Cor. 5.2, rigid-analytic local Shimura varieties, the lisse duality and ULA results, and the Drinfeld-lemma inputs. Wrong or missing prerequisites: ES1:spectral-center into ES4/ES6/ES7, HS1, GS1, VS2, VS3, LP2:integral-invariants, and the integral-properness ordering. Duplicates: FS VIII.4.1 in ES0 and LP2, FS X.1.1 in LP4 and ES2/ES3, Keel positivity, Tannakian reconstruction, Weyl modules (LP3/PA.1), cocycle schemes (LP1/SR.6), excursion theory (LP2/GS.5), B(G) theory (BG0/BG1 with IG.0/ET.5), and adelic infrastructure in ES7. ### 1. error, high: `GeometricSatakeAndFusion:GS2:Satake-closure (atlas edge GeometricSatakeAndFusion:GS3:fusion -> GeometricSatakeAndFusion:GS2:Satake-closure; decomposition node GeometricSatakeAndFusion:GS2:Satake-closure/convolution-preserves-satake-and-dualizability)`

**Claim.** The atlas orders fusion before closure: GS2:Satake-closure requires GS3:fusion, and its text says the closure is proved 'Using GS3:fusion' and 'after GS3's geometric fusion construction, as in FS VI.8-VI.9'. Fargues-Scholze prove it the other way round. VI.8.1(iii) (convolution preserves Sat) and VI.8.2 (dualizability) are proved in VI.8 without the fusion product. VI.9 then uses them: the fusion product is 'a functor of monoidal categories' on Satake categories that are already monoidal under convolution, the symmetric monoidal structure 'refin[es] the monoidal convolution product', and VI.9.5 starts from VI.8.2. As the atlas stands, GS3:fusion needs outputs of a stage that sits after it. Lifting the decomposition's node link to stages gives a 2-cycle, which the decomposition's last gap records without resolving.

Fargues-Scholze, Geometrization of the local Langlands correspondence, arXiv:2102.13459v4 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.13459, read 2026-09-24; PDF page = printed page), p. 225, proof of Prop. P. 226, proof of VI.8.2: 'This follows from Proposition IV.2.24 ... also using Proposition VI.7.12, the functor sw* D(A) preserves the Satake category.' P. 228, VI.9.4: 'defining the fusion product ... a functor of monoidal categories'.
