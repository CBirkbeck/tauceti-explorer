### RT-AUDIT-20/1 — high — error

**Where:** research/blueprint/audit/AUDIT-20.result.json:75; roadmaps.DrinfeldModulesAndTModules.layers['DrinfeldModulesAndTModules:DM.0'].targets[2].target

The extracted definition adds 'nonzero constant term' to a general Drinfeld A-module. Applied to nonzero a, this forces the characteristic map to be injective and excludes special characteristic, which the roadmap expressly includes. The constant coefficient of phi_a is the image of a under the characteristic map, and it may vanish for nonzero a. This changes the object that would be built, rather than merely shortening its description.

**Evidence:** Read on 2026-09-24 at explorer commit 3f97d1cfefd35287c8d3cc55ddc06cb8bc2f9897. The target at https://github.com/CBirkbeck/tauceti-explorer/blob/3f97d1cfefd35287c8d3cc55ddc06cb8bc2f9897/research/blueprint/audit/AUDIT-20.result.json#L75 says 'nonzero constant term'. The actual roadmap at https://github.com/CBirkbeck/tauceti-explorer/blob/3f97d1cfefd35287c8d3cc55ddc06cb8bc2f9897/content/campaign/DrinfeldModulesAndTModules/README.md#L5 explicitly includes 'Generic characteristic, special characteristic'; DM.0 at lines 15-17 requires 'constant term the characteristic map' and 'a special-characteristic example detects inseparability'. Counterexample: take A=F_q[t], L=F_q, the characteristic map t -> 0, and phi_t=tau in L{tau}, corresponding to X -> X^q on G_a. For a nonzero polynomial a(t) of degree d, phi_a=a(tau) has tau-degree d, so this is a rank-one Drinfeld module of characteristic (t), but phi_t has constant coefficient zero. Its t-torsion kernel is the inseparable group scheme cut out by X^q, exactly the sort of case the roadmap retains.

**Correction:** Replace the target text by 'Rank-r Drinfeld A-module: characteristic map iota: A -> L, constant coefficient of phi_a equal to iota(a), and deg_tau(phi_a) = r deg(a) for nonzero a with the chosen infinity normalization; generic and special characteristic retained separately.' Require nonzero constant coefficient only when iota(a) is nonzero, e.g. nonzero a in generic characteristic or a outside the characteristic ideal. Keep the absent library classification and the special-characteristic acceptance case. Refresh the corresponding integrated coverage entry through the normal audit integration.
