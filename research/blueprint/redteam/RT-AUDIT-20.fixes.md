## /1 (high, error): a Drinfeld module's φ_a has constant coefficient ι(a), which may vanish

**Where.** `DrinfeldModulesAndTModules:DM.0`, `targets[2]`.

**Checked.** The roadmap's DM.0 requires the constant coefficient of φ_a to be the characteristic map ι(a), and it retains special characteristic.

Counterexample to the printed target: A = F_q[t], L = F_q, ι(t) = 0, φ_t = τ.
- φ_a = a(τ).
- deg_τ φ_a = deg a.
- The constant coefficient a(0) equals ι(a).
- So φ_t has zero constant coefficient.
- Its t-kernel is Spec F_q[X]/(X^q).

**Change.** The target now reads: "Rank-r Drinfeld A-module: characteristic map ι: A → L, constant coefficient of φ_a equal to ι(a), and deg_τ φ_a = r·deg a for nonzero a with the chosen infinity normalization; generic and special characteristic retained separately". The note records:
- the special-characteristic example;
- that the constant coefficient is nonzero only for a ∉ ker ι;
- that the τ-degree r·deg a is not the polynomial degree q^(r·deg a).

**Kept.** The `absent` classification and the special-characteristic case.
