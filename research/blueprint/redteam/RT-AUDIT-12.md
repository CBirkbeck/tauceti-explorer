## Summary

Red team of the accepted library audit AUDIT-12 (Tau Ceti ModularCurves, following Katz–Mazur: about 60 layers and 290 targets). All cited declarations exist at the cited file and line (70 in Layer 0, 85 in Layers 1–4 and 103 in Layers 5 onward), and no layer verdict changes. There are 35 findings: 2 high, 8 medium and 25 low. The two high findings change target statuses from absent to partial. Under 0D, transport of group objects along an equivalence is Mathlib's `Equivalence.mapGrp`/`mapCommGrp`. - The 2A note misses Tau Ceti's degree and finiteness results for isogenies.

### 1. library-claim, medium

**Where.** research/blueprint/audit/AUDIT-12.result.json: layer tauceti:TauCetiRoadmap/ModularCurves#0b-finite-locally-free-group-schemes-and-cartier-duality, target 'Scheme-theoretic kernel of an arbitrary homomorphism, with base change (and no claim that it is finite locally free)'; also layer tauceti:TauCetiRoadmap/ModularCurves#layer-0-scheme-theoretic-prerequisites, target '0B: finite locally free commutative group schemes over a general base, …' (its note)

**Claim.** The note's 'Missing: kernels of homomorphisms of non-affine group schemes (such as E ⟶ E′) over a general base, and a base-change theorem for them' is false as to the kernels. Over an arbitrary base scheme S, Mathlib's Grp (Over S) has pullbacks, and the forgetful functor creates them. So every homomorphism f : G ⟶ H of group schemes over S, including E ⟶ E′, has a scheme-theoretic kernel: the pullback of f along the unit, with underlying scheme G ×_H S. This is the same pullback square Tau Ceti's affine kernelSpec is stated by. Base change of group schemes along any g : S′ ⟶ S is (Over.pullback g).mapGrp. Tau Ceti proves, over an arbitrary base, that base change identifies point groups compatibly with homomorphisms, which is the functor-of-points form of kernel base change. Only a named kernel with an explicitly stated base-change isomorphism is missing. The status stays partial; the note and the Layer 0 summary misdirect a builder into constructing general-base kernels from scratch.

**Evidence.** Stage 0B: 'Construct scheme-theoretic kernels of arbitrary homomorphisms and prove their base-change property'. Mathlib 082e2d3: CategoryTheory.instHasLimitsOfShapeGrp, Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Cartesian/GrpLimits.lean:52 ('instance : HasLimitsOfShape J (Grp C)' for any cartesian monoidal C with limits of shape J; line 49 'CreatesLimitsOfShape J (Grp.forget C)'). AlgebraicGeometry.instCartesianMonoidalCategoryOverScheme, Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Pullbacks.lean:711 ('instance : CartesianMonoidalCategory (Over S)'). CategoryTheory.Over.instBraidedPullback, Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Cartesian/Over.lean:198 ('instance : (Over.pullback f).Braided'), so mapGrp applies. Over.pullback is a right adjoint, Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Comma/Over/Pullback.lean:91. Lean at the pin: 'example (S : Scheme) : HasPullbacks (Grp (Over S)) := inferInstance' compiles, and '#synth HasLimitsOfShape WalkingCospan (Grp (Over (Spec (CommRingCat.of ℤ))))' returns instHasLimitsOfShapeGrp. Tau Ceti f790474: TauCeti.GroupScheme.baseChangePointMulEquiv, TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/GroupScheme/CentralIsogeny/BaseChange.lean:142 ('(s : Y ⟶ X) (G : Grp (Over X)) (T : Over Y) : (T ⟶ ((Over.pullback s).mapGrp.obj G).X) ≃* ((Over.map s).obj T ⟶ G.X)', X arbitrary). baseChangePointMulEquiv_pointMap at :185 intertwines the base-changed homomorphism with the original, and HasCentralKernel.baseChange at :196 uses it for point kernels. TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/HopfIdeal/Scheme/Kernel.lean:11 imports Mathlib.CategoryTheory.Monoidal.Cartesian.GrpLimits, and isPullback_kernelSpec (:221) states the affine kernel as exactly this pullback.

Replace the note's last sentence with: 'Over any base S, Grp (Over S) has pullbacks created by the forgetful functor (Mathlib), so the kernel of an arbitrary homomorphism, including E ⟶ E′, exists as the pullback of f along the unit section, with underlying scheme G ×_H S. ### 3. library-claim, high

**Where.** research/blueprint/audit/AUDIT-12.result.json: layer tauceti:TauCetiRoadmap/ModularCurves#0d-finite-étale-schemes-and-galois-actions, target 'The equivalence preserves products and transports group objects, commutative group objects, homomorphisms and alternating pairings' (library 'absent')

So once the equivalence of the preceding 0D target ('Over a field K, the equivalence between finite étale K-schemes and finite continuous Gal(Kˢ/K)-sets') exists, this part is an application of existing Mathlib. Only alternating pairings, and that equivalence itself, are missing. The status should be 'partial', not 'absent'.

**Evidence.** Stage 0D: 'Prove that it preserves products and transports group objects, commutative group objects, homomorphisms, and alternating pairings.' CategoryTheory.Equivalence.mapCommGrp, Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/CommGrp_.lean:296 (braided case). CategoryTheory.Functor.Monoidal.ofChosenFiniteProducts, Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Monoidal/Cartesian/Basic.lean:948, supplies those structures for any finite-product-preserving functor. Lean at the pin: for any e : C ≌ D between cartesian monoidal categories, 'letI := Functor.Monoidal.ofChosenFiniteProducts e.functor; letI := Functor.Monoidal.ofChosenFiniteProducts e.inverse; e.mapGrp : Grp C ≌ Grp D' compiles. **Fix.** Change the target's library from 'absent' to 'partial'.
