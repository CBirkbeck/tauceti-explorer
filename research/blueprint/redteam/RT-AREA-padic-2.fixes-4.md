### Faltings, Lemma 6 node: cyclotomic inertia at a place above l

Node: `R28.2/inertia-triviality-on-the-quotient-by-the-l-divisible-group-of-the-formal-completion`.

- **Old reader bullet:** "on T_l(E_q) itself inertia acts through a nontrivial unipotent matrix when l ∤ v(q)".
- **New reader bullet:** the packet's `acceptance[2]`. At v_i | l, the toric submodule ℤ_l(1) of the split Tate curve carries the cyclotomic character, whose restriction to inertia has infinite image. So inertia on T_l(E_q) is neither trivial nor, in general, unipotent. The unipotent-inertia statement belongs to an auxiliary prime different from the residue characteristic.
- **Independent check.**
  - K_i/ℚ_l is finite and ℚ_l(μ_{l^∞})/ℚ_l is totally ramified of infinite degree, so K_i(μ_{l^∞})/K_i is infinitely ramified. Hence χ_l(I_i) is open in ℤ_l^×, in particular infinite.
  - Since T_l(E_q) has the subrepresentation ℤ_l(1), some σ ∈ I_i has eigenvalue χ_l(σ) ≠ 1, so the action is not unipotent.
  - The node's setting is v_i | l throughout, so the old bullet described the wrong situation. The node's statement, which concerns only the quotient, is unaffected.
- **Header.** The reader's opening paragraph now records this round and links this report.

### Perfectoid, `P1/distinguished-element-criterion`: the two generators of ker θ are associates

- **Removed item:** "p − [p♭] and Σ_{i<p}[ε]^{i/p} generate different ideals, so neither divides the other". It contradicted part (b) of the node itself, and the neighbouring node `P1/fontaine-theta-and-primitive-kernel`, whose acceptance already calls Σ_{i<p}[ε]^{i/p} "another generator".
- **Added:** the packet's corrected `acceptance[1]` (both generate ker θ, hence are associates) and its new negative example `acceptance[2]`.
- **Independent check of the negative example.**
  - μ = [ε] − 1 = ([ε^{1/p}] − 1)·ξ_cyc, because x^p − 1 = (x − 1)(1 + x + … + x^{p−1}).
  - θ(μ) = 1 − 1 = 0.
  - The zeroth Witt coordinate of [ε^{1/p}] − 1 is ε^{1/p} − 1. Normalising v(p♭) = 1, this has valuation 1/(p−1) > 0. By the unit criterion (d) of `P1/witt-vectors-of-perfect-plus-ring` ("Σ p^n[x_n] is a unit iff x_0 is a unit of R+"), the factor is a nonunit.
  - ξ_cyc is regular. So (μ) = (ξ_cyc) would force that factor to be a unit, and therefore (μ) is strictly smaller.
  - ξ_cyc is distinguished: it maps to p in W(k̄), so its first Witt coordinate is a unit.
- **Sources read for this item** (7 October 2026, in the scratch directory; the hashes match the round-three review's):
  - Scholze, *p-adic Hodge theory for rigid-analytic varieties*, [arXiv:1205.3463v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1205.3463v2), PDF pp. 35–36. Lemma 6.3 says that ker θ is generated by a nonzerodivisor ξ, "unique up to a unit". SHA-256 `ed9187b3269adb7e9964369470073ce8760ef56ef0c0b89538c0a1509f811959`.
  - Bhatt–Morrow–Scholze, *Integral p-adic Hodge theory*, [arXiv:1602.03148v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.03148v3), PDF pp. 22–23. Lemma 3.10 and Remark 3.11 say that ξ ∈ ker θ "generates Ker θ if and only if it is distinguished". SHA-256 `285f7d2088607688365ca92222dcf44c0acd6c4788dd872fe6a6191b9c4e072a`.
