## Ownership and attempted counterexamples

All 23 owner records were compared with the relevant descriptions. The
principal separations survive: bounded versus locally analytic theory;
generic completed algebra versus arithmetic norm towers; local Coleman
quotients versus global main conjectures; determinant/base-change algebra
versus arithmetic control; finite Fitting carriers versus order-specific
comparisons; exterior biduals versus system contractions.

Several tempting simplifications are false but are not made by the accepted
proposal:

- In Z_p[[T]], p^n tends to zero in the (p,T)-adic topology but not the
  T-adic topology. For p=3, the first cyclotomic relation is
  3T+3T^2+T^3, not T^3. The report's upstream topology correction gate
  therefore matters; the anchor is not silently accepted as corrected.
- Over Lambda = Z_p[[T]], Lambda/(p,T) has unit height-one characteristic
  ideal but zeroth Fitting ideal (p,T). PM L4 explicitly retains that
  distinction and its pseudo-null/finite-error content.
- Tensoring the two-term free complex [Z_p --p--> Z_p] with F_p gives a
  zero differential and two nonzero cohomology terms. Underived
  specialization cannot replace the retained derived/Tor comparison.
- Convolution of two Dirac masses at 1 lands at 2 for the additive law,
  but at 1 for the multiplicative law. Inclusion of units into the additive
  group is not an algebra map. PM L1 does not identify these products.
- The characteristic function of a proper clopen ball is locally analytic
  with zero derivative and is not globally constant. LAD's primitive
  ambiguity is correctly modulo locally constant functions.

These are mathematical boundary checks, not Lean tests.

PM becomes "Profinite and pro-p groups, Part II: p-adic measures and Iwasawa
algebras", using the actual base direction. The basic arbitrary-profinite
Z_p algebra/action is already in ProfiniteProPGroups; the new general-adic
comparison must extend that carrier rather than rebuild it.
StableReduction supplies the finite-presentation Fitting construction
already required for its singular locus. Neither anchor is edited.

The one deliberately exceptional suppliedBy entry is LAD L4 in PM L0a:
it records relocation out of the early stage. There is no mathematical
L4 -> L0a prerequisite. OverconvergentAutomorphicForms O0, PadicFamilies
L2a and PhiGammaModulesAndIwasawaCohomology PG.7 receive L4 links for the
relocated action. Scalar LAD L3 needs the retained character, not those
families. Forcing L4 -> L3 would cycle with the existing L3 -> L4 edge.
REV-RS-16 already documents and accepts this exception and asks the
orchestrator about the wording of section 15. It is not a newly discovered
missing theorem; a future generic suppliedBy interpreter must preserve this
distinction.

The existing CC.5 need for noetherian p-adic-Lie algebra is not met by
arbitrary-profinite completed algebra alone, and the proposal report says
so. Likewise a general logarithmic lower-bound roadmap is not a verified
Baker–Brumer independence theorem. Neither gap is declared solved here.
