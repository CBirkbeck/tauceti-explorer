roadmaps — `DeformationAndDerivedPatchingAlgebra`, `HabiroCyclotomicCompletions`, `HabiroRings`, `ComputationalNumberTheory`

Eligibility: `cc-2aeb03` wrote neither `AUDIT-17` nor `REV-AUDIT-17`.

## Method

- **Every declaration claim.** All 435 `declarations` entries (194 targets, 34 layers) were located at the pins, reading
  Mathlib at `082e2d3` and Tau Ceti with `git show f790474:<file>`. All exist in the cited file; 434 are at the cited line, and
  the remaining one, `Manifold.riemannianEDist`, is an `irreducible_def` at the cited line that a first textual pass missed.
- **What each declaration says.** For every target marked `mathlib`, `tauceti` or `partial`, each cited declaration was read at
  the pin and compared with the target and the note (generality, hypotheses, the `fit` label).
- **The absence claims.** Every target marked `absent` and every `not built` layer was searched for in both libraries under
  other names (Mathlib naming conventions, synonyms, the key concepts of the target). None of them was found: the absence
  claims hold. What the searches did turn up is related material the audit does not cite (findings below).
- **Verdicts, summaries and duplicates.** Each layer verdict was checked against its targets, each roadmap summary against its
  layers, and each `duplicates` list against the atlas (whether the named layer plans the same mathematics or only consumes it).

Each roadmap was attacked separately; `checked` in the result file records what was read, roadmap by roadmap.

### HabiroCyclotomicCompletions — 10 findings (1 high, 5 medium, 4 low)

- **RT-AUDIT-17/7** (library-claim, high). The note says 'Mathlib's substitution into power series requires exactly the nilpotence hypothesis the layer insists on'. That is false.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/8** (error, medium). The note says 'each P_N is monic'. It is not.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/9** (missing, medium). The cyclotomic congruence behind adjacency, Habiro's Lemma 4.1(1) eq. (4.1) (Φ_{p^e n} ≡ Φ_n^d mod p), is in Mathlib in characteristic-p form, and the audit misses it.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/10** (error, medium). The target and note treat injectivity of the polynomial map as needing unidentified hypotheses on R and S ('the cyclotomic-completion statements and their hypotheses are absent'). The only hypothesis needed is S ≠ ∅. For any commutative ring R and any n ∈ S, the kernel ⋂_{f∈Φ_S^*}(f) is contained in ⋂_k (Φ_n^k), which is 0 because Φ_n^k is monic of degree k·φ(n) ≥ k. For S = ∅, R[q]^∅ = R[q]/(1) = 0. The proof is a composition of Mathlib lemmas, so a builder who follows the note would state…
- **RT-AUDIT-17/11** (library-claim, medium). The note says 'Module completion with functoriality and finite products exists for a single ideal (adic case) only'. That is false.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/12** (missing, medium). The note cites only polynomial-level Taylor expansion.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/13** (missing, low). Comaximality of distinct cyclotomic factors over a coefficient ring in which their common order is a unit follows from three Mathlib lemmas, and the notes do not record it.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/14** (library-claim, low). Seven citations carry fit 'exact', but none of them is the target.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/15** (library-claim, low). The note credits uniqueness in the completion's universal property to Tau Ceti ('existence, and uniqueness in Tau Ceti'). Mathlib already has it for continuous maps into any Hausdorff space.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/16** (duplicate, low). HC.1 needs a carrier for P_N(q) = ∏(1 − q^i) = (q;q)_N, and the note says it 'has no carrier'. HabiroNahmSeries:HB.4 also plans to 'Define the q-Pochhammer products', but it is listed as a duplicate of no HC layer.

### ComputationalNumberTheory — 11 findings (4 medium, 7 low)

- **RT-AUDIT-17/26** (library-claim, medium). The note says 'No general class-group computation or completeness certificate exists', and the only Mathlib class-group citation is the Minkowski-bound lemma `NumberField.exists_ideal_in_class_of_norm_le` (fit related). But Mathlib at the pin has the completeness certificate for class number one.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/27** (missing, medium). The note ("Mathlib proves an integral basis exists; ... There is no Round 2 or p-maximal-order algorithm and no general certificate that a proposed basis is integral") and the citations leave out certified integral bases the libraries already have.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/28** (library-claim, medium). The note says Tau Ceti has 'the rank bound from any finite group containing the descent image ... its own docstring records that no finiteness or effective computation is done'. The docstring quoted is that of MordellWeil/SelmerGroup.lean and concerns only the local-conditions group `selmerGroup₂`. Tau Ceti at the pin also proves that the descent image lies in the S-unramified square-class group A(S,2) and that A(S,2) is finite (given finite class groups and finitely generated unit groups of the factor rings, which hold over number fields). With `pow_rank_le_card_of_range_μ_le` this gives the classical explicit-2-descent rank bound 2^rank · #E(K)[2] ≤ #A(S,2) with no conditional hypothesis.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/29** (duplicate, medium). CN.3 asks for Hecke matrices, isogenies and descent 'through existing owners'. Its duplicate list, even after REV-AUDIT-17's two additions, leaves out the layers that own three of its own cited declaration families and that already plan the missing parts.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/30** (missing, low). The target leaves out two Mathlib results that a builder of irreducibility certificates over ℤ would start from.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/31** (missing, low). The target cites only `GaloisField` and `ZMod.commRing`. Mathlib also has the conversions to the canonical carrier: an isomorphism from any finite field of order pⁿ to `GaloisField p n`, and from any degree-n extension of a finite field k to `FiniteField.Extension k p n`. Mathlib's own proof of the cited distinct-degree lemma applies the second to `AdjoinRoot f`. The note ('no computable presentation F_p[X]/(f) with a proved isomorphism to it exists') is right about computability, since both…
- **RT-AUDIT-17/32** (library-claim, low). All three citations carry fit 'exact', but they are the noncomputable truncation map on the abstract carrier ℤ_[p]. They are not the 'p-adic approximations with explicit precision' that CN.0, which asks for 'canonical mathematical carriers with separate computable presentations', means here.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/33** (other, low). The note says 'Mathlib has the local L-factor' but cites `WeierstrassCurve.LFunction`, the global formal Dirichlet series over a number field.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/34** (other, low). The note says Mathlib's interval arithmetic's 'docstring says it is not yet integrated', but the cited declaration is the order-theoretic `NonemptyInterval` in Mathlib/Order/Interval/Basic.lean, whose docstring says only 'This is a prototype for interval arithmetic'. The arithmetic and the quoted remark are in Mathlib/Algebra/Order/Interval/Basic.lean, which the audit does not cite.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/35** (other, low). The note says Tau Ceti has Smith normal forms 'for integer matrices with uniqueness'. The cited theorem covers only square integer matrices with nonzero determinant, and the uniqueness theorem the note relies on is not cited.
- **RT-AUDIT-17/36** (other, low). Both verdicts are consistent with REV-AUDIT-17's rule ('not built' = no target fully present), but the machine-readable verdict hides a lot of reusable material.

  point is recorded, at medium severity, for HR.5.

  states.
