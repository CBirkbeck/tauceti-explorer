### 2. missing, high: `AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry:A0-extension; AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry:R09.6`

The input list of A0-extension ('limit preservation, representable diagonal, deformation and obstruction functors, openness of versality and algebraization') omits approximation, and R09.6's text does not mention it. Two paper extractions nevertheless record Artin approximation as 'planned' at R09.6: PAPER-KISIN-17/P10, and PAPER-KISIN-PAPPAS-ZHOU-26/P10, which is Artin 1969 Cor. 2.6. So the omission is hidden. Neither library has any of this.

**Evidence.** A0-extension: 'For the selected Artin route prove the criterion itself: limit preservation, representable diagonal, deformation and obstruction functors, openness of versality and algebraization.' R09.6: 'Relate the deformation functor at a geometric point to the completed local ring ... Any invocation of Artin representability must include its hypotheses and a proof or an assigned supplier of the theorem itself.' Stacks project, read 2026-09-24: Prop. 98.17.2 (Tag 07Y5) assumes '$\mathcal{O}_{S, s}$ is a G-ring for all finite type points s of S'.
