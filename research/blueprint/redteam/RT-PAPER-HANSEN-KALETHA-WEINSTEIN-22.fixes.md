## 2. The proper-correspondence trace formula

Item 059 and route 2 require **both X/S and Y/S proper** for c:Y→X×_S X. In this setting properness of c₁ is equivalent to Y/S proper. Theorem 4.3.8 applies with p:Y→S and q:X→S, giving the induced endomorphism of the perfect RΓ(X,A) and the proper integral of its fixed-locus trace class. An automorphism has Y=X, so this requirement is automatic. No unspecified support generalization is promised.

The omitted condition is substantive. Take X=S and Y=⊔_{n≥1}S, with both correspondence maps the structure map and the identity local cohomological correspondence on every component. Its fixed locus is Y and its trace section is `(1,1,…)` in `H⁰(Y,Λ)=∏Λ`. Compactly supported sections form `⊕Λ`; this section has infinite support, so there is no compact-support integral giving the asserted operator. The finite controls check 256 proper finite-component cases; those finite sums do not establish the infinite case. E16 records the omitted hypothesis in [published Example 4.3.10, p. 33](https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2022.7), against the proper p,q requirement on p. 32. This does not disprove its proper-correspondence applications.
