## Corrections and reasoning

1. **Separate two support constructions.** BP26 Lemma 2.9.3 and Theorem 2.9.7, pp.27–29, use completed closed locales and compact-support graded pieces. The corrected IC.3 target states that filtered object, its j! triangle and equivariant spectral sequence. BCGP21 Proposition 3.9.11, pp.229–230, instead computes ordinary local cohomology by the Kempf/Ext/Koszul Cousin resolution. The latter no longer depends on the former. Integral Hecke relations use discrete proper formal functions from SF.4/P7; solid comparison remains G-solid. Completion now precedes its consumer in both packet and reader.
2. **Fix the stage order.** Localized perfect models depended on the integral convolution relation proved in IC.5 while being placed in IC.4. The model, non-Eisenstein specialization and complex congruence localization now follow that relation inside IC.5. IC.4 retains individual lifts, normalization, generator comparison and GAGA. Recomputed every stage requirement from actual fine-node parents, including `SchemeAndStackFoundations:key/coherent-duality` in SF.2. All internal node and stage edges now point backward in build order. IC.5 has six planets after removing two redundant landmarks; all stages meet the planet bound.
3. **State the fundamental-class input correctly.** The dimensions in the coefficient construction are those of C/S and X/S. The class needs projective embeddability and the SF.2 hypotheses, followed by the perfect-coefficient projection formula. Equal dimensions do not construct it. Boundary classes retain A0's relative Cartier conditions and the additional hypotheses of the determinant route in P20 Lemma 4.2.4.1.
4. **Strengthen reusable APIs and counterchecks.** Added two operation/compatibility clauses to every definition/construction, giving five each. The trivial Čech torsor check now uses A_n throughout. The ramified trace-dual example uses the inverse different. The complex congruence example uses O[t]/(t(t−ϖ)): two distinct generic characters reduce to the same residual ideal. Its API preserves the entire congruence summand, including generalized eigenspaces. Other additions cover identity/composition, coefficient naturality, orthogonal projectors, boundary maps and transport of the actual geometric constructions.
5. **Repair locators and source attribution.** Corrected Su's Propositions 3.14/3.16 to manuscript pp.36–38 and Proposition 4.1 to p.38; Lan–Suh Corollary 7.24 to p.43; CG18 trace to printed p.400; CG20 Appendix A.3.2 to pp.886–888. The totally real boundary source now includes BR16 Theorem 1.12 as well as Proposition 1.14. Both source catalogues and the reader agree.
6. **Read Harris directly and narrow its remaining gap.** H90 Theorem 2.7 and Proposition 2.7.2, pp.58–59, verify the comparison previously checked through secondary sources. The growth construction is Theorem 2.4.4, p.57. The singular large-weight bound is imported from AF.4/bhr-large-weight, reported in P20 p.108 and BCGP21 pp.239–240; H90's regular all-root bound in Theorem 3.6.3, p.64, does not prove it. H90 and the published GK19 correction are now cited. Full-group transport and the original BHR proof remain explicit obligations.

Every changed mathematical target, API/check and source locator is synchronized in the reader and roadmap definition. The suggested file needs no change: it already states exactly the native additive-image and integer-chamber slice, with heavy missing carriers represented only by reader targets.
