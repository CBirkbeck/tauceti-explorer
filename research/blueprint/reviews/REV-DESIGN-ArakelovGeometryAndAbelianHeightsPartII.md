## Material corrections

1. **Boundary completion and metrics (§§1.1–1.7, 2.2–2.7).** Mixed-coefficient effectivity needs both the rational model divisor and its integral restriction effective. A geometric projective boundary is zero and gives a discrete topology; an arithmetic projective boundary can have a positive constant Green function and gives a sup norm. Norm one requires a nonzero boundary; the underlying divisor of a Cauchy sequence is eventually constant. The non-model example now uses an irrational coefficient at a removed prime. Complex-base objects are over the specified Banach field. A constant arithmetic Green function scales along archimedean rays and vanishes at non-archimedean points. Nonproper canonical-measure mass uses adelic degree, and the trivially valued metric is characterized by its proper model prescription rather than curvature alone.
2. **Intersection theory and descent (§§0.3–0.4, 3.1–3.4, 4.1–4.4).** Nef nonnegativity uses ample perturbation and arithmetic Nakai–Moishezon, not small sections of the original nef bundle. The nonflat extension of Deligne pairing has finite-Tor, normality and flat-locus base-change restrictions. Pushforward formulas carry the required flatness. A false nonnormal-flat-base-change counterexample was removed. Integral Picard pullback needs geometrically reduced connected fibres: Frobenius on the explicit five-point elliptic curve in E3 disproves connectedness alone. The positive mixed-intersection argument now telescopes through nef perturbations instead of multiplying pseudoeffective classes. Arithmetic bigness is not generic-fibre bigness.
3. **Heights (§§4.7–4.9, 5.1, 5.5, 6.1–6.4, 7.7, 10.10, 11.3, 13.1–13.4).** Distinguished raw intersections, absolute number-field heights (division by the degree over ℚ), and geometric degrees over a fixed constant field. Number-field extension invariance applies to normalized values; geometric raw invariants scale with the ground-field extension. Choice of a field representing a point is a different operation. The variety height, small-sequence limit, generic-point Hodge degree, Moriwaki degree-zero specialization and Gross–Schoen fibre formula now follow the same conventions. Wazir’s theorems were checked in the public preprint with the big polarization, relative symmetric ample line bundle and degree-one height on the base curve specified; G1 is closed.
4. **Admissible metrics and theta (§§7.2–7.5, 8.1–8.9, 9.1).** Restricted the canonical curve-pair to genus greater than one. Bad reduction alone does not imply the model metric differs from the admissible metric: the tests now compute a banana-graph counterexample and a compact-type example. The theta twist test uses a nonzero constant over ℚ. Relative Hodge index uses the universal Poincaré pairing, not multiplication on the base. The arbitrary-degree relative theta identity is proved from section evaluation and the universal diagonal, rather than imported from JC3’s narrower degree-one field statement.
5. **Globalization, graph constants and moduli (§§10.1–10.14, 11.1, 13.4, 13.8).** The hybrid obstruction is at a nontrivial divisorial valuation over trivially valued constants, with explicit rescaling of a complex degeneration. It rules out a universal arithmetic ε rule, while special good-reduction families remain possible. Complex boundary growth is justified by model approximants and a boundary error below 1/78. Stable compactifications and universal families are used on the finite cover actually supplied by MC.6 and descended by surjectivity. Cinkir’s sharp genus-three bound was checked directly, closing G6. The bound for g≥5 is 109.625. The unit tetrahedral graph has δ=6, φ=17/48 and ε=11/6: the claimed coefficient-one boundary inequality has defect −181/48, whereas the corrected coefficient c(3)=17/288 has margin 15/8. These rational calculations are also checked in Lean.
6. **API and tests.** Added 34 API entries selected from the actual uses: isometry extensionality, uniqueness under model limits, canonical sections, intrinsic membership criteria, universal properties and base-change compatibility. Replaced misleading tests while retaining the total of 161. Every definition and construction has at least three tests that distinguish plausible wrong choices.
7. **Suggested Lean.** The ordered rational module now assumes monotonicity of positive scalar multiplication; the definiteness theorem also assumes an effective boundary. Without the latter, a negative boundary makes the displayed Archimedean implication vacuous. Without scalar monotonicity, an ordered rational module can have positive cone ℤ≥0 and violate the intended norm-zero law. Only light real-bodied data definitions and statable algebra/numeric targets occur; all remaining `sorry`s prove propositions.
8. **Reader and roadmap.** Regenerated all 93 reader nodes, APIs, tests, hypotheses, prerequisites, locators and match descriptions from the corrected packet. Aligned source editions, gaps, supplier requests, stage descriptions and the roadmap introduction.
