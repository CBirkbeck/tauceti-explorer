### Explicit convention witnesses

The ordinary two-variable calculation d(X dY)=dX∧dY fixes slot order; swapping X,Y negates the answer and a repeated slot is zero in F₂. The cotangent presentation for dual numbers uses multiplication by 2ε in degrees −1,0, giving the characteristic-two zero differential. The one-element regular quotient is N[1] in degree −1, and L∧^i(N[1])[−i]=Γ^iN has degree zero. The two-term identity complex gives Ext¹_Ch(A)(A,A[−1])=A, confirming i+c rather than i−c and distinguishing Ch(A) from D(A). Inverse Cartier on F₂[t] has [t dt]≠0 while the Frobenius endomorphism kills dt. For the semistable two-coordinate chart the relation is dlog x₁+dlog x₂=0 over the genuine π-chart, including ramified O_K. These are retained named README checks; the representable ordinary/cotangent/Cartier-scalar examples are in Suggested.lean, and the enhanced Beilinson/log constructions are named in its closing comment.
