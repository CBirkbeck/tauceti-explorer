## Target and hypothesis comparison

The common hypotheses are a prime p and a complete rank-one characteristic-p
perfectoid field F with valuation ring O_F. Algebraic closedness appears only
in specified geometric examples and residue-field reductions. Y, X, q,
Frobenius and the interval Huber pairs are the actual AdicSpaces Layer 6
objects. The conventions retain continuity, boundedness, plus subrings and
the (p,[varpi])-adic Witt topology. The open Y is the intersection D(p) and
D([varpi]), rather than the integral analytic domain with its characteristic-p
boundary. These hypotheses apply throughout F0–F4.

F5 separately fixes the coefficient hypotheses. Discrete Lambda is killed by
an ell-power, with ell different from p. The adic case requires I-adic
completeness, I generated by a finite regular sequence and an integer prime
to p in I, together with the enhanced, derived-complete supplier interfaces.
These are stated both in the conventions and in the coefficient layer.

## Ownership and prerequisite boundaries

Read the accepted packet and its independent review, the package handoff,
the relevant RS-20 ownership decision, supplier node statements, reviewed
library audit and both copies of the AdicSpaces link map. RS-20 assigns this
fixed-field branch to “Foundations of adic spaces, Part II”, extending
AdicSpaces unchanged. The upstream Layer 6 → F0 edge imports the adic objects
and chart structure. It is not evidence for a general coefficient-field or
relative-base construction.

The package imports the AdicSpaces ring, interval topology, plus rings,
wandering maps and quotient; A1's analytic Yoneda products; P1/P2's general
untilting and tilting; and D6's diamond and site functors. F2 adds the graph
comparison, rather than duplicating the quotient construction. F3 specializes
the general site comparison and effective descent. RelativeFarguesFontaine is
a consumer of the product, quotient/site and divisor seeds, with no reverse
prerequisite. Vector-bundle classification and general Div¹ geometry remain
outside this branch.

All six stronger requests in the accepted plan are stated as mathematical
interfaces in the package: complete AdicSpaces Layer 6 output; the integral
Q0 recognition bridge; R0's all-rational closed Cartier criterion; D0's
effective étale-sheaf descent; C2's enhanced left completion, operations and
finite-primary devissage; and L0's derived-complete finite-level limits on
both sides. In particular, the existing quotient-stack node is not treated
as a proof that the étale-sheaf pseudofunctor is a stack, and R0's closed
subspace construction is not substituted for the full Cartier criterion.

The library audit has no F0–F5 entries. Its L0 audit does not establish the
derived-complete adic category, and the C2 enhanced interface is not supplied
by an ordinary `CategoryTheory.Equivalence`. The package makes no contrary
claim. Supplier node names are specifications to import, not evidence that
those specifications have been implemented or all their reviews resolved.
