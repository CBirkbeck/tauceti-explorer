## 1. Claims that a target is in the libraries

I checked every citation with a script: TSV entry, name on the cited line, `private`, `@[deprecated]` and `sorry` in the file. All 153 original citations resolved, and none uses `sorry`; the one hit, in `Isogeny/Frobenius/Basic.lean`, is a docstring. One citation was `private` and has been removed: `TauCeti.Isogeny.isIntegral_eval_of_isIntegral` in E.7.

I read every present-labelled target's declarations in context:

* **Held.** Tau Ceti's `pointEquivDegreeZeroDivisorClass` gives Pic⁰(E) ≅ E(F) over any field. Its `IsDedekindDomain` hypothesis is discharged for elliptic curves by `isIntegrallyClosed_coordinateRing`. The degree splitting, the torsion-divisor function, `ExactStructure`, `finiteProjectiveModulesExactStructure` (any `Ring R`), Mathlib's `dedekindZeta` and its continuous cohomology also hold.
* **K.3, resolution theorem: `tauceti` → partial.** `ExactStructure.resolutionEquiv` assumes `P ≤ E.isProjective`, and its file says the general resolving-subcategory case "is not carried out". It is also degree zero only. K.3 is now **not built**.
* **K.7, product vs tensor product on K₀: `tauceti` → partial.** `SplitK0.of_mul_of` is a ring structure on split K₀ of a monoidal category. It is not instantiated for Proj_fg of a commutative ring, and the higher product it should be compared with does not exist. K.7 is now **not built**.
* **K.1, small models: `tauceti` → partial.** Transporting the exact structure is built. Independence from the small model exists only for K₀.
* **R.2, continuous cochains.** The note credited Mathlib's object with Tau Ceti's corestriction, cup products, Shapiro and long exact sequence. Those live in Tau Ceti's separate explicit model (degrees ≤ 2). The cochain complex itself, `TopRep.homogeneousCochains`, is now cited.

## 3. Absent and partial claims, re-searched

I re-searched every absent or partial target in all five roadmaps in both source trees, by name and by concept, and read each hit. These claims survived:

* Adams operations and λ-rings, the Q-construction, K_n for n ≥ 1, Waldhausen categories and spectra.
* Tame symbols, K₂, SK₁, coniveau, the projective-bundle theorem, Tate modules and the Hasse bound (only its inputs exist), Néron models.
* The scheme of a Weierstrass curve, uniformisation, the Tate curve, algebraic de Rham cohomology, hypercohomology, Deligne cohomology.
* Hecke characters, CM, conductors, modular curves and units, Rankin–Selberg, syntomic cohomology, p-adic L-functions and p-adic regulators.
* Bloch groups, Bockstein, norm residue, quantum dilogarithms, Habiro rings.

These were wrong or understated and are now corrected:

* **R.1, reduction theory: absent → partial.** Mathlib has `ModularGroup.exists_smul_mem_fd`. Tau Ceti has `ModularGroup.volume_fd_lt_top` and `isFundamentalDomain_fdo`. Together they give covering and finite volume for SL₂(ℤ)\ℍ.
* **R.1, finite generation: absent → partial.** Mathlib has `SpecialLinearGroup.SL2Z_generators` and Schreier's `Subgroup.fg_of_index_ne_zero`; Tau Ceti proves SL_n(ℤ) is generated by transvections. The note had said "even SL₂(ℤ) has no finite-generation theorem".
* **R.1, Weil restriction: partial → absent.** Nothing was cited and the note conceded absence.
* **K.1, Q(C): absent → partial.** Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Span.comp` and `SpanBicat` give spans with restricted legs composed by pullback.
* **K.2:plus, cofinality: partial → mathlib.** This is `Module.Finite.exists_comp_eq_id_of_projective`, a named lemma the note said did not exist.
* **K.3, dévissage: absent → partial.** Tau Ceti's `simpleClassBasis` makes G₀ of an Artinian ring free on the simple classes, contradicting "not even the K₀ form is present".
* **ER.8, p-adic Beilinson: partial → absent.** Only ℤ_p was cited, and the note said the statement cannot be made.

## Most important finding

The audit's absence verdicts are sound: no K-theory above K₀, no Deligne or syntomic cohomology, no dilogarithm as a function of z, no Habiro ring. Its duplicate lists were not. Two thirds of them were suppliers, consumers, same-roadmap layers or checkpoints, while real owners were missed, including the Tau Ceti roadmap layers that own the built Pic⁰(E) ≅ E(F) result.

At target level, two degree-zero results were overstated as built: the K₀ resolution theorem, which covers only projective resolving subcategories, and the K₀ product comparison. As a result, K.3 and K.7 are not built. In the other direction, SL₂(ℤ) reduction theory and finite generation, f.g. projectives as retracts of Rⁿ, spans composed by pullback, and degree-zero dévissage were missed.
