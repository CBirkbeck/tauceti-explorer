## Material corrections and proof boundaries

1. Global polarized pseudodeformations are separated from unrestricted local ones. Conrad's finite-extension assertion now requires quasi-separation and finitely many components. Polarized lifts are strictly conjugate by a Hensel square root, not equal, and their multiplier is fixed as part of the datum.
2. Trianguline continuation uses the closed locus and accumulating classical points. The character-family argument keeps the correct unit congruence group and the finite-étale component lifting. The base-change field's splitting conditions, rather than a blanket exclusion of CM forms, ensure cuspidality.
3. The boundary-annulus argument handles weight two separately, uses positive twin slopes for noncriticality and unequal slopes at the newly introduced forms. Tests that would reject valid CM points or absolutely irreducible residual loci are replaced.
4. Local type calculations distinguish the residue and coefficient primes, retain the required unitary sign, and state Frobenius independence modulo the uniformizer, which is all that survives in the added rank-one case. The integral Hecke presentation uses positive braid generators unless q is a unit. Rank-nine exponents and recursions are now explicit.
5. CTIII's pairing uses the missing contragredient module N and two mutually annihilating spaces; it does not silently identify a lattice with its dual. The l=5 two-member packet and l=7 four-member packet are separated. The disputed Z/W socle step remains G4.
6. Residually reducible arguments use weak-source PL.6/PL.7 contracts with geometric primitivity and an initial subgroup-splitting coefficient field. The generic local representation in CTIII is handled by Clifford theory and unequal residual block dimensions, not an unsupported primitivity inference. Partition rings constrain Frobenius polynomials, not every point's monodromy partition.
7. Thorne's Theorem 6.11 uses **residual** genericity and concentration of a **Hecke-localized derived tensor**, not bare Drinfeld compact cohomology. Its even-multiplicity weight condition and finite place set are explicit. G3 and restructure R4 agree with this correction.
8. The NTI seed route is conditional even at n=3; fixing the GL₁ range does not fix the non-self-dual GL₂ block. Only powers of two avoid that seed obstruction. The first prime switch in Theorem E is nonordinary. Conditionality propagates through the NTI base used by NTII; low-rank endpoint theorems are not substituted for stronger intermediate seed data.
9. Symmetric-power patching has a separate n=1 endpoint. The unitary modules explicitly require automorphy of the second form's symmetric power. Freeness uses the stabilizer exponent level offset, with N≥1. The regularity assertion uses P localized at the automorphic prime, and the dyadic torsor is a formal-functor statement.
10. Seasoning uses a fixed n≥3, the PSL/PGL sandwich and the >5 bound. The auxiliary compositum lemma is Galois theory, not finite-field theory. The removal step preserves the conductor bounds in M; the H_k/H′_k induction uses ≤k with the two implications spelled out.
11. NT26's tensor setup states local-degree and congruence bounds, already assumes SP_{r−1},SP_{r+1} when defining the blocks, and uses types with monodromy. Equal cycles imply equal reduced fibres with the flatness and dimension hypotheses retained. The auxiliary-place finiteness has two calls and retains its supplier boundary. The final characteristic-zero point needs a second application of Proposition 3.13.
12. Tensor lifting distinguishes Σ_π (quaternion ramification) from Σ_π∪Σ_σ (S and unitary level). Its local tensor-map proof uses the stable torsion-semistable quotient and density, not an ordinary-filtration shortcut. The ordered cumulative Hecke eigenvalue products, dyadic local-torus rank and level offsets are corrected. Residual perfect-subgroup prime selection is separated from characteristic-zero enormousness for Selmer vanishing.
13. Former G6 is repaired: independent projective fields give a perfect SL₂×SL₂ subgroup in the joint residual image. The cyclotomic quotient is abelian, so the product remains on cyclotomic restriction and the tensor is absolutely irreducible there. Chebotarev also excludes the finite bad set, including p. In the two-dihedral-place theorem the characteristic-two strict field-size bound uses both distinct local primes. Modular two-block decomposition is semisimplified, and all r=1 branches avoid SP₀.

The target API additions are the local determinant-restriction compatibility, extensionality of level-raising data, coefficient-change/duality for the CT setup, universal points for deformation data and tensor problems, the ordered symmetric-character compatibility, the companion's local connection, and tensor diamond augmentation.
