## Material corrections

- The normalized coefficient system now uses the enhanced limit of stable categories from EnhancedDerivedSheaves §E4.5, with all higher coherence and homotopy-coherent morphisms. A triangulated 2-limit with identity/triple equations is insufficient. Uniform ordinary bounds, Tor bounds and common strata remain separate witnesses. The point tests distinguish the underived residue tower from the derived levels of the actual integral torsion object O/λ.
- EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.5 supplies finite-level biduality on ctf inputs. The compact-vanishing proof no longer imports it for arbitrary nonperfect lisse finite modules: it uses residue-field dévissage over the Artinian self-injective coefficient rings. For integral DVR complexes, stratumwise perfect models give uniformly perfect derived reductions, so the ctf import really applies. Integral torsion is retained.
- Existing ComplexComparisonPartII §§C6.0–C6.6 ordinary/compact Artin and adic comparisons, proper direct-image and separated lower-shriek ctf exchange are imported rather than planned again. Its §C5.4 already supplies the integral Chern/exponential identification. The remaining categorical/perverse, ramified-coefficient and stable-lattice rational refinements are identified explicitly. The additional nonproper direct-image exchange is the only remaining SF.2 comparison request. BBD 6.1.2 supplies the non-ctf localization argument.
- ClassicalAdicEtaleCohomology §5.4 already supplies general-base nearby cycles, oriented products and Orgogozo's constructibility/base-change-after-modification theorem. The corresponding unowned-gap entry is removed and its consumers routed to that exact contract. Its proper and nonproper scheme/adic comparisons retain their different coefficient hypotheses.
- ECD 27.1–27.4 supply right-adjoint recovery of dualizing complexes and internal Hom. Diamond acceptance checks now assert these Rc_* identities. Strong c^* transport still requires an essential-image/counit theorem; full faithfulness alone is not used to infer it.
- The global blow-up decomposition, exceptional Gysin columns, inverse signs and compact version already belong to EtaleCohomologyFoundations §F.7. They are imported in full. The sheaf-level construction and trace, support and Frobenius refinements remain here. The LTXZZ citation uses the published Lemma 5.11.3(2–5), pp.251–252, rather than an ambiguous preprint page.
- Corrected geometric edge cases: Cartier sections, connected curves for a scalar top trace, geometric bases for projective-space tests, separated finite-type schemes for compact supports, and bounded constructible coefficients in the projective-bundle application. Added the two-disjoint-fibre nef-divisor counterexample and primitive degree-zero, projective-space and quadric computations. No unconditional complementary decomposition is inferred from orthogonality.
