**Verdict: accept.** Independent review by Claude Code, session `cc-48533a`, 30 September 2026, issue #4488. The extraction was made by Claude Code, session `cc-f805bf`, in issue #4487.

- **Paper.** Fargues and Scholze, "Geometrization of the local Langlands correspondence".
  - **Read:** arXiv:2102.13459v4 (356 pp., SHA-256 1f804075…2027, the file the extraction read), using its LaTeX source and page images; PDF page and printed page coincide.
  - **Not read:** the published version, Astérisque 466 (2026), which is paywalled. Every finding is scoped to v4.
  - **Other versions:** readers compared the v1–v3 TeX sources to date slips. The PDFs on Fargues's and Scholze's pages match v4.

## Method

Twelve readers worked in parallel:

- nine on the items, by chapter: I; II; III with IV §§1–3; IV §§4–6; V with early VI; late VI; VII; VIII; IX–X;
- two on the recorded mistakes, E1–E35 and E36–E70;
- one on the seven routes and the prerequisites.

What every item reader did:

- Read its chapters in full in the TeX.
- Checked each item's statement and locator.
- Checked its status against:
  - the stage descriptions;
  - the accepted decompositions in `data/decompositions/`;
  - the draft packets in `research/blueprint/packets/`;
  - other papers' accepted routes;
  - the libraries at the pinned commits.

The lead reviewer:

- merged the readers' changes;
- resolved conflicts, which were all between compatible wordings;
- removed six duplicate findings that two readers found independently;
- re-checked the two most substantive new mistakes (see below);
- made the decisions below.

`python3 scripts/check_paper.py` reports the corrected file `ok`.

**One rule was applied throughout.** PROTOCOL §6 says a packet goes live in the atlas only when its review accepts it. The layer's plan is otherwise its stage description, together with the accepted decomposition. So an item counts as planned only if one of the following plans it:

- a stage description;
- an accepted decomposition node;
- a promoted blueprint;
- another paper's accepted route.

Several readers cited nodes of draft packets that have not been reviewed: BunGAndNewtonStrata, the §VIII.2 part of LanglandsParameterStacks, and ExcursionOperatorsAndSpectralAction. The lead reviewer reverted those 36 status changes. The items stay missing, are routed to the layers the draft nodes name, and record the draft node in their notes. The VectorBundlesAndIsocrystals and RelativeFarguesFontaine packet nodes the readers cited are almost all present in the accepted decompositions, so those changes stand.

## Outcome

The extraction reads the paper carefully. Its statements and locators are almost all right, and the mistakes it records are real. Its weakness was the status check: it read only stage descriptions, not the accepted decompositions that plan much of FS node by node.

**Corrected file:**
- 847 items: 733 planned, 2 library, 112 missing (834 as submitted: 661, 2, 171);
- 12 routes, all accepted: seven corrected, five added;
- 129 recorded mistakes: the extraction's 70, all confirmed, and 59 new ones.

**Changes:** 74 items moved from missing to planned and 11 from planned to missing. 245 items were changed in all, and 13 items added.

**The substantive corrections:**

- **Statements.**
  - Twenty-eight items in §§VI.7–VI.8 wrote Div^d_Y where the paper has Div^d_𝒴 = (Spd O_E)^d/Σ_d. For the comparison with the Witt vector affine Grassmannian this makes the statement false.
  - Several items dropped a bar, writing Ĕ for its algebraic closure or F_q for F̄_q.
  - Several items still carried printed text that `sourceIssues` corrects: E2, E30, E36–E38 and E44.
- **Planned after all.** Examples:
  - the classical points and PID statements (VB2:classification);
  - the Artin v-stack and ULA theory of Chapter IV (VS0–VS1);
  - the strata of Bun_G as classifying stacks (VS4);
  - Chapter VI's ULA and constant-term lemmas (GS1);
  - the dual-group identification lemmas (GS4);
  - Remark VIII.5.18 and Proposition VIII.5.19 (LP3).
- **Missing after all:**
  - the Chapter X conjectures credited to ModularityAndLanglandsExtensions ML.5, whose text names categorical Langlands only as an area;
  - Example V.3.4;
  - the Lubin–Tate formal group, which an accepted Part II owns.
- **Routes.**
  - Four items belong to existing owners outside the FS roadmaps, and one to the accepted StableCenter Part II; five routes were added for them.
  - Route 6's premise, that §VIII.2 is unplanned, is only half right.
- **New mistakes.** All were found by readers. The two marked "re-checked" were checked again independently by the lead reviewer.
  - A sign error in Lemmas IV.4.25–IV.4.26: φ − π^{−d} should be φ − π^{d}. Re-checked: the paper's own H¹(O(n)) computation (§II.2.2) uses φ − π^n : B_{[1,q]} → B_{[1,1]}, and the proof of IV.4.26 reduces to H¹(O(d)) = 0.
  - The false identification HH²(B/A) = Ext¹(L_{B/A}, B) in §VIII.2. Re-checked: for B = k[x,y], HH² ≅ ∧²T_B ≅ B, while Ext¹(Ω_B, B) = 0.
  - Failing steps in the proofs of:
    - Theorem II.2.6 (the norm estimate);
    - Proposition II.3.1;
    - Theorem VIII.5.15 (the inner case);
    - Proposition IX.6.3.

    None of them changes a stated main result.
  - "Ẑ^p has global dimension 1" in the proof of VII.2.3. It is false, but only weak dimension one is needed.
