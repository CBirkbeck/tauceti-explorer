- Corrected the C_p gauge. For x≠0 its real value is 2^(-log_{‖ϖ_0‖}‖x‖); gauge(0)=0 and gauge(ϖ_0)=1/2. If ϖ_0^p=p, gauge(p)=2^{-p}, giving 1/4 at p=2. Restriction from the perfectoid closed disc to |ϖ|≤|T|≤1 is not individually dense: the coefficient of T^{-1} vanishes on its image and is nonzero on T^{-1}. Added continuity of the field algebra map to the separable quadratic-extension control. An open immersion is strongly finite étale only when also closed; a proper rational open is the negative case.
- Restored the all-factoring-affinoid-neighborhood quantifier in the tilde-limit density condition. A one-point topology alone does not give density: Q_p is not dense in C_p. An uncompleted dense colimit can satisfy the tilde-limit relation; completeness is an additional perfectoid requirement. Added diagram identity/composition laws, spectral transition-map hypotheses, and actual rings-of-definition conditions to the representative Lean models.
- Removed the false implication from uniform κ-smallness to existence of a smaller uncountable strong-limit cutoff.
