## RT-AREA-analytic/1 — confirmed, medium

The need is real. [Schmidt–Stix, published pp.839–840, Proposition 5.1 and Lemma 5.2](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v184-n3-p05-p.pdf#page=23) uses generalized Chebotarev on regular connected arithmetic models to compare finite Galois covers through their split closed points. This is not just the prime-ideal theorem for number fields. [Abdurrahman–Venkatesh v1, §§2.8 and 2.14, pp.18 and 25–26](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.13436v1#page=25) uses closed-point Frobenius information and a weighted density argument to trivialize an étale double cover over a characteristic-zero, normal, finitely generated integral ring. These passages establish the consumers; they do not authorize an unrestricted replacement statement.

The owner does not fit. [AN.4](https://github.com/CBirkbeck/tauceti-explorer/blob/ae16efd59f8727140b163dd6a3318e0da593cd2a/content/campaign/AnalyticNumberTheory/README.md#L61) is a number-field analytic interface. The accepted [RS-07 AN.4 entry](https://github.com/CBirkbeck/tauceti-explorer/blob/ae16efd59f8727140b163dd6a3318e0da593cd2a/research/blueprint/restructure/RS-07.result.json#L70) narrows it to arithmetic-to-analytic comparisons, prime-ideal applications and specified Artin interfaces. The [upstream Chebotarev scope](https://github.com/CBirkbeck/tauceti-explorer/blob/ae16efd59f8727140b163dd6a3318e0da593cd2a/content/tau-ceti/Chebotarev/README.md#L1), its `HeightOneSpectrum (𝓞 K)` carrier and Layers 10/14 establish number-field scope too. **Correct repair.** Give the missing arithmetic-scheme interface a single source-qualified supplier. A **Chebotarev, Part II** is the natural extension; [§15–16](https://github.com/CBirkbeck/tauceti-explorer/blob/ae16efd59f8727140b163dd6a3318e0da593cd2a/research/blueprint/PROTOCOL.md#L585) forbids replanning a Tau Ceti roadmap directly. Import its number-field specializations and existing étale-cover/fundamental-group infrastructure. Reconcile the two routes, replace the mismatched acceptance rationale, and obtain the appropriate review instead of automatically accepting Schmidt–Stix when a name has been chosen.

The finding's proposed theorem is too broad. A normal integral ring finitely generated over ℤ can be `𝔽_p`. Its spectrum has one closed point. In the connected degree-two constant cover `Spec 𝔽_(p²)`, that point has the nonidentity Frobenius in `C₂`; the identity class is not realized. Thus the asserted every-class/dense-Frobenius interpretation cannot be adopted for arbitrary such rings.

Begin with the dominant characteristic-zero arithmetic models required by the verified consumers; state regularity/normality, nonemptiness, connected finite étale Galois covers, conjugacy-invariant subsets and density normalization explicitly. Treat any positive-characteristic extension separately with its degree and constant-field restrictions. For quantitative density, distinguish a relative density from an absolute weighted count and verify its dimensional normalization against the primary theorem. I have not read the full Serre §9 proof or Serre 1965 Theorem 7 here: source/proof extraction and normalization remain explicit obligations of the supplier work, not facts certified by this ownership review.

The same page distinguishes this ordering from discriminant ordering. The accepted plan already identifies this result as a theorem; the proposed repair would instead present the provable content as only bounds towards a conjecture.

Keep the accepted ownership split and the exact family/ordering. A historical conjecture label or cross-reference in the short ST.3 paragraph may help a reader, but its absence is not an unplanned mathematical target. Do not duplicate the Part II theorem in ST.3 or downgrade it to a conjectural endpoint. Any distinct conjectural variant must be stated separately.
