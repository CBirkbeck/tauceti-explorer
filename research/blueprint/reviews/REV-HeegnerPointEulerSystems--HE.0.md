New E5 concerns Khayutin Lemma 5.8, printed p.184, and persists in arXiv v3. In the split algebra Q₃×Q₃, take Λ={(a,b)∈Z₃²:a≡b mod 3}. The basis (1,1),(3,0) has trace discriminant 9; the different is (3,−3)Λ and its generator has norm −9. A unit of Λ has norm congruent to 1 modulo 3, so no other generator has norm +9. The correct statement uses the discriminant ideal or local absolute norm. Principal invertibility survives this correction.
