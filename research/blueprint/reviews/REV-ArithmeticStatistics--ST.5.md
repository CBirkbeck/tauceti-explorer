The clear corrections were applied in the packet and, wherever they appear, the suggested file:

1. **Use the source denominator.** PR Conjecture 1.1, printed p. 2, specifies a fixed global field and coefficients of bounded height, with long Weierstrass equations in characteristics 2 and 3. Conjecture 1.4(a)–(c), pp. 3–4, uses that convention. The input instead attached its joint squarefree prediction to minimal integral short models over Q ordered by invariant height. The revised node states the source conjecture. Its first moment has the same numerical σ(n) as the parent, but no transfer between those denominators is asserted. PR Conjectures 1.7 and 1.8, p. 4, likewise require global coefficient-model families; ST.0's existing integral even-degree family is too narrow. `R-family-adapters` now asks for the exact global-field, odd/even-degree and characteristic-specific extensions. `G-source-family` records the missing comparison. Distribution convergence and unbounded moment convergence remain separate conjectural assertions.
2. **Preserve the quadratic sign and existing theta owner.** PR Corollary 4.6, pp. 15–16, gives the negative Weil-cup polar pairing; there is no division by 2 in the dyadic polar convention. In odd degree the quadratic refinement is −(1/2)x cup x. The existing `AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli:A3/theta-group` already plans the central extension, while `A3/dual-isogeny-and-cartier-kernel` supplies the kernel duality. They are imported. `R-theta-quadratic` moves from A2 to A3 and asks only for the further quadratic obstruction and fppf comparisons. PR Theorem 4.13 still has the finite-group-scheme Sha¹ quotient, which must not become elliptic Sha.
3. **Import generic probability already in Mathlib.** Removed `R-probability`, which had asked a p-adic-distribution layer to supply product measures and Borel–Cantelli. Added five actual pinned declarations listed below. The application-specific finite-isotropic inverse-system construction and its Borel comparison remain `G-lc-duality`; finite-marginal uniqueness alone is not an existence theorem on that carrier.
4. **Repair two proof sketches.** The harmonic mass bound uses all prime powers, so ζ(2) alone does not give the proposed upper bound. Bound the factors by the product over all integers m≥2: the finite product of (1−m⁻²)⁻¹ telescopes to 2M/(M+1), tending to 2. Separately, fixed finite powers of N are well-ordered lexicographically; the input's criticism of Wood's lexicographic induction was false. The corrected sketch follows that induction and retains the essential absolute-convergence justification.
5. **Request the general abelian chain, not an elliptic near miss.** The parent's isogeny-factorisation Selmer sequence states an elliptic result. The conditional general-abelian rank node now requires `SelmerIwasawaCohomology:L0` to supply the sequence for arbitrary abelian varieties in BKLOS Lemma 9.1, printed pp. 12–13. The additional positive uniform rank-index constant remains mandatory.
6. **Complete the user API and diagnostics.** Added `LCQuadraticData.maximalIsotropics_isotropic` to expose quadratic vanishing without unfolding the subtype. Strengthened the global-splitting test with the actual Z/4 diagnostic: the local distinguished zero subgroup splits, whereas 2(Z/4) has no module complement. This is a logical test, not an arithmetic counterexample.
7. **Correct locators and ownership.** Joint moments cite PR Conjecture 1.4(c), p. 4, as well as (b). BKLOS's signature argument is Section 11, pp. 17–18, not nonexistent Sections 10.3/10.4. The BKLPR author-copy harmonic argument is on pp. 24–25. Current Tau Ceti already implements scalar real moment determinacy; StandardDistributions delegates that work to completed OrthogonalL2Bases. The ownership note now names the implemented theory, whose exponential-integrability hypotheses do not give Wood's partition-moment theorem.
