## Corrections

1. **Monic fixed-modulus sieve (§2.13).** Its error constant omitted dependence on Σ, although BSW I Theorem 4.1 p.21 states that dependence and its proof p.23 absorbs the exceptional-prime product. The constant now depends on the fixed family. Expanded the truncation Y=X^(1/κ): excluded squarefree-modulus density is O_(Σ,ε)(m^(−2+ε)), the summed congruence error is O_(Σ,κ,ε)(X^(D−1/κ+ε)), and the product remainder is O_(Σ,ε)(X^D/Y^(1−ε)). Added full, empty and large exceptional-prime negative controls. Excluding a whole fixed weighted box at one arbitrarily large exceptional prime refutes a constant uniform over Σ.
2. **Quartic singleton Check (§2.3).** An ordinary GL₂ stabilizer always contains ±1. Replaced the impossible trivial stabilizer by `{±1}`, made the height indicator explicit, and stated 0≤N({f};X)≤1 with zero when X≤H(f). No strict positivity is asserted for an arbitrary singleton/averaging region.
3. **Quartic reducible main body (§2.3).** The all-h multiplicity bound n_i conflicted with the retained modular boundary. A fixed vector has at most 2n_i≤8 preimages under its real orbit map; the sharper orbit-total formula is only used on the common conull averaging set. The asymptotic bound is unchanged.
4. **Integral orbit weights (supporting cubic contract 2).** Defined m₀ as the prescribed orbit selector, stated the positive-denominator domain and the zero-selector case, and required a product of local selectors for the factorization claim. An arbitrary rational-orbit function is not asserted to factor locally.
5. **Ownership and names.** Reused Part II's normalized cubic discriminant, rather than introducing it again; formatted the local kernel expression as mathematics rather than an accidental Markdown link; aligned tail, acceptability and kernel API names with the actual `Counting` namespace. Clarified that the rectangular curve bound uses an occurring pure monomial, not a reducible pure-power defining polynomial. Added the singular real-cubic sign-region Check and removed process wording without removing mathematics.
6. **Lean witnesses and omissions.** Added native polynomial discriminant/central-action and rational-matrix Jacobian examples. Added a closing list of README definitions whose arithmetic or geometric carriers are not introduced in this file. No new heavy carrier or auxiliary theory was developed.
