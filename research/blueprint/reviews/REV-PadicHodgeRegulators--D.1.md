## Mathematical and interface corrections

- L1: the semilocal logarithm requires injectivity of each exponential. The original purported non-example comparing a product with diagonal Galois action on a direct sum is false; two independent component classes versus the diagonal subspace give a valid test. Replaced finite/discrete class-formation Ext duality as a supplier for rational continuous representations with the actual D7 local pairing interfaces plus an explicit rational local-duality/Euler-characteristic request. Removed the hidden cycle in which duality used dimensions whose proof used duality. Fontaine–Ouyang 9.27 supplies the semistable case; de Rham H_f descent remains unverifiable. The ℓ≠p clause is restricted to the finite unramified p-primary modules of the read supplier. Finite Tau Ceti Kummer classes are not the continuous p-adic identification; added the Selmer L0 supplier.
- L1 integral lattice: independently evaluated BNQD §§2.2.4/2.3.2 rather than inferring a lattice from its index. For r≥2, the trace of Ξ_{r,1}(a(1+X)) is −p^r(1−p^{r−1}σ^{-1})(1−p^rσ^{-1})^{-1}σ^{-1}(a). The three remaining factors are integral automorphisms, and (r−1)! is a p-unit for 2≤r≤p−2. Thus the source lattice is exactly p^r O_L, not just a lattice of the same index. The r=1 case remains the unit logarithm, and the argument does not extend the stated weight range.
- L2: distinguish the chosen representation basis ε^{⊗j} in twists from L0's canonical de Rham vector t^{-j}⊗ε^{⊗j}; they transform differently. Removed the unsupported assertion that the CC99 Kummer sign was computed here. The sign remains a precise gap. The integral torsion-free conclusion from the ψ-fixed module and the CC99 generator-change chain map were checked and retained.
- D.1: import local-unit decomposition and unramified Frobenius from upstream. The logarithm clause is restricted to finite extensions of Q_p. IsArithFrobAt is a congruence predicate, not existence of an automorphism. The total dilogarithm extension is componentwise; its identities assume admissibility. Its free additive extension now has domain FreeAbelianGroup A^adm, matching the mathematical construction. Clarify rational units modulo torsion in the Leopoldt criterion; add the missing Tate representation factor in the normalization dictionary.
- D.2: the σ-semilinear rigid syntomic cone is Q_p-linear, not K_0-linear. At a point its differential is b↦(−b,Ab), A=1−σ/p^n, and η[(a,c)]=a+A^{-1}c. Removed the doubled Tate period in the exponential comparison. Besser 8.8 is a proposition. Higher-weight Gros values are conditional on comparison with de Jeu's normalized symbols; E103's claim of a source error is rejected. Classical integral/log-syntomic ownership moves to the accepted early CohomologyComparisons Part II producer requirement. CN §5.1.1 genuinely uses p^r−φ: do not call that notation wrong. A p^r-exact fundamental sequence is not an integral quasi-isomorphism that can be inverted; the actual directed period morphism and divided/undivided comparison are missing. Use the proved N(K,p,r) bound rather than asserting the stronger introduction-only N(e,p,r) bound.
- D.3: generic-field formal unramifiedness cannot detect arithmetic ramification. Removed that baseline and import the actual upstream unramified substrate. The residue inputs are nonzero and different from 1. For s>1 there are q−2 inputs and fibres of size at most p−2, giving more than q/p values; for s=1 the degree/root-multiplicity argument gives a nonzero value as well as f(−1)=0. Translated images have the same cardinality, so differences span as well. Product difference-spanning uses p^{-2}D values in O_L, not unscaled values in p²O_L. Correct the F_5 test: μ_4 minus 1 has three values; li₂,₅(2)=1, li₂,₅(3)=3, li₂,₅(4)=0 and f₅ takes 1,4,0 there. Add the direct inversion and equivariance prerequisites. The regulator isomorphism still follows from equal finite free rank and Orzech after surjectivity.
- D.4: removed totally-imaginary-only integral K-theory suppliers from statements for arbitrary number fields. Require the denominator integer N to be nonzero; remove an unsupported global sharpness claim; root classes are the coefficient-localized ⟦ζ⟧, not arbitrary raw symbols. Q-linear injectivity is equivalent to rank of the Z-span of the regulator values; it does not imply Q_p-linear independence or Z_p-span rank. Add StrongPadicK3RegulatorInjective and only the valid one-way implication. For α outside Q, (a,b)↦a+αb is injective over Q but its K-linear extension has kernel (−α,1).
- D.5: replace the impossible proof using the cokernel of an invertible operator with the full Besser fibre sequence. The raw fixed-q-Frobenius boundary and the normalized exponential identification need an actual modified-to-rigid comparison and transported K-module structure; neither the point computation nor AC's Q_p footnote supplies the general map. Add homotopy independence to the open-curve splitting. Replace the ill-defined residue-free-complement non-example by dlog(t) on P¹ minus {0,∞}, whose nonzero residues project to zero. Narrow the semistable boundary to NN's actual H_st factorization and BZ's integration comparison; they do not prove the asserted weight-two ker N/convenient-quotient formula.

Added specific extensionality/equality criteria to all remaining definitions and constructions: pointwise map equality, submodule membership, polynomial coefficients, free-generator evaluations, or induced cohomology maps as appropriate.
