The source-issue review added five v2 findings, each checked on a rendered page and against adjacent formulas:

**E1 — arXiv:1810.04198v2, Theorem 8.1 proof, p.36, the compact induction immediately before the citation to MP96 Proposition 6.6; no claim about the journal version.** The superscript of this depth-zero compact induction is G_{n+1}(k). The later compact induction of π_K̃ to G(k) is unchanged. This sentence verifies the depth-zero input for Yu on the terminal group G_{n+1}. MP96 is invoked before adding the positive-depth oscillator factors; induction to G(k) occurs only after that construction. When G_{n+1} is a proper twisted Levi, its point stabilizer is not the open subgroup of G(k) required for this depth-zero assertion.

**E2 — arXiv:1810.04198v2, Lemma 7.8 proof, p.31, opening sentence; no claim about the journal version.** Replace G_{x,0+} in this opening sentence, including its character restriction, by (G_{n+1})_{x,0+}. Lemma 7.5 gives exactly K_{0+}=(G_{n+1})_{x,0+}K^H_{0+}; the same terminal-group positive parahoric lies in K_+ by its definition on p.28. The proof only uses this smaller scalar-acting subgroup. G_{x,0+} is not generally contained in the deeper half-depth product.

**E3 — arXiv:1810.04198v2, Theorem 6.1 proof, p.24, first line of Case 1; no claim about the journal version.** Read X_j∈(g_j)*_{x_j,−r_j}. The function f on p.22 chooses a dual element at negative depth −r_j. The ensuing pairing is with the positive-depth quotient of h_j, and the same p.24 proof explicitly uses g_j*_{x_j+ελ,−r_j}. Both the missing minus sign and the unspecialized point are notational slips.

**E4 — arXiv:1810.04198v2, Corollary 5.2 proof, p.20, first sentence and final quotient calculation; no claim about the journal version.** The generic element has depth −r_j; the numerator index is r_j−ε, and the final Lie denominator is (h_j)_{x,r_j+}. Definition 4.1 and the invocation of Lemma 5.1 use depth −r_j. Corollary 5.2’s hypothesis on p.19 has numerator r_j−ε and denominator r_j+. The index i is not bound in the last sentence and r_ji is undefined; the correction agrees with the adjacent group quotient and the stated conclusion.

**E5 — arXiv:1810.04198v2, Lemma 7.7 proof, p.31, notation for the scalar extension and its polarized quotient; no claim about the journal version.** The occurrences of K^H_0 and K^H_0(E) in the polarization quotient refer to K^H_+ and K^H_+(E); the kernel denoted N(E) in the setup is N^H(E) in the subsequent formulas. Only K^H_{0+}, K^H_+ and N^H were defined. The symplectic quotient is K^H_{0+}/K^H_+ on p.30; quotienting the Heisenberg group by its center must give that same space over E. No subgroup K^H_0 was introduced.
