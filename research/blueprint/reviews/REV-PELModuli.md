The corrections made in the packet are:

1. Lan's official errata: multiplier uniqueness needs flatness and nonzero L; ψ=5J reduced to F5 makes id admit multipliers 1 and 2. Integral MH uses actual prime-to-box polarizations; rationalized Mrat uses its separate quasi-isogeny convention. Quasi-polarizations are symmetric and clearing factors are positive **locally constant** sections. Positive-dimensional hypotheses exclude zero-dimensional degree/sign counterexamples.
2. Lemma 1.2.5.10's descent of V0 to F0 is retracted. Use an auxiliary finite extension F0′ unramified at prescribed good primes; on nilpotent bases use finite-etale splitting and constant ranks, not equality at points. Type-D change-of-prime failure is also retracted by Lan: no counterexample is asserted. The self-dual lattice uniqueness clause excludes D; its further component condition is retained separately.
3. Rationalization into a faithful rational datum is restricted to faithful scalar extension. The rational carrier now records finite-dimensional semisimple B, positivity and real adjoint compatibility; it still needs the actual O tensor Q identification. The integral lattice now records finite generation. nPhi_sub requires N>=1 and the selected embedding in Phi. The hermitian trace dictionary is restricted to rational F/Q data. LTXZZ GU(0) as maps is distinguished from Lan's pair convention.
4. Versioned citations: Lan 2.2.3.10 is p245, 2.2.1.4 p229, 2.2.4.16 p260; the latter is corrected to **formally smooth** in thesis errata p15 and references 2.2.4.15. 6. Wedhorn's primary §§1.3–1.6 were obtained and read. At a chosen v above a good p, connected G has ordinary nonemptiness iff density iff E_v=Qp (equivalently its residue degree is one). Complete splitting gives this at every v, and is stronger than good reduction. The former global iff and BPS-hypothesis equivalence were corrected. 7. Global ker¹ classes cannot be distinguished by their local invariants, which are all trivial. Canonical-piece descent now requires global CM reciprocity/density. Rigidity concerns finite-order automorphisms and their individual eigenvalues, not identification of the whole automorphism group with roots of unity. Properness additionally requires descent of potential good reduction by killing finite inertia using neat level, now included in R11.3's request.
8. Removed C3/C4 proper coherent GAGA as a direct route for the nonproper comparison; added the actual C0 local faithful flatness interface. The definite unitary SV3 obstruction requires rank at least two; rank one is a torus. Lattice changes transport the level rather than creating a non-equivalent rational moduli functor. A nonneutral residual gerbe has a band with geometric fibre Aut, not a canonically descended k-group.
