## Objections requiring mathematical revision

1. **Multiplicative logarithm exactness (NE.6/phi-integral-log-sequence).** Kakde v3, Lemmas 86–88, pp.61–65, omit scalar norm exponents and use an incompatible formula for the last map. The corrected embedding is `(ζ,g) ↦ (ζ^[G:U_P] ver(g))_P`, and the additive inverse forces `ω_ψ=ω_A∘δ`. For p=3 and an unramified coefficient field of degree 2, a Teichmüller scalar of order 8 has rank 3 norm ζ³, which differs from ζ. For G=Γ and Z=Γ^p, the generator-truncated component kills the central class γ^p while ω_A retains it. The corrected maps alone do not prove exactness. The kernel argument must work at finite levels and pass compatibly to the limit; completed group units include infinite-order γ and cannot all be called torsion. The cokernel argument needs the untruncated trace in the cyclic case. These tasks also affect main-algebraic-theorems and k1-injects-localisation.
2. **Hilbert weights and cusp comparison (NE.6/basic-congruence-values).** Kakde Propositions 115–117, pp.83–86, use absolute indices where restriction to a normalizer fixed field has degree `[N:P]`. In a Heisenberg group of order 27 and a noncentral subgroup P of order 3, this degree is 3 whereas `[Ḡ:P]=9`. The reader now gives the relative weights, retained absolute Hecke indices and candidate cusp powers. Check those powers through the cusp/Hecke comparison and orbit bijections, including the proof of (29) omitted in the source. A corrected weight formula is insufficient to claim all value congruences proved.
3. **Global determinant orientation (NE.7).** BV Definition 2.5, p.6, and Definitions 3.7/3.14 require `[C,a]` with `a:1→d(C)`. Their Conjecture 4.1 and (21), p.26, instead give a global map to `d(RΓ_c)⁻¹`; Remark 5.6 uses it as `[RΓ_c,ζ]`, and (24) assigns t(C) an inverse-line source. Similarly (36)–(37), p.33, use positive Selmer determinants while the proof p.35 uses their inverses. These mismatches occur in the preprint and author final, and in the published text: Conjecture 4.1 p.188, Remark 5.6/proof pp.194–195, (38)–(39) pp.201–202 and proof p.204. Supply an explicit shifted/dual complex and typed map comparison, then recompute r_G, the boundary, the c_γ factor and normalized interpolation. Merely replacing ζ by its inverse changes the leading term and the normalization. The definition of the FK conjectural map is accurately recorded, but its intended use and the Tate, rational Tate and critical-motive formulas remain unverifiable in this packet’s convention.

The six unverifiable node verdicts are precisely phi-integral-log-sequence, basic-congruence-values, fukaya-kato-zeta-isomorphism, tate-motive-interpolation, tate-motive-interpolation-rational and critical-motive-interpolation.
