## Method

Nine readers worked in parallel:

- items 1–53 (Chapter I), with routes 2–5;
- items 54–112 (Chapter II);
- items 113–171 (Chapter III);
- items 172–223 and 224–275 (Chapter IV, two readers);
- items 276–319 (Chapter V);
- the recorded mistakes E1–E30 and E31–E59 (two readers);
- all eight routes and the prerequisites.

How the checks were done:

- **Page reading.** Every reader read their pages in full on the images for coverage.
- **Library citations.** Every cited declaration was opened in the pinned Mathlib and Tau Ceti trees.
- **Planned items.** Every cited stage description was read, together with the packet and decomposition nodes that decide a status.
- **Missing items.** They were searched for in the atlas (stages, packets, decompositions, proposed roadmaps and other papers' routes, accepted or not) and in the declaration index.
- **Computation.** Where a constant or a sign mattered, the reader recomputed it:
  - Theorems IV.5.5, IV.5.6 and IV.5.8 against the known Petersson norms of Δ (level 1, D = −7) and of (η(z)η(3z))⁶ (level 3, D = −7 and −11);
  - the σ_𝒜(−n) sign of E38 in Theorems IV.5.8 and IV.6.9 and on p. 315 (also against the exact value G((1+i√7)/2) on p. 318);
  - Proposition IV.4.6(b) with and without hypothesis (4.2), over 81,406 cases;
  - the Fourier coefficients of Proposition IV.3.2 by FFT, and V_s(t) and (3.3a)–(3.3e) by direct integration;
  - the theta transformation law (Lemma IV.2.3) in 144 cases;
  - the action of T_m on cusps, by brute force;
  - Kronecker's congruence at N = 4 and N = 9;
  - Lemma III.(8.2) at the elliptic points in residue characteristics 2 and 3;
  - the N = 11 and N = 65 tables of Chapter V;
  - the period identity for 11a1.
- **Merging.** The lead reviewer merged the readers' changes, resolved the few conflicts (all between compatible wordings) and made the routing decisions below.
- **Recorded mistakes.** Every recorded mistake has a verdict from the reader assigned to it. Other readers confirmed many of them independently.
- **New mistakes.** Each was checked by the reader who found it. The lead reviewer re-checked the cusp claim (E60) and Corollary V.(1.3) (E68) independently.
- **Checker.** `python3 scripts/check_paper.py` reports the corrected file `ok`.

## Outcome

The extraction is careful and complete in its reading, and its statements hold up. Almost every constant the review recomputed agrees with the corrected statements the extraction adopted.

Its weakness was routing. It sent nearly everything to the Gross–Zagier roadmap, including generic analytic objects that the atlas already owns and weight-2k > 2 statements that the weight-two roadmap does not cover.

The review changed 141 fields on 86 items and added 16 items (one by splitting Theorem IV.5.6). It rerouted 45 items. The corrected file has:

- 335 items: 149 planned, 24 library, 162 missing (against 319 items as submitted: 142, 22, 155);
- 11 routes, all accepted: seven corrected, one unchanged (route 6), three added;
- 76 recorded mistakes: the extraction's 59, all confirmed, and 17 new ones.

The substantive changes:

- **Statuses.**
  - Now planned: Kronecker's first limit formula (EllipticRegulators ER.7), Hankel's formula (QM.2), the cusps of Γ₀(D) (Tau Ceti ModularForms 10A), the class number formula (AN.4) and the coefficient identity of V §1.
  - Now library: the genus character (Tau Ceti `genusCharFunNarrowClassGroupHom`), L(E,s) (Mathlib `WeierstrassCurve.LFunction`) and the quadratic character ε.
  - Now missing: the Atkin–Lehner group on X₀(N), the fibre of X₀(N) at p | N, both copies of the theta series, and Sturm's lemma in weight 2k > 2.
- **Routes.** The classical analytic inputs go to their existing owners instead of GZ.6/GZ.7:
  - AutomorphicSpectralTheory for Legendre functions and non-holomorphic Eisenstein series;
  - AL.0 for K-Bessel integrals;
  - QM.3 for Poincaré series and E₂*;
  - MP.5–MP.6 for the theta series;
  - AN.4 for partial zeta functions and the genus factorization.

  The weight-2k > 2 material joins the proposed Part II HigherGreenFunctionCMValues. The class-number application becomes a Part II of AnalyticNumberTheory.
- **Mistakes.**
  - E5, E19, E21 and E37 were recorded as gaps. Each is a false statement with a counterexample, so they are now errors.
  - E19, E21 and E25 are already corrected in print, by Mann's appendix and by Conrad (Corollary 7.15; Theorem 9.2 and Remark 9.3).
  - E38 now also covers p. 315, where the γ^m formula has the same wrong sign.
  - Among the new mistakes:
    - Proposition III.(1.4) omits the ordinary points with extra automorphisms on the components 𝓕_{a,b}, a, b ≥ 1 (E62; Edixhoven 1990 has the right locus).
    - The choice of q before (0.5) does not give an order (E63, the analogue of E19).
    - "T_m maps each cusp to itself" is false (E60).
    - The fourth line of Corollary V.(1.3) is unproved when the sign is +1 (E68).
    - The Néron differential of the twist is ω/√D, not ω/√|D| (E67).
    - The k = 1 case of Conjecture (4.4) needs Chapter II's normalized Green's function (E69).

## Route decisions

- **Route 1 (source, GrossZagierAndArithmeticHeights, 168 items): accept.** The chapter-to-layer mapping (Chapters II and III to GZ.7, Chapter IV to GZ.6, Chapter V to GZ.8 and GZ.5) matches the stage descriptions of GrossZagierAndArithmeticHeights, whose README names 'Gross–Zagier, Chapters II–IV' as a primary source, and it is acyclic in the stage graph. As submitted the route had four defects, all corrected in place. (a) Weight: GZ.0–GZ.9 are weight two, but fifteen weight-2k > 2 statements of Chapters I, IV §5 and V §4 were routed to GZ.6/GZ.8; they moved to route 8, and Theorem IV.5.6 was split so that its k = 1 case (used in Chapter V §3) stays here. (b) Owners: the claim that 'no other layer plans' the classical analytic inputs is false. The accepted routes of PAPER-DUKE-IMAMOGLU-TOTH-16 put non-holomorphic Eisenstein series, their Fourier expansions and the resolvent kernel in AutomorphicSpectralTheory and the partial zeta functions and genus factorization in AnalyticNumberTheory AN.4; PAPER-ZHANG-21 puts the K-Bessel kernels in AL.0; QM.3's packet has E₂* and Poincaré series; ER.7's packet plans Kronecker's first limit formula. Those items moved to routes 3, 4, 9, 10 and 11 or became planned. (c) Order: the Legendre function Q_{s−1} was planned in GZ.7 (items 64, 65, 108) and in GZ.6 (item 258), although GZ.6 consumes it and sits upstream of GZ.7; it now has one owner (route 9). (d) The Hecke-algebra faithfulness and pairing of V §1 (items 276, 277) belong to ModularCurvesPartII (route 5). The route now has 168 items. Two points remain for the design job and the maintainer: GZ.5's definite identity (items 309, 310) uses Theorem IV.5.6 and Proposition IV.4.4 from GZ.6, which is downstream of GZ.5; and the stage graph lacks edges from CM.5, HE.2, AN.4, MP.5, ER.7, QM.3 and AS.2 into GZ.6/GZ.7 (the review checked by reachability that adding them creates no cycle). Eichler's connecting-ideal theorem (item 166) has no general owner in the atlas; it stays here, but a quaternion-order layer (GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic GN.2 is the nearest) would be its natural home.
- **Route 2 (source, ComplexMultiplicationAndExplicitReciprocity, 4 items): accept.** CM.5 owns the reduction of CM elliptic curves and the supersingular case, so Deuring's classification, the reduction of CM curves and the structure of End at a non-split prime (items 128, 129, 147) belong there; CM.5 must state Deuring's classification for all elliptic curves in characteristic p. The route also named CM.3 for Kronecker's congruence (item 122), but nothing in CM.3's description or its RS-04 narrowing covers modular polynomials, and the congruence describes the bad fibres of X₀(N); it moved to route 5. The dictionary between CM points, positive-definite forms of discriminant D and ideal classes, added in review, joins CM.1, which identifies isomorphism classes of CM curves with ideal classes.
- **Route 3 (source, MetaplecticAutomorphicForms, 6 items): accept.** The route sent the theta series θ_𝒜 and its modularity to GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic GN.3, but the GN packet and README say 'MetaplecticAutomorphicForms owns theta', and the accepted route of PAPER-LI-23 follows that. The route now names MetaplecticAutomorphicForms MP.5–MP.6 and carries both copies of the definition (items 19 and 177, now both missing), the modularity statement (178), its proof by the SL₂(ℤ) transformation law and Poisson summation over an ideal lattice (194, 195), and the conjugation symmetry r_{𝒞⁻¹} = r_𝒞 added in review. Poisson summation for lattices is imported from AL.0.
- **Route 4 (source, AnalyticNumberTheory, 7 items): accept.** The route named AnalyticNumberTheory AN.1, which the accepted restructuring RS-07 dropped (Mathlib supplies Dirichlet L-functions and their functional equation, with the root number unevaluated). AN.4 keeps the Dedekind/Hecke comparison, from which root number +1 for ε_D, Gauss's sign and L(1,ε) follow. The partial zeta function of an ideal class, its expression through E(τ_𝒜,s) and the genus-character factorization (items 95, 96, 172) join from route 1, following the accepted route 6 of PAPER-DUKE-IMAMOGLU-TOTH-16. The class number formula (item 229) is planned in AN.4 and stays as a planned item.
- **Route 5 (source, ModularCurvesPartII, 8 items): accept.** R13.5 ('Bad fibres and regular/semistable models') owns the regular model of X₀(N), but its text limits it to the cases its proof needs, so it must add Gross–Zagier's case: arbitrary N, Katz–Mazur 13.4.7 for Γ₀(pⁿM), and the corrected singular locus of Proposition III.(1.4) (E62, from Edixhoven 1990). The route now also carries the fibre at p | N (item 121), Kronecker's congruence (122), the Atkin–Lehner involutions and the group W (items 3 and 57, at R14.1), the modular polynomial and the function field of X₀(N) (added in review, at R12.6), and the faithfulness of the Hecke algebra and the perfect pairing of Chapter V §1 (items 276, 277, at R12.5 and R14.2, whose packet nodes state the Γ₁(N) versions).
- **Route 6 (source, RankZeroOneBSD, 2 items): accept.** Conjecture V.(2.3) and the N = 11 and N = 65 examples are statements about the Heegner index in BSD, which BSD.5 ('Rational leading-term quotient and Heegner index formula') owns. - **Route 7 (part-ii, EffectiveClassNumberProblem, 6 items): accept.** Nothing in the atlas plans Goldfeld's effective bound, and the brief's statements match pp. 231–232. But the extraction's claim that nothing plans class-number lower bounds is false: accepted routes put Siegel's lower bound in AnalyticNumberTheory AN.3/AN.4 (PAPER-DUKE-IMAMOGLU-TOTH-16 route 6, PAPER-BINYAMINI-22 route 5). - **Route 8 (part-ii, HigherGreenFunctionCMValues, 23 items): accept.** A Part II is justified: nothing accepted plans complex heights of Heegner cycles, higher Green's functions or weight-2k Gross–Zagier formulas, and the parent roadmap is weight two. But PAPER-LI-23 and PAPER-BRUINIER-EHLEN-YANG-21 already propose the Part II HigherGreenFunctionCMValues of the same parent, and both say to keep exactly one; their reviews rejected only details of the brief. The route is coalesced with it (same id, parent, title and area). It now also carries the weight-2k > 2 items of Chapter IV §5 and item 50. The brief is rewritten to state Sturm's lemma in weight 2k and Theorems IV.5.5, IV.5.6 (k ≥ 2) and IV.5.8 with the corrections E38, E42, E43 and E46. It records that Conjecture (4.4) is now proved at individual CM points in level one (Li 2023), with earlier cases by Mellit, Viazovska and Bruinier–Ehlen–Yang, and that the height interpretation is S.-W. Zhang's 1997 theorem.
- **Route 9 (source, AutomorphicSpectralTheory, 19 items): accept.** Added in review. The Legendre function Q_ν (items 64, 65, 108, 258), the point-pair invariants and E_N (66, 70), and the non-holomorphic Eisenstein series of level M with character with their Fourier expansions through V_s(t) (179, 185, 192, 207–209, 212–217, 265) belong with the classical Eisenstein series, resolvent kernel and Whittaker/Bessel functions that the accepted route 2 of PAPER-DUKE-IMAMOGLU-TOTH-16 puts in AutomorphicSpectralTheory AS.0–AS.2.
