The coefficient calculation retains three different indices: cochain degree p, divided-power degree n and model weight w. Integral cohomology is triangular in p≤n with binomial product factors, and the relative algebra kills dt without specializing t to 1. The normalized simplicial coefficient homology includes the constant (0,0) exception. Finite-coefficient filtered colimits precede inverse limits; completion is bidegreewise, then rationalization precedes t-stabilization. No global coefficient-tensor equivalence is inferred merely from the simplexwise construction.

For E10, independently checked the discrete infinite-set example over R=Q[x₁,x₂,…] with I=(x₁,x₂,…). A finite expression in I(∏R) uses finitely many scalar polynomials, all in an ideal generated by finitely many variables. A fresh x_j is outside that ideal, so the sequence (x_i) cannot be such an expression. It maps to zero in ∏(R/I), proving failure of injectivity of the asserted unrestricted quotient comparison. The packet correctly keeps only principal quotients and records their proof separately in G1. No published erratum is claimed.

For weights, finite-dimensional cohomology does not imply finite-dimensional graded pieces of a general connected minimal model. Generalized eigenspaces therefore use finite invariant blocks; the cohomology-degree product bound has the required inequality direction. The common-filtration statement concerns the original automorphisms. Rational descent is reserved for complex varieties; the general algebraically closed statement is over Q_l. Identity on Gr^W cohomology is the conjugacy conclusion, with no strengthening to a homotopically trivial conjugator.

The formality proof uses model weight≥cochain degree and pure diagonal cohomology. Diagonal elements are closed, and projection to their classes respects d and multiplication; the minimal differential need not vanish. The model of P¹, with dy=x², and higher-genus curve models detect this distinction. Normal-variety Lie generators have weights −1,−2 and relations −2,−3,−4. The nodal rational curve test excludes an invalid nonnormal extension. Finite nilpotence depth is distinguished from a weight cutoff. Morgan's corrected rigidity statement is respected throughout.
