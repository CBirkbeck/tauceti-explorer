## Corrections made in place

1. **Character tensor on disconnected bases.** The multiplicative finite character comparison must tensor the locally constant character sheaf with the structure sheaf, or use the chosen constant fibre `(ℤ/pⁿ)^r`. Tensoring all global points over R′ gives the wrong module when R′ is disconnected: over 𝔽_p×𝔽_p, global sections of a rank-one constant sheaf have two generators, and their tensor has rank two on each component. Corrected the statement, API, counterexample and actual Lean tensor carrier. The calculation remains the étale-local `dt_i/t_i` basis of Pilloni §9.4 (PDF p.56). Added direct conormal-base-change dependencies to the finite and multiplicative maps.

2. **Normalized determinant pullback.** Qualified RT /26 puts the general Pilloni Lemma 6.3.4.1 (PDF p.34) in R07.2. T0 keeps its C4 boundary application. The character map need not be p times an automorphism: only its determinant factors as p^r times a unit. Normalize that determinant over ℤ_p before base change. Removed the extra Lean helper with the false stronger hypothesis and retained the determinant-line signature. The trace argument also distinguishes the different from its discriminant norm.

3. **Canonical induction.** Added `T3/pointwise-quotient-hasse`, marked `addedBy` this review. Given a Frobenius-congruent height-d subgroup in a BT₂, the congruence modulo p^{1−w} yields min(Ha(E),1−w)=min(pw,1−w). This is the calculation in Fargues Theorem 5's proof (§7.4, PDF pp.37–38), independent of a higher canonical subgroup. The HN existence induction starts with Theorem 4 and applies induction to the actual level-(n−1) quotient p^{−(n−1)}D/D; it does not call the later family quotient-radius theorem. Updated direct edges, the proof and E18's corrected quotient. Ordinary Frobenius-isomorphism tests refer to the special fibre; the numerical 1/(2p) example is restricted to p≥5.
