2. **Heegner trace and roots of unity in the correct field.** GZ83 Corollary 6 and its proof, printed p. 87, place the CM value in the Hilbert class field H, with u(x)^12=λ^12, where λ=(3+√−139)/2 has norm 37. Knowing the units of K is insufficient while the quotient lies in H. Since [H:K]=3, neither K(i) nor K(ζ₃), each of degree two over K, can be a subfield of H. Every twelfth root of unity in H is consequently ±1. Thus u(x)∈{λ,−λ}⊂K; Galois equivariance gives a common value for the three conjugates. The source divisor and its multiplicities then imply the principal trace divisor. The CN.3 request now specifies the CM relation, field, equivariance and divisor data. This corrects the existing target's proof without adding small lemma nodes.
