Buzzard's switch uses Γ1 false-point curves, not that Γ0 specialization. - **Local weight and multiplicity boundaries.** Edixhoven Definition 4.4 includes any extension of one unramified character by itself, including a ramified extension; it is not limited to unramified scalar Frobenius. The odd-p update and the dyadic minimum-Katz-weight scope remain separate. Unramifiedness and a dyadic Katz weight-one witness are inputs; they are not derived from an odd-p theorem.
