## Mathematical corrections

The corrections are recorded individually in the table below. The main changes affect what a correct implementation must do:

- Completion is the valuation-ring span in the completed vector space. The old suggested file used the fraction-field span and therefore identified ℤ₂ and 2ℤ₂. The index formulas now require full lattices and projectivity/Dedekind hypotheses where used. The global simultaneous-pseudobasis existence proof is distinguished from local invariant-ideal reconstruction.
- Global genus-character evaluation requires a residual discriminant congruent to 0 or 1 modulo 4; distinct prime-discriminant factors have at most one even factor. Local dyadic support additionally requires D/Δ in 4ℤ₂ or 1+4ℤ₂. At unramified primes the local function is the integral-lattice indicator, rather than a blanket constant. IntegralLattices owns binary forms and their local normal forms; GN.2 retains character compatibility. Existing Tau Ceti genus-field and degree theorems supply the exponent-two consequence.
- Signed Witt parity agrees with total dimension parity. Quadratic transfer image/kernel assertions apply to sign +1; the symplectic zero-Witt-group case is separate. Auxiliary determinant transfer uses the +1 line, and the proof works for alternating forms without diagonalizing them. The β-line requires β≠0.
- Hermitian trace-form classification has an actual induction via represented norms and cancellation. The dyadic-safe unramified diagonalization argument uses surjectivity of the integral trace, not an assertion that every unit has unit trace. The matrix domain includes invertible field-valued hermitian matrices and an integral-entry subset, not only unit-determinant integral matrices. Norm ideals over the two integer rings are explicitly compared; projective lattices are compared inside a fixed labelled ambient space.
- Stable-lattice flags use a subgroup acting trivially on the associated graded pieces, not the unipotent radical of a reductive centralizer. Monogenic trace-dual identities state separability and integral-closure hypotheses. Quaternion identities require a genuine order, nonzero quaternion parameters and characteristic different from two; the Hasse–Schilling proof separates split and division cases.
- Quadric surface types require geometric irreducibility. Integral spinor statements record their rank and fraction-characteristic hypotheses and import the existing ℚ_p cases. Cayley and Zassenhaus proofs identify their determinant and unipotent inputs exactly.
- The Frobenius discriminant bound requires a similitude and an integer characteristic polynomial. Compatible-family prime selection uses a finite list of rational square classes, with an explicit norm representation argument; the unsupported arbitrary splitting-field requirement was removed. The auxiliary Λ₂d has rank three, not the rank of primitive K3 cohomology.
- Trace self-duality is distinguished from O_F-unimodularity, and adelic discriminant maps use completed quotients. Twisted symmetric Witt signatures have a nonzero image dℤ, not an asserted surjection onto ℤ. The ternary classification has a complete local, Hermite-bound, Hasse–Minkowski and primitive hyperbolic-splitting argument, including the dyadic smooth-zero step.

All seven source issues are independently confirmed, with hypotheses or counterexamples recorded in the packet and reader:

- `GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic/E201` (Voight2026): Both hold for R-orders (more generally for lattices containing R·1 as a saturated direct summand, so that the index can be computed in B/F); for general lattices discrd(I)² = disc(I) fails. The packet states them for orders. For I = 2M₂(ℤ) ⊆ J = M₂(ℤ): m is trilinear, so discrd(I) = 2³discrd(J) = 8ℤ, while [J : I] = 2⁴ and disc(I) = 2⁸disc(J) = 256ℤ ≠ 64ℤ = discrd(I)². The proof of 15.4.7 uses m(α_1, α_2, α_3)² = −d(1, α_1, α_2, α_3), and the values d(1, α_1, α_2, α_3) generate disc(I) only when 1 ∈ I behaves as for an order. new
- `GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic/E202` (BEY2021): At p=2 dividing Δ additionally require D/Δ∈4ℤ₂∪(1+4ℤ₂), and set the character to zero off this support. At p∤Δ use the published characteristic function of L′_p. For Δ=−4, p=2 and Q=[1,0,2], D=−8 satisfies the printed support conditions, but Q represents 1 and 3, with Hilbert symbols (1,−4)₂=1 and (3,−4)₂=−1. The quotient D/Δ=2 fails the missing condition. GKZ I.2 Proposition 1 explicitly includes the quotient-discriminant restriction. new; no correction found in the bounded search recorded below
- `GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic/E203` (BFP2024): Require geometric irreducibility. A nonsplit binary norm equation gives another irreducible-over-𝔽_q surface of geometric rank two. For odd q and nonsquare d, x²−dy² is irreducible in 𝔽_q[x,y,z,w], so it defines a reduced irreducible surface in ℙ³ over 𝔽_q. Over 𝔽_{q²} it is the union of two conjugate planes and is none of the three listed geometric types. The correction preserves the rank-three/four application. new; no correction found in the bounded search recorded below
- `GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic/E204` (Charles2016): Assume the initial embedding is primitive, as already recorded in PAPER-CHARLES-16/E2. The sublattice diag(8,8) generated by 2h and 2(e+f) in Λ₂ has no primitive embedding into Λ₂. Modulo 2 a primitive embedding would give a two-dimensional zero subspace for q=x²+yz on 𝔽₂³. Its three nonzero zeros are (0,0,1),(0,1,0),(1,1,1), and the sum of any two is not a zero. Taking m=1 disproves the unrestricted target. Already recorded and independently confirmed as PAPER-CHARLES-16/E2; this entry links the GN.2 dependency to that existing finding.
- `GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic/E205` (Voight2026): Require M projective (in particular R Dedekind), or retain [M:rM]_R=r^n[M:M]_R rather than identifying the self-index with R. Take R=k[t²,t³], F=k(t), M=k[t] and r=1. M is generated over R by 1,t and spans F. Rank-one endomorphisms preserving M are multiplication by exactly k[t], so [M:M]_R=k[t]≠R. new; no correction found in the bounded search recorded below
- `GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic/E206` (BEY2021): The count and indexing bound are 2^{s−1}, equal to the genus quotient order and, under exponent two, the class number. For D=−84, s=3 but the four genus characters correspond to the four sign-selected splittings. The proof’s own class-number formula gives 2^{s−1}; the count s is inconsistent. Already recorded and confirmed as PAPER-BRUINIER-EHLEN-YANG-21/E4.
- `GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic/E207` (LMS2018): Add (φx,φy)=q^w(x,y). Also interpret integral characteristic polynomial as one in ℤ[T] for the uniform finite-polynomial argument. For any odd ℓ, let M=ℤ_ℓ² with pairing diag(1,−1), v=(1,1+ℓ), w=(0,1), and let φ act as +1 on v and −1 on w. This is an integral endomorphism since v,w form a basis; its characteristic polynomial X²−1 has Weil weight zero and semisimple eigenvalue 1. The ambient discriminant is a unit while the fixed-line discriminant is −ℓ(2+ℓ), of valuation 1. This violates the large-ℓ bound for arbitrarily large ℓ. It is not an isometry, exactly the missing condition. new; no correction found in the bounded search recorded below

## Closure and remaining work

The checker confirms the 58-node graph, prerequisite references, API/test requirements and six planets. No target marked closed hides a gap. Target-level proof steps were repaired in place without creating proof-fragment nodes. The four remaining inputs are precise:

- **Dyadic ramified unimodular hermitian lattices.** For a ramified quadratic extension of dyadic local fields, unimodular hermitian lattices with equal determinant and norm ideal are isometric (Jacobowitz, Hermitian forms over local fields, Amer. J. Math. 84 (1962), Proposition 10.4, cited by Lipnowski–Tsimerman Proposition 5.6). Jacobowitz is not freely available and was not read; the unramified and odd ramified cases are proved in the node.
- **Integral spinor images of non-split dyadic lattices.** The node proves θ(SO(L)) ⊆ R^×K^{×2} for every regular unimodular lattice and equality when L is nondyadic or contains a hyperbolic plane. The full computation of θ(SO(L_v)) for arbitrary dyadic lattices (Beli, Integral spinor norm groups over dyadic local fields, J. Number Theory 2003, and Earnest–Hsia) was not acquired; no target of the atlas uses it beyond these cases, but the proper spinor genus count of a general lattice over a number field with dyadic places of non-split type needs it.
- **Global simultaneous pseudobasis over a Dedekind domain.** Voight Theorem 9.3.9, printed p.139, states the common-pseudobasis theorem but omits its proof. Supply the global elementary-divisor argument for two projective full lattices, including the passage from local Smith exponents to vectors and coefficient ideals. Local Smith normal form and unique ideal factorization prove existence/uniqueness of invariant ideals, but do not alone construct a global simultaneous pseudobasis. Steinitz classification and the index/discriminant nodes do not use this missing step.
- **Line-valued symmetric Witt localization away from 2.** Supply the isomorphism W^s(R;M)[1/2]→W^s(K)[1/2] after trivializing M over K, for a Dedekind domain with global fraction field and symmetric +1 line coefficient M. Calmès et al. Remark 3.1.9, p.52, explicitly states this input and uses it to prove the signature composite nonzero. The proof requires the line-valued localization/residue theory; it does not follow from quadratic-field Witt cancellation or from choosing a generator at a single real embedding. The untwisted splitting is closed independently by ⟨1⟩.

These inputs remain implementation/planning work with explicit affected nodes and coverage; they are not represented as established by an unread source. The two inherited gaps are retained, and the two newly identified proof gaps are isolated from the closed arguments that do not need them. Their honest recording permits acceptance under PROTOCOL §0 and the issue’s acceptance instructions.
