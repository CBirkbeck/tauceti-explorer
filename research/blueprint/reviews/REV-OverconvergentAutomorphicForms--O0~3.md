## What round 3 establishes

**Finite coefficients.** FiniteCoefficientCore uses actual Representation,
linear equivalences, tensor products, contragredient dual and algebraic scalar
extension. Its continuous finite-projective structure uses the canonical module
topology. StableIntegralSubmodule supplies stability and spanning as separate
data; it does not supply openness or boundedness of a Banach lattice. The eight
examples test algebraic/continuous prerequisites. The finite analytic coefficient
node still needs analytic orbit maps on genuine Lie charts, the Banach topology
and completed scalar-extension compatibility; BP §6.2 pp147–153 and the L0/R0
supplier contracts retain those obligations. No auxiliary is counted as that node.

**Valuation translation.** The new aip_translation_valuation_units signature uses
an arbitrary linearly ordered commutative value group with zero and assumes both
multiplier bounds. It types the desired pointwise equivalence without an embedding
of the value group into the reals. Finite-order valuation one, including p-primary
order, and a dependent family of value groups are also typed. Six examples include
the lexicographic two-rank group, sign and the failure of a one-sided bound.
The full node additionally obtains both bounds from admission on the actual AIP
frame torsor. AIP ADIC Lemma4.4 p16 and §6.4 p29 support its analytic/finite-factor
argument; that geometric admission and its sheaf application are still omitted.

**Affine completion.** OrdinaryAffineCompletion forms actual rings
R_i/(p^m), their directed colimits at each precision, then the subring of
precision-compatible families. Projection, compatible affine restriction,
localization at p after completion and the constant-tower AdicCompletion
comparison are typed, with four examples. They preserve the ordered-limit
contract. They supply no formal Igusa patch rings, topology, deck action,
gluing or analytic O+ comparison. All required geometric O7 names remain absent.

These helpers introduce no Prop-valued substitutes or artificial geometry. They
are honest partial work. Completing §13 requires the actual supplier carriers
and the requested signatures on them, with genuinely unstateable conditions
omitted explicitly under the protocol’s rule.
