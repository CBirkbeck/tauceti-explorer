## How the review was done

Every node was read against its source at the pinned version: six readers, each given one group of
nodes and the source files, worked in parallel under the reviewer's direction and reported findings
(367 in all) and then machine-applicable edits; a seventh pass read the supplier node or stage of
every cross-roadmap prerequisite (72 pairs in the original packet) in the other packets. The reviewer
checked by hand the 91 baseline declarations and the 63 added ones in the pinned trees, the 18 source
issues and all 48 new ones at their locators, the findings of high severity, the statements of the
added nodes, and every rewritten statement of CR.1–CR.4. Two further passes followed: one against
Illusie's 1979 paper, which the packet recorded as not acquired and which is on Numdam; one reading
Langer–Zink §§2.3–2.4 and BLM §§5.4–5.5, which the packet recorded as unread. All edits are applied
by one re-runnable script to the packet as it is on `main`; excerpts are verified as literal
substrings of the source text on every run, and Stacks locators against the tags file of the pinned
commit.

  (`DividedPowers.le_equalizer_of_isDPMorphism`); it now cites it.

  Mathlib's `WittVector.fontaineTheta`; the explicit description of A_cris is conditional on the

- Missing and added: the canonical divided powers on (p) (the stage asks for them, with p = 2),
  compatibility of divided powers with a base (Stacks Definition 60.4.1), Lemmas 60.2.5, 60.3.1,
  60.3.2 and pushouts of PD rings.

**CR.1.**
- `taylor-equivalence`: the Taylor formula had the wrong sign (ξ_i = p₀(x_i) − p₁(x_i) with the
  conventions of the neighbouring nodes; Stacks, proof of Lemma 60.17.3).

- `pd-differentials` and `envelope-differentials` moved from CR.2 to CR.1: the CR.1 nodes on
  connections use them and CR.2 requires CR.1. New node `integrable-connection`.

**CR.2.**
- `crystalline-cohomology`: Ru_* was typed as D(O_crys) → D(O_X). There is no such functor; the
  target is D(X_Zar, f⁻¹O_S).

  Remarks 60.24.11, 60.24.14. The direct-sum decomposition of End(E) is stated with its exact

**CR.3 and substages.**
- `torsion-and-models`: "smooth proper curves with H⁰ = W" needs geometric connectedness.

## The second reading

Three readers who had not seen the review's findings read the corrected packet, one per group of
stages, all 164 nodes in full. They reported 72 points: 2 of high severity, 22 medium, 48 low. The
reviewer checked them; 31 of the proposed replacements, 18 prerequisites that proofs named without

numerical example, test or acceptance item was found apart from the two below, no dangling id and no
cycle.

Applied:
- `CR.0/regular-envelope` (high): parts (1)(b), (1)(c) and the Tor statement of (1)(d) identify the
  principal envelope of f_t with A⟨x_t⟩/(x_t − f_t), which needs f_t to be a non-zero-divisor on A; a
  later member of a regular sequence can be a zero-divisor (A = F_p[x,y,w]/(w(1−x)), f = (x, (1−x)y)).
  The hypothesis is added; Bhatt's proof assumes it silently. The Lean declaration states the part
  that does not need it.
- `CR.0/pd-square-zero-thickening` (high): an acceptance example written by the review called the
  ring ℤ[ε]/(ε³); it is ℤ ⊕ ℤε ⊕ ℤη with ε² = 2η. Corrected.
- `CR.1/structure-sheaves`, `CR.1/crystal`: "c_f is an isomorphism for open immersions" holds on the
  big site only when U = U′ ×_T′ T. Stated so (the Lean form already had the condition).
- `CR.4/finite-witt-cohomology` now states BLM Corollary 2.7.4, which a proof step of
  `classical-regular-comparison` used and no node stated.
- `CR.3:duality/trace`: the compatibility Tr∘F = p^d·σ∘Tr was an API item without a proof step; the
  step (Ekedahl I, Lemma 3.4, read on the page) is added.
- `CR.4/logarithmic-witt-sheaf`: an API item used a map constructed in a later node; restated.
  A test of `relative-witt-complex` about the initial object moved to `relative-de-rham-witt`.
- Two gaps added: limits and the representability criterion for PD rings (behind Stacks Lemma

  `degree-scaled-frobenius` uses and `CR.3/frobenius-map` does not supply.
