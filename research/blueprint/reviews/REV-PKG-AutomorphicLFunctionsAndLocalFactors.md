## Corrections made in this review

- **Tate convergence.** For an unramified quasicharacter with value t on a uniformizer, the initial geometric-series region is `‖tq^(−s)‖<1`, equivalently `Re(s)>log‖t‖/log q`. The old `Re(s)>0` statement required unitarity. The general region and the unitary specialization now appear in both the README and the omission manifest.
- **Multiplicity and purity ownership.** Removed the upward AS.4 smooth/Hilbert comparison and specified that AL.3 proves the comparison using AF.1 globalization, AF.2 restricted tensors and AF.3's cusp Hilbert subspace. Replaced the remaining “GS purity” hypothesis by AL.3's own unramified pure realization and cohomological determinant.
- **Native Jensen reuse.** The order-one zero-count argument now cites Mathlib `AnalyticOnNhd.circleAverage_log_norm` and `AnalyticOnNhd.sum_divisor_le`. The latter requires a nonzero value at the centre and a bound on a larger sphere; use a nonzero centre and positive radii. The missing canonical-product/Hadamard argument remains an AL.2 target. Mathlib's Hadamard three-lines theorem is a different result.
- **Curve geometry.** Specified that the no-level compactification is smooth and proper over the full product X × X, with relative normal-crossing boundary. Distinguished commuting partial Frobenius maps from fixed-point comparisons on distinct Frobenius orbits; an intersection of infinitely many diagonal complements is not silently called an open subscheme. Kept finite stabilizers without adding a Deligne–Mumford assertion to the compactification. Clarified that matching tensor local factors does not assert irreducibility or cuspidal tensor transfer.
- **Source and errata references.** Corrected the Fields lectures locator for poles to Lecture 9 §3, printed p.71, and the functional equation to §4, pp.71–72; Theorems 9.1–9.2 are in §6, p.74. Removed the internal real-residue correction identifier without changing the correct derivative orders. Explained why the function-field index uses inertial equivalence and why its pole clearer must remove periodic poles.
- **Critical values.** Fixed the complex dictionary's full nonzero normalization `N=(2πi)^(j+1)Ω^sign/(G(χ)j!)`, with analytic twist f⊗χ⁻¹ and the specified sign. The field-membership conclusion is an actual algebraicity input `F/N∈E`. The Lean comparison now takes `E : Subfield ℂ`, this hypothesis, `C=γF`, and nonvanishing of γ and N, and returns both the scalar equality and membership of `C/(γN)` in E. This supplies no independent algebraicity theorem and claims no general-number-field conclusion.
