## What was wrong, and what was changed

1. **Schmid's argument was planned in the wrong order.** The limiting mixed Hodge structure (Schmid (6.16)) is proved from the SL₂-orbit theorem (Schmid (5.13)). The plan had no node for (5.13): it was folded into `one-variable-sl2`, which listed `limiting-mhs` as a prerequisite, while `limiting-mhs` had no SL₂ input. Cattani–Kaplan's cone theorem in turn uses the one-variable limiting structure ((3.2) of their paper), so it has to come before the multivariable one. Added `sl2-orbit-theorem` (statement read on pp.239–240, with the rational form of (5.17), (5.19)) and `limiting-mhs-one-variable`; the chain is now nilpotent orbit → SL₂-orbit theorem → one-variable limiting structure → cone weights → limiting structure for the cone. `one-variable-sl2` keeps Lemma (5.25) and Theorem (5.26) and no longer depends on `limiting-mhs`.
2. **Existing supplier nodes were requested instead of cited.** Seventeen prerequisites named the bare stage H.3 or LPV.1 (against two citations of a node of those packets), and the gaps said those layers had no finer nodes. They have: H.3 plans the exponential chart at a flag for a specified complement (its statement names this use by H.6), its local biholomorphy, the filtration of the Lie algebra, horizontal tangents, the period manifold, marked holomorphic horizontal period maps and their monodromy equivariance; LPV.1 plans the finite logarithm, the monodromy filtration centered at an integer, uniqueness of relative filtrations, the tensor formula, primitive decomposition and maximal unipotence; ShimuraData D3 plans polarized integral variations. These are now cited by node: 36 node citations of H.3, LPV.1 and ShimuraData D3, with the bare stage kept in 11 places where a request is still open. `quasi-unipotence` cited curve-test admissibility of mixed variations, which Borel's lemma does not use; it now cites the variation, the period map and Kronecker's theorem. The H.3 and LPV.1 requests and gaps G2 and G3 list only what is really missing: the invariant distance and the distance-decreasing property of horizontal maps, the canonical Cartan involution, naturality of the finite logarithm, distributive families of filtrations with their splitting, and exterior powers. Two stage prerequisites had no request naming their node, and several requests named nodes without the stage prerequisite; both are consistent now.
3. **The relative-filtration clause of the cone theorem was not a statement.** "For disjoint cone faces the successive filtration is the relative monodromy filtration of the combined face" is replaced by what Cattani–Kaplan prove: for subsets J⊂J′ and N positive on J′∖J and nonnegative on J, W(J′) is the weight filtration of N relative to W(J). The proof outline now follows the source. Their part (ii) as printed lacks the hypothesis that one face lies in the closure of the other; see E-H6-5 below.
4. **Three hypotheses or conventions were garbled.** The rank hypothesis of the three norm estimates read "constant dim(F^p∩⋂_{j∈J}W(J)_ℓ)"; it is Kashiwara's (2.4.3.3), dim(F^p∩W(J)_ℓ) constant for every subset J. The label formula attributed to Bakker–Klingler–Tsimerman (σ_j=p+q_j−k) is off by the weight for the convention they print (filtration centered at zero, pieces with p+q_j≤s, p.931); both readings are now stated, in the node, the reader and the source issue. The nilpotent-orbit definition required rational generators; the sources define it over ℝ, and rationality is now a named extra property.
5. **The semistable base-change node had no proof.** `log-cohomology-basechange` applied "the requested theorem", and the C5 request asked C5 for the same statement, so nobody owned Steenbrink's theorem. The node now has the argument presented by Illusie (§2.2.4): the special log complex computes nearby cycles, proper base change makes the fibre dimension constant, and Grauert gives local freeness. The C0, C3 and C5 requests ask for the general tools. `special-residue` now says where nilpotence comes from (eigenvalues in [0,1) by Illusie 2.2.2(b), unipotence by §2.1.3).
6. **Gap G5 misdescribed the fixed-compact problem.** The plan said Schmid's Corollary (5.29) is for "its compatible SL2 reference compact" and that the erratum forbids changing the compact. Schmid's K is the unique maximal compact containing the isotropy group of his base point (his p.314), so it is the canonical one, and a change of base point only translates Siegel sets on the right (erratum, Remark 1.1(1)). What is open is different: Schmid builds Siegel sets on a maximal ℚ-split torus containing the image of the diagonal torus, which need not be stable under the Cartan involution of K, while AA.3 and H.7 use the stable split component. For a torus that is not stable a set of Schmid's shape is not contained in finitely many Siegel sets; `one-variable-siegel` has a worked SL(2) example. The node now separates Schmid's statement (one Siegel set, in his sense) from the consumer form, and G5 and the AA.3 and H.3 requests say what has to be proved.
7. **A missing input: the bigrading of the isometry Lie algebra.** `negative-lie-chart` used "the adjoint native Deligne splitting". The pin has tensor products, internal Hom and Tate twists for pure structures only. For all endomorphisms the bigrading follows from the pinned direct-sum theorem; for isometries it needs the polarization to pair the Deligne pieces compatibly. This is the new gap G6, requested from H.2. `logarithms-type` is restated through the bigrading of V, with the pinned functoriality theorem as a baseline citation and the re-indexing of the target recorded as part of the node.
8. **The suggested file stated false theorems.** Twenty statements quantified over arbitrary data with their hypotheses dropped and were false as written, and one more (`oneVariableSL2`) was vacuous: `hodgeWeightDistributive` asserted distributivity for any three subspaces (the packet's own acceptance item gives three lines in a plane as a counterexample), `quasiUnipotentMonodromy` for any matrix, `logCohomologyBaseChange` bijectivity of any linear map, `untwistedExtension` extension of any function analytic on a punctured polydisc, and so on. A statement proved by a placeholder must still be true. The file is rewritten: linear algebra and frame calculus with real hypotheses; the five definitions and constructions with every API item and test; and the remaining theorems as relations between stand-in types and functions for objects other layers own, in the form of the reviewed precedent in `FaltingsFinitenessAndIsogenyTheorems.lean`. Each docstring says what is left out.
9. **Unit tests and API.** The three tests of `negativeLieCorrection` exercised only the matrix exponential; two tests of the chart itself were added (the lift of a line in the elliptic case, and a stabilizer direction that cannot be a chart direction). `NilpotentOrbit` gained the constructors `ray` and `face`, which four nodes use.
10. **Planets.** Six per layer are allowed. The limiting mixed Hodge structure and the SL₂-orbit theorem are named landmarks of this layer and had no planet; they replace "Monodromy cone weight filtrations" and "One-variable Siegel containment". The six are: Logarithmic Gauss–Manin connection, Nilpotent orbit, Nilpotent orbit theorem, SL₂-orbit theorem, Limiting mixed Hodge structure, Hodge norm estimates.

- **E-H6-5** (Cattani–Kaplan, Theorem (3.3)(ii), p.113; misprint, affects nothing; new). As printed, for faces τ_1, τ_2 and N in τ_1 outside the closure of τ_2, W(τ_1) is the weight filtration of N relative to W(τ_2). For V=V_1⊗V_2 with the two standard nilpotents, τ_1 and τ_2 the two rays, this fails: the relative filtration is W(N_1+N_2), with graded dimensions 1, 2, 1, not W(N_1), with 2, 2. The proof treats N in the open cone and τ_2 a face of it; the statement needs τ_2 in the closure of τ_1. A web search found no erratum; the journal's listing and MathSciNet were not consulted, which the entry says.
