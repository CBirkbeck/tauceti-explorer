The contour repair uses `F(w)=exp(2πbw)/(cosh(πw)(w−z))` on the integer-sided rectangle with coordinates `±N±iN`, for `N>|z|+1`. An open rectangle enlarged by one quarter contains its finite poles and is convex. The curve avoids the poles and has winding number one around each interior pole. Horizontal integrals are bounded by `Cb/(N−|z|)`, where `Cb=∫ℝ exp(2πbx)/cosh(πx) dx` is finite for `|b|<1/2`; vertical integrals are bounded by `2N exp(2π|b|N)/((N−|z|)sinh(πN))`. All four sides tend to zero. Evaluating the two residue types therefore supplies the symmetric conditional sum. This is a target-level proof sketch, without new proof-step nodes.
