1. Added `TensorProduct.map` as a direct prerequisite of the lecture-kernel
   functoriality. Added the tensor carrier and inherited antisymmetric quotient
   directly to the curve-definition dependencies.
2. Clarified the rational-cycle argument. Clearing coefficients first gives
   an integral symbol sum whose boundary may be torsion. Multiply once more
   to kill that boundary. Flatness identifies the resulting rationalized
   integral kernel with the rational kernel; it does not assert that the
   first denominator-cleared sum already has zero integral boundary.
3. Required quasi-compactness and separatedness in the suggested smooth-curve
   bundle. Smoothness supplies local finite presentation; together these
   conditions express finite-type separated algebraic curves. Added the two
   read baseline predicates and made this scope explicit in the packet.
4. Corrected the inherited angle prototype. The assignment
   `[u]+[u⁻¹]` is available over every field, but its homomorphism signature
   requires at least four elements. The subgroup generated by its values
   equals that homomorphism's range under the hypothesis. The published
   comparison's angle API uses the explicit assignment and retains its
   Bloch-membership hypothesis.
5. Strengthened two existing tests without changing their mathematical
   claims: the lecture F₃ signature now exhibits the full tensor as ℤ/2
   and its generator image; the negative-target F₅ signature exhibits the
   full tensor as ℤ/4 and the negative relation as three times its generator.
6. Repaired the malformed reduced-generator excerpt. Recorded separate
   independent source-fetch hashes without replacing the original provenance.
7. Removed the unused F(t)-inductive comparison from the all-curve node's
   prerequisites. Its infinite-field, F(t)-only scope remains a related
   comparison. It supplies no step of the all-curve quotient-map proof and
   does not establish that its rational kernel vanishes.
8. Gave every source issue an independent verdict. Separated the false
   integral inversion-cycle assertion as E104 from E102's coefficient typo.
   The original packet noticed both, but their classification and effect
   should remain distinct.
9. Clarified the dictionary request: Layer 12A currently discusses proper
   regular curves, while this construction quantifies over smooth algebraic
   curves, including open curves. The supplier must justify the local contract
   there, directly or through a model and restriction.

The lecture sixfold calculation is a derivation from the inherited algebraic
relations, not a theorem attributed to an unread source. If σ is complement,
then λσ=τλ. For a lift α of β, λ(α−σα)=0 and
q(α−σα)=2β−deg(α)c. Also 2[−1] is a full-tensor cycle
with image 3c. These identities give the specified lift of 6β. The relation
kernel is removed before coefficient change: 4[−1] over ℚ remains a nonzero
infinite-order element in the raw map's kernel.

For the generic configuration comparison, the five normalized faces of
(0,∞,1,x,y), in order of omission, are
(1−x)/(1−y), (1−x⁻¹)/(1−y⁻¹), y/x, y, x.
Their alternating sum is exactly the inherited five-term relation. The
foundation needed here is the existing V.4 cross-ratio node, not the whole
homological K₃ comparison.
