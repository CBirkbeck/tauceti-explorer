## Resolution of the preceding review's gates

1. **Enhancement and coherence.** Proper refinement arrows now retain the cartesian-open condition, making the adjunction-unit comparison a proper-base-change equivalence. Common diagonal closures give a cofiltered index; equalizing refinements use the closure of the distinguished open in the equalizer. EDC constructs this geometric diagram using E3's ringed-topos diagrams, units and mates. It imports neither a finished stack six-operation theorem nor an unjustified termwise K-injective model. The enough-points restriction holds for the small étale topoi by the checked geometric-point family.
2. **Smooth purity and its trace.** The pro-object is the untwisted `R(f_U)_!Λ_U`, augmented to `Λ(−d)[−2d]`. Effacement, 2.14.4 and the bounded pro-isomorphism criterion 3.1.17(iii) identify this specified augmentation. Contravariant Hom produces the correctly shifted stalk map. Its unit/counit construction identifies it with the adjoint of the same trace; uniform amplitude then gives the unbounded extension.
3. **The dimension-one base.** The base is the étale constant object `Λ_S`, with local `i!Λ_S=Λ(−1)[−2]`. Strict-trait Kummer/tame-inertia computations are exact lower finite-coefficient supplier requests, not coherent O-module duality or an upward LocalGaloisGroups import. Underived `j_*` and point-supported dévissage give the self-injective constructible case. [Th. finitude] 4.1 supplies the arbitrary-noetherian finite-Tor variant, now stated and typed separately.
4. **Noncircular constructible evaluation.** The formal proper exchange transports evaluation to evaluation on the base. Dimension induction leaves a finite-support cone, and its proper image detects those finitely many stalks. This is the argument in [Th. finitude] 4.3–4.7 and Angéniol §3. Reverse duality exchanges are deduced afterwards. Arbitrary noetherian coefficients use D_ctf; all Dᵇ_c uses self-injectivity.
5. **Singular cycles and intersection.** Weighted supported classes handle singular generic points. Derived restriction to the two graph fibres gives principal-divisor classes, and projective-bundle decomposition equates the two section pullbacks. The derived tensor product gives alternating Tor lengths. The SF.5 Tor node has geometric-field scope; the general-field formula is explicitly taken from [Cycle] 2.3.9 and the diagonal/regular-local comparison. Refined pullback is on Chow groups, with a global graph-immersion/smooth-projection factorization between smooth schemes, so SF.5's unresolved locally factorable-lci gluing extension is not used.
6. **Self-intersection and signatures.** Purity with supports and A¹ invariance on the support identify the original operator with the normal zero-section operator. No ambient smooth-family cohomology invariance is assumed. The flag filtration and successive regular zero loci prove the zero-section Chern expression. Every original API/test name now has a typed counterpart, including the formerly comment-only theorem targets. Canonical identity, unbounded Tor, crossing-support, finite-flat degree and nonzero P² Chern tests were strengthened in this review.
