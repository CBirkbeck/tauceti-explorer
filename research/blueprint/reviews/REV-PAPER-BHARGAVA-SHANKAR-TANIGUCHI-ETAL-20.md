## Mathematics checked independently

**Lattice and quadratic-tower replacements.** In the trace metric, complex coordinates have weight two and covolume is sqrt(D); with ordinary mixed-space volume it is 2^(-s)sqrt(D). AM–GM makes 1 shortest and O_K intersect Q=Z makes it primitive. In a saturated-prefix quotient choose a shortest primitive lift. Divide any comparison vector by the gcd of its quotient coordinates, round its prefix coefficients, and obtain c_i=1+(1/2)sum_{j<i}c_j. The argument works at the last index and in dimension two. Second Minkowski supplies the product upper bound; Hadamard gives the lower bound. The top-coordinate multiplication argument then gives the last-minimum estimate.

For the quadratic replacement use A=Cl(E)/2Cl(E), T=sigma-1 and V=A/TA, with T^2=0; rank(A)<=2 rank(V). The ordinary Hilbert-class-field quotient is central over the cyclic quadratic quotient. A ramified finite or infinite place supplies an order-two lift; killing at most t inertia generators gives rank(V)<=rank_2 Cl(F)+t-1. In the everywhere-unramified case the central extension may have a C4 factor, but |2G|<=2 yields the needed bound with max(t,1). Thus h_2(E)<=4^(max(t,1)-1)h_2(F)^2. Prime-support absorption and D_F^2<=D_E give the strong induction. This validates the replacement’s mathematics, conditional on its declared class-field and minimum suppliers; it does not validate the original one-factor assertion.

**Norm curves and cubic feedback.** Keep P_beta=Norm(X-beta)=minpoly(beta)^e. Odd e gives absolute irreducibility of Y^2=+/-P_beta over C even when the curve is singular; its plane degree is the full field degree. The squarefree replacement can have smaller degree (E12). Choose a representative of every translation coset meeting B(R), and inject its integer translates with |m|<=floor R into B(2R), to justify the coset count without assuming long boundary fibers. The even-index locus has e>=4 when there is no quadratic-over-subfield case, giving the D^(1/4) exception. Both norm signs and the nonzero restriction are retained. In the cubic case a rational square-ideal generator contributes only the identity class; nonrational generators are primitive and give smooth cubics. The formulas b≈0.2782386677, a=1/(6(1-b/log 2))≈0.2784337427 and c=ab/log 2≈0.1117670760 are consistent, and the feedback absorption uses b/log 2<1. This does not fill the missing HV/BK proof interiors.

**Bombieri–Pila replacements.** Read the supported-coefficient equivalence, rectangular kernel, iterated Rolle, entrywise interpolation witness, Newton determinant factorization, Taylor endpoint and absolute-remainder arguments, followed by the full-rank row-minor, monomial-product and specialization arguments. Separate interpolation entries need separate witnesses. The constant monomial has norm 1/N, not 1/(DN); the factorial saving restores the determinant estimate. The row-minor proof expands a retraction and includes the empty determinant. BP1–BP6 correctly use integer translations, stop at N<1, and include singular/boundary fibers. For delta=floor x, x=2 sqrt(log N/(d loglog N)), the objective is increasing on [delta,x], preserving constant 11. The finite-point cover of size 8d^3 absorbs into constant 12. These mathematical repairs do not prove the stronger whole-curve 3d^2 decomposition or identify the remaining analytic continuation/Bezout suppliers.

**Finite-field first bound.** For a divisor E of degree g, nonzero sections of L(2E) with even principal divisor are stable under every nonzero constant. Their projective classes map by half the principal divisor to 2-torsion. Riemann’s inequality supplies an effective representative for E-D, hence surjectivity onto the two-torsion class, not a unique chosen injection. High-degree RR gives dim L(2E)=g+1; projective cardinality gives (q^(g+1)-1)/(q-1). This works in characteristic two and without a chosen rational point, once the degree-one-divisor and Picard comparison imports are supplied. The stronger gonality result remains separate.

**Two counterexamples and their scope.** Kane’s published hypotheses apply to y^2=x^3-x, since the root-difference products are -1,2,2. His positive limiting probabilities at every Selmer rank give the split-etale-algebra twist counterexample to E9. This is not a rank counterexample. For E10, Y^2=X in degree-at-most-two polynomial boxes has q^2 points against q^(3/2+3 epsilon). For E13, X^2-Y^2-f(T) is generically absolutely irreducible but becomes two lines modulo any chosen irreducible f, with 2|f|-1 points. These establish the specified failures, not all suggested repairs.

The torsion direction and completed parent are appropriate, and the lattice/norm-curve replacements check mathematically. The independent HV/BK supplier direction is appropriate and avoids the feedback cycle, but G3 leaves their key proof inputs unextracted. The item list also includes unproved arbitrary-genus hyperelliptic corrections and del Pezzo geometry not specified in this elliptic brief. Correct G5/G6 and reconcile general descent/height owners before creating this Part II.

### 4. ArithmeticStatistics: reject

ST.0/ST.1/ST.3 are appropriate owners, but the source route has not acquired or decomposed the Baily/Cohn/fixed-resolvent counting originals. Quoting their conclusions and deferring G8 does not provide a buildable proof route. Keep the fixed-quadratic-field dependence explicit.

### 5. FunctionFieldArithmeticPartIITorsionBounds: reject

The first Picard bound has a valid projective-section surjection proof and correctly imports divisor/RR/Picard suppliers. The same route includes an unproved Sedunova repair and the epsilon-free gonality target. E10/E13 and G9 remain open; split the established first-bound scope from unresolved diagnostics and finish base-field gonality/characteristic-two interfaces before release.

### 6. GeometryOfNumbersAndQuadraticArithmetic: accept

GN.5 owns certified basis reductions. The prescribed-primitive-prefix construction and its recurrence c_i=1+(1/2)sum_{j<i}c_j prove the comparison at every index, including the last; attainment uses lattice discreteness. Apply the existing primitive decomposition/basis API in each free quotient. GN.1 owns successive minima and Minkowski II; Hadamard gives the other product inequality. These two quantitative adapters do not duplicate the built qualitative primitive-vector engine or number-field consumers.

### E13 — confirmed
