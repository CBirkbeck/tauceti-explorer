## What remains

**G1 — specialization-map compatibility in `CohomologicalBK.tracePrismaticAgreement`, §AI.7.3, clause (b).** BS22 Example 1.9(3), pp.5–6, §15.2 and Proposition 15.7, p.105, support the trace/prismatic equivalence and the relative Nygaard calculation. They do not supply the three asserted squares identifying the actual A_inf, de Rham and crystalline specialization maps with those of BMS2 Theorem 11.2. The package's universal-property construction and its base change to the perfected coefficient ring explain part of the comparison, but still do not identify all those arrows. BS22 Theorem 18.2, p.122, concerns symmetric monoidal functors on **all** p-completely smooth algebras over a perfectoid ring, over a **perfect** prism, together with the Hodge–Tate structure map. The Breuil–Kisin functors extended from smooth O_K-algebras have a smaller domain. Their completed Čech overlaps are not perfect prisms. That uniqueness theorem cannot supply the omitted argument on that domain or those overlaps.

To resolve G1, construct the three squares for the particular equivalence of clause (a), identify the relative-to-absolute trace base-change maps with the prismatic maps on quasiregular presentations, and verify that the de Rham and crystalline reductions use the same Frobenius-normalized coefficient maps. The rank-zero/point calculation checks normalization but does not establish those natural transformations on general R. Clause (a) and the independent trace and prismatic specialization theorems remain available.

**G2 — agreement of the wider smooth comparison diagrams in `CohomologicalBK.comparisonDiagramAgreement`, §AI.7.4, clause (b).** BS22 Theorem 17.2 and Remark 17.3, p.117, its proof and Lemma 17.4, p.120, and Theorem 18.2, p.122, establish the AΩ/prismatic equivalence with the Hodge–Tate structure map and its Bockstein consequences. The package does not establish agreement of all the θ-de Rham, crystalline, étale, B_dR⁺, Breuil–Kisin and trace comparison maps. In particular, uniqueness over A_inf is not uniqueness over the non-perfect prism (A_cris,(p)). BMS1 Theorem 14.1(ii), pp.388–389, obtains the θ-de Rham comparison through crystalline cohomology; the alternative Lη route mentioned in its proof still needs comparison with that map.

To resolve G2, give the actual θ-de Rham square comparing the two Frobenius factorizations, identify the crystalline maps on PD/Koszul generators, establish G1 for the Breuil–Kisin/trace part, and identify the generic étale/B_dR⁺ arrows using the earlier map targets. An equivalence of underlying complexes or a calculation of H⁰ alone is insufficient. The supported Hodge–Tate statement is retained as clause (a). Downstream claims of agreement that cite either clause (b) remain conditional on these two gaps.
