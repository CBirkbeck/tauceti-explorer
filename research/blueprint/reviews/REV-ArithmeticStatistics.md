## Mathematical corrections

ST.0 fixes the eventual-positive denominator and epsilon conventions for densities, the finitely many height-rescaling pieces, representative multiplicities and exceptional quadratic twists. Native Northcott counting and square classes now have direct Tau Ceti interfaces. The binary quartic representation is not described as prehomogeneous; the singular eligible example and the minimal-pair versus DVR-minimality distinction are kept explicit.

ST.1 corrects the row-action convention, cubic multiplication/index forms, changes of basis, full cubic-resolvent data and the nonzero scaling restriction. Quartic resolvent identities alone are insufficient as a definition. Quintic stabilizers account for the central μ₅ kernel before the S₅ stabilizer. The cubic covariants, integral discriminant normalization, explicit projection matrix and projective scaling are corrected. Reducibility is separated from existence of a linear factor; finite-field characteristic and nonsingularity hypotheses are retained. The 2-Selmer interface uses the native curve, étale algebra, restriction and Selmer carriers.

ST.2 replaces the false elimination step by a component/projection induction with an explicit SF.0 request. A support count cannot replace a multiset count, and arbitrary measurable matrix sets cannot replace semialgebraic bounded regions in a Davenport cusp estimate. The misleading cusp Lean signature was removed until its supplier contract is available. Haar averaging is almost-everywhere and weighted by stabilizers; the SO₂ ambiguity, constants C≥2, uniformity ranges and order of limits are explicit. General residue conditions decompose into a finite union of CRT rectangles; they need not themselves be one product. Cyclic cubic orders retain their 1/3 stabilizer weight.

ST.3 separates primitive quadratic-order classes from all-form unit weights: the former indefinite mean has constant π²/(18ζ(3)), whereas the latter has π²/18. The discriminant −83 versus −332 and the fundamental-unit relation ε₂₀=ε₅³ check the index conventions. Local maximality needs mod-p² data. Integrating the singular |t|^(−2/3) bound replaces the false uniform root bound. The stated quartic field constant and the weak/strong Malle distinctions are corrected; quoted second-order and higher-degree counting inputs remain gaps.

ST.4 uses relative closure away from Δ=0 for locally soluble invariant sets. The p-adic maps, Jacobian factor, rational-orbit mass, minimal invariant congruences I∈3ℤ, J∈27ℤ and local action invariance are explicit. A zero local density is treated by an upper bound, separately from the positive Euler-product case. Generic fibers and exceptional loci are not interchanged. The limsup average needs its boundedness assumptions.

ST.5 corrects finite-abelian surjection and automorphism formulas, rectangular/symmetric kernel probabilities, conditional weights and moment-to-law hypotheses. In characteristic two the quadratic form, not its alternating polar form alone, controls isotropic quotients. Poonen–Rains hyperplane fibers exclude those containing the distinguished vector. BKLPR finite-ring injection moments cannot depend only on group order; their product formula needs the reciprocal in its last factor. The second Selmer moment does not follow by multiplying marginal averages. Smith ordering, integer-height passage, 3-isogeny local ratios, maximal CM order and the Hecke-character twist are qualified. Primitive-residue normalization includes modulus 1 and the empty-vector case. Function-field limits keep the degree limit before q, exclude the m=0 prime-power filter, and preserve lower/upper densities rather than assert an unsupported fixed-q limit. - `mathlib:isInteger_of_is_root_of_monic`: For a monic polynomial over a UFD A, any root in its fraction field K belongs to the image of A (IsInteger A r). This is stronger than merely being integral; the fraction-field hypothesis is essential.
