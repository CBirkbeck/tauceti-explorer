## Five contracts requiring revision

1. **H.1/category-homology-derived-colimit.** `categoryHomology_isDerivedColimit` states only vanishing in positive degrees for one free representable. It does not state a natural comparison with the left derived colimit functors, despite its name and docstring. Quillen §1, LNM p. 91 gives the comparison; the current Gabriel–Zisman gap honestly leaves its proof open. Rename the representable contraction as a helper and give the intended comparison signature, using the diagram-category projective-resolution assumption explicitly if its construction remains open. The existing ModuleCat and generic derived-functor interfaces are the target carriers.

2. **H.1/maximal-tree-presentation.** The only theorem under this node is `classifyingSpace_fundamentalGroup_singleObj`, the case of a one-object group. Weibel IV Lemma 3.4, pp. IV.27–28 treats arbitrary connected categories with a spanning tree. Keep the useful group example, but state the general generators/relations or the equivalent quotient universal property, using the already imported free-groupoid/spanning-tree carriers and the composition convention in the packet. E11 must continue to retain all vertices when the tree has no edges.

3. **H.3/plus-pi2-universal-central-extension.** The advertised result allows a perfect normal subgroup P of any G, whereas `plusConstruction.pi2_eq_H2` requires G perfect and takes P=G. Weibel IV Proposition 1.7 and its universal-cover argument, pp. IV.9–10, give the general result. State the subgroup-sensitive comparison, including its pi1 identification and universe lift. Keep the classical UCE supplier and the separately recorded unrestricted-target plus gap; neither licenses narrowing the promised result to P=G.

4. **H.4/segal-gamma-space-delooping.** The repaired mathematics correctly uses Core C and iterated Gamma-space delooping. However, the test `segalGammaSpace_pi0_K0` asserts only a bijection between the original component monoid and isomorphism classes of C. That is before group completion; the packet asks for the K0 group after completion. For projective modules, the rank monoid already distinguishes these: natural-number ranks are not the integer-valued K0 group. Replace the proposed test with the group-completed comparison to the algebraic Grothendieck group, retaining a separate pre-completion component comparison if useful. Carlsson §1.2, printed pp. 6–9, and the packet’s corrected E16 explain the model.

5. **H.6/exact-couple.** The suggested file has the ModuleCat-valued exact-couple and page carriers, but a single comment lists test names and refers to the packet. It supplies neither a typed example nor the full proposed statement for the zero-E and two-stage checks. State those contracts under the packet names; the zero-E check can already quantify an `ExactCouple`, its `i` maps and its `page` objects. State the two-stage filtration’s kernel/cokernel extension with its connecting-map convention, and a precise non-example distinguishing the couple from a complex. The packet’s three tests must appear as actual proposed statements, even where a missing auxiliary carrier requires a full commented signature. See Higher Algebra §1.2.2, pp. 47–53, and the H.6 skeletal-filtration comparison.
