# Independent package review: Polylogarithms

Issue #11071; job `REV-PKG-Polylogarithms~adv`. Reviewer: Codex (GPT-6), session `codex-4Sobgu`, 2026-10-19. The package author was session `codex-ckSqbd`; this review is independent.

**Verdict: needs_changes.** The review is complete. Correctable source, ownership, degree and test defects have been fixed in the package. Three substantive construction/normalization gaps remain below; the package is not ready for a TauCetiRoadmap draft PR. This verdict does not reclassify the published weight-three or weight-four existence theorems as conjectures.

## Remaining defects and the required completion

1. **G1 — unplanned suppliers (Scope; exact contracts; §§2.4, 4.5–4.6).** Neither the current upstream roadmaps nor the atlas packages supply the named Polylogarithms Part II. Its contract includes motivic correlators, the combinatorial coalgebra and its extension theorem, decorated flags, the regulator-class comparison and conversion from `L₄*` on cycles. GR Theorems 1.13–1.14, Corollary 1.15, Theorem 7.6, Theorem 9.1 and §9.3 locate the mathematics, but listing those endpoints does not plan their prerequisite chains, definitions, APIs and Checks. Similarly GeometricTopology Layer 7 supplies metrics and volume, not ideal-boundary regions, measurable truncations or their convergence. Supply actual prerequisite layers for these two continuations, or develop those constructions in this package at the required density. Do not assume the volume formula or the K₇ period map as the supplier's definition. These require substantial new planning beyond a correction of an existing citation.
2. **G2 — top configuration normalization (§§3.5–3.7).** The candidate `r₆=18M₃`, equivalently `Alt₆[T]₃=-90M₃`, has no verified quotient-level derivation in the package. Its proposed consequence `Alt₆ M₃=-8 Alt₆[T]₃` must not be substituted for GR Proposition 7.2 on the strength of numerical periods or boundaries. G95 p.264 does support `r₄=18f₀⁽³⁾`; it is a different normalization from G94 (3.3), p.17. Complete the skew-symmetrization using the stated geometric relations, transport G95 (3.5), p.266 through the inverse cross-ratio, and check the elementary triangle normalization and duality. Otherwise choose a fully verified coherent comparison and recompute its constants. The exact boundary computations below test necessary identities but cannot detect a discrepancy in `ker δ₃`.
3. **G3 — universal incidence with multiplicities (§§5.3, 5.11).** AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.6 supplies Chow parameters, point cycles and a closed universal support. It does not supply a relative incidence cycle with the fiber multiplicities and admissible base-change theorem required by the Radon construction. G04 §3.1, pp.26–27 uses incidence without establishing this construction at the promised generality. The new named target `admissibleIncidenceCycle` now makes the missing fiber, face, base-change and proper-support API explicit. Construct it, state the exact allowed bases and pullbacks, and connect it to the resolved family-specific form construction. A reduced support is inadequate: two colliding points must specialize to a length-two cycle. This cannot be repaired by renaming the existing closed subset.

The retained proof obligations for exact Borel calibration, higher differentials, specialization, the raw-current dictionary and the common elliptic regularization are described below. They are explicit targets or conditional boundaries, not claims that the roadmap already proves them. In particular no new general transfer or motivic isomorphism has been silently assumed.
