## Corrections made

1. **Invertible special weights.** The suggested `special` constructor previously allowed `q=0`. On Sp(2), the claimed degree-one action would then send the nonzero vector e₁ to zero; a group representation cannot have that action. The constructor now explicitly requires `[NeZero q]`, and signatures using it carry that instance. The arithmetic hypothesis q>1 supplies it. A new geometric-degree test checks r(F)e₁=q⁻¹e₁ and fixes the Frobenius convention independently of the monodromy shift test.
2. **The finite residue subfield.** The old statement initially demanded an unramified U/K with residue cardinality p^n, then correctly warned that this is impossible when the residue degree f of K does not divide n. The statement now chooses an absolute residue degree divisible by both f and n, for example lcm(f,n), and its subfield S=F_{p^n}. The general coefficient field need contain only S. The old whole-residue adapter is explicitly identified as a special case requiring k_U→k. A new `residualFundamentalSubfield` adapter restricts reduced values to S before using S→k; its API gives evaluation, order bound, kernel and surjectivity. Three tests check agreement for the whole subfield, the trivial component and preservation of primitive order. Constructing and transporting the actual Kummer component remains in the existing supplier-binding inventory.
3. **The extension test.** `tameLevel_one_from_extension` previously assumed χ^p=χ and tested only its level. It now takes a one-dimensional character on a group, the inertia map, finite inertia image and the arithmetic p-power conjugation relation. Its conclusion checks that the restriction has level one. The proof route explains why this excludes a primitive level-two character as the tame inertia restriction of a character of G_{Q_p}.
4. **Usable morphisms and biproducts.** Added WD morphism composition associativity, WD isomorphism identity/inverse/composition and their value formulas. Added the explicit unique morphisms into and out of a binary direct sum. These supply the universal properties asserted by the packet. A further test evaluates the actual arithmetic action on Sp(2)⊕Sp(1), which the old dimension assertion alone could not detect.
5. **Source precision.** Bushnell–Henniart's direct-sum, tensor and dual formulas in §31.2 are printed on p. 200; p. 201 contains the classification exercise. Corrected the operation locators and their use records. The classification citation now distinguishes the exercise's indecomposable clause from finite decomposition and uniqueness derived by the packet's string argument. Deligne §3.12 concerns finite-image complex representations. Its matches now distinguish that source motivation from the general continuous restriction derived using R01.1 and the pinned closure map.
6. **Recorded source misprint.** Added the missed classification-exercise dimension-index slip as E7947, with its edition, counterexample, correction searches and independent confirmed verdict. The intended classification and its independent parameters already match the corrected result.
7. **Direct prerequisites.** Removed the bundled parent Frobenius-semisimplification/classification target as a redundant prerequisite of the predicate and the two semisimplification constructions. The predicate now cites the pinned Jordan declarations directly; Frobenius semisimplification already did so, and categorical semisimplification uses R01.1's finite-length and Brauer–Nesbitt inputs. The parent identifiers remain refinement and assembly references.

All changes are reflected in the reader and suggested file. The packet's sixteen `baseline.checked` records now include the independent verification. Assembly notes and the reader's counts were updated; the source list, gap and request counts, implementation statuses and six-planet selection were preserved.

Its references to the parent's confirmed E202 and E250 were checked on Deligne p. 569: scalar coordinate change rescales N inversely, and the Frobenius-change coefficient depends on the multiplication side. For geometric F′=Fτ, the corrected conjugator is exp(t(τ)N/(q−1)); the τF case has coefficient (1−q⁻¹)⁻¹. No duplicate errata entry was created.

In Bushnell–Henniart §31.2 Exercise, p.201, the printed condition indexes the irreducible Weil factor by the same n as the length of Sp(n). Section 28.7, p.185 defines that index to be the factor’s dimension. The parameters must be independent: Sp(2) has dimension two and nonzero monodromy, whereas the literal n≥2 constraint gives dimension n²≥4 and n=1 gives dimension one and zero monodromy. The page image confirms the symbol. E7947 records this as a misprint affecting no intended mathematics; the packet already states the independent-dimension result. The atlas registry, [Springer book record](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/3-540-31511-X) and targeted title/author/exercise errata searches yielded no existing correction. This finding is scoped to the maintainer-cleared 2006 edition, recorded in `sourceVersions`; the searches do not establish the absence of an unpublished correction.

The monodromy existence proof retains finite residue field, ℓ≠p, finite ℓ-adic coefficient field and joint continuity. Shrinking to the ℓ² congruence subgroup handles ℓ=2 as well. Frobenius forces the tame eigenvalues to be roots of unity, and the congruence forces them to be one. The finite logarithm at a nonzero tame coordinate gives the unique operator. The suggested selector consumes that explicitly spelled-out unique-existence witness; it does not supply the omitted arithmetic theorem by a new proposition field.

For Frobenius semisimplification, a power of arithmetic Frobenius centralizes the finite inertial image. Its unipotent Jordan factor commutes with the Weil action and with N. Removing that factor by u^{-deg(w)} retains inertia and monodromy, and is independent of the lift on the same carrier. Categorical semisimplification instead has zero N: the nonzero kernel of N in a simple WD object is stable, forcing N=0. Its representative is chosen, and only its isomorphism class is canonical.

For special-block classification, semisimplicity of the Weil action and Schur's lemma reduce N to maps between multiplicity spaces along norm-twist orbits. A finite orbit cannot cycle: an isomorphism r₀≃r₀⊗ω^j would force q^{j dim r₀}=1 by determinants, contradicting q>1 in characteristic zero. Finite strings give the blocks and their unique multiplicities. The source's normalized special object differs from this packet's object by ω^{(1−n)/2}; its irreducible factor absorbs that twist. No general Jacobson–Morozov or unstated Krull–Schmidt input is assumed.
