# Independent adversarial review: AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry

Verdict: **needs_changes**. This is a completed independent review of issue #8027, by Codex, session `codex-rOaFhN`, on 2026-10-09. The package contains false special cases, incomplete prerequisite contracts and targets missing from its continuation packets. The concrete corrections below are applied. The unresolved defects prevent a TauCetiRoadmap draft submission; successful elaboration does not establish their mathematics.

## Scope and evidence

Reviewed the package README, representative Lean signatures and metadata against the four `AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry*.json` packets, public primary sources, and the binding package-review and blueprint protocols. The packets are unchanged. The earlier accepted verdict is superseded by this independent adversarial verdict.

The library statement audit uses Mathlib `082e2d3` and Tau Ceti `f790474`. The separate duplication audit uses current Tau Ceti `a91d3aaf` and the current upstream roadmap checkout. I read AlgebraicVectorBundles and StableReduction as the two upstream models, including their relevant signatures. I searched the current roadmaps by objects, hypotheses and operations: quotient bundles, projective constructions, quasi-coherent pullback and descent, banded groupoids, profinite limits, Picard rigidifications, deformation groupoids, blowups and analytic boundary charts. The nine roadmaps newer than the atlas snapshot were included, as were Completed/ContourIntegration, EffectiveBounds, OrthogonalL2Bases and RestrictedProducts. Their contour integrals, number-field bounds, Hilbert-space bases and topological restricted products do not provide the geometric contracts missing here. HodgeStructures supplies linear-algebraic Hodge structures, not the resolution or analytic-chart prerequisites.

The README has 555 retained target labels, including 283 bare lemma labels and 81 constructions described only by a test count and test categories. Those sparse entries do not state enough mathematics to perform the required complete adversarial pass. I walked the target families and inspected the representative signatures, but **do not certify every title as a theorem with verified hypotheses**. Restoring exact statements, prerequisites, APIs and concrete tests is an outstanding defect, not an assumption made by this review.

## Coverage of the remaining families

R09.1 and R09.2 received particular scrutiny because their statements were assembled without an accepted complete plan. The table covers their quotient conventions, degenerate ranks, regularity, graph constructions, polarized classes, boundedness and GAGA boundary. It does not manufacture missing Chow or numerical boundedness statements from the introductory promise.

For R09.3 I checked native descent data, coalgebras, the identity and faithfully-flat comparisons, restrictions of scalars, affine tilde comparisons and overlap maps. I removed the duplicate restricted quasi-coherent pullback construction. This checks the existing module-descent strand, not the later algebraic-space Hom/Weil-restriction continuation, which is absent.

For R09.4 I inspected the native gerbe and banding definitions, intrinsic centre sections, restriction/transport constructions, sheaf Hom, fixed-band equivalences, object and morphism descent, neutralization, root gerbes, affine/profinite stages and twisted inertia. I tested trivial/nontrivial bands and the distinctions between natural, band-preserving and arbitrary strong transformations. The long transport strands include many title-only lemmas, so their intended conjugation, restriction and associator equations are not all specified. Their full adversarial verification requires exact equations; the existence of named Lean declarations does not supply missing README statements.

For R09.5 and R09.6 I checked the difference between partial and total rigidification, group-action versus arbitrary-inertia hypotheses, normalization and closure, G-ring approximation, framed deformation versus unframed automorphisms, algebraic-space completions versus stack presentations, and formal effectivity. The missing obstruction-theoretic and proper-space contracts are recorded below.

For R09.7 the local arithmetic and geometric counterexamples show why the original algorithm outline did not imply its end theorems. The corrected outline distinguishes a sourced boundary-free theorem from a closed formalization plan. It still lacks the actual recursive invariant and the full embedded/pre-existing-boundary interface.
