## Corrections applied

1. **Kummer restriction (1.1).** The injectivity assertion needs a finite **Galois** extension not containing `√−3`. The pure cubic extension `ℚ₃(∛3)` kills the nonzero class of 3 without containing that square root. The README and a Lean negative-control statement now record this. The target is expressly the explicit basis and ramification lines; LocalGaloisGroups already owns the general dimension formulas.
2. **Uniformizer and descent scope (1.2, 2.1–2.4).** Use a complete mixed-characteristic DVR with perfect residue field consistently. Fix the polynomial representative of `H_g` of degree below `e`; otherwise invariant-uniformizer formulas are not canonical. Distinguish coefficient Frobenius from substitution in its power-series formula. Correct the relation-word scalar action to `u(1+f)`, with `H=1+f`. The rank-one formula with constant term 1 requires **wild inertia**, not merely `g(π)/π≡1`. An unramified quadratic twist of `μ₃` supplies the counterexample. In the ideal categories, Dieudonné modules are taken for groups of `ℓ`-power order, and the Jordan–Hölder set is nonempty.
3. **Monodromy (2.1, 2.2, 5.1, 5.9).** Replace assertions of an identically zero `N` by its vanishing on the specified generators. The Leibniz rule gives `N(ue)=ue`, even when `N(e)=0` and `N∘φ₁=0`. A proved polynomial example pins the sign `N(u)=+u`.
4. **Coefficient fields and induction (0.3, 1.9, 2.2, 5.5).** Induce the one-dimensional scalar character before taking a prime-field form; inducing its two-dimensional restriction of scalars gives dimension four. The negative square-class check is scoped to `𝔽₃`; over `𝔽₉`, `−1` is a square. The coefficient-filtration argument first uses the degree-twelve field with residue field `𝔽₃`, then extends to `𝔽₉`; it does not assume arbitrary coefficient fields are disjoint from `𝔽₉`.
5. **Local classification and filtrations (1.11–1.12, 3.3, 3.6, 4.3).** Norm subgroups include the cyclic subgroup generated by the displayed cube class. Supply all three conductor-exponent-five columns of Manoharmayum's table and label the conductor-three equations as representative families. Remove an unsupported claim that failure of a cyclic descent argument proves failure of descent. Correct the group-scheme filtration order and the pullback/pushout directions in the cubic rank-three argument. Nonzero residual Frobenius follows from the degree-three Frobenius of the good elliptic special fibre; `F+V=0` and `FV=3` on 3-torsion alone allow both operators to vanish.
6. **Definitions with witnesses (2.7).** Replace the assertion that a third ideal generator is additional by an actual module satisfying the first two relations and failing the third. The vector `(0,1)` maps to `(−1,0)` under `(γ₃−γ₃⁻¹)γ₂`. The unipotent matrix calculation is proved in Lean.
7. **The twisted level-five curve (1.15–1.16).** Use the reference level structure `μ₅×ℤ/5`, with cyclotomic determinant, rather than treating the constant full symplectic level structure as defined over an arbitrary ground field. State the determinant-one twisted cocycle and its projectivization into `PGL₂`. Transport the chosen alternating form with the local torsion isomorphism. Local constancy (6.1) is tested within a fixed mod-five family whose mod-three fibres differ.
8. **Global interfaces (6.5, 6.11).** Give the finite-order determinant argument needed by the Kisin supplier, and cite current ClassFieldTheory Layers 11–12, not Layers 2–4, for global reciprocity. In the exceptional classification, the mixed `(p,q)=(5,3)` branch has the CM `j=0` point; it is not impossible. Quadratic twists retain the hypotheses and `j`, but need not retain conductor. Name the actual level-50 and level-338 curves and the two required nonconstant modular-map targets, including extension at poles and cusp normalization. Delete the dependency on ComputationalNumberTheory CN.5: its current text is metadata, not a mathematical supplier. Polynomial certificates for these two maps are targets here. CM induction supplies all sextic twists of `j=0`.

The additional rational curves and discriminant/`j` examples in Lean use real Weierstrass coefficients and real ellipticity instances. No sorry'd data, new theory development, or dummy carrier was added.

The auxiliary Dieudonné algebra and filtration definitions now have explicit coefficient-semilinearity, empty/two-step filtration and wrong-constituent checks. Their names, together with the auxiliary Breuil definitions, are recorded in the Lean closing list.
