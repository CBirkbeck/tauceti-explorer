## Corrections and ownership

The review retained the package's detailed targets, hypotheses and worked examples. It corrected these substantive points.

1. **Categorical suppliers.** Replaced stale GeneralAlgebraicKTheory layer references by the current thirteen-layer contracts: plus/Q and absolute low degrees in §§3.12/3.14; localization in §§4.14/4.16; Waldhausen tools in Layers 5–6; pairings in §§7.2–7.7 and §8.13; Frobenius invariance in §§11.7–11.16; Bass and enhanced comparisons in §§12.10–12.11/13.6. The scheme models explicitly verify the additional acyclic-retract approximation condition from Thomason 1993 §4, pp. 213–215.
2. **Actual geometric owners.** JacobianChallenge Layer C supplies ordinary proper coherence without flatness over locally Noetherian bases. StableReduction supplies Rees blowups, rather than general proper coherence. EnhancedDerivedSheaves E1.9/E2.8 supplies the proper-support, finite-relative-Tor perfect-preservation and target-flat arbitrary-base pushforward theorems. Their repeated preservation targets were replaced by named strict/flasque K/G-model comparisons. The perfect/compact target likewise became the compatibility of the chosen K-model with the supplied compact subcategory. The arbitrary-base perfect-projective approximation argument remains a distinct target. No upstream file was changed.
3. **Other supplied constructions.** The package imports the ordinary derived, pseudo-coherent, tensor, base-change, perfect-generator, Chern and geometric GRR interfaces. It retains their strict-model/K-spectrum comparisons. EnhancedDerivedSheaves E2.8 already supplies derived Čech charts over perfect F_p-schemes: the retained chart target explicitly extends them over the integers and compares in characteristic p. The geometric leading-Chern identity is a named new target, rather than a fictitious supplier claim. The affine Cartan comparison identifies the existing Tau Ceti map and its finite-projective-resolution inverse.
4. **Missing operation prerequisites.** StableHomotopyKTheory supplies spectra, Moore coefficients, tower homotopy limits and filtered spectral sequences, not the integral Bousfield–Kan completion or the unstable pointed-sheaf comparison. Section 6.7 now specifies those constructions, the uniform affine GL-homology stability input, both K-coherence comparisons, and the finite-cell/support-cofiber arguments. KTheoryLowDegrees Z.3 does not supply the entire nonunital weighted λ-module theorem; §§6.2/6.5 construct that bridge with coefficient weights.
5. **Localization and Bass signs.** Fixed the Bass boundary to ∂_T[T]=+1, as computed by TT90, proof of 6.1(b), printed p. 354. Right-linearity gives ∂_T([T]∪x)=x and the section [T]∪−. Named boundary/section targets and parameter-inversion checks now fix the convention. The DVR convention remains ∂[π]=[k], ∂([π][u])=[ū] and ∂([u][π])=−[ū].
6. **Gersten scope and proof.** The geometric projection lemma uses an infinite field, closed points and a nonzero nonunit regular element. Finite-field transfer gives classwise effacement, without falsely producing one global nullhomotopy. The equicharacteristic semilocal extension now has its own Popescu approximation and cohomological-continuity proof: local Panin exactness alone does not imply semilocal exactness by localization of one local ring, and termwise codimension complexes need not commute with nonflat transitions.
7. **Products and tests.** Perfect external products do not require flatness. The coherent K×G external pairing retains flatness of the appropriate source over the base and Noetherian hypotheses. The square-zero negative control for λ-module specialness now uses a nonzero torsion-free ideal, so its unequal multiples really are unequal.
8. **Projective and blowup degeneracies.** Quillen's regular coherent resolution has its class formula in G₀; the vector-bundle and perfect versions have their own K₀ carriers. Over the dual numbers, a rank-one projective bundle and F=k distinguish these statements. Exceptional recovery requires d≥2, and the approximation has 1≤k<d (Thomason 1993, Lemma 2.4, pp. 200–202). The Cartier case d=1 has no exceptional summand and does not satisfy the old recovery formula.
9. **Bott hypotheses.** The positivity k≥1 and finite λ-dimension of the conormal/vector-bundle input are explicit. Rank alone does not imply finite λ-dimension for a virtual class: N=1−u has rank zero but λ_t(N)=(1+t)/(1+ut). Its proposed θ² cannot be formed by the vector-bundle polynomial in an arbitrary λ-ring.
10. **Supported degree zero.** Added `SupportK0.strictComparison`, carrying the GS strict presentation to TT supported perfect K₀. Separated regular Noetherian schemes are quasi-compact with affine diagonal, so the resolution property follows from Stacks 0F8A and §1.6. Supports survive the strict comparison. No groupwise Zariski sheaf argument is used: 1−[O(−1)] on P¹ vanishes on the two standard affine charts and has Euler characteristic one.
11. **Finite coefficients.** Soulé §2.7, p. 498 concerns Serre classes; it does not supply coefficient Adams operations. Section 6.9 now gives the Moore-space comparison, finite-cell K-coherence extension and postcomposition construction, including degree m=2. It fixes P_m(n)=cofib(n:S^{m−1}→S^{m−1}); Moore self-duality supplies the suspension shift to π_m(K/n). Addition comes from the K-target, without assuming P₂(n) is a cogroup. The Bockstein square follows from precomposition with the cell maps. The finite CRT decomposition retains integral localized endpoint splittings; rational endpoint weights alone do not suffice. Geisser §1.1, pp. 3–4 supplies the narrower published model, and the supported extension is an explicit proof target.
12. **Lean shape.** Removed admitted examples and unrelated arithmetic examples, unused Tau Ceti imports, and pseudo-signatures over absent carriers. Kept one real strict-perfectness predicate, two named theorem targets on existing carriers, and one proved discriminating unbounded-term example. The closing inventory now covers the heavy README constructions, including the coherator, completion, coefficient operations and supported comparison. Their full mathematics remains in the README.
