## Mathematical and dependency corrections

1. **R.2 invariant forms.** The source is now `(H^q(g,k;R))^{K/K°}`, equivalently full `(g,K)` cohomology, rather than unrestricted infinitesimal relative cohomology when real points are disconnected. The previously vague nonexample is now PGL₂(R): its nonidentity maximal-compact component reverses the tangent orientation, killing the degree-two compact-dual class. Torsion descent uses a normal torsion-free finite-index subgroup.
2. **R.3 compact duals.** Added finite-rank presentations, including the even-rank SU/SO Euler class and its square-zero relation, to the existing key cohomology theorem. Read Borel 1953 Propositions 31.3–31.4, pp.203–205. The conservative `n≥2q+3` bound now follows from those generators and the transgression restriction maps; Borel 1974’s bare assertion of stationarity alone did not justify that number. This remains one target-level theorem, not a split into proof lemmas.
3. **R.4 coordinates.** Evaluation in the native real-function model no longer divides by the Tate generator a second time. Division by `(2πi)^{j−1}` occurs once in the complex-to-real model conversion. The coordinate measure still gives the fixed integer lattice covolume one.
4. **R.5 compact fiber.** Added `R.5/compact-factor-comparison`, declaration `TauCeti.Borel.arithmeticCompactFactorComparison`, with `addedBy: REV-BorelRegulators`. Borel 1977 §§3.4–3.5 and 5.2 give the absolute/relative invariant-form square, commuting spectral sequences, odd-rank compact-fiber degeneration and primitive splitting. Its hypotheses retain odd N and the arithmetic degree bound. R.6 cycles and the R.5 period theorem now depend directly on it; the period theorem also depends on the actual R.6 pairing. A compact-dual relative map alone does not place the full algebraic primitive classes on Γ\G. The determinant proof retains Borel’s rational-structure coefficient table, rather than calling an arbitrary complex complement rational.
5. **R.6 form conversion.** Integration is linear on all rational real top forms, including zero; its volume specialization requires a nonzero form. Introduced the computed positive scalar `c_η` with `μ_∞^Tam=c_η|η|`. The formula is `c_η|I_D(η,U)|vol_f(U)=τ(H)`. A new API lemma and doubled-form test enforce `c_{2η}=c_η/2` while `I_D(2η,U)=2I_D(η,U)`. The previous displayed equation omitted its conversion scalar despite claiming it was explicit.
6. **R.7 integral Bloch test.** Under Suslin’s antisymmetric tensor quotient, diagonal tensors can survive as 2-torsion. The test now distinguishes integral `2[ζ₆]` from rational `[ζ₆]`, using `1−ζ₆=ζ₆^{-1}`. Added the three actual V.3 boundary/group prerequisites. The positive Bloch–Wigner value does not settle the unresolved exact Burgos/Suslin conversion scalar.
7. **ALS supplier scope.** The current early `ALS.5:finite-level-duality` explicitly excludes the extra Betti/de Rham/relative-Lie identifications. Requests/prerequisites now identify full ALS.5 and request a split early **nonautomorphic** comparison export. Only its further automorphic suffix should depend on AS.5. This is a precise supplier refinement and gap, not a claim that the existing early prefix already supplies the comparison.
8. **K-theory supplier scope.** Narrowed the ring-model prerequisites to `K.2:plus` so the downstream low-degree K₃/Bloch comparison is not pulled into the early rank argument. Exact-functor additivity and the finite-projective transfer/base-change specialization are separately requested from K.3. Ramified O_E/O_F remains finite projective and is not assumed finite étale. The existing AF Part II request for logarithmic-growth/L²/Matsushima inputs is now explicitly included in the gap list.

The dependency order is read at node level: R.1–R.3; the early R.5 compact-fiber comparison; R.6 volume/cycle/pairing inputs; the R.5 period/proportionality theorem; and the final Bloch comparison. The displayed stage numbers are not a claim that all of R.5 precedes all of R.6.
