The corrections made in place are:

- Corrected the tame-cover argument to work over the perfect closure and then descend the finite-presentation data to a finite purely inseparable extension, which need not itself be perfect. Restored the dimension-one curve scope. Replaced the purportedly necessary example `y²=x^p−t`, which already has a dense smooth open, by `Spec K[x,y]/(y^p−t)` over `K=𝔽_p(t)`, which is nowhere smooth over K. The general boundary-trait inertia input is requested from LPV.1 rather than the narrower finite-residue-field Tau Ceti local-fields roadmap.
- Read SGA 7 XXI §5.0–§5.2.2 and its original proof. The integrality node now gives the finite-point evaluation and top-degree duality endpoints, the curve Euler-product argument, and dimension induction with the twisted Leray terms. Added Poincaré duality as a direct prerequisite and precise trace-formula, cohomological-dimension and fibration inputs to the existing supplier requests. Removed the unavailable-source gap and its stale summary/coverage text.
- Added `q≠0` to `newtonCouple_mul`, and “τ fixes q” to the raw automorphism API. The new zero-base test uses weights 1 and −1 and detects the false unrestricted multiplication formula. The Lean signature and example match. The valuation triangles now also cover smooth schemes with components of different dimensions, as the source does.
- Replaced the insufficient quotient-duality justification by the self-adjoint averaging projector on the finite pushforward of the constant sheaf. It supplies self-duality of the invariant summand, including when the residue characteristic divides the group order.
- Qualified the different-weight direct-sum claim for pure complexes by requiring the two summands to be nonzero. Added its mixedness-under-six-operations prerequisite; the zero complex remains pure at every weight.
- In generic mixedness, restored the cone of `ℱ→Rj_*j*ℱ` from 6.1.9. Expanded 6.1.8's finite-boundary argument and added the direct mixed-complex and finite-morphism prerequisites. The finite-level adic refinement remains explicit.
- In six-operation stability, restored `RHom(j_!ℱ,L)=Rj_*RHom(ℱ,j^!L)` for locally closed j. Exceptional inverse-image stability is proved first by smooth purity and localisation, then RHom, then duality. Added mixed-complex triangle stability as a direct prerequisite.
- Added the existing pinned Clifford restriction theorem to the baseline and to geometric semisimplicity. The wrapper is explicitly finite-dimensional and derives its semisimple case by a finite sum of irreducibles. Corrected the ordinary/Weil lattice distinction: a Weil Frobenius need not preserve a lattice. Added image membership and conjugacy APIs, and requested the reductive-closure equivalence from Tau Ceti ReductiveGroups layer 6.
- Restricted the stalk Hochschild–Serre sequence with surjective degree map to geometrically connected X₀. For a merely connected scheme, the proof uses its finite constant-field extension and `F^e`; the global splitting/coinvariant argument stays general.
- Removed the universal constructible-adic-sheaf spreading assertion from potential properties and L2's request. A model is a supplied witness. Finite sums require a common model refinement and the indicated stability of P; base extension is forward in general, with a converse only when the witnessing data descend. Added transport by isomorphism and made its invariance hypothesis explicit.
- Required nonempty X for strict positivity of the top Lefschetz degree, while retaining the degree equality for empty X.
- Replaced the graded primitive-decomposition sketch by the direct primitive split, both degree-zero/one boundary cases, iteration by two, and the hard-Lefschetz bijection for the upper degrees. This avoids the ambiguous single-parity downward induction.
- Made the smooth incidence pullback and the Tate target of duality explicit in hard Lefschetz for pure complexes. To obtain a perfect pairing of the two invariant spaces, the invariant-form lemma is applied to their semisimple hyperbolic direct sum.
- For the orthogonal invariant/vanishing decomposition, imported LPV.7's direct fixed-part proof for the given Lefschetz pencil. The sufficiently-general-line clause of the universal hyperplane adapter does not justify an arbitrary pencil.
