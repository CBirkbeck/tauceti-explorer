The paper compares integral log-syntomic complexes with p-adic nearby cycles, with quantified torsion losses, and deduces semistable period comparisons using Banach–Colmez spaces. Its local engine changes Frobenius, passes from differential to Lie and group Koszul complexes, and uses almost-étale descent and decompletion. The atlas already owns several underlying objects and the proper formal semistable comparison; the new construction boundaries must respect those owners.

The mathematical corrections distinguish false formulas from incomplete proofs. E1 is rejected: a weaker bound depending on K is not a contradiction of a bound depending on e,p, and invoking a conductor does not show the latter impossible. E16 remains a proof gap for the printed p^8 bound; p^(2(p+7)) is a safe consequence of the displayed square. E22 remains a narrower quantitative descent gap after correcting both the scalar transcription and the meaning of K_0. These findings do not establish that the main comparison theorems are false.

Three checks materially change the extracted formulas. First, rounding the different gives δ_R+1≤e_0/(2p), not strict inequality. For K=Q_3(ζ_27)(α) with α³+(ζ_27−1)α²+(ζ_27−1)=0, f=3,e=54,δ_K=4, hence 4+3<9 but δ_R+1=3=e_0/6. The later convergence argument retains its second strict inequality.

Second, Lemma 2.11’s filtration loss is too small, and the later 2r loss also fails at p=2. Take K=Q_2(ζ_16), u=3/4,v=3/2, P_0=1+(1+T)^8 and t=16 log(1+T). At v_2(T)=3/16, v_2(P_0)=1 and v_2(t)=5/2. The element g=P_0/T^5 is integral on the annulus (endpoint valuations 9/32 and 1/16) and lies in F^1, but v_2(4g/t)=−7/16. Thus even multiplication by 4 does not put g in t times the integral annulus ring. An endpoint argument gives the coarser arithmetic 3r bound; later numerical constants must be recomputed. No claim is made that this disproves existence of a universal comparison constant.

Third, Lemma 3.17 is not a filtered quasi-isomorphism for the usual Hodge filtration. On the formal disc with divisor x=0, gr^0 is K⟨x⟩→O(disc minus {0}), missing 1/x. [Kiehl’s Theorem 2.3](https://www.numdam.org/article/PMIHES_1967__33__5_0.pdf), printed p.19, supplies an unfiltered comparison. The stated range in Corollary 3.16 can instead be obtained by composing its inverse with log de Rham→log de Rham/F^r: that projection is an isomorphism below degree r−1 and injective in degree r−1, then Proposition 3.12 applies.

Other direct checks correct the Lie differential to ∇_0−q p^i, the bracket to [∇_0,∇_j]=p^i∇_j, the decompletion projection to ϕ^(−n)ψ^kϕ^(n+k), and the Taylor expansion in V_j. The plus-sign Lie square has defect 2p^i tX_j on X_j. The projection must be the identity when k=0. E31 leaves the modified integral Tate twist unresolved: for p=3,r=2 the printed endpoint interval admits both (a,b)=(0,2) and (1,0), which give distinct lattices. A supplier-qualified normalization is required.
