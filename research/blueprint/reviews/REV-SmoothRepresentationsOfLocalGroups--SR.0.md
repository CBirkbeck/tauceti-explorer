Added `SR.0:abelian-category/countable-schur`, marked with this review’s `addedBy`. Bernstein92 Ch. I §4.2, pp. 19–20, and BZ76 Theorem 2.11, p. 18, give scalar endomorphisms from a countable orbit and uncountably many complex scalars. The argument uses partial fractions in one endomorphism inside a possibly noncommutative division algebra. It is available before Harish-Chandra compactness and admissibility, removing their hidden circular use of a central character.

The other important proof-order repairs are cuspidal support before its finite-fiber consequence, the ordinary block center before its noetherianity application, and construction of the big-cell map before the counit and triangle identities. Presentations by a projective generator use direct sums. Induction commutes with those sums because the compact-quotient invariant formula is finite; the argument does not use second adjointness.

For the pinned modular convention Δ(g)=μ(Eg⁻¹)/μ(E), inversion has density Δ and the convolution anti-involution is Δ(g)f(g⁻¹). In the p-adic affine group, left Haar measure is du db/|u|² and Δ(a,b)=|a|. This supplies a nonunimodular sign check. The complex positivity arguments additionally need conjugation.
