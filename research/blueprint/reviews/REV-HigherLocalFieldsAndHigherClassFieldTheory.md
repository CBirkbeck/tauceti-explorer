## Corrections

The substantive change is `HL.0/topology-on-the-multiplicative-group`. The original identified the graph topology with the Zhukov/Fesenko convention in every characteristic. Invitation I §1.4.1–1.4.2, pp. 14–16, and Fesenko I §6.2–6.3, pp. 63–64, require a split. For positive first-residue characteristic the saturated parameter/graph description is retained. When the first residue has characteristic zero, the source convention pulls back the rank-unit topology from the lower mixed-characteristic field. It can be non-Hausdorff. In Q_p((t)), the constant sequence 1+t converges to 1 in that pullback and is killed in K_1^top; it cannot converge to 1 in the Hausdorff graph convention. The statement, hypotheses, API, third rejection test, reader conventions and G02 consumers now carry this distinction. The additive Laurent component in the suggested file is unaffected; its introduction now makes this boundary explicit.

`HL.7/motivic-residue-comparison` confused the complete-DVR norm/residue commutation theorem with the Galois-cohomological norm-residue isomorphism. The actual `K2SymbolsBrauer:T.4/complete-norm-residue` statement is ∂N=N∂. The full invertible-coefficient isomorphism comes from `MotivicEtaleKTheory:M.5/KU-normresidue`, already used by HL.2; its direct prerequisite/request is now explicit for HL.7. K2 T.4 remains the transfer/residue supplier. The survey is cited as motivation and for its actual residue construction, not as the proof of the generic comparison.

The ramification construction’s third test now evaluates a jump exactly: v(σt−t)=r₀ excludes σ at r₀ under the source’s strict inequality and includes it below r₀. This replaces a methodological warning with a test that rejects ≥.
