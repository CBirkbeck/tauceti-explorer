## Mathematical corrections

The unconditional class-group equality in ES.7 had no planned construction producing enough Euler systems. Applying the Kolyvagin derivative gives inclusions but does not prove its surjectivity. The review adds four target-level nodes, each marked `addedBy` with this review's job id:

1. Vertical determinantal systems: the compact-support arithmetic adapter, localization-normalized determinant transitions, their augmentation kernels and the free rank-one inverse limit (Burns–Sano I Definition 2.8, Proposition 2.10 and Proposition 2.21, pp.12–17).
2. Horizontal determinantal systems: determinant localization isomorphisms, surjective vertical reduction and the comparison with full-S Stark systems, with its local dual-invariant obstruction (Definition 3.2, Theorem 3.3 and Theorem 3.11, pp.22–28).
3. Basic Euler systems: the integral arithmetic determinant-to-bidual map, its Euler relations and the entire-system Bockstein/regulator square (Theorem 2.17, p.15, and §§4.3.2–4.3.4, pp.36–47).
4. Higher derivative surjectivity: the square proves surjectivity first at E and then at its target subfields. It yields the finite and integral all-system ideal equalities with the exact local hypotheses and the distinction between primed Stark-transported ideals and ordinary Kolyvagin ideals (BSS II Remark 5.3, p.26, and Corollaries 6.17–6.18, pp.42–44).

The dual convention is explicit: Burns–Sano I takes the dual lattice B=Tᴰ and forms cohomology of B*(1)=T. The horizontal comparison targets the full condition F_S, and the natural map from canonical Stark systems is injective; surjectivity of the determinantal square proves it is onto under the extra local hypotheses. The plan does not assume those local conditions equal in advance. Quotient base change on determinant lines is distinguished from multiplying by a group-ring norm; the degree-p residual test detects that mistake. Conductor orientation and the two-prime negative cross term are retained.

This addition consumes merged PadicMeasuresIwasawaAlgebras §5.1 for generic determinant lines and extends the exact L6 Part II request by the admissible-complex determinant-to-bidual comparison of Burns–Sano I Proposition 2.20 and Appendix A.9. The arithmetic compact-support/perfectness input is specified in L2. ES owns the arithmetic systems and comparison square; it does not redevelop generic determinants, biduals or Fitting ideals. The class-group application now lists surjectivity as a direct prerequisite. Its all-system ideal equality does not assume Rubin–Stark Conjecture B′; only the named Rubin–Stark family does.

The application also permits p=3 when χ²≠ω. The regulator, bounds, basic-system comparison and surjectivity now state the residual non-self-dual p=3 variant from BSS II Remark 6.19, p.44. The standard p>3 statements remain valid; no extension to self-dual residual coefficients at 3 is claimed.

The review corrects a second substantive boundary in ES.5/structure-theorem and ES.6/stark-structure/regulator-isomorphism. Over a DVR, equality between the dual-Selmer length and initial divisibility detects primitivity **when the initial component is nonzero**. If it is zero, both values are infinite. A nonprimitive uniformizer multiple of a positive-order primitive generator still has infinite equality. The corrected unguarded equality condition is therefore “zero initial component or primitive.” The eventual divisibility criterion and the finite torsion-quotient formula are unchanged. The positive-corank control uses T₅ of 389.a1: rank two and full 5-adic image, together with MR04 §6.2 and the local Kummer corank bound. It needs no BSD or Sha-finiteness assertion.

Source finding E6 records this boundary at the published MR16 Theorem 8.7(ix), p.167, and Theorem 13.4(ix), p.176, with the separate preprint and MR04 Corollary 5.2.13(ii) locators. Bounded searches of the arXiv history, Numdam version of record, author/errata pages and exact theorem searches found no correction of this boundary. That is a bounded search result, not a claim of exhaustive absence.

The other corrections add the freeness needed for scalar quotient injections, restrict the combined canonical/unramified definition to p-adic integer rings, restore irreducibility to Rubin's error-group finiteness, specify the full χ-ray setup and its three alternatives, make r≥1 explicit for Kolyvagin and derivative targets, and repair the bidual torsion counterexample by requiring a non-zero-divisor.
