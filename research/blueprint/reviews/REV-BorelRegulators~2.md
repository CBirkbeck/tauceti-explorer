## Corrections

1. **Arithmetic modules and duality (R.1).** Quillen uses projective right order modules, so `P⊗_O D` is a right D-space and matrices act on columns from the left. The arithmetic application now uses Mathlib’s `Module Dᵐᵒᵖ V`; the generic building prototype stays a left-module construction. The GL Borel–Serre symmetric space removes the connected real points of the maximal ℚ-split central torus before taking its dimension. For D=F this gives `d=r₁n(n+1)/2+r₂n²−1` and `ν=d−(n−1)`, consistent with Putman–Studenmund Proposition 2.1, p.8. The existing orientation twist and nonfree-projective scope are retained.
2. **Building edges (R.1).** The pinned pair-face theorem includes equal vertices, yielding a singleton. An edge must have two distinct vertices. Corrected the API and executable suggested signature and added the repeated-vertex test. Also restricted the Steinberg module’s finite-classifying-space example to a torsion-free subgroup of finite index.
3. **Real components (R.2).** For an arithmetic subgroup Γ put `G_Γ=G(ℝ)°Γ`, `K_Γ=K∩G_Γ`. Its invariant-form source is `H^q(g,K_Γ)`, so van Est and continuous restriction use G_Γ. Before finite descent, a normal neat Γ′ lies in G° and constants live in `C∞(Γ′\G°)`. Full real-group cohomology supplies only full-K invariants when Γ misses components. PGL₂(ℤ) and its identity-component intersection now test both possibilities without asserting a nonzero arithmetic degree-two image. Naturality and ALS.5’s request use the same convention.
4. **SL Hopf structure and GL/SL comparison (R.2–R.3).** Defined the SL product directly from finite block sums, Künneth and filtered-colimit homology. Stabilized block exchange is conjugation inside SL after correcting a possible reduced-norm sign on an unused coordinate. The forward map to GL⁺ preserves this product; it is not used to transport a product backwards. Added direct H.1 imports for filtered homology and conjugation. The GL/SL proof now explicitly requests H.3’s universal-cover H-space and trivial deck-action result, which the existing cover-identification node alone does not export. Torsion abelian quotient homology is justified by filtered finite subgroups. The K₁ wording allows products of degree-one generators.
5. **Cutoffs and boundary coordinates (R.3).** Borel’s criterion uses a positive real m′. Taking `c+1/2` preserves the desired integer degrees when c is finite, including c=0; anisotropic c=∞ uses any sufficiently large finite cutoff. The `A/q` definition is restricted to positive q, with degree zero treated by constants. AA.3 uses left quotients with root coordinates tending to infinity, whereas Borel’s right-quotient coordinates tend to zero. The proof now transports charts, frame and metric by inversion `a_B=a_AA⁻¹`, preserving the source’s weight and volume signs.
6. **Bott pairing (R.4).** Burgos Theorem 4.24 and Remark 4.25, p.32 distinguish the Hurewicz factor `(j−1)!` from the reciprocal factor in suspended `ch_j`. The pairing with the Bott homotopy generator is one signed Tate unit `(2πi)^j`. Corrected the proof, API and RT.4 suspension request; the existing `ch₃`/Chern-class test is retained.
7. **Division-algebra existence (R.6).** A bare prime-congruence theorem does not provide cyclotomic irreducibility. Imported Chebotarev Layer 7.1’s auxiliary prime, applied to F/F, and Layer 7.2’s full cyclotomic degree before applying the pinned automorphism equivalence. Layers 5/10 of ClassFieldTheory are now separated into finite local invariants versus global/archimedean terms. The two inert finite places, period/index proof and archimedean splitting are unchanged mathematically.
8. **Compact-dual injection (R.7).** Reconciled Burgos’s conjugate-diagonal compact inclusion with the diagonal coordinate by conjugating the first factor. The quotient map is then literally `(M,M′)↦M⁻¹M′`, with section `u↦(1,u)`. This proves injectivity on all cohomology; no unspecified transpose sign is needed. The coordinate change does not rescale the regulator classes.
9. **Bloch–Wigner scalar (R.7).** P.2 supplies D and its cocycle, not the asserted rational Borel comparison. Goncharov’s trace-class comparison supports universal nonzero real proportionality. Rationality after the `2πi` coordinate division requires a separate calculation and is no longer asserted. The exact real scalar, its sign and rational/π factors remain a gap. Extended the AF.1a request to the natural continuous/measurable comparison and supplied the additional source locators. This does not change Burgos’s independently proved all-weight factor two.
10. **Source-issue argument.** E2’s old K₂(ℤ)-injection assertion did not follow from vanishing of residue-field K₂, because a preceding K₃ boundary is present. Replaced it with `K₂(ℚ)↠⊕_p𝔽_p×`, using injectivity of `K₁(ℤ)→K₁(ℚ)`. E1, E2 and E3 are independently confirmed under this job; E2/E3 remain scoped to public author copies, not the uncollated printed book.
