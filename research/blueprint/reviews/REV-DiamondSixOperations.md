## Remaining acceptance blockers and revision instructions

A revision must keep the corrected mathematical packet and make its suggested signatures and tests faithful. Merely adding unconstrained predicates with names resembling the missing hypotheses would not solve this.

1. **Identify actual supplier types and coefficients.** SupplierContext does not identify its V, D, primes, coefficient rings, tensor/unit/internal-Hom or t-structure with the requested geometric/derived suppliers. The primitive finite-dimension predicates are unrelated to the newly added numeric invariant, whose final carrier should also handle the empty-object convention. Use faithful supplier-backed types/interfaces with the required laws. The current global defect affects every generic theorem claimed under this interface.
2. **Repair S1 classification and continuity.** The classification needs compact Hausdorff spaces **over π₀X**, the specified product functor and full equivalence. The topological comparison needs actual sheaves on |Y|, bounded-below derived categories and essential surjectivity. Continuity needs qcqs diamonds and the **canonical** transition maps/cocone. Specifying only objects permits a zero cocone with the right vertex, for which IsColimit is false.
3. **Express coherent S2 data and universal properties.** A hypercover must contain its augmented simplicial diagram, matching-cover conditions and stages. The support diagram needs actual fibres, coCartesian edges and relative adjoints. Replace the tautological fibre predicate and comparisons unrelated to H. State both existence and uniqueness of the left Kan extension, preserve the quasiseparated hypotheses, and state equality of the canonical base-change/composition pastings, not just existence of some isomorphism.
4. **Restore S3 coefficient and trace APIs.** Ring changes require both rings, their map and scalar functors. Finite étale trace needs summation and degree normalization; twist compatibility must be a commuting diagram of the specified transformations.
5. **Retain all S4 criteria and coefficients.** The source has four criteria, arbitrary sums, and open comparisons over connected Spa(C,C⁺) components. The practical criterion includes the open-extension condition (iii). The prototype drops that condition and fixes arbitrary coefficients instead of F_ℓ. The degree API needs a genuine locally constant function with a nonzero coefficient hypothesis.
6. **Name the ball, twist and quotient action.** Ball.app currently quantifies any `plus : V → Type`; taking plus empty at T=B contradicts the identity map. Name perfectoid pairs and O⁺ instead. Tate twist needs μ_n and classical comparison. SpdQp currently quantifies an arbitrary V-object and thus asserts smoothness for every object. The geometric-base criterion permits an arbitrary, even empty, ballOpens set. Identify these objects and test families.
7. **Repair averaging and quotient tests.** The averaging input needs K, its continuous action, pro-order/indices, the actual image-relation quotient and normalized Haar trace. Eligibility alone cannot split the unit: the empty map to a nonempty geometric point would split Λ → 0. The free quotient theorem cannot replace a prime-to-ℓ action with mere local sections. The nonfree theorem must use the paper's geometric-fibre hypothesis, not assume smoothness after all strictly totally disconnected base changes. Supply a distribution-sheaf computation rather than assuming a failure of sum preservation.
8. **Restore the S6 base and duality APIs.** Spa(C,C⁺) with C⁺≠O_C is itself strictly totally disconnected and gives the packet's own 25.2/25.5 counterexamples. Thus the prototype positive theorems with only hgeom=strictlyTotallyDisconnected are false. Name complete algebraically closed C and **O_C**, distinguish F_ℓ and perfect-constructible torsion cases, and express the actual duality/global-sections/extension-by-zero comparisons. closedPoints_detect_zero currently repeats a baseline lemma assuming Jacobson; it does not establish the required geometry or sheaf vanishing.
9. **Provide real unit-test signatures.** All 19 definitions/constructions have at least three mathematical tests in the packet, but comment-only names or examples that assume the desired obstruction do not meet §13. Several tests only restate an API under its full conclusion-like hypotheses. The added unequal-shift and normalized finite-trace tests should become concrete supplied examples. Existing comment-only examples include finite cyclic/ℤ_p Haar, point/affinoid ball and twist comparisons, the nonfree/pro-ℓ obstruction and valued-plus duality examples.
10. **Synchronize the reader in the revision issue.** This review issue permits editing only packet, suggested file and report (plus its handoff). Accordingly `research/blueprint/readmes/DiamondSixOperations.md` is not edited here. Its affected statements must be updated for local dimension bounds, nonzero/empty hypotheses, coefficient reduction, finite averaging normalization, the corrected source-issue verdicts and new supplier gaps. The C4/D3 ownership routing should be preserved.
