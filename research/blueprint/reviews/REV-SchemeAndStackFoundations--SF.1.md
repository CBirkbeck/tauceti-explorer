## Main corrections and their mathematical reasons

1. **Descent and universes.** General finite locally free descent cannot be proved by intersecting Galois translates. The corrected orbit-neighbourhood argument uses [Stacks 39.25.2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0CCI), 39.24.1 and 39.25.3, with the ModularCurves 0C affine quotient input. For quasi-finite descent, the stable neighbourhood is quasi-compact because the covering map is quasi-compact; an fppf covering need not have finite fibres. [37.57.1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02W8) uses quasi-affineness [37.43.2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02LR) and effective quasi-affine descent [35.38.1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0247), now explicit gaps. A universe convention does not prove the uniform atlas-size bound in 0ADV.
2. **Local properties and topology.** Separate source and target locality needs the open-immersion postcomposition hypothesis of [35.32.6](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/04QW). The absolute diagonal comparison uses the base scheme diagonal. Space points and stack points now have separate nodes, avoiding a stack construction whose prerequisites only supplied spaces. Base change of scheme charts need not stay a scheme for arbitrary maps of spaces; small-etale inverse image must be constructed through the enlarged site and its comparison equivalence.
3. **Groupoids and torsors.** An associative arrow multiplication, identity arrows and a map interchanging endpoints do not constitute a groupoid without unit and inverse laws. Those laws are now actual Lean fields. Torsor triviality requires an equivariant isomorphism; sheaf torsors and already representable torsors are separate, making the representability theorem substantive. The Frobenius fibre over 1 has a section, so the diagnostic instead uses s^p=u over F_p(u). H1 comparisons require the actual multiplicative group or an isomorphism of group objects. For commutative torsor addition use the anti-diagonal action. Left frame and inner-form inverse conventions are fixed.
4. **Quotients.** Geometric quotients need not be categorical among all algebraic spaces. [Rydh](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.3333v2), Definition 2.2 and Remark 2.8 pp. 6–8, Theorem 3.16 p. 18, supplies categoricality for a strongly geometric universally open quotient. The false suggested implication and incomplete predicate are removed. Rydh 4.1/4.4/4.7 pp. 19–22 supplies the nonfree finite-group argument; the free-equivalence-relation theorem is only its special case.
5. **Stacks and moduli.** Stackification has an equivalence of Hom groupoids, not unqualified unique 2-isomorphisms. Isom spaces being locally of finite type (101.3.1, 101.5.1) supplies the inertia argument; the locally finite type diagonal theorem is another valid route. The line-bundle groupoid is the core of invertible sheaves. The root-stack DM criterion requires a DM base; a unit section over BG_m is a counterexample without it. The empty moduli functor is the initial sheaf, singleton at the empty test scheme. A universal object over an algebraic space M is a morphism h_M -> X, with evaluation X(M) requiring descent extension.
6. **Quasi-coherent pushforward.** Arbitrary groupoid QCoh descent uses [96.13.2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/06WR), invariance under stackification. Unrestricted big-fppf pushforward need not preserve quasi-coherence, even for the punctured-plane open immersion. [Stacks 103.11](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/070A), Proposition 103.11.1 and Lemma 103.11.2, gives the different quasi-coherent right adjoint and its flat comparison. The corrected packet records that construction and its required comparison as a gap rather than identifying the two functors.
7. **Coarse and tame spaces.** The general finite-inertia Keel–Mori target uses Rydh Theorem 6.12 p. 28, with the AF presentation and fixed-point-reflecting descent of 6.5/6.10/6.11 pp. 25–28; Conrad Theorem 1.1 pp. 1–2 is a comparison with additional diagonal hypotheses. Tame flatness goes from the stack to its coarse space. The [AOV author corrigendum](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/aif.2869.pdf), p. 945, requires the cotangent-complex obstruction for nonreduced stabilizers. The original Lie-algebra-only step is invalid. Residual gerbes, stabilizer extension, Artin approximation and the 3.7 chart argument remain key inputs.
8. **Galois-gerb interfaces.** Semilinear automorphisms precede Galois gerbs; the backwards prerequisite is removed. Infinite continuous Galois descent needs a finite-dimensional orbit-span and finite-model theorem before taking invariants. In the conjugator proof, only kernel equations cut out the base-changed transporter; lifts give its descent action. In neutral coordinates that action is a_2(tau) tau(h) a_1(tau)^-1, applying tau once. Crossed-module APIs now include tensor on arrows and the conjugation and map functors, with Kisin 3.2.1 p. 40 as the correct locator.
