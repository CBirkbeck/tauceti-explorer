- **E27 confirmed:** the accepted manuscript's residual-induction and coefficient-extension steps require justification. The selfdual lattice repairs actual induction over the normalized field; E76 separately tracks normalization.
- **E31 confirmed as a proof elaboration boundary:** ANT indicates the arbitrary-d substitution. The corrected plan spells out the needed invariant, cotangent and equivariant patching contracts. This is not a claim that the theorem is false.
- **E74 confirmed:** the published ANT erratum removes generic reducedness of the trivial-character special fibre; Proposition 3.1 supplies the nilreduction repair. Added the localized component-use locator, Lemma 3.40(2).
- **E75 confirmed:** no-order-l quotients of the full image do not imply the analogous cyclotomic-subgroup condition. The dihedral example tests that implication. The scalar Frobenius repairs the arithmetic proof bridge; it gives no counterexample to the theorem.

No descent from the complete arithmetic setup was established here. The corrected theorem requires primitivity over the normalized field, geometrically, or under the all-subgroup splitting-field convention.
