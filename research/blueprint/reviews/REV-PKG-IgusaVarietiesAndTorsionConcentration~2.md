## Required correction: distinguish the two similitude Hecke algebras

The source uses the symbol 𝕋^S for two different rings:

- In Caraiani–Scholze, [arXiv:1909.01898v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.01898v2), §2.1, pp.11–12, it is the **subalgebra generated by T_{i,v}** inside the full spherical similitude Hecke algebra. Function restriction to G⁰ gives the map to 𝕋^{0,S}. Lemma 2.1.1, p.12, gives cohomological equivariance for that map.
- In the same edition, §5.1, p.64, it is the **full spherical similitude Hecke algebra**, used for the trace-comparison argument.

The accepted IG.0 datum explicitly retains the first definition. In `Suggested.lean`, `HeckeAlgebra` at line 252 instead takes Δ to be the whole restricted-product group. This is a correct native carrier for the second ring. But `UnitarySimilitudeDatum.heckeRestrict` at line 585 has domain that same full carrier and is declared as a unital ring homomorphism. There is no operator subalgebra, support condition or inclusion adapter in its type.

### A concrete obstruction

Choose one prime q outside S split in E. The split description is

$$G(\mathbb Q_q)\simeq\mathbb Q_q^\times\times\prod_{w\mid\mathfrak q}\mathrm{GL}_{2n}(F_w),\qquad
K_q\simeq\mathbb Z_q^\times\times\prod_{w\mid\mathfrak q}\mathrm{GL}_{2n}(\mathcal O_{F_w}).$$

Let g have first coordinate q and identity in every GL factor. It is central in this product and normalizes K_q. In the full integral convolution ring, normalized so that the K_q basis element is the identity, put U=[K_qgK_q] and V=[K_qg⁻¹K_q]. Then UV=1.

Every element in the support of U has similitude valuation 1; every element in the support of V has valuation −1. Neither support meets G⁰(ℚ_q), whose similitude is 1. Literal restriction of functions therefore sends both U and V to zero, but sends 1 to the nonzero identity of the unitary spherical algebra. Consequently

$$\operatorname{res}(UV)=1\ne0=\operatorname{res}(U)\operatorname{res}(V).$$

Use this as a non-example test for the algebra interface. The T_{i,v} of §2.1 have similitude coordinate in ℤ_q^×, so this obstruction does not invalidate the source's map on their subalgebra. Checking only the images of T_{i,v} misses the error. An abstract ring map from the full ring, obtained by assigning the extra invertible similitude variable a value, is a different construction; it does not establish the function-restriction or cohomological comparison asserted here.
