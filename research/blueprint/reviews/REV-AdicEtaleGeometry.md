## Corrections: the checkpoint's findings

Each finding was re-derived before it was applied.

1. **F1, `A3/perturbation-of-generators`.** Part (c) must use the inverse substitutes, θ_ε^{−1}(Fᵢ) = Fᵢ(X + ε′), where
   θ_ε^{−1} = θ_{ε′}. The reason is that ker(π ∘ θ_ε) = θ_ε^{−1}(ker π). The Jacobian identity is now stated with ε′.
   - Check: for evaluation at 0 moved by X ↦ X + 3 over ℚ₃, the forward substitute X + 3 maps to 6, not 0. This is now a test.
   - Downstream: `A3/integral-cutoff-graph-presentation` used the forward substitute and is corrected.
2. **F2, same node.** The dilation witness was wrong: X = image of ϖ^{−1}X. The correct witness is Σ ϖᵐXᵐ, whose only candidate
   preimage, Σ Xᵐ, is not restricted.
3. **F3, `A3/restricted-power-series-newton`.** The iteration with the Jacobian frozen at t converges linearly. For F = T² − 4 over ℚ₃,
   the error satisfies e_{n+1} = e_n(3 − e_n/2), so v₃(F(t_n)) = n + 1. The proof step now proves the linear bound, which is enough for
   existence and uniqueness. The regression test is added.
4. **F4, source issue E7.** Rejected; see below. The two A1 nodes whose hypotheses cited E7's argument are reworded:
   - `A1/pro-etale-site-corrected`;
   - `A1/corrected-covers-pretopology`.

   The packet's reading keeps finite étale steps pulled back from X_ét. It is kept because it makes the erratum's "for countable limits,
   the definitions are unchanged" immediate. It is not presented as forced.
5. **F5, `A3/conormal-description`.** The global step inferred pseudocoherence of I/I² = I ⊗ O(V) from the base-change item, which needs
   Tor vanishing. Tor vanishing fails even for regular embeddings: Tor₁^R(R/I, I) ≅ R/I for R = ℂ_p⟨T₁, T₂⟩ and I = (T₁, T₂). The step
   now argues as follows.
   1. I is finitely generated, being stably pseudocoherent, so I/I² is finitely generated by right exactness alone.
   2. δ maps onto a free module and therefore splits. Its kernel K is finitely generated.
   3. Kiehl gluing for the pseudocoherent ideal sheaf gives I(V′_β) = I ⊗ O(V′_β), which identifies (I/I²) ⊗ O(V_β) with the local
      conormal module.
   4. So K ⊗ O(V_β) = 0 on every piece of the covering, and Nakayama gives K = 0.

   The prerequisite `A3/pseudocoherent-kiehl-gluing` is added, and `A3/fpd-local-to-global` is no longer needed.

## Corrections: further errors found in this review

6. **`A3/tubular-neighbourhood` is false in the stated generality.** It allowed any e ∈ O⁺(R)^N on a rational R ⊆ B^N_X. The Newton step
   needs integral Taylor coefficients, which an element of O⁺(R) involving the rational coordinates of R does not have.
   - **Counterexample.** Take A = ℚ_p (p odd), R = {|T| ≤ |p|}, e = (T/p)², k = 0 and m = 2. Then Δ = 2T/p², and the tube is
     W = {|T| = |p|²}. It is nonempty, and e is a unit on it, so C = 0 while O(W) ≠ 0.
   - **Fix.** The hypothesis is now e ∈ A⁺⟨T⟩^N, restricted to R. The counterexample is a test. The Lean core
     `exists_section_tube_sousperfectoid` already assumed this.
   - **Consumers.** Every consumer satisfies the corrected hypothesis once the following changes are made:
     - `A3/fs-iv-4-13-regular-sequence` is restated;
     - in `A3/integral-cutoff-graph-presentation`, the graph tube of v − w is taken over the base O(R″), where its equations are integral
       power series in v, and T − G(v) is rescaled into A⁺⟨T, v⟩.
7. **`A3/local-equations-near-closed-subspace`.** It used a tube with k = 0 around e ∈ I ⊆ O(V′) for a general rational V′, which relies
   on the false generality of item 6.
   - Its only consumer, `A3/global-standard-etale-presentation`, takes V′ = B^N_X, where e ∈ A⟨T⟩. The node is restricted to that case.
   - The equations are rescaled to ϖᵃe ∈ A⁺⟨T⟩, and W₀ is a tube with exponent k = aN and radius |ϖ|^{2k+1}.
   - The rest of the argument (the idempotent step) is unchanged, and so is the Lean comment's `tubeSet`, apart from the exponent.
8. **`A3/natural-topology-strict-exactness` (d).** It said that every finitely generated submodule of a complete, finitely generated
   module is closed.
   - Kedlaya–Liu II, Corollary 1.2.11 assumes that the completion N̂ is finitely generated. The hat is lost in the PDF's text layer, so this
     is not a source error.
   - **Counterexample.** In C(ℤ_p, ℚ_p), take g(x) = p^{⌊v_p(x)/2⌋}. It is a uniform limit of elements of x·C(ℤ_p, ℚ_p), but g/x is
     unbounded, so the principal ideal x·C(ℤ_p, ℚ_p) is not closed.
   - **Fix.** The statement, its source `match`, a new test and the Lean theorem `finite_submodule_isClosed` (hypothesis: the closure is a
     finitely generated submodule) are corrected. Tau Ceti's `isClosed_of_module_finite_topologicalClosure` has exactly the corrected
     hypothesis.
   - **Consumer.** `A3/restricted-power-series-pro-projective` (b) used the false form. It now uses strictness of Aᵏ → N, as in Kedlaya–Liu
     II, Remark 1.2.3.
9. **`A1/finite-etale-strongly-sheafy`.** In the spreading-out step, the rational neighbourhood U must also lie in the open and closed
   locus where B has rank r. Otherwise O(U)[T]/(χ_b) → B ⊗ O(U) need not be an isomorphism on U.
10. **Grammar.** Three statements began "Let X is"; they now read "Let X be":
    - `A1/pro-etale-morphism`;
    - `A1/pro-etale-site-corrected`;
    - `A1/pro-finite-etale-site-corrected`.

**E7: rejected.** Its reason assumes that the closed unit disc over an algebraically closed complete field of characteristic 0 has no
nontrivial finite étale covers. That is false:

- ℂ_p⟨T⟩ → ℂ_p⟨U⟩, T ↦ U^p − U, is finite étale of degree p, since pU^{p−1} − 1 is a unit;
- it is connected, since ℂ_p⟨U⟩ is a domain.

So the record does not show that the Kummer steps over the annulus fail the X_fét form of the erratum's condition. This is the checkpoint's
F4, confirmed here. It does not show that the packet's reading is wrong.

**E21: added.** The proof of Kedlaya–Liu I, Proposition 8.2.20 (p. 164) says "where Zij is finite étale and Yij is an open immersion". It
means the morphisms Zij → Y and Yij → Zij.

- This misprint is already corrected in Kedlaya–Liu II, Appendix A. It affects nothing.
- It is recorded because `A1/etale-covering-reduction` cites that proof.
- It was found by comparing that appendix with the packet's Kedlaya–Liu I citations.
