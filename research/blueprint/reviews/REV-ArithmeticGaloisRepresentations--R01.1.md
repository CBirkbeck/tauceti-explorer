## Corrections and dependency boundaries

- Added a fourth determinant check on the nonfree projective ideal `(1,0)(F₃×F₃)`. Its local ranks are one and zero. The generator acting by negation has projective determinant `(−1,1)`; Mathlib’s finite-basis determinant fallback is `1`. Free examples alone would miss this wrong definition.
- The dual-lattice scaling test now requires a nonzero scalar and explicitly uses a uniformizer to distinguish direct from inverse scaling. Scaling by a unit need not distinguish the two lattices. The prototype checks both the general identity and a discriminating one-dimensional instance.
- A stable subspace over a Hausdorff topological field gets its module topology by a linear splitting. The pinned finite-dimensional closedness theorem requires a complete nontrivially normed field and cannot supply this general statement. Replaced that prerequisite and added an actual typed `submodule_moduleTopology` API theorem. Removed coarse parent-semisimplification prerequisites from subspaces, quotients, composition factors and their direct-sum semisimplification, so these construction interfaces assemble in the proper order.
- BHKT Lemma 10.5 uses algebraic induction and Frobenius reciprocity. It does not identify a compact-open topology with Mathlib’s continuous coinduction. Corrected that inherited source attribution in all three topology contracts and the induction carrier. Their finite-transversal proof remains valid. `assembly.sourceOverrides` records this correction for the later parent assembly.
- The retained Brauer–Nesbitt catalogue claimed a local proof avoiding determinant theory, despite the refinement’s explicit IHG route. Its algebraically closed comparison now imports the owned Amitsur/reconstruction contracts. Arbitrary imperfect-field multiplicity descent remains the separating-idempotent argument; the perfect-field refinement uses preservation of semisimplicity and Noether–Deuring on the simultaneous image algebra. These are different descent routes, with their hypotheses retained.
- Reconciled the retained reductive integral proof and its prerequisite list with the existing RG2.3 compact-subgroup contract. Explained residual independence using the **entire invariant family on tuples** `f∈Z[Ĝ^m]^Ĝ`, rather than leaving `tr ρ` ambiguous. The missing general reductive reconstruction export remains at IHG.1.
- Current Tau Ceti already implements the inertia/Frobenius kernel and coset assertions, scalar-extension continuity, and exterior-power base-change comparison. Recorded the exact imports for packaging at an updated pin. There is no new local programme for these implementations.

These are errors or ambiguities in the plan’s attribution and interfaces, not newly discovered errors in the papers. `sourceIssues` remains empty. The confirmed finding `RT-AREA-langlands-1/17` is addressed: lattice independence has explicit IHG.0 Amitsur and IHG.1 reconstruction edges, perfect-field scalar extension and finite-image-algebra descent. Equality of traces with an unrecorded dimension bound is never substituted for equality of full characteristic polynomials.
