## Mathematical corrections and required reader synchronization

1. In target 1, `StandardNgon.smoothPoints`, replace units times one `Z/nZ` label over arbitrary R by units times locally constant functions from `Spec R` to `Z/nZ`. A point of a constant group scheme is a locally constant component label. For R=F₂×F₂ and n=2 there are four smooth points; the old product has two. For a nonempty connected spectrum constant labels suffice. The Lean carrier now uses native `LocallyConstant (PrimeSpectrum R) (ZMod n)`. The old scaling/inversion fragments are renamed `constantLabelScale`/`constantLabelInversion` on `constantLabelPoints`; their general componentwise gluing and scheme naturality are explicitly omitted. Add the disconnected-ring control to the reader.
2. In the same target, use the cyclic coequalizer identifying 0 and infinity sections. Split `xy=0` charts are étale local, including the one-gon. An irreducible one-gon over an algebraically closed field has no reducible Zariski neighborhood of its node, whereas the crossing model does. Replace the Zariski chart-gluing sketch and unrestricted chart API, retain two relative-normalization flags, and add the irreducibility control. The original geometric coequalizer statement remains correct (Česnavičius Definition 2.1.1, p.6).
3. In target 5, require **Deligne–Rapoport semistability**: fibres smooth connected genus one or Néron polygons, equivalently connected nodal genus one with trivial dualizing bundle. General semistable genus-one curves may have rational tails. An elliptic core joined to P¹, with D on the tail, has dualizing multidegrees 1 and −1 and H¹(O(mD)) of dimension one for m>0. The original vanishing sketch therefore does not cover that input. Apply the restriction to the statement, `GenusOneCurve.contractDivisor`, bare-divisor example and StableReduction request. Add the tail control. Conrad Definition 2.1.2, p.4, and DR IV.1.1–1.2, DeRa63–64 (printed collected pp.205–206), confirm the intended domain.
4. In target 14, include singular generic objects in the properness argument. After a finite extension a form of the n-gon becomes standard; the constant polygon extends it. The smooth generic branch still needs G-trait-model. This is existence after extension, the correct stack valuative criterion.

All four existing findings now have this review's explicit confirmed verdict and reason:

- **E1:** [Olsson Remark 2.1.6, p.299](https://library.slmath.org/books/Book59/files/65olsson.pdf). On the real elliptic curve y²=x(x−1)(x+1), P=(0,0) lies in the nonidentity real component, outside 2E(R). O(2O) and O(O+P) induce the same degree-two polarization but admit no real translating point. Equality gives an A∨ section; a global translate additionally requires membership in λ(A(S)). Fppf local translation remains valid.
- **E2:** [Conrad author copy dated 18 July 2006, p.5](https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/papers/kmpaper.pdf). The displayed y²z=x³−x²z has tangent cone y²+x², nonsplit over R and repeated in characteristic two. The split replacement y²z+xyz=x³ has branches u=0,−1 in the stated normalization, distinct in every characteristic. This finding concerns the accessed author copy; an unread journal artifact is not called erroneous.
- **E3:** [Kass, Lemma 1 proof, p.2](https://people.ucsc.edu/~jelkass/files/AbelianSchemes.pdf). The final η pushforward has the wrong sheaf domain. η_*O_S is the well-typed conclusion after π_*O_X≃O_S.
- **E4:** [Česnavičius arXiv v2 §3.2.1, p.19](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.07475v2), repeated in [the published paragraph, p.2025](https://msp.org/ant/2017/11-9/ant-v11-n9-p02-s.pdf). The retained-component open is inside E^sm. With a subgroup meeting every component, the identity contraction disproves an interpretation of the complement inside the whole proper singular curve. This is an ambient-locus clarification, not a failure of the contraction theorem.
