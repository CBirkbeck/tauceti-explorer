## Two counterexample checks

**Finding 2, uncompleted perfection.** Let `B = Z_p[[q−1]]`, `d = [p]_q`, `φ(q)=q^p`, and `M = ∏_{k≥1} B/(φ^k(d))`. Form the square-zero ring `A = B ⊕ M` and the Frobenius lift `φ_A(b,m) = (φ_B(b), p σ(m))`, where σ shifts coordinate k to k+1 through the φ-semilinear induced map, with first coordinate zero. The square-zero formula is multiplicative. The factors are p-torsionfree cyclotomic integer rings, so `(φ_A(a)−a^p)/p` defines a δ-structure. They are `(p,d)`-complete, d acts regularly on every factor, and their reductions modulo d are killed by p. These properties hold uniformly on the product: `(A,(d))` is a bounded prism.

At colimit stage j≥1 let e_j be the unit in coordinate j of M. The image of the initial d is `φ^j(d)`, which kills e_j. After i further transitions its image is `p^i e_{j+i}`, nonzero in the p-torsionfree factor. Hence the class survives in `colim_φ A` while being killed by d. This disproves the uncompleted regularity clause. Lemma 2.34 requires p-adic separatedness; apply it after p-completion instead. The completed perfection theorem is not disproved.

**Finding 18, unrestricted global tensors.** For an infinite disjoint union of copies of `Spec F_p`, base extension to `F_p[x]` sends `(∏ F_p) ⊗ F_p[x]` to `∏ F_p[x]`; the sequence `(x^n)` has no finite uniform polynomial span and is outside its image. The p-complete version follows after reduction modulo p. For the Frobenius variant take infinitely many variables: a finite span over the image of Frobenius cannot contain the coordinate sequence `(x_n)`, as distinct p-basis monomials remain independent. These are failures of the unrestricted global tensor assertion, not the sheaf comparison. Preserve a proved qcqs or otherwise sufficient global-descent hypothesis.
