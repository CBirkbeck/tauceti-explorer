## Corrections made in place

1. **Remove an upstream duplicate.** `function-field-fundamental-equality` is planned by [AlgebraicCurves Layer 6](https://github.com/CBirkbeck/tauceti-explorer/blob/85e12faced69f184b09205c0a5abed1cabdd7594/research/blueprint/atlas/roadmaps/tauceti_TauCetiRoadmap_AlgebraicCurves.json), explicitly without perfectness and with inseparable normalization in Layer 2. It is removed from route 3. The pin's function-field wrapper is separable, but that does not make the general theorem unowned. The algebra identity requires finite flatness and a rank comparison; these adapters are now explicit.
2. **Repair the niceness formula in characteristic two.** The former test used `v(4a_1a_0)` and marked dyadic by noninvertibility of 2 alone. Both fail in characteristic two. Dyadic now requires characteristic zero. For local isotropy, scale the four-variable nonsingular form to have integral coefficients, choose a primitive integral vector and a nonzero partial derivative, and require the strict derivative Hensel inequality. The written converse uses density in a rank-one henselization. This works in equal characteristic two and at dyadic places. Real-ordering tests are used only for number fields.
3. **Close the alteration interface.** I read [ILO Exposé X](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1207.3648), the prime-to-degree definition and Theorem 2.4 with proof 2.6 (printed pp.148,152–155). The extraction now specifies the excellent one-dimensional base, allowed odd-degree extension of its fraction field and normalization of the base. The projective regular output does not promise a separable function-field extension. `odd-degree-injectivity` therefore factors into a separable part and a purely inseparable part; the latter uses [topological invariance of the small étale site](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/04DY) for prime-to-characteristic coefficients. No wild-coefficient invariance is inferred.
4. **Credit existing carriers.** The combined finite-generation and constant-field items explicitly consume `IntermediateField.FG`, `Field.fg_iff_essFiniteType` and `algebraicClosure`. Only their extra DP dictionaries/classification remain new. The coarsening item consumes `ValuationSubring.ofPrime`, its localization instance and unit criterion; its explicit prime-ideal proof is now given. These supporting imports do not falsely mark the larger downstream items wholly built.
5. **Tighten statements.** The CRT generator argument distinguishes every nonidentity embedding from the chosen prime, without claiming that all other induced primes are pairwise distinct. The coefficient-specialization statement now requires every residue coordinate to be nonzero. The strong-approximation consumer explicitly omits a finite place. Baseline declaration anchors now identify the actual statements, with per-name locations for multi-declaration entries.
6. **Independently dispose of all source findings.** Sixteen are confirmed; E18 is rejected as a new mathematical error while its useful low-dimensional adapter is retained. E8 now has a version/erratum search for its actual Kuhlmann–Novacoski source. E17's proposed repair is updated as described above. Historical extraction prose is marked as such and the current verdicts are explicit.

The structured `verification.independentReview.changes` records each changed item field. No item ID was deleted, no extra roadmap was proposed, and no claim of library implementation was added.
