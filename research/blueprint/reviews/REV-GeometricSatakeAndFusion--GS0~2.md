## Corrections in this review

1. **Demazure recursion.** In BS Proposition 7.11 and its proof, pp.29–30, choose a locally free quotient `Q/pQ→G` of rank `n_λ(0)` and recurse on `ker(Q→G)`. The previous proof step treated `Q/pQ` itself as that rank. For `λ=(2,1,0)` and `Q=k³` killed by `p`, it has rank three, whereas the chosen quotient has rank two. The kernel lines form the expected `P²` boundary fibre. Corrected the proof, the acceptance example, and the suggested-file construction comment; the submodule-chain core remains explicitly partial.
2. **Baseline hypotheses and carrier scope.** `PerfectRing R p` is bijectivity of the power map on a type with powers; prime `p`, a commutative ring and `CharP R p` are additional assumptions in the geometric uses. Tau Ceti’s scheme `InvertibleSheaf` supplies the Witt determinant-line carrier after scheme representability; it does not supply the adic Cartier line on the divisor. Corrected these two descriptions, retaining both valid citations.
3. **Erratum identifiers.** The finite-rank repair at BS Lemma 7.7 p.29 is `PAPER-BHATT-SCHOLZE-17/E37`; the boundary-representability gap in the projectivity proof pp.35–36 is `PAPER-BHATT-SCHOLZE-17/E39`. Qualified the two proof-step references and the SF1 request, so they cannot be mistaken for nonexistent local packet findings.
4. **Collision degree, E24.** In the exact author-hosted FS PDF, p.202’s geometric-point description adds multiplicity weighting to local cocharacters that already combine colliding ordered legs. Its opening ordered-label sum and Lemma VI.3.2 pp.203–204 instead use ordinary total local position. Restricted the local orbit formula `LU·λ(t)` to a one-leg geometric fibre with primitive equation `t`; general total-weight strata are unions of products whose distinct-support labels sum to that weight. Corrected the constant-term node’s convention, statement, proof and acceptance example, the reader’s conventions, and the suggested-file comment. At two coincident `G_m` legs with labels `(1,0)`, the lattice `tB⁺` has position one, while the product Cartier equation is `t²`; weighting the sole local cocharacter again gives two. This is a descriptive misprint with the intended mathematics unchanged. The finding is limited to that author-copy hash, without claiming an arXiv-PDF or Astérisque comparison.
5. **Evidence and history.** Added current individual source-issue verdicts, exact-pin baseline review evidence and current source-version review metadata. Preserved the previous top-level verdict and prior source reviews in history. Reconciled equivalent node/source-assertion wording and narrowed E7/E17 counterexample explanations to the checked versions, removing inherited first-person and unread-version claims. Updated the full-file compilation limit and distinguished the current projection from earlier historical checks throughout the reader and packet gap.
