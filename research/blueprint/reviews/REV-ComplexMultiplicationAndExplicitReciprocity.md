The principal mathematical corrections are:

- **Primitive and reflex scope.** Milne Definition 1.8 and Proposition 1.9 concern CM fields. General CM-algebra induction remains available, but a product algebra has no single primitive core of the asserted field kind. The trace reflex field of an algebra uses a common Galois CM field containing all images of its field factors; the product algebra itself does not embed in that field. The inverse-coset reflex type and type-product identities are now explicitly field-scoped. The corresponding primitive-core and stabilizer API statements were corrected.
- **Polarization comparison.** With `alpha(fx)=sigma(x)`, the comparison map scales the pairing by `chi_cyc/(f*fbar)`. The natural Galois map scales it by `chi_cyc`. The changed lattice and polarization parameter retain `fI` and `xi*chi_cyc/(f*fbar)`. The proof now derives the comparison multiplier by changing the input, and the suggested-file comment fixes the same convention.
- **Reflex-order discriminant.** Yuan–Zhang §2.2 defines an order over the reflex integers and its relative trace-discriminant ideal. For a quadratic CM field the selected factor is the field itself, and the first tensor factor supplies every element of its integers. Thus the reflex image is the maximal order and its relative discriminant is the unit ideal even for the input order `Z+3Zi` of absolute discriminant −36. The packet's acceptance properties and third test now distinguish these discriminants. The Lean test uses the actual tensor-image shape and an ideal-valued relative discriminant, with the GN11 identifications explicitly omitted.

The corrected contract uses Endo v2 Corollary 4 for every `r^k | v`, `r>3`: with `j=nu_r(v)-k+1`, compare orders of discriminants `D1=(v/r^j)^2*D_K` and `D2=r^(2k)*D_K`, with verified counts `c1>c2`. The curve decision is `cA<c1` iff `r^k` divides the claimed conductor. Exact valuations at 2 and 3 come from certified climbing; Proposition 5 guarantees separating relations only for `r>3`. The verifier proof now determines all valuations. The suggested record, relation API and forged-relation test were corrected; a fourth test distinguishes `v=25,u=5` from conductor 25.
