## RT-AUDIT-03/1 — confirmed: Deleted Euler factors change the residue

Confirmed. For a finite deleted set S, write P_S(s) = product of (1 - (Norm p)^(-s)). Then L_1 = zeta_K P_S, so its residue is kappa_K P_S(1), not kappa_K unless S is empty. Each factor at 1 is positive and less than one. Mathlib's positive real one-sided residue already refutes the claimed equality; no missing complex continuation is assumed for that argument. The rational norm-2 factor changes residue 1 to 1/2. Keep the logarithmic-derivative normalization: -L_1'/L_1 = -zeta_K'/zeta_K - P_S'/P_S has residue 1, since the finite correction is analytic near 1. Correct the audit target and record the same upstream error for the maintainer; the missing continuation and boundary-continuity package stays missing. [Mathlib/NumberTheory/NumberField/DedekindZeta.lean:65](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/082e2d37e8b0463410cdb532e111cd43d5a66174/Mathlib/NumberTheory/NumberField/DedekindZeta.lean#L65); [Mathlib/NumberTheory/LSeries/RiemannZeta.lean:242](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/082e2d37e8b0463410cdb532e111cd43d5a66174/Mathlib/NumberTheory/LSeries/RiemannZeta.lean#L242); [content/tau-ceti/Chebotarev/README.md:497](https://github.com/CBirkbeck/tauceti-explorer/blob/1ba3304ed9e2a49a934653dad040f92e92dd2b4a/content/tau-ceti/Chebotarev/README.md#L497).

The audit's first Layer 12 target repeats the roadmap's “same residue” assertion. With 
\[
P_S(s)=\prod_{\mathfrak p\in S}(1-(N\mathfrak p)^{-s}),\qquad
\lim_{s\to1^+}(s-1)L_1(s)=\kappa_K\prod_{\mathfrak p\in S}(1-1/N\mathfrak p),
\]
the discrepancy follows from continuity of a finite product and positivity of the pinned real residue. The product is strictly between zero and one when S is nonempty. The analytic continuation statement, once constructed, has the same corrected complex residue. Its logarithmic derivative has principal part (1/(s-1)), so the intended density coefficient stays (1/\#\operatorname{Gal}(F/K)).

The convention is independently corroborated by [TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/EulerProduct/Analytic.lean:386](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/f790474821cf4256814db967cb154e7af3d0c369/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/EulerProduct/Analytic.lean#L386) (Euler factors are inverses of (1-N\mathfrak p^{-s})) and [TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/EulerProduct/Data.lean:208](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/f790474821cf4256814db967cb154e7af3d0c369/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ArithmeticDirichletSeries/EulerProduct/Data.lean#L208) (restriction removes coefficients supported at the deleted primes). Exact rational arithmetic gives (1-1/2=1/2); this is a sanity check, not a numerical approximation to the analytic limit.

## RT-AUDIT-03/2 — confirmed: Conditional rational form scaling

Confirmed, with the missing work kept small. The roadmap explicitly requires nonzero rational form scaling, while IntegralLattice.scale, signature_smul_of_neg and dualCarrier_smul quantify over integers. The rational unit on the right of the dual formula is not a rational action on bundled lattices. The integer API therefore cannot justify the combined target's exact/full coverage or the summary Nothing required is missing. Mark the combined target partial (or split it), Layer 1 partly built, and qualify the summary. Reuse IntegralLattice.ofSubmodule for the scaled form under the explicit integrality hypothesis and QuadraticForm's generic scalar-signature lemmas; do not plan a new carrier or rebuild integer scaling. On Z, (1/2)(2xy)=xy is integral, while (1/2)xy is not. No unconditional rational action is possible. [TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Scaling.lean:22](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/f790474821cf4256814db967cb154e7af3d0c369/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Scaling.lean#L22); [TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Scaling.lean:74](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/f790474821cf4256814db967cb154e7af3d0c369/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Scaling.lean#L74); [TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Dual/Scaling.lean:71](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/f790474821cf4256814db967cb154e7af3d0c369/TauCeti/LinearAlgebra/IntegralLattice/Dual/Scaling.lean#L71); [content/tau-ceti/Completed/IntegralLattices/README.md:212](https://github.com/CBirkbeck/tauceti-explorer/blob/1ba3304ed9e2a49a934653dad040f92e92dd2b4a/content/tau-ceti/Completed/IntegralLattices/README.md#L212).

A vector-scaling theorem such as `norm_smul` is a different operation.
