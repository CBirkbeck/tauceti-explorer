GeneralAlgebraicKTheory §§8.4–8.9 supplies them. 4. **Absolute K-space identifications.** Removed a duplicate π₀ comparison and absolute plus/K₁ equivalence. GeneralAlgebraicKTheory §§3.12–3.14 supplies the based zero-component comparisons under the projective nerve; §1.5 supplies coherent functorial plus maps. Layer 13 retains Euler/Cartan compatibility, transfer on automorphism loops, and the concrete relative matrices, paths and signs.
5. **Previously unresolved U.5 interfaces.** Replaced the four unspecified refinements with actual supplier sections and local proofs. The quotient-root normal-closure assertion follows from the root presentation in K2SymbolsBrauer T.1/T.6. StableHomotopyKTheory §§2.2, 2.8, 2.11–2.13 and 3.30 supply based fibres, pasting, the exact sequence and the plus-fibre universal central extension. GeneralAlgebraicKTheory supplies the under-nerve model and based plus square. No unprovided general fibre theorem is silently assumed.
6. **Actual categorical owner and symmetric comparison.** `EnhancedCategoryFoundations` does not exist in the roadmap/package inventory. Replaced it with EnhancedDerivedSheaves E0, E2, E3 and E5 and StableHomotopyKTheory §§4.29, 5.40–5.42. Added `projectiveGroupCompletion_spectrumComparison`: direct sum acts coherently on every iterated-S diagram, its associators and permutations are module isomorphisms, and group completion extends this E∞ map. The supplied underlying space comparison then yields an equivalence of connective spectra under the nerve. An arbitrary H-space equivalence is insufficient. GeneralAlgebraicKTheory §§5.7–5.11 supplies the iterated-S spectrum and Q comparison.
7. **Geometric duplication.** Removed a second general ample-family/resolution-property theorem and the former supported-Witt-input subsection. SchemeKTheoryOperations §1.6 owns the former; §3.8 owns Witt support, its actual α-map, supported continuity/completion and the full regular-noetherian-perfection comparison. Its §§4.10–4.11 already own perfect-complex v-descent and the sheafified first-truncation equivalence for α. These are opening contracts, with the original five Witt-input checks preserved there. Layer 12 keeps the curve-specific affine-diagonal and length-one-resolution comparisons; Layer 14 keeps graded descent, the determinant extension, its uniqueness with comparison homotopy, and filtration/finite-length calculations. The full regular-noetherian scope was preserved without assuming Witt vectors commute with filtered colimits.
8. **Generality and finite bounds.** Euler reduction and decomposition maps now work over every Dedekind base. The PID hypothesis remains on the surjective-decomposition criterion for generic extension to be an isomorphism; a nonprincipal Dedekind base disproves its removal. The cancellation argument for a residue comodule uses the exact sequence associated with vP⊂Q⊂P, rather than assuming the residue representation lifts. The tame formula requires m prime to the residue characteristic. The rank-two completion/metaplectic subsection states positive S-unit rank throughout. The retained new target is the continuous SL-to-SO deformation, now named `specialLinear_QR_deformation` with a normalized QR formula and a positive determinant denominator. All removed targets remain available through the opening contracts; their finite-size variants and explicit normalization checks were retained. The closing Lean inventory was updated to match this ownership. No upstream roadmap, packet, atlas data or unrelated job file was changed.
