## What prevents acceptance

1. The previous `complete` status counted numbered endpoints while expressly deferring the proofs of Fontaine–Messing, Yoshioka, twisted moduli, IHS Torelli, special-endomorphism lifting and lattice classification. PROTOCOL §§0,3,16 require those paths to be exposed, not deferred beyond the extraction’s completion claim. G3–G6 remain open.
2. G1 is reopened. Item50’s filtration calculation is useful, but the asserted existence of two original linear-system members with distinct tangent directions is not justified when that system is inseparable. A local repair is plausible: for a doubled curve, the quotient by `(x²,y²)` surjects onto the ambient first-jet quotient by `(x,y)²`. The global choice of curve and the degree-two function-field argument still need to be proved. This review does not certify the proposed constants as a completed proof.
3. Proposition3.17 is applied to twisted partners X_n, but the nonsuperspecial reduction was made for X. Item127 records the missing hypothesis transfer. Ogus’s crystalline discussion does not provide an independent shortcut via Picard rank22: the NS identification in Ex.1.10 is conditional on Tate. A proved crystalline comparison or a separate finiteness treatment of superspecial partners would close this gate; neither is supplied here.
4. Item105 still says “eligible” realization without specifying the coefficient prime, denominator prime and integral correspondence hypotheses. E25 also shows why the integral twisted lattice needs a norm restriction. These are mathematical interfaces, not notation cleanup.
5. Definition/construction APIs retain invalid roles (`integration`, `computation`, `transport`), numeric contract names, and `unitTests` without the required `tests`/`kind` structure. Some examples are useful; others restate generic properties without a fixture detecting a plausible wrong definition. G7 records the required revision. Passing the paper-v1 structural checker does not validate these semantics.
