## Corrections made in this review

1. **Determinant sign.** The substitution X₀=-T and the other variables equal to 1 already gives the characteristic polynomial. The extra factor (-1)^rank was removed from the packet's test and the Lean regression. The generic polyCharpoly equality and a rank-one negative-unit example distinguish this from the former even-rank test.
2. **Actual centre and zero signature.** Conjugate-signature comparison now assumes the embedding field maps onto the actual centre. The zero test concerns the raw integral Hodge-rank adapter, rather than a nonexistent faithful rational zero datum. Positive rank is native in imaginaryQuadraticDatum, and retained mathematically for the general unitary construction.
3. **Local lattices and integral levels.** The type-D local transitivity conclusion explicitly excludes p=2. Kottwitz Lemma 7.2 is at pp.395–396 and Corollary 7.3 at p.396. The integral-level comparison requires H inside the completed integral lattice stabilizer. Lan's corrected Remark 1.4.3.8 keeps the order and enlarges the lattice; corrected Remark 1.4.3.11 qualifies the generic Shimura interpretation.
4. **Hecke and full adeles.** First right-precomposing by h and then by g gives h*g. The full-adelic moduli API and tests now concern the actual family, full rational Tate module and its translated marking orbit. Empty box means all nonzero integers are inverted and Hom is rationalized.
5. **Rigidity and deformation.** Finite order is asserted on geometric fibres. The complete-local deformation problem retains its special-fibre marking and uses rigidity of automorphisms reducing to the identity; neatness is not needed for that marked functor.
6. **Finite forgetful morphism.** A disjoint union of projective Hom loci is insufficient. The O-linear Tate marking fixes the trace of bb* for each order generator; Rosati positivity/boundedness bounds graph Hilbert polynomials. Generic graph representability, rigidity and DVR extension then give a proper quasi-finite action locus; finite level refinements preserve finiteness. A1/A2/A6/R09.2/R11.1 are direct suppliers. Importing PEL M2/Isom here would create M1→M2→M1, so the generic suppliers are used directly.
7. **Kodaira–Spencer.** The converse differential criterion requires local finite presentation. The actual affine-chart cotangent map is an API item and the suggested criterion uses the native LocallyOfFinitePresentation hypothesis. E6 records the infinite separable base-extension counterexample and Stacks tag 02GU.
8. **Full real orbit uniformization.** On the full similitude orbit, psi(x,Jx) can have the opposite sign. The analytic construction uses ε(h)q_g psi, with ε(h) selecting the positive component and q_g>0 making q_gc(g) an integral adelic unit. Under rational gamma, ε becomes sign(c(gamma))ε and q becomes q/|c(gamma)|, so the polarization transports correctly. A5's opposite alternating-form convention is explicit. A native negative-component test was added.
9. **SV3 and type D.** Shimura-datum/canonical-model terminology is conditional on nontrivial projection of h to each rational simple adjoint factor. The ordinary arithmetic quotient still treats compact factors. Type D has an independent full-group/all-form family comparison, not a dependency on the A/C theorem, and an actual open-and-closed analytic subspace interface. Lan's type-D errata is p.11, for thesis p.168.
10. **Canonical pieces.** The twist construction depends on M3 algebraic comparison, rather than on the CM theorem that uses twisting; canonical-model identification now imports the twist node directly. Global classes remain distinct even when all their localizations are neutral.
11. **CM models and trace.** Definition 3.5.4 uses the selected rank-one CM type's reflex-field integer ring localized at p, not the separate reflexive closure. The torus trace assumes pure dimension d and is functorial through equivariant cohomology maps; arbitrary geometric pullback is not asserted. Published Construction 3.4.4 and Notation 3.4.7 are cited with their actual labels.
12. **Polarized stack and Hodge line.** Faltings–Chai I.4.11 is a principal quartic Hilbert discussion, not a general cubic very-ampleness proposition. The general-degree cubic Hilbert route imports three exact A2 fibre/cohomology/Pic0 targets. Field-of-moduli descent now imports the forms/gerbe node. The Hodge-line Siegel KS claim is restricted to the good principally polarized setting.
13. **Closure and provenance.** Missing AA.1 and effectivity/R09.3 edges were added; A6's finite-polarized-automorphism/Rosati-boundedness contract explicitly serves the forgetful map. Lan's survey URL and downloaded hash were corrected. Current ProfiniteArithmetic uses Additive TauCeti.zHat with zHat.nonempty_ringEquiv_pi, not upstream declarations named CompletedIntegerRing or CompletedIntegerAwayRing. The local product notation is identified with that existing interface. Source-version kinds now follow the protocol vocabulary, and historical AMS provenance is distinguished from the mirror actually read.
