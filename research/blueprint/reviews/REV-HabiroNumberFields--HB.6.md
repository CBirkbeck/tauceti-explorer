For the essential local proof, write B=Z_p[q], Φ=Φ_m and I=(p,Φ), with p∤m. Each monic chain product f gives a finite power-basis module B/(f). To justify its p-completeness using `PadicInt.lift`, first form the **scalar** ring S of compatible ZMod(p^a) families. Its evaluation maps are ring homomorphisms, so `lift_spec` and `lift_unique` identify S with Z_p. Apply this equivalence as a function to each compatible power-basis coordinate, then check multiplication after reduction. Basis coordinate functions on B/(f) are generally not ring homomorphisms; the revised wording avoids that misuse.

Modulo p, f reduces to Φ^E, where E=Σ e_k w_k, w_0=1 and w_k=p^k−p^(k−1) for k>0. Thus Φ^E∈p in B/(f,p^a), and Φ^(aE)=0. The ideals (p^a,Φ^(aE)) are contained in (p^a,f), while each Φ^s is itself a permitted chain product. The double limit therefore uses (p^a,Φ^s). Finally I^(a+s−1)⊂(p^a,Φ^s) and (p^n,Φ^n)⊂I^n identify this limit with `AdicCompletion I B`. These are cofinalities after adjoining p^a. Raw ideal cofinality fails: no positive-degree chain product over Z_2 divides the constant 2. The packet asserts a ring equivalence, not an equivalence for the original discrete-quotient topology.
