Corrections made in place:

- **Thickness under ramification.** A node of old thickness n becomes thickness e*n under ramification e. Its regular resolution has e*n unit edges; the old e-edge wording only applied to n=1. Corrected the statement, proof sketch, StableReduction layer-4 request and Lean comment. Added the acceptance case n=2, e=3: six unit edges, with pairing three times the original thickness-two form.
- **Stabilized spectral-sequence page.** The general finite column range is [-d,d]. The former suggested page d+2 was unjustified without a degeneration input. Both proper and nonproper suggested abutments now use the safely stabilized page 2*d+2. The packet API, proof, test and filtered-realization gap explicitly require that column bound and the convergence maps. The curve E2 result remains a separate support argument.
- **Continuous Wang scope and closure.** The pure-complex local theorem consumed a Wang node stated only for constant coefficients in the smooth spreading setup. Weil II proof6.2.9 explicitly uses the row for general bounded constructible K. Expanded that node and its coefficient hypotheses accordingly, added EDC.0 directly, updated its request and locator, and clarified the Lean shadow. Neither total-space smoothness nor generic smoothness is required for that row.
- **Residue/trace closure.** Added EDC.2:pairings directly to nodal nearby sheaves and to that request's consuming nodes. The trace-dual branch identification requires it before the later graph-cohomology comparison.
- **Normalization API.** The former prose promised cohomology identifications while the suggested lemma only characterized a kernel and surjectivity. The API now states those actual algebraic properties and the quotient-map kernel; the Lean lemma adds that equality. The étale/two-term-complex cohomology interpretation still requires the supplied realization.
- **Arithmetic witness APIs.** Base/family/realization descriptions now match their exact single-scheme, scheme-pair and complex-isomorphism return types; the remaining maps are structure projections. Both witnesses now list their real structure constructors and have extensionality prototypes retaining every data field, including complex isomorphisms. Proof irrelevance applies only to proof fields. No missing arithmetic-purity predicate is disguised as a proposition field.
- **Source attribution.** Split the Illusie21 citations embedded in Saito locators into independent source entries for the stalk, graded and page-monodromy comparisons. In the stalk node, Illusie's kernel-lattice formula corroborates ranks/vanishing; the canonical diagonal-quotient presentation follows Saito. No canonical integral identification of those two branch lattices is claimed. Replaced generic source-match boilerplate with individual checks, distinguished derived I2 calculations from existence of an algebraic model, and identified RS-17 as the authority for the aggregate's export ownership.
- **Source mistakes.** Added and independently confirmed E13 and E14, preserving LPV.0's existing E1–E12 IDs. The author-copy proof of Lemma1.1.6(1), p.11, points to Proposition1.1.1(2) where the needed trivial-inertia statement is (1). The proof of Lemma2.2.1(1–3), p.19, points to Proposition1.1.2(2) where the displayed stratum isomorphism is (1). Both are cross-reference misprints affecting no intended mathematics. Page images and the actual target propositions were compared. Searches of the author's publication list, public journal page and erratum/corrigendum search found no correction of these manuscript references; those searches and their scope are recorded.
