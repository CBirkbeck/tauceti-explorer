## What was fixed

Mathematics:
- `positive-hecke-homomorphism` (1) claimed U m U m' U = U m m' U and [UmU][Um'U] = [Umm'U] for all U-positive m, m'; this is false for a non-abelian Levi (M = GL_2 × GL_1 in GL_3 with U_M ⊇ GL_2(O)). Restated as [UmU][Um'U] = t([U_M m U_M][U_M m' U_M]), equal to [Umm'U] when U_M m U_M m' U_M = U_M m m' U_M (tori); the "commutative subalgebra" clause and the API item `doubleCoset_mul_of_positive` restricted accordingly. Mirrored in the Lean comment block.
- `second-adjointness`: the unnormalised right adjoint of Ind_P ∘ infl is δ_P ⊗ (−)_{N̄}, not δ_P⁻¹ ⊗ (−)_{N̄}, with the README's own conventions (i_P = Ind(δ_P^{1/2} ⊗ ·), r_{P̄} = δ_P^{1/2} ⊗ (·)_{N̄}); this also matches the "Adjunctions" convention and SR.6 `integral-second-adjointness`. Fixed in README and Lean comment block.
- `iwahori-decomposition`: both product orders are required (ACC §2.1.9), and strong positivity is stated for compact open subgroups of U_N, U_{N̄} as in ACC rather than of N, N̄ (the consequence ⋃ z^{-n} U_N z^n = N was unsourced). API items adjusted; mirrored in the Lean comment block.
- `square-integrable-tempered` / `casselman-criterion`: Casselman's notes contain no definition of temperedness and no "≤ 1" criterion. Temperedness is now defined by the exponent condition (Konno §2, p. 390), the L^{2+ε} characterisation is mentioned as Waldspurger's and not used, and the tempered half of the criterion is the definition.
- Unsupported clauses removed: `noetherian` (Z(G)-admissibility of finitely generated objects, already in `finite-type-corners` (2) from BD84), `bernstein-decomposition` (K-finiteness of components, already in `finite-type-corners` (1)), `finite-length` (3) (not in Cas95), `generic-irreducibility` (discrete-series case, only an exercise in Ber92), `isotypic-quotient-regular` (restated for admissible irreducible σ with surjectivity via Burnside on each σ^K; sources Lemma 10, Proposition 13, Theorem 8 instead of Proposition 12).
- `steinberg`: no longer assumes G split (Casselman does not); its one-dimensional Iwahori invariants are derived from `borel-casselman-invariants`, now in Needs.
- `van-dantzig`: Ber92 Definition 1 only defines l-groups; the equivalence (a) ⇔ (c) is now attributed to Mathlib's `compact_exists_isClopen_in_isOpen` and `IsTopologicalGroup.exist_openSubgroup_sub_clopen_nhds_of_one`.
- `smooth-rep-category` no longer asserts limits, colimits and abelianness ahead of `smooth-rep-abelian`, which now states them.
