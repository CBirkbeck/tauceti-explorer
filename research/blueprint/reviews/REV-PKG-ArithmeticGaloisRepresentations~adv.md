# Review: Arithmetic Galois representations and conductors

Job: `REV-PKG-ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations~adv`, issue #8066. Independent reviewer: Codex, session `codex-KDhvAZ`. Reviewed on 2026-10-09. The package was written by another worker; this review changes only its README and suggested signatures, adds its verdict, and leaves the blueprint packets unchanged.

**Verdict: needs_changes.** The corrected suggested file elaborates at Mathlib `082e2d3` and Tau Ceti `f790474`, with only declarations using `sorry` as warnings. That establishes well-typed signatures, not true theorems. The package still has incomplete prerequisite chains, duplicate local objects relative to current Tau Ceti, missing geometric examples, and a layer order that is not a build order. These are mathematical and interface defects, not a README size objection. The current package-review instructions allow 100–300 KB documents; splitting Layer 7 into a Part II is an organisational option, not a substitute for supplying the mathematics.

The review used the current upstream LocalGaloisGroups and ProfiniteArithmetic documents and signatures as form and ownership models, the upstream guide, the protocol, the packet's 38 recorded gaps, the earlier package handoff, and the current library in addition to the elaboration pins. Source conclusions below distinguish locating a primary statement from establishing the complete chain needed to formalise it. None of the computations described here is represented as a Lean proof.

## Corrections applied

1. The conventions now give the rational Tate module of an elliptic curve Hodge–Tate weights `0,1` when the cyclotomic character has weight `+1`; its dual `H¹` has weights `0,-1`. The previous convention gave the dual the same weights. The determinant witness is the cyclotomic character, of weight `1`.
2. The dyadic involution statement now says that the sum of the integral eigenspaces **contains** `2T`, so its quotient is killed by two. It need not equal `2T`. A local `Involution.eigensum` API and five examples distinguish the split integral involution, the integral swap, the rational swap, characteristic two, and the split dyadic involution. For the swap on `ℤ²`, the sum consists of pairs of the same parity. For the split involution it is already all of `ℤ²`.
3. Prime-field projective automorphisms now distinguish `PSL₂` from `PGL₂`. Conjugation by a nonsquare-determinant matrix is outer on `PSL₂(F₅)`, although it is inner on `PGL₂(F₅)`. The blanket claim that prime-field automorphisms are inner was false for the first group. The tests include that distinction, a field automorphism of `PSL₂(F₄)`, and the nonsimplicity of `PSL₂(F₂)` and `PSL₂(F₃)`.
4. The adjoint equivalence in the algebraic Goursat discussion is restricted to the **graph** case. The full product has no such equivalence. The explicit pair `(diag(2,1),1)` multiplies `E₁₂` by two in its first adjoint and fixes it in the second. The abstract projective Goursat target is separately stated, without confusing abstract automorphisms with algebraic automorphisms in characteristic zero.
5. The canonical nilpotent monodromy filtration is now a local Layer 2 target, with the kernel/image formula, monotonicity, lowering by `N`, eventual bounds, zero-operator behaviour, and conjugation invariance. Typed zero-module and Jordan-block checks accompany it. This removes the upward ownership request to LefschetzPencilsAndVanishingCycles for the elementary filtration. Primitive decompositions and tensor/dual compatibility remain explicit gaps; they were not inferred from the formula alone.
6. The incorrect inverse in the rank-two trace negative control is pinned by diagonal matrices: the correct expression is `0`, the expression using the wrong inverse is `25`. The test does not require noncommuting matrices to distinguish the formulas.
7. The continuity negative control for dense-set recognition now uses the product of countably many groups of order two. Its finite-support subgroup is dense, and a nontrivial character of the quotient to `F₃ˣ` is trivial there. This is an actual algebraic character with discontinuous action, rather than an unsupported assertion about a discontinuous embedding of a local field.
8. The two Haar measures in the local-constant duality identity are identified as Fourier-dual measures, rather than independently normalised measures. Rescaling one forces the reciprocal rescaling of the other. A typed Fourier-measure example is still missing.
9. Cyclic tensor formulas have a positive cycle-length hypothesis. The rational Sen-weight existence statement has `x ∈ ℤ` and positive denominator `d`. The unitary and solvably induced tensor statements specify positive ranks `n,m`.
10. The two new projective simplicity targets were deleted. Mathlib's `Matrix.ProjectiveSpecialLinearGroup.rank_two_simple'`, `rank_two_simple`, and `isoPSLOfAlgClosed` already supply their content. The current Tau Ceti pointwise central quotient identification is cited in the boundary. The retained target is the Goursat application, not a second proof of simplicity. No removed-target stubs remain in the README.
11. The trace-pairing theorem is attributed to its matrix-unit proof, followed by finite-projective localisation. BCG25, Theorem 2.1, pp. 513–514, uses the pairing and does not prove the stated general theorem over an arbitrary commutative ring. Likewise DDT §2.1, p. 53, is identified as context for the more general restriction/averaging statements, not as their exact statement.
12. The references fix several malformed entries and pin relevant versions. Nekovář Proposition 3.10(3) is located at printed pp. 1196–1198. BCGP25 Proposition 4.11.1 is at pp. 109–110 and Proposition 4.11.2 at pp. 110–112. The two distinct page conventions for CG20 oddness are retained. The tensor-induction boundary uses `Subgroup.tensorInducedRepresentation`.
13. Explicit source/prerequisite gap paragraphs now sit beside the affected local factors, local constants, infinite-residue-field conductors, Ogg–Saito comparison, semisimplicity criteria, geometric Tate-module inputs, algebraic monodromy, finite cohomology, adequacy, enormity, small `Sp₄` calculations, and unitary tensor results. Obtaining a source was not treated as closing a missing proof chain.

## Work still required before acceptance

### Current-library objects must actually be imported

The boundary correctly names current Tau Ceti objects, but the suggested file does not consistently consume them. Its `GaloisRep.inertiaGroup`, `arithFrobLift`, `localWildInertiaGroup`, `tameCharacter`, and roots-of-unity carrier helpers are opaque proposed constructions rather than identifications with the existing definitions. In particular `IsUnramified` and the subsequent Frobenius polynomial interfaces are expressed using the new opaque inertia object. Merely putting a current declaration in a boundary paragraph does not make those types refer to it.

The next edit must remove these duplicate raw constructions and use the actual carrier/predicate, adjusting the dependent signatures. Where the current API cannot be imported at the older elaboration pin, omit the unavailable signatures and name them in the short closing comment, as PACKAGE_REVIEW requires. Do not keep an arbitrary local definition just to make dependent declarations elaborate. This is a substantial connected edit: removing the carrier alone breaks its downstream declarations and examples.

### The layer dependencies and geometry must be completed

ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations is tier 7. AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli A1–A3 and InverseGaloisAndArithmeticFundamentalGroups IG.1 are lower-tier suppliers, not upward citations. Their exact contracts should be retained. However A6's characteristic-polynomial target consumes the present Tate-module stage: citing A6 back here creates a stage cycle even though its roadmap is lower tier. The integral, ℓ-independent endomorphism polynomial and the general finite-field Frobenius endomorphism must be targets here with complete inputs, not assumptions named after the result being sought.

The current README's asserted build order is also inaccurate. Layer 1's symplectic integral model uses Layer 7's similitude groups; Layer 4 uses `adZero` in Layer 7; Layer 5 uses Layer 7's monodromy groups and similitude groups; Layer 6 uses Layer 7's adjoint/image conditions; Layer 7 itself uses Layer 5. Moving elementary group, adjoint and algebraic-monodromy preliminaries earlier, and isolating the later image applications, is needed to produce an acyclic order. The explicitly delayed Layer 3 elliptic comparison after Layer 6 is documented, but does not resolve those additional dependencies.

Layer 6 now has genuine integral-involution examples. It still lacks typed examples for its geometric definitions: the abelian-variety Tate module, its finite-level reduction, the Weil-pairing/determinant comparison, and its first-cohomology/Euler-factor objects. Most of the geometry is named in the closing comment because supplier structures are absent at the pin. That is allowed for signatures which cannot be typed, but it does not meet the requirement that the definition's discriminating checks be represented and its prerequisites be complete. A typed algebraic fragment must not be described as testing the absent geometric object. Other definitions, including the Fourier-dual measure normalisation, also require a completed definition-by-definition test crosswalk; a total of many `example`s is not proof of three useful examples per definition.

The reopened top-level namespaces were consolidated into one namespace, with sections preserving each original variable scope. The combined layer headings and duplicate Layer 1 heading were corrected, and every README layer now has an Examples subsection without losing its existing checks. Late supplements after Layer 7 still need to be placed with their topics in the corrected mathematical build order. Process identifiers were removed from several layer comments, but a full comment/docstring cleanup remains. Do not drop targets or examples to achieve the reorganisation.
