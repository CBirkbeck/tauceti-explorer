## Corrections made

1. **Whole-catalogue derived-limit count: 8 → 14 papers.** Added `PAPER-FARB-KISIN-WOLFSON-24/033` and `/034`, `PAPER-BHATT-MORROW-SCHOLZE-19/038`, `PAPER-COLMEZ-DOSPINESCU-NIZIOL-20-B/in-stein-spaces`, `PAPER-COLMEZ-DOSPINESCU-NIZIOL-21/2-zp-hat` and `/3-kummer-and-artin-schreier`, `PAPER-COLMEZ-NIZIOL-25/807`, and `PAPER-COLMEZ-NIZIOL-17/151`. The catalogue statements and fresh primary passages exhibit countable derived limits, their first derived defects, or sequential homotopy limits. The limit in BMS's derived category is already derived; no underived replacement is inferred from the notation. These are prerequisites of the particular completion/Stein/crystalline constructions, not additional ownership of those constructions. The entry now sorts first. Limits over arbitrary prismatic/fppf sites, finite homotopy squares and cosimplicial totalizations were excluded from this sequential count. BKO21's carrier/comparison item stays elsewhere; a general comparison brief alone is insufficient to add a new sequential-construction instance.

2. **Existing constant ell-adic objects were omitted.** [Mathlib's pinned file](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/082e2d37e8b0463410cdb532e111cd43d5a66174/Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/ElladicCohomology.lean) defines `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.ellAdicSheaf` at line 63 and `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.EllAdicCohomology` at line 75. The first is the continuous-map Z_ell sheaf on a scheme's pro-étale site; the second takes its sheaf cohomology in nonnegative degrees. Its own notes leave the comparison with classical étale cohomology for future work. These declarations now appear in `library.has`. The remaining entry concerns arbitrary inverse systems, bounded-below complexes, equivariance, rational realization and the necessary comparisons. It is substantial work, rather than another definition of the constant sheaf or its H^n.

3. **D7 does not own the general scheme-system construction.** D7 explicitly develops arithmetic/Galois coefficient systems. The submitted `owners: [D7]` did not cover the entry's arbitrary étale sheaves and continuous equivariant realization. I changed that entry to `owners: []` and explained the scope gap. `ClassicalAdicEtaleCohomology` concerns analytic adic spaces; “adic” there names the space, not Jannsen's coefficient system. `AdicCoefficientsAndComparisons:L0` concerns v-derived diamond coefficients, and its scheme comparisons do not automatically own the entire construction. EDC.0's bounded finite-coefficient enhancement is an import. A maintainer must assign or extend one geometric supplier and make D7 consume its Galois comparison.

4. **Kato's scheme hypotheses were too broad.** The first implementation and its finite-support, d²=0, proper-pushforward and localization API now explicitly use separated finite-type schemes over a field k, with n invertible in k. Degrees use the dimension of the closure of a point. Jannsen §4 provides this coniveau setting; an arbitrary finite-type scheme with an unspecified dimension function does not supply it. The wild and more general arithmetic extensions retain their separate hypotheses. The top-field compatibility example now says integral regular scheme so that k(X) has its intended meaning.

5. **Local lattice and density scope is explicit.** The quadratic entry now assumes char(F) ≠ 2, retaining dyadic residue characteristic such as Q_2. Equal-characteristic-2 nonsingularity is a different construction. The density dimension exponents refer to a nonempty generic representation space in the stated n ≤ m and residue-characteristic scope. Ramified, fractional and dyadic counting conventions remain source-specific; the simple dyadic congruence example is not a claim of an unrestricted geometric density theorem.

6. **Upstream degree-zero GW/W must be imported.** Added `QuadraticFormInvariants#layer-4-the-witt-ring-and-the-fundamental-ideal` as a dependency of the higher/general-ring GW entry. GN.6 owns the extension and comparison, not a second field GW_0 construction. The real-field example tests that comparison. Ordinary exact K_0 alone is still insufficient for forms group completion or a higher spectrum.

7. **Current cross-survey dependencies are now visible.** The zero-cycle complex imports `algebraicgeometry/chow-intersection` and `algebraicgeometry/scheme-brauer`. Geometric stabilizers import the finite algebraic-kernel portion of `algebraicgeometry/galois-gerbs`; their new part is the geometric realization, basepoint transport and stable cyclic filtration. This does not create another general gerb. Logarithmic de Rham–Witt (CR.4), enhanced categories (E0) and spectrum/group completion (H.4/H.5) remain named supplier interfaces in the reader where no suitable key-definition ID exists. The combined dependency graph is acyclic.
