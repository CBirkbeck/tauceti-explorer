## Corrections in this pass

1. **Source matches, all 48 nodes.** Replaced formulaic assurances with the mathematical support at the exact locator. The first nine fixed-vector nodes abstract PAP Lemma 3.3.7/Proposition 3.3.8 beyond pre-t-motives to arbitrary semilinear modules. Their finite-relation proof establishes the extension; invertibility of the semilinear module map is unnecessary. The entire ring deliberately uses the narrower arithmetic ABP conditions, while PAP's analytic ring is larger. Consumer sources are identified as consumers, without importing their specialized multizeta targets.
2. **Existing analytic work.** Current Tau Ceti already implements one-variable preparation, its uniqueness, the unit criterion and the principal-ideal/noetherian result. The analytic proof, audit, upstream note, gap description, supplier request, reader and Lean omission comment now import those exact exports. AdicSpaces is requested only for the C∞ normalized zero/pole comparison and bounded-disk entire zero/growth estimates. These results postdate the packet pin, so they are recorded as current-library interfaces rather than misrepresented as additional pinned baselines.
3. **Singular solution matrices.** The source uses invertible Φ, while the packet also bounds solutions for singular square Φ. On the L-span W of its solutions, σ(W)=Φ(W) and the field automorphism preserves dim_L W. Thus Φ is injective on W; applying σ to a minimal relation and cancelling this restriction proves coefficient descent. This justifies the broader stated dimension bound without inverting Φ on all L^r. Rectangular solution arrays are columnwise uses of the same equation.
4. **Period signature.** `period_transcendence` previously required the determinant to be a unit in the full formal-power-series ring. Its packet requires Ψ∈GL_r(T). The Lean hypothesis now maps entire entries into the native radius-one restricted-series subring and requires the determinant to be a unit there. Formal invertibility is weaker: 1−t has nonzero constant term and a formal inverse, but vanishes at t=1 and is not a Tate-algebra unit. The field equality and documented category-valued omission are unchanged.
5. **Fresh evidence.** All seven source reading receipts, six version records, 56 baseline confirmations and eight source-issue verdicts identify this pass. The HAL endpoint returned different bytes from the prior receipt; the cover identifies hal-03298790v1 and the cited printed/PDF pages were checked afresh.
