## Corrections made

1. **Finite-length Schmid–Witt duality.** For finite k, F=k((T)) and n≥1, the discrete ASW quotient Q_n(F) pairs perfectly with **F×/(F×)^(p^n)** with its quotient topology. It does not pair perfectly with the entire pro-p completion: at n=1, a uniformizer raised to p annihilates every Q_1 character, but its valuation coordinate p is nonzero in that completion. The infinite-length symbol has different domains and topologies. Corrected the statement, hypotheses, proof, source and acceptance control, and added the Lean power-annihilation helper. [Kosters–Wan](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.00523v1), Theorem 3.4, p.8; §4.1, Remark 4.1, pp.14–15; Proposition 4.2, p.15; §4.4, Theorem 4.7, pp.18–19, supply the pairing and lifted-residue formula. The old conductor-theorem locator did not supply that formula.
2. **Ray conductor and certificate semantics.** Use U_v^(0)=O_v× and U_v^(r)=1+P_v^r for r≥1. The expression 1+O_v is not a multiplicative group. Unramified characters therefore have conductor zero. The chosen finite degree quotient remains separate from the full ray class group. The Lean certificate now requires finite character image and gives its selected degree-one class valuation degree +1, equivalently **Yu degree −1**. The normalized completion certificate now carries an actual valuation-preservation proof field; its theorem uses that field.
3. **Affine group points at the pin.** Mathlib's `AlgHom.convGroup` exists, but at this pin requires a bialgebra structure on the value algebra. That is too restrictive for arbitrary adelic R-algebras. Replaced this receipt and the FA.6 prerequisite with the existing `TauCeti.AlgHom.instGroup` in `TauCeti/Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/FunctorOfPoints.lean`, for a Hopf algebra and any commutative value R-algebra. No new group-points mathematics is planned. Corrected `TauCeti.ReductiveAffineGroupSchemeCat` to the actual module `TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/AffineGroupScheme/Reductive.lean` and kind `abbrev`. All other receipts were retained after statement audit. Clarified that `LaurentSeries.ratfuncAdicComplRingEquiv` is a ring equivalence whose underlying uniform comparison is `LaurentSeries.comparePkg`; valuation compatibility uses `LaurentSeries.valuation_compare` separately.
4. **Coefficient-accurate trace and determinants.** Read upstream [CohomologicalPointCounting/TraceFormula PR #196](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/pull/196), head `4bd72379658126cbe9be935656396f0c9dac4de0`: finite-coefficient conventions and Layers 0–4, 9–11, 14–15, together with the suggested file. Its finite-coefficient trace and perfect-complex determinant algebra are reused. Layers 14–15 state the sheaf L-function determinant result in characteristic-zero adic coefficients. The finite coefficient-field identity needed by [Abdurrahman–Venkatesh](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.13436v1), §3.2, equations (3.1)–(3.2), pp.26–27, needs a precise enhancement. Added **TORSION-DETERMINANT** and corrected the proof boundary. Equal power traces in characteristic ℓ cannot recover the determinant: the identity on a space of dimension ℓ has every power trace zero but determinant 1−T^ℓ. No characteristic-zero lift of the given finite-field representation is assumed.
5. **Trace ownership and characteristic-zero semisimplicity.** EDC.8 already says that ordinary trace is imported from TraceFormula; it is an import receipt, not a second trace-construction owner. The atlas does not register PR #196's layer identifiers. `request-torsion-trace` therefore uses the registered EDC.8 receipt, names the actual upstream owner and forwards the explicit finite-coefficient determinant enhancement. Removed DWP.1 from the torsion target. Changed the characteristic-zero Brauer–Nesbitt enhancement to DWP.0, whose functorial linear-algebra direction is relevant; DWP.1's Weil-bound direction is not. The weights request now has only the semisimple-determination consumer. The packet's upstream note records the exact PR and head; no upstream file was edited.
6. **Hypotheses, normalizations and citations.** Added finite constants to the Lean self-dual Haar/Fourier signatures and tests, and finite constants plus an explicit function-field proof to the zero-coefficient signature and example. Clarified local units versus all F×, completed residues versus algebraic repartitions, full central-degree conditions, coefficient-specific Hecke/Satake ownership, and FYZ inert/split density and source-rank n+2j conventions. Removed a repeated SR.4 prerequisite. The integral coefficient assertion for R⊂C is correct: C is flat over R through Frac(R); no extra flatness hypothesis or counterexample is warranted. Corrected Parshin additive-character locators to §2.2, pp.8–9, the Kosters–Wan ramification-contract locator to §4.5, and the FYZ global coefficient locator to Theorem 2.8, equations (2.10)–(2.12), pp.14–15. Corrected Yoshida's nonexistent “Corollary 5.16(iv)” to §5.3(iv), pp.425–428, after that corollary; Theorem 6.15 is on pp.432–433. [Yoshida's public article](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/afst.1188.pdf) supplies the existence proof with the stated Frobenius convention.
