## Method

Four readers worked in parallel:

- items 1–160 (Rubin: the Introduction, Chapters I–II, Appendix B, Chapter III §§1–2), with coverage checked paragraph by paragraph;
- items 161–319 (Rubin: Chapter III §§2.4–2.6 and §5, Chapters IV, V and IX), with coverage of the same;
- the 84 Gross items, the Gross mistakes E33–E39, and a fresh reading of Gross for further mistakes;
- the 32 Rubin mistakes E1–E32 against the draft and the final version, a search for published corrections, the five routes and the prerequisites.

How the checks were done:

- **Statements.** Every statement and locator was compared with the text, on the page image where a symbol mattered.
- **Statuses.** Every cited library declaration was opened at the pinned commit. Every cited stage description was read, and so were the packet and decomposition nodes that cite Rubin or Gross (SelmerIwasawaCohomology, ArithmeticGaloisDuality, EulerSystemsCyclotomicMainConjecture, KatoEulerSystems, HeegnerPointEulerSystems). Many items the extraction called missing are planned by those nodes.
- **Missing items.** They were searched for in the atlas (stages, packets, decompositions, accepted paper routes) and in the Mathlib and Tau Ceti declaration index.
- **Conflicting corrections.** Where two readers proposed different corrections to one item, the lead reviewer chose. The Appendix B notes follow the first reader. The signs and wording in Chapters III and IX follow the fourth. The rest were applied after these.
- **Duplicate findings.** Two readers found the sign in Rubin's (III.2) and the misprint in the definition of R_M independently. Each was recorded once.
- **Routes.** After the status changes, every route was rechecked: each missing item is in exactly one route, no library item is in a route, and a planned item stays in a route only when the route names one of its planned layers.
- **Checker.** `python3 scripts/check_paper.py` reports the corrected file `ok`.

The extraction is thorough and honest about what it read, and its routing is mostly sound. The review changed 160 fields on 90 items, added 11 items and deleted three. The corrected file has 411 items (304 planned, 14 library, 93 missing), against 403 (286, 12, 105) as submitted. It has 9 routes (five reshaped, four added, all accepted) and 51 recorded mistakes (39 confirmed from the extraction, 12 new).

The substantive changes are:

The universal Euler system and its freeness, δ_L and the induced modules, the derivative classes and their local behaviour, the congruence, and the Chapter IX variants are system objects, derivative maps and descent arguments. The EulerSystemsAndKolyvaginSystems README gives ES exactly these ('This roadmap owns only the extra auxiliary-prime conditions, system objects, regulator/derivative maps, core vertices and descent arguments'). As submitted the route carried 70 items, and fifteen did not belong. Proposition IV.4.5(ii)–(iii) is in Tau Ceti at the pinned commit (TauCeti.ContCohomology.DiscreteShortExact.explicitDelta0_apply, explicitDelta0_res, explicitCor_delta0). (b) Remark II.2.7, the Selmer form of Leopoldt's conjecture, goes to SelmerIwasawaCohomology L4 (route 6). They go to PadicMeasuresIwasawaAlgebras L1/L6 (route 7). (d) Example IX.6.2 builds a specific Euler system, so it goes to EulerSystemsCyclotomicMainConjecture L0 (route 3). (e) Three items were deleted. Two are the Introduction's one-sentence history (Flach's classes, elliptic units), which nothing on the way to the main results uses. The third is Remark B.2.6, an unused illustration whose printed claim is false (E5). The route gains three missing items: the general-χ derivative classes of IX §4 and Remark IX.4.4 (HE.4 plans only the Heegner case d = 2), and Appendix A Corollary 2.6, the source of the 2b error term of Chapter V §3.
