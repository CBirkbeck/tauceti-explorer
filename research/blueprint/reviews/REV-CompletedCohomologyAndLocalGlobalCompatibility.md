## Mathematical corrections

1. **Arithmetic carriers.** R31.1’s raw definite adelic quotient is distinguished from the profinite limit of finite-level sets. The discrete-stabiliser and p-adic manifold assertions of Dospinescu–Le Bras, Lemme 4.4, pp. 20–21, are used over Q. Pan’s general totally real construction keeps the closure of global central units and his smallness hypothesis (§§3.1–3.2, pp. 17–21); it does not follow by asserting that global units are discrete in the finite adeles. The central-character test now requires compatibility, and density of integral smooth vectors is restricted to smooth characters. An infinite-image central character can have no nonzero smooth vectors.
2. **Descent and boundary.** The Shimura finite-level comparison uses the CC.6 Hochschild–Serre edge sequence and an explicit localised H⁰-vanishing input, rather than neatness alone. The exclusion of Q is restricted to CDN’s quaternion algebra unramified at every finite place. In the modular vanishing proof, the index is taken after intersecting with SL₂, as in EM06 Proposition 4.3.6, p. 53. The dyadic cusp-boundary calculation is a separate supplier extension: the present R14.3 statement assumes residue characteristic at least 3.
3. **Classical density and Galois conventions.** The span of vectors algebraic for the fixed maximal compact gives crystalline classical points (EM11 Corollary 5.4.5, p. 55). The former purported counterexample from smooth weight-two forms is removed: allowing all p-levels already gives the dense classical colimit. Pan’s algebraicity conditions on the fixed central character are retained. The missing ℓ in EM11’s determinant is corrected; Pan’s ψε⁻¹ determinant is retained on reconstruction, while Tung’s ψ_Tε convention requires an explicit dictionary. The weight-varying determinant test now gives χ(ℓ)ℓ^{k−1}, not a universal weight-two determinant.
4. **Families and multiplicity.** R31.4 distinguishes the co-Whittaker family W from its coadmissible smooth O-dual Π. The derivative and arbitrary-coefficient endomorphism assertions belong to W. For an empty away-from-p product, W is T and Π is the continuous O-dual T′, not T. The cotorsion-free part of X(θ) is a submodule. Current SR.5 already owns the unconditional Helm–Moss family and its APIs; only the exact mod-p smooth-dual fibre comparison remains requested.
5. **Refined compatibility.** EM11 Theorem 6.4.16, pp. 88–89, supplies canonical compatibility under scalar local residual endomorphisms. For split distinct characters Theorem 6.4.20, p. 89, proves the θ-family form, Conjecture 6.1.9. Remark 6.1.11, p. 68, does not identify θ with the canonical family in that case. The weaker form suffices for the corresponding main-theorem branch. Injectivity of the evaluation map also needs the mod-p weight bounds and Ihara consequences; irreducibility of a socle by itself does not make every nonzero map injective.
6. **Block and patched modules.** Pan Corollary 3.4.12, p. 28, provides a finite central map, not a general integral centre isomorphism. The projective generator is the envelope of the sum of block simples. CEGGPS Corollary 4.26, pp. 69–70, gives projectivity in the specified compact K/ZK category, not in a category of G-representations. Unsupported rank-one and rank-growth examples are removed. The q = 0 quaternionic specialisation retains framing variables and central-character compatibility.
7. **Support.** EP Remark 6.4 and Corollary 5.5, pp. 25–26, give full projected support over an irreducible local framed ring; full patched support additionally retains the auxiliary components. Full annihilator support means a nil ideal, nilpotence follows under Noetherianity, and faithfulness follows under reducedness. Tung Proposition 3.9, p. 12, covers every odd p without residual exclusions, using unrestricted framed-ring irreducibility/reducedness. Representability in R08 does not supply those facts. In the dyadic case, Lemma 5.3.2(1)(a) is an unpatched typed-module statement and (b) its patched counterpart; Theorem 5.3.3 is on p. 29. Its ordinary branch needs a dyadic extension beyond the present odd-p R21 contract.
8. **Pan patching.** The nice-prime proof retains the nontrivial tame-character irreducibility input, the characteristic-zero tame-character transfer and the descent of full support in Pan §§4.2, 4.8, pp. 40–43, 66–70. A dimension bound plus equidimensionality alone does not replace them. The Hecke dimension estimate retains its noncommutative Iwasawa/Gelfand–Kirillov input; R03’s commutative finite-module support results are insufficient.
9. **Globalisation and exports.** The auxiliary CM argument needs full predicted-weight occurrence, not merely a minimal-weight theorem. The det^b/det^{b+1} discrepancy in EM11’s global proof is retained as an explicit normalisation gap. Both CM constructions inherit R17.5’s prescribed Hecke-character existence gap. The audit distinguishes odd-prime CEGGPS inputs from the dyadic Lemmas 4.3.3–4.3.4 and certifies only inspected leaves. The export table no longer cites downstream R30.6 back into its own supplier chain.

Every target’s statement, hypotheses, API, tests, proof steps, direct prerequisites and source matches are reproduced in the corrected reader. Its introductions and ownership table have been reconciled with these changes. The suggested file’s comments now keep the same scope, submodule convention and support criterion; its reduction-surjectivity wrapper uses the existing library theorem directly.
