## 4. Mistakes in the paper: 14 of 15 confirmed, 1 rejected, 1 added

- **Confirmed on the page images:** E1 (p. 276), E2 (p. 285), E3 (p. 285), E4 (p. 291), E6 and E7 (p. 297), E8 and E9 (p. 298), E10 (p. 301, read at 260 dpi), E11 (p. 300), E12 (pp. 301–302), E13 (p. 303), E14 (pp. 305–306) and E15 (p. 302).
  - **E2.** The translation's footnote 10 flags the same sentence but reads it as "u a closed point of U". x is used afterwards as a closed point of U₀ (F_x, deg(x)), so the extraction's correction stands. The `known` field now records the footnote.
  - **E8.** The equation q^{−n} = μ(g) uses n both as the Ẑ-coordinate and as the fibre dimension (ψ has values in Q_ℓ(−n), p. 295). It is right only for n = 1.
- **E5 rejected.** The extraction calls "𝔏 est engendrée par les N_s" a gap, but the deduction is standard:
  - The image lies in the Q_ℓ-points of the algebraic subgroup H of Sp generated by the unipotent groups exp(G_a·N_s).
  - In characteristic 0, Lie(H) is the Lie algebra generated by the N_s (Chevalley: the Lie algebra generated by algebraic subalgebras is algebraic).
  - A closed subgroup of H(Q_ℓ) has Lie algebra inside Lie(H), and each exp(Z_ℓ·N_s) lies in the image, so 𝔏 equals that generated algebra.
  - Deligne's preceding sentence points to the Zariski closure for exactly this reason. The recorded reason, that the closed subgroup "can be larger" than the analytic subgroup, is false here.
- **E16** (new, error, affects nothing; proof of Corollaire (3.8), p. 286).
  - "Sur la droite affine, il y a qⁿ points … donc au plus qⁿ points fermés de degré n" is applied to U₀, but U₀ is only an open of P¹ and can contain the rational point at infinity. The reduction after (3.2) only makes U affine.
  - Counterexample: take q = 2, U₀ = P¹ minus the point x² + x + 1 = 0, and ε = 5. The three rational points alone give Σ_x q_x^{−1−ε} ≥ 3/64. The printed bound is Σ_n 2^{−5n} = 1/31 < 3/64.
  - Counting on P¹ (qⁿ + 1 points) keeps the series convergent, so (3.8) stands.
