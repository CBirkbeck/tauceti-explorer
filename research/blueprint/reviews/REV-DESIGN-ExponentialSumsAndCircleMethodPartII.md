## Corrections made in place

1. Replaced the open finite-projection audit with a direct finite-grid degree argument, resolving G1. For an irreducible positive-dimensional geometric component W, choose a nonconstant coordinate, slice by its values in the finite set S, and induct on dimension. Each proper coordinate-hyperplane section has cumulative degree at most deg W and dimension one lower. Sum the slices and then the geometric components. The resulting bound is Σ_i Δ_i|S|^(dim W_i)≤Δ|S|^(dim V). It uses the original coordinates, so no polynomial-height correction is needed. The polynomial-box target now has explicit constant Δ, and handles empty sets, dimension zero and h≤0. The node id `finite-projection-degree` is retained for existing consumers, but its title and planet now name the finite-grid result.
