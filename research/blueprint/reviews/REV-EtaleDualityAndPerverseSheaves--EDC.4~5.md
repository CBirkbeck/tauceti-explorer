## Corrections made

1. Generic affine weak Lefschetz now follows the actual exchange argument in Katz Appendix A.1–A.5, pp.218–221: the universal complement supplies smooth exchange, generic base change supplies the other open exchange, and localization gives closed restriction. The coefficient extension is explicit. Finite coefficients use finite dévissage; integral coefficients use one residue complex, perfect coefficient change and derived Nakayama; rational coefficients use a constructible integral model. No infinite intersection of dense opens is used, and the residue complex need not be perverse.
2. Connected iteration stops in positive dimension, at the curve section. On the smooth affine quadric surface in affine three-space, a generic plane cuts a connected conic and the next cuts two reduced points. The range at step i is an isomorphism for q < d−i and an injection for q=d−i. The Bertini smoothness supplier is still requested with its unramified-map hypothesis.
3. The projective-bundle formula is stated for arbitrary bounded constructible finite-coefficient K, while F.2.7's imported projection formula assumes ctf inputs. The proof now checks the canonical columns on geometric stalks using proper base change, free resolutions and the uniform fibre cohomological-dimension bound. The ℤ/4 coefficient test with K=ℤ/2 detects an accidental finite-Tor restriction. Relative smooth proper trace works over every allowed base, supplying the projective and flag retractions without assuming the base smooth. The lines-convention relation and both section classes retain their correct signs. A nontrivial P¹-bundle check distinguishes a trace retraction from the coordinate projection of the decomposition.
4. The ample-divisor statement now preserves each branch's own amplitude hypothesis: p≥ for restriction, p≤ for supported restriction, and p⁺≥ for integral saturation. The Gysin corollary also preserves smooth-pair purity. Properness remains essential for the Stacks 0EKE affine-complement argument.
5. Arbitrary-base complete-intersection families are reduced locally to noetherian models before invoking the upstream finiteness theorem, then transported by proper base change. High quadric generators are trace-dual integral sections γ_i with η^i=2γ_i; this does not treat two as invertible at ℓ=2.
6. Orthogonality for a smooth proper divisor uses only finiteness, duality, the actual adjoint maps and the divisor class. The unnecessary weak-Lefschetz prerequisites were removed, and complementary decompositions were qualified by nondegeneracy. The finite-coefficient quadric diagonal remains an explicit counterexample.
7. The three normalized-system API lemmas now cite their precise reconstruction, boundedness and common-stratification results. The GlobalShtukas resolution specifies the direction of the normalization shift: K_rel[s] is absolutely perverse for s=dim X^I. The Katz link now points to the readable author-hosted published scan, with its own checksum. Historical reading receipts are preserved separately from this review's receipts.
