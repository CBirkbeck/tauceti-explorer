The high-severity correction is the power-series substitution boundary (7, also 18): algebraic nilpotence of the constant coefficient (`HasSubst`) does not provide the p-adic re-expansion. Use evaluation at a topologically nilpotent element of a proved complete, separated, linearly topologised target. Tau Ceti's substitution/evaluation compatibility theorem retains `HasSubst` and does not bypass its hypothesis.

Other substantial reusable ingredients include integral dimension and lying over, finite-module support through residue-field fibres, completed-map constructors, squarefree polynomial field-factor decomposition, the class-number-one certificate, quadratic/cyclotomic integral bases, finite A(S,2), and the maximal-integral-curve and local norm-comparison APIs. The review retains each specific completion, computation, descent or Riemannian assembly obligation.

The corrected fixes matter as much as the verdicts:

- **8, 10, 18, 20, 21:** retain characteristic 2 and zero-ring exceptions; p-torsion-free does not mean reduced; toric `expand` alone fixes coefficients, so its Frobenius interpretation needs the stated coefficient assumptions; the square-root-of-five result assumes 2 is nonzero.
- **12:** construct compatible maps from one fixed Habiro-completed source through the quotients by powers of the chosen cyclotomic polynomial. An arbitrary polynomial quotient need not map to every Taylor truncation, and the single-root family need not be cofinal in the full completion system.
- **17, 25:** a field-factor decomposition is an ingredient of the componentwise completion/descent target. Do not label the whole target exact or more general merely because an arithmetic subfact is available.
- **19:** assign one shared abstract lambda/Adams interface, including the torsion-free comparison, while retaining S.6's separate higher-K operations. Cross-checks of AUDIT-29 and AUDIT-30 confirm that their present overlap lists omit HR.1.
- **26–28, 31–35:** distinguish certificates and noncomputable carriers from executable algorithms. Keep the number-field, normal-form and finiteness hypotheses. Smith existence is nonsingular; its uniqueness theorem also covers singular diagonals. The interval division theorem does not cover signed real intervals.
- **17, 26–28, 38:** select or replace citations to obey the audit's maximum of five per target. Several proposed additive fixes exceed it.
- **37–43:** retain the spray/maximal-geodesic identification, compatible Riemannian metric, smoothness and finite-dimensional hypotheses where used. Compact confinement applies to a compact speed subbundle, not by declaring the whole tangent bundle compact.
