## Remaining arithmetic errors

### R1. Enforce Selmer support before promising the same dual support

The packet's Selmer structure requires its finite support to contain the places above p and all infinite places, as well as ramification. The suggested `SelmerStructure` omits the first two requirements. Its `dual_sigma` nonetheless promises the same support for the Cartier dual. The finite representation T=F_p with trivial G_Q action and empty support meets the displayed unramifiedness fields, for odd p. Its Cartier dual is mu_p, which ramifies at p. Consequently its dual cannot satisfy the displayed outside-support unramifiedness requirement with the promised empty support. MR04's Selmer support convention (Chapter2, p. 14, and the triples on p. 19) prevents exactly this example.

Add the arithmetic coefficient scope and a field enforcing that `pInfinity p` lies in the support. That predicate already exists in the suggested file. Thread the requirement through duality, propagation, restriction of primes and every constructor. Supply typed examples excluding empty support and preserving support for a genuine finite-p or adic lattice input. A note that geometry is unavailable does not justify omitting this already-expressible guard.

### R2. Restrict the arithmetic coefficient/topology dictionary

`Rep K R` is an abbreviation for arbitrary `TopRep R (GK K)`. Several arithmetic evaluation and duality signatures therefore range beyond the finite-p-primary/discrete and complete-adic coefficient categories of MR04/MR16 and the L1/L2 supplier statements. For a nonarchimedean local field, take discrete R=T=Q with trivial action. Continuous unramified H1 is continuous Hom(Z-hat,Q)=0: a compact group's image in a discrete torsion-free group is finite and hence zero. Frobenius coinvariants are Q. The proposed `finiteEvaluation` linear equivalence is false on this input. Compare MR04 Lemma 1.2.1, p. 10, in its coefficient setting from Chapter1, p. 7.

The same missing dictionary affects the arithmetic Cartier dual, compact/discrete pairing, quotient-dual propagation, torsion identification, global length and core-rank consumers. It is not enough that the resulting expressions typecheck. In particular, the proposed general-R `dualSelmerTorsionEquiv` still accepts residual invariant vanishing alone, while the revised mathematical target now separates the stronger MR04 general coefficient hypotheses from the repaired finite self-injective version.

Define an actual arithmetic coefficient carrier or explicit predicates with mathematical content: finite residue characteristic p, the required finite/free module hypotheses, the finite discrete or adic topology, continuous action, and the correct discrete Cartier dual. Pass that data to Frobenius/tame evaluation and duality, and separate finite principal-artinian, DVR lattice and broader self-injective scopes. Use the BSS repair only within its finite coefficient hypotheses. Add typed finite coefficient and adic examples and an exclusion of the discrete rational counterexample. This central dictionary must be chosen consistently across the consumers; adding a single unused hypothesis to `finiteEvaluation` would leave the other false signatures in place.

### R3. Replace the proposed MR16 F_ur at p and infinity

The suggested `unramifiedStructure_condition` identifies its condition everywhere with `finiteLatticeCondition`, the pullback of the rational inertia kernel. `unramifiedStructure_eq_canonical_empty` records the same incorrect identification. MR16 Definition 5.1, pp. 9–10, instead takes the saturation of the intersection of corestriction images over finite unramified local extensions; the infinite-place condition is relaxed. Lemma 5.2 and Corollary 5.3 justify comparisons at their stated places and hypotheses, rather than an everywhere inertia-kernel definition.

For T=Z_p(1) over Q_p, p odd, the rational inertia-kernel condition is zero, since Q_p(1) has no inertia invariants. The unramified universal norms contain the Kummer unit subgroup, of rank one over Z_p: norms on units are surjective in finite unramified extensions. This distinguishes the two local conditions directly. The proposed consumer of MR16 Theorem 5.4, p. 10, therefore uses the wrong structure even when the module is a genuine adic lattice.

Define the finite-unramified corestriction intersection and its saturation, retain the relaxed infinite condition, and prove the away-p comparisons under Lemma 5.2/Corollary 5.3. Retarget the MR16 core-rank theorem to that structure. Add a typed local Tate-twist/unit example that would fail for the current zero condition. These changes affect a construction and its consumers, and cannot be repaired by changing its source label.
