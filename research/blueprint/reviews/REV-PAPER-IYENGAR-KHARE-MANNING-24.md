## Corrections that affect the mathematical contract

The numerical criterion retains **depth at least c+1 and positive generic rank**. Defect nonnegativity retains **grade at least c**. For Theorem 7.4, nonzero localization is weaker than positive generic rank: the equality converse needs the latter. The extraction's theorem already had the rank factor, but its note incorrectly used only nonzero localization. Both now agree.

The category C_O now explicitly uses **split O-algebra augmentations**, as required by the power-series presentation and acyclic-closure arguments. The §6.12 Ext calculation retains the full cohomology of the Tate-Hom DG complex. For dηᵢ=πχᵢ, the degree-three class χ₂η₁−χ₁η₂ survives modulo π; the printed algebra presentation loses it. The torsion-free exterior-algebra conclusion remains the intended supplier.

E34 and E35 are linked proof gaps. The cited BKM finiteness lemma has finite-free and complete-intersection/equidimensional hypotheses that are not established in the positive-defect application. The extractor's substitute equality dim Qbar=dim R∞ was not proved either. Deleting the finite-Λ_n hypothesis cannot be described as leaving the proof unchanged. The full Theorem 10.6 bundle is therefore `missing`; P7–P9 still supply its planned components. A single fixed ultrafilter and natural compatible duality maps are also needed before taking inverse limits (E39).

E42 is **rejected as written**. The proposed ℓ=5, odd-degree coefficient-field counterexample violates the standing requirement that every residual image element have its eigenvalues in k. SL₂(F₅) contains nonsplit elements. A useful replacement must distinguish a minimal projective-image field from the enlarged splitting coefficient field. This is not a certification of a nonvacuous corrected supply theorem. E41 remains valid: oddness alone does not impose the cyclotomic Frobenius value.

For the ordered degeneracy pair (π₁,π₂), the U-matrix is `[[0,q],[-1,T]]`. The matrix printed in the paper belongs to the reversed order. Both the item and its note now use the same convention. The Atkin–Lehner construction must retain the Iwahori and unchanged auxiliary-level factors.

The weight-one determinant issue E57 remains, but its proposed repair is narrower. Twisting ρ by η with η²=ψ·det(ρ)⁻¹ also changes the Frobenius trace to η(Frob)T_v. The deformation-to-Hecke map and Δ_Q action must be transported and proved compatible. The input's assertion that perfectness forces H₀ to be free over O[Δ_Q] was false and has been removed. E55 separately needs U_v membership and compatible scalar B_v–U_v identities at every auxiliary level, not just an equality of ideals at Q=∅.

E64 is a **citation gap**, not a disproof of Theorem G. Newton–Thorne Theorem 1.3 gives a nilpotent quotient with I⁴=0; its nilpotent-free Theorem 1.4 assumes strictly regular weights, excluding the trivial coefficients used here. The safe endpoint keeps Conjecture A(ii) as an explicit gate. The input's ramified example was already excluded by IKM's standing assumption that ℓ is unramified in F and has been removed.

E65 is a missing orientation input. The matrix `[[7,2],[25,7]]` has determinant −1, lies in the indicated auxiliary level and reverses the real symmetric-space orientation. The corrected duality request retains the orientation local system or requires a proved Hecke-equivariant untwisting after localization. This example does not prove that every localized untwisted self-duality is false.

## Remaining gaps

- **G1: Cited-source closure and granularity.** The 40 bibliography prerequisite requests are retained. Only the bounded supporting portions listed below were independently read. Lescot, Grothendieck local duality, Buchweitz’s precise Tate norm sequence, full DG Γ/acyclic-closure theory, quasi-complete intersections, FKR, Gross/Coleman–Voloch, Khare–Thorne and the quaternionic doubling suppliers are not a completed proof DAG. Split bundled multipart input claims when completing that DAG.
- **G2: Generic local duality ownership.** R03.3 plans depth/CM/AB/CI, but its read contract does not explicitly provide normalized dualizing complexes and Grothendieck local duality in the generality of §4. A0-extension and coherent-curve duality are adjacent geometric suppliers, not automatically this ring theorem. Establish the unique generic owner and precise extension before route 3 can be activated.
- **G3: Local-ring Tate cohomology ownership.** StablePeriodicCurved layer 2 already owns complete resolutions, total acyclicity and the Buchweitz comparison. Build on it; do not replan it. The local-ring Tate cohomology/norm exact sequence used here needs an explicit supplier contract or Part II. Finite-group Tate cohomology and finite-group/sheaf Tate proposals are different notions.
- **G4: Patching finiteness.** E34/E35 require one joint proof: construct the coefficient map for the whole functor and justify the hypotheses used in its finiteness argument without assuming global R finite over O before R=T. The extractor’s dim Qbar equality and unchanged-proof assertion were withdrawn. The full Theorem 10.6 bundle is now missing, with existing P7–P9 subcomponents imported.
- **G5: Weight-one determinant and Hecke transport.** E57 needs an actual fixed-determinant/diamond convention and its compatibility with traces, levels, covering groups, duality and deformation-to-Hecke maps. A scalar twist changes traces. Perfectness does not imply free H₀. Import the accepted CG defect-one and coherent-complex proposals, then prove the additional Shimura adapters.
- **G6: All-level scalar identities.** E55 requires U_v membership and B_v–U_v scalar identities with compatible units at every auxiliary Q_n, before patching. Neither Q=∅ ideal equality nor reduction modulo the augmentation gives this.
- **G7: Integral Galois representation for trivial coefficients.** For Theorem G keep nilpotent-free Conjecture A(ii). The cited NT1.3 has I⁴=0; NT1.4 requires strictly regular weights. A correct source for the actual coefficient system could close this gate. Ramification was already excluded by standing assumptions.
- **G8: Orientation and coherent patching duality.** For real places retain orientation coefficients or prove a Hecke-equivariant untwisting after localization. E65 does not establish failure of every localized self-duality. E39 also requires natural finite-level duality maps over one fixed ultrafilter and compatibility before inverse limits.
- **G9: Residual image-field convention.** E42 is rejected because its counterexample violates the standing splitting coefficient-field assumption. Separate the minimal projective-image field from the chosen coefficient field before supplying a nonvacuous level-raising-prime theorem. No proposed ℓ≥7 repair is certified.
- **G10: Remaining definition interfaces.** 33 definition/construction items still lack supplier-derived API outlines and three meaningful acceptance tests.
