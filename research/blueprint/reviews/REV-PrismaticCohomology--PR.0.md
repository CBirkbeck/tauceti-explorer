## The corrections that change mathematics

Each of these was re-derived by the lead reviewer.

1. **Derived crystalline comparison (`PR.2/derived-crystalline-comparison`, `PR.2/qrsp-char-p-acrys`).** The nodes took "the p-completed derived de Rham cohomology of S over A = W(k)" for the object on the crystalline side and asserted that it is the left Kan extension of crystalline cohomology of polynomial k-algebras. It is not: for S = k it is (W(k)⟨x⟩/(x − p))^∧, not W(k). BMS2 Proposition 8.12 and Theorem 8.14 are about LΩ_{S/F_p} and LWΩ_S. The nodes now use derived crystalline cohomology, defined by that Kan extension; the false identification is gone from the proof, the acceptance examples, and the requests to DD.2 and DD.4; a gap records that no stage states BMS2 8.13–8.14.
2. **The flat topology (`PR.1/prismatic-structure-sheaf`, `relative-prismatic-site`, `prismatic-to-etale-morphism`).** With covering sieves "those containing a flat cover", the two projections of a product of prisms do not cover and the functor to the étale site is not cocontinuous. The topology is the one generated by finite families that are jointly completely faithfully flat, as in Bhatt–Lurie Remark 4.1.2.
3. **Framed q-PD data (`PR.6/framed-q-pd-datum`, `framed-q-de-rham-complex`, `q-de-rham-comparison`).** The node said a morphism "need not be injective on coordinates". For D⟨X_0, X_1⟩ → D⟨X⟩ one has ∇_q(X_0X_1) ↦ 2X dX and ∇_q(X²) = (1+q)X dX, so such a map is not a map of complexes. Morphisms are injective on coordinates; in the proof of Theorem 16.22 only the cofaces are maps of complexes, and the argument is rewritten for that.
4. **Formula (9.3) (`PR.4/syntomic-complex`).** Z_p(n)(R) ≃ (φ^{−1}(d)^n A → A, φ/d^n − 1) was stated for every generator d, "independently of d". For R = Z_p^cycl, n = 1, the generator d = [p]_q gives H⁰ ∋ q − 1, and d′ = u[p]_q with u ∈ Z_p^×, u ≢ 1 mod p, gives H⁰ = 0. The formula is stated for generators adapted to a trivialisation of the twist.
5. **Weight one (`PR.4/syntomic-low-weights`, `picard-perfectoid-uniquely-divisible`).** "Pic(R)^∧[−2] ≃ Z_p(1)(R) for every quasisyntomic R" contradicts part (3) of the same node and fails for R = F_p(t), where H² of the right side contains T_p Br(R). It holds for perfectoid R, the case used.
6. **Perfection of a prism (`PR.0/prism-perfection`).** "d is a nonzerodivisor of colim_φ A" is false: for A = Z_p[[u]] ⊕ ∏_i Z_p[[u]]/(u^{p^{i+1}} − p), d = u − p, a bounded prism, the image of d kills a nonzero element. d is a nonzerodivisor after p-adic completion, which is what the lemma needs.
7. **The unbounded-torsion example (`PR.0/unbounded-torsion-example`).** In the ring as printed, f·x_{1,1}² = 0 ≠ x_{1,1}². The x_{i,j} must span a square-zero ideal; the packet, its API and the Lean model now say so.
8. **A missing lemma (`PR.0/witt2-derived-pullback-square`, added).** The proof that Δ_{R/A} is a δ-ring when it is discrete (BS22 Lemma 7.7 (1)) uses the pullback square for W_2 of a simplicial ring from Remark 2.5, which the packet's gap said no target uses.
9. **Three requests asked for false statements** (to DD.0 for Lemma 4.8 without J-completeness, to DD.1 for a residue-field criterion of flatness, to DD.0 for "surjectivity on H^{−1}" as a property of every surjection of quasiregular semiperfectoid rings); each now states what is true and used.
10. Smaller ones, each in the list below: Theorem 3.10's proof now shows that ker θ is principal; the A_inf → A_crys base change is along φ, not the inclusion; "very small" includes "small"; φγ_i = γ_iφ, not γ_i^pφ; ξ is not p times a unit modulo q − 1; the I-adic filtration of the Lη factorisation is Z-indexed; the key case of the Nygaard filtration is over Z_p[ζ_{p^∞}]^∧ and needs André's lemma to reach a general perfectoid base; PR.7's embedding of 𝔖 in A_inf is the Frobenius twist of Kisin's.
