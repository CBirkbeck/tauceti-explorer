### The nonsplit factorization and the exact remaining Hecke problem

In the generic conditional group identity, `t ∈ P` is necessary. Take a nontrivial group G, P={1}, opposite subgroup and H both G, nonidentity t, k=t⁻¹ and k′=1. Both assumed H factorizations hold, P intersects the opposite subgroup trivially, and ktk′=1 lies in P. The proposed conclusion would nevertheless force t=1. The missing premise is now present in both the reader argument and the Lean theorem. Its negative control elaborates with a complete elementary proof. In the intended application t′ belongs to T⊆P.

The stronger repair attempted by revision 3 does not work in general. Let L/ℚ_p be unramified quadratic, take G=Res_{L/ℚ_p}GL₂ and splitting field F=L, and identify G_F with GL₂×GL₂. Rational points are (g,σg). Set K_p=Iw(GL₂(𝒪_L)), m=n=1, w=1, P=B×GL₂, and H=K_p𝒢¹_{1,1}. Modulo p² the two lower-left entries of an element of H remain Galois-conjugate: the analytic depth factor changes those entries only by p². For H∩P both lower-left entries therefore vanish modulo p². They also vanish for H∩(U_-×1), whose second matrix is the identity. Products of the two intersections miss (u_-(p),u_-(p)), which belongs to K_p⊆H. Hence H need not factor in the required parabolic/opposite-unipotent order. This control is now a reader Check and a packet test.

The normality of the analytic depth subgroup and formation of its product with K_p are retained. The separate root vanishing claimed in Boxer–Pilloni Lemma 4.6.20 remains invalid because nonidentity unipotents can cancel; its replacement cancellation proof is valid only under the stated extra factorizations. No counterexample to the actual projected Levi-image identity with rational t is asserted. That identity, with its quasi-split group, splitting-field parabolic, K_{p,m′,b′} and depth inequalities, is retained as an exact formula with its scope stated in our own words as the seventh proof obligation. A Galois-compatible argument is needed. Boxer–Pilloni Example 4.6.10 already illustrates why the projected compact group need not be a product Iwahori.

### Hartogs endpoints, locality and finite limits

Scholze Lemma 3.2.10 allows ε=0. At that endpoint the relation uf=p^ε makes f invertible; the positive-ε u-adic completion argument cannot be reused. For f=u=1 the u-adic completion is zero. The corrected proof separates ordinary localization, normal Hartogs on the special fibre, and p-adic lifting at ε=0, from the positive-radius completion argument. The conclusion is retained; E45 records the failed uniform proof step in the published text. Likewise the characteristic-p ε=0 application inverts the Hasse invariant while keeping a topologically nilpotent scalar parameter, rather than rescaling it to 1.

Good-triple completeness at t=0 uses sheaf completeness and sections preserving inverse limits on arbitrary opens. The open complement of a closed subset was not proved quasicompact. Independence of the chosen scalar radius uses comparison with powers. Trace self-duality is an isomorphism with Hom, without asserting finite projectivity over a singular base. The finite-étale variant avoids the codimension-two hypothesis and includes the curve application. The characteristic-p scalar fields are explicitly completed.

The rigidified spaces need only be separated and locally of finite type; finite quotient/torsor arguments are applied on invariant qcqs opens above affinoids. Nonproper analytic modular curves are not globally quasicompact. In the Siegel glueing argument, inverse-image quasicompactness is asserted for a cofinal family of quasicompact neighbourhoods of the compact rational flag locus. The log-site finite-cover argument uses the actual quasicompact-cover property of that site before lifting points; quasicompactness alone would not extract a finite cover from arbitrary image subsets. It does not use the separate universal-openness gap needed for density.

The finite inverse-limit arguments now give a uniform bound from a fixed initial deck group. In the adjoint comparison this is Γ/Γ-triple-prime after taking a normal core. At fixed tame level in the abelian comparison it is Δ₁. The fixed-tame stabilized finite Δ_{K^p} is distinguished from the full adelic profinite Δ. Injections between finite groups of unbounded order alone would prove neither claim.
