## The findings

- **/1 (high): repeated CM fields.** Definition 5.1 has "a collection of CM-fields", with no distinctness, and model-ring
  adds it. Over F_5, y² = x³ + x and y² = x³ + 4x have 4 and 8 points. They are ordinary, not isogenous, and both have
  Frobenius field Q(i), so E × E′ needs a repeated field.
- **/2 (filed high; I would grade it medium): Lee's equation (11).**
  - **The definition.** Lee's d′ is the product over pairs of distinct root values counted with multiplicity, and (11)
    bounds it by a Vandermonde estimate that only holds when no roots repeat.
  - **The counterexample.** For E^g with E : y² + y = x³ over F_2 (3 points, Frobenius X² + 2),
    |d′| = 2^{3g²} > (2g)^{2g}·2^{g(2g−1)}, already at g = 16.
  - **The severity.** This affects only the optional 45/4 route, not the main chain.
- **/3 (medium): the p-Weil restriction.** §5.4.1 assumes that K is generated by a p-Weil number, and the model-count and
  model-concentration items drop that assumption. Quadratic 2-Weil fields are only Q(√−2), Q(√−7) and Q(i).
- **/4 (medium): unregistered corrections.** The corrected compact subgroup and the narrow class group are not registered
  as sourceIssues. Q(√3) has class number 1 but narrow class number 2.
- **/5 (medium): E17 overstates.** For p ≥ 5 the Hasse intervals force a = b = 1, so Lemma 5.19's printed proof is valid
  there and fails only at p = 2, 3. E17 calls it wrong for every p.
