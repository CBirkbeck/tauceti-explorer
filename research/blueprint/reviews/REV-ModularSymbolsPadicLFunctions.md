## Mathematical corrections

- **Boundary sequence and descent.** The explicit base-cusp cocycle constructs the map to native H¹. Direct kernel and parabolic-surjectivity arguments replace a wrongly covariant coefficient long exact sequence and an unsupported first-variable Ext shortcut. Normal descent uses Γ∩Γ(M), M≥5, rather than Γ∩Γ₁(M); its finite-coset transfer and cusp double-coset identities are stated as proof obligations. The pinned inflation–restriction theorem supplies only its actual three terms.
- **Odd weights.** Write μ for the projective index and c_k for the sum of cusp invariant dimensions. Negative parabolic lifts have no odd-degree invariants, so odd degree subtracts regular cusps only. Γ₁(4), degree one, has μ=6 and two regular cusps, giving parabolic dimension zero; subtracting all three cusps gives −1. The current ModularForms 10C already owns the corrected forms dimension formula. The boundary-dimension and Eichler–Shimura arguments now use it.
- **Period comparison.** Current BinaryForms and periodMap′ are imports. The row-adjugate action requires a transpose dictionary; the determinant pairing requires Q(−Y,X), whose degree-one value is −1, rather than an unsigned swap. Current periodMap′ uses the raw integral, whereas this packet uses 2πi times the integral. Added `L0/period-cocycle-injectivity`: Wiese Propositions 6.13–6.14, pp.66–70, and the proof of Theorem 6.15, pp.70–71, give relative cup-product/Petersson detection of the combined holomorphic and antiholomorphic map. Separate injectivity of its summands would not suffice.
- **Character and Hecke hypotheses.** Γ₁ Hecke coefficients must include the bad-prime matrices. Diamond operators themselves require Γ₀ coefficients, while their Hecke and infinity compatibilities require the corresponding extensions. Over an arbitrary ring parity gives an annihilation relation; distinct parity values alone need not force zero. Character decomposition uses the specified characters from a cyclotomic splitting algebra, avoiding extra mixed characters over product rings.
- **Base change and eigenspaces.** Finite coset Manin equations work for every finite-index group and require no finite presentation of the coefficient module. The pinned multiplicity-one theorem covers the newspace only. The full primitive-level eigenspace therefore imports old/new separation and cross-level uniqueness from ModularForms 4/5. Polynomial boundary spectral projection establishes lifting of quotient eigenspaces; zero intersection with the boundary alone would not establish it.
- **Lifts and ordinary integrality.** Pollack–Stevens Theorem 5.1 and Corollary 5.4, pp.21–22, give global dagger lifts and bounded lifts on nonzero generalized U_p eigenspaces, respectively. The packet no longer asserts global bounded surjectivity. Proposition 5.7, pp.24–25, uses 0≤h≤k+1; this bound is now explicit in both packet and Lean. The requested operator theorem is the exact field/lattice ordinary–nilpotent decomposition of Proposition 5.6, pp.23–24. The affinoid supplier's (Pr) hypothesis is not silently assumed. Ordinary integrality follows from compact-open masses and complete integral limits, so all moments are integral, including p≤k.
- **Critical slope and examples.** Bellaïche's imported theory has tame p∤N, distinct roots and decency; the signed-label dictionary includes (−1)^k. Noncritical cuspidal refinements are automatically decent by Definition 1(ii), p.5; CM decency is Proposition 2.15(iv), p.20. The primary θ-critical vector specializes to zero; the top generalized vector carries the classical coordinate. The family identity principle explicitly requires a reduced affinoid. At p=2 the torsion factor is μ₂ and the wild generator lies in 1+4ℤ₂. Numerical level-11 observations are certification targets rather than unconditional theorems; the p=11 trivial zero is exact, while μ=0, λ=1 requires an integral unit certificate.
- **Rational splitting and coefficient transport.** Elkik §III, pp.64–66, covers weight two. Shokurov Theorem 4.3 and Corollaries 4.4–4.5, pp.638–641, supply the higher-weight source input. The remaining gap is its exact coefficient/Hecke transport and the full-component arithmetic realization, not an unread-source claim. Nakamura Lemma 3.1, p.206, gives the usual/adjoint operator relation. Current ModularForms 8G owns f^σ and the rational structure; only chosen symbol coordinates and specified isometric p-adic places are transported here.
- **Tame twists and depletion.** The local tame formula requires n≥1; n=0 follows by finite additivity. Positive-slope objects are distributions, with bounded measures only at slope zero. Good/bad coefficient recurrences and levelRaise are now direct prerequisites of Euler depletion. Its scaling excludes ℓ=p.

Direct owner edges were added wherever a request was previously merely implicit. Minor test corrections exclude the zero ring from the rank assertion, allow the zero odd-weight symbol space in the diamond scalar identity, and require a nonzero integral eigenline vector. These changes and each source/locator correction are recorded individually in the packet.
