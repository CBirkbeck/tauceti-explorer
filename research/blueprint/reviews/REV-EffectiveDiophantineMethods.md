## Findings and revision requirements

### F1. ED.0 needs carrier and valuation identifications

The enclosure contracts and isolating-box mathematics are sound. The original Lean `RealRootIsolation` admitted the zero polynomial on a singleton, although the packet required a nonzero polynomial. Its record and constructor now require `polynomial_ne_zero`; `root_spec`, `root_unique` and a zero-polynomial rejection test were added. The integer Hensel certificate now permits an exact zero residual explicitly: the pinned totalized `padicValRat p 0` is zero, not infinity. The norm-based Hensel theorem already had the correct exact-root behavior.

`toPadicApproximation` is presently a ball-membership lemma, not an adapter returning CN.0's approximation carrier. `valuation-certificate` needs the identification between the height-one-prime valuation, its completion and the chosen local absolute value. Mathlib's codomain is `ℤᵐ⁰`; a uniformizer has `WithZero.exp(-1)` (the negative-one exponent in the multiplicative integers, not a real exponential). Converting this to additive order or `exp(-ord)` is a real interface obligation, including normalization by the ramification index. CN.2's prime decomposition does not itself prove that dictionary. The existing finite-extension remaining item is honest and remains open; the split-prime dictionary is an additional gap.

For the height formula, `charpoly_leftMulMatrix` at the pin is a theorem about a **power-basis generator**. The arbitrary-element formula requires a restriction-of-scalars/tower characteristic-polynomial theorem and basis invariance. The packet's mathematical height formula is correct, but naming the generator theorem does not discharge this bridge. Relative versus absolute height tests, including the golden ratio, are correct. The Q(i), D=1 boundary test is not rejected.

### F2. ED.1 needs a raw finite verifier and an accessible enumeration source

The real and p-adic reduction inequalities match the sources, including the negative p-adic target. Nonnegative coordinate bounds were added to both exclusion records: the geometric box estimates require them. The integral-target test now says there is **no positive lower bound**, which allows distance zero without claiming that no lower bound exists.

`RealExclusionCertificate.check` takes an already proof-bearing certificate. Its Gram–Schmidt and approximation fields involve real inequalities; it does not implement the promised Boolean check of raw finite rational/integer fields. Define the raw schema, computable checks, and `check = true → Valid` theorem, then show that the supplier produces the raw schema. A conditional soundness lemma on validated data remains useful, but is a different deliverable. Fincke–Pohst's primary algorithm could not be read independently; both enumeration nodes remain unverified against that source. This is an access limitation, not evidence that their mathematics is false.

### F3. ED.2 has several weaker computational prototypes

The Matveev and Yu hypotheses and constants were checked at their stated primary locators. The packet already has admissibility predicates for the analytic constants and already deletes zero logarithms from the Thue family; these are **not** missing hypotheses. The bound `n(n-1)(n-2)` is now justified explicitly by successively adjoining three distinct roots of deflated polynomials. Three unrelated degree-n adjunction estimates would give only `n³`.

Discrete-log pivots must be chosen among nonzero linear coefficients; a zero constant cannot be treated as an incompatible finite valuation. The Lean record now requires primality and exponent ranges. A modulus-six residue has behavior that would contradict the prime-power argument if primality were omitted; the zero-coefficient regression exercises the normalization. Finite extensions and noncyclic residue groups remain explicitly outside the present producer.

The exponent-reduction chain is scalar in Lean, whereas the packet advertises componentwise boxes. Its representation and invariants must agree with the per-prime updates. `ThueFactorCovering` needs nonzero representatives and a norm/ideal-factorization producer; a `Finset K` allowing zero is weaker than the packet's `Kˣ` representatives. The generic factory must state the actual degree, root and norm/divisor hypotheses required by its construction. The Thue and Thue–Mahler certificate tests must construct the promised finite data instead of receiving the main covering or complete output as an assumption.

The `v=0` Thue–Mahler compatibility is correctly restricted to coprime solutions in the packet; it is not a reduction of every Thue solution. For `X³-2Y³=8`, `(2,0)` is a simple excluded nonprimitive example. Preserve this scope in the reader. The degree-one ideal-factor test should assert its intended exponent zero, rather than a weaker existential exponent identity. The exact moderate/small-solution searches and the semantic completeness implications otherwise match the stated contracts.

### F4. ED.3 abstracts away the local descent computation

`LocalDescentImage` stores points and a square-class isomorphism; it does not construct the actual local étale factors, valuations, residue square classes or rational-abscissa descent map. Several numerical tests receive their cardinality as a hypothesis. The local cardinality theorem and good-place identification need the genuine elliptic/local carriers, then the finite producer can prove that its local image has the required size. The cardinality-equality completeness argument itself is valid.

Increasing the bad-place set changes `A(S,2)`. The suggested `selmer_mono` assumes that the two ambient sets are equal, so it does not supply the advertised inclusion under enlargement of S. Give the natural ambient map and compatibility with all local conditions. The Selmer rank identity, isogeny descent and saturation arguments are valid conditional on genuine descent maps and complete local images.

The torsion-by-reduction packet statement now requires an auxiliary prime different from each primary prime, making every displayed minimum nonempty. This condition was already present in Lean. The source torsion counts are consistent. Existing gaps for E11, the rank-zero genus-two curves, Fermigier's explicit homogeneous-space points and the corrected Poonen descent remain genuine blockers to their advertised concrete certificates.

### F5. Heights require the free quotient and finite torsion fibres

The pinned canonical height is half the Cremona/Silverman convention. ED.3 correctly uses twice the pinned value; the upstream EllipticCurves reader uses the doubled convention under the same informal name. This mismatch is recorded in `upstreamNotes`, with no upstream edit.

Silverman's primary text could not be fetched independently. Cremona Chapter III, Proposition 3.5.1, p.77, gives an accessible candidate replacement for the explicit comparison bound, including the constant 1.922. A revision must check its exact normalization and hypotheses before changing the citation. The doubling enclosure and regulator arguments are valid given an explicit validated bound.

RP.0's general heights do not supply a positive-definite canonical-height form on a Jacobian's Mordell–Weil group modulo torsion. A semidefinite form on an arbitrary additive group does not yield finite bounded enumeration: the kernel may be infinite. Construct the finitely generated free quotient, its positive-definite form and finite torsion representatives. The ED.1 enumerator then applies on the free coordinates, with each finite torsion fibre included. `InnerProductSpace.volume_ball` requires nontrivial dimension; the rank-zero branch is separate. These obligations affect ED.3's height search and ED.5's bounded-height/integral-point modes.

### F6. ED.4 needs actual curve geometry before tiny integrals

The suggested `GoodReductionChabautyDatum` has arbitrary point carriers and a set map `abelJacobi`, without the analytic or geometric conditions of an actual Abel–Jacobi map. `toGoodReductionPair` returns a product rather than the supplier's good-reduction pair. The packet's P1 test is outside its genus-at-least-one definition, and `Option ℤ_p` models the infinity disc as a single point. Replace that test by a valid genus-one case or state an explicit genus-zero extension with the full disc chart.

The previous `abelianIntegral_eq_primitive` declaration was false for the abstract datum. A discontinuous map from `pℤ_p` to the additive group `ℚ_p`, with zero reduction, meets the abstract reduction compatibility but its pairing has no analytic primitive. The theorem was **omitted**, with a precise gap and explanation, under PROTOCOL §13. Restore it only after identifying the actual smooth proper curve, its regular differentials, the geometric Abel–Jacobi morphism and formal disc chart with their suppliers' carriers. Do not restore a theorem whose conclusion is assumed in a proposition field.

The pullback API currently establishes base-point independence, not the isomorphism on regular differentials that is required. The hyperelliptic prototype does not prove that the listed differentials are an integral basis or compute their local orders. The Coleman comparison assumes the nonroutine Frobenius relation rather than deriving it from the actual integration and cohomology suppliers. The relative symmetric criterion similarly receives the main Box criterion as input. These generic arguments can be retained as supporting algebra; the missing geometric statements need genuine signatures or exact omissions/gaps.

The packet's abelian-logarithm extension was corrected: the union over finite local extensions defines a map on algebraic-closure points, not all completed `ℂ_p` points. KRZB's analytic logarithm, or its continuous extension, is required for `ℂ_p`. Its pairing has kernel zero in the differential carrier and torsion in the point carrier. The reader reverses these kernels; Siksek's pairing (4) reverses them both in the preprint p.5 and the published version p.770, now source issue E22; pairing (3) is correct on published p.769. The displayed order must always be specified.

### F7. Zero valuation must be handled consistently

For residue-disc Strassmann indices the mathematical valuation of zero is infinity; Mathlib's totalized `Padic.valuation 0` is zero. The packet now explicitly excludes a zero dominant coefficient and either excludes zero competitors or treats their valuation as infinity. `F(T)=T` has index one and a zero at the origin. It cannot have index zero merely because its zero constant coefficient has totalized valuation zero. The suggested file includes this regression. The general restricted-series Strassmann theorem is correctly nonzero and is a reasonable single owner; ordinary consequences belong in its API.

### F8. Finite certificate claims need finite checks

The Chabauty certificate's `ofRankHypothesis` receives finite index; it does not derive it from a labelled rank bound and independently certified generators. Its abstract validity predicate does not enforce the full genus/rank restrictions or construct a nonzero annihilator. State the real rank condition, prove independence, derive finite index and construct the annihilator before using the disc verdicts.

`SieveCertificate.Checked` quantifies over every natural index j, although the certificate has a finite length. Restrict verification to `j < length` and prove the resulting finite induction theorem; give finite quotient presentations and computable section/image checks. QC disc certificates need actual analytic functions, coefficient/tail data and a finite zero verifier. An arbitrary function with a universally quantified zero condition is a semantic certificate, not a finite numerical producer. No conditional soundness implication here is rejected; the claimed executable interface is unestablished.

### F9. ED.5 is over-decomposed at target level

The review issue explicitly asks for target-level density. ED.5 has separate nodes for ordinary membership, representative congruences, adding constraints, top initialization, unchanged local images, lift membership/cardinality, prepared-step containment/progress and exponent relevance. These are useful API lemmas, not separate roadmap targets. Consolidate them into the admissible-class, lifting and chain API in the revision, preserving any references through aliases or a coordinated restructuring. This review does not remove identifiers while other packets consume them.

The genuine targets—finite quotient sieve data, exact lift/filter correctness, coprime-index transfer, Jacobian reduction, bad/deep information, height/Chabauty completion and certified outputs—are appropriate. All 40 planets obey the six-per-layer and 60-character constraints and select meaningful targets. Source locator labels occur in some nonplanet titles; the planet names themselves are usable.

### F10. ED.5 geometry and explicit arithmetic remain open

The abstract finite quotient sieve is sound. Coprime-index transfer uses Bezout correctly, including modulus one; GetSubgroup does not claim that arbitrary decidable membership implies finite index. The prepared-step fix E1 is confirmed: the target quotient must be taken before the kernel. The independently read author implementation already does that. E12, E13 and E17 correct the Kummer proof estimate, coordinate projection and sufficient separating-prime product.

The upstream AlgebraicCurves model layer does not supply Cassels–Flynn Kummer formulas. Actual Mumford/Cantor arithmetic, finite Jacobian presentations, component/reduction maps and explicit height constants remain existing recorded gaps. Deep local information uses translation from the Jacobian to `Pic^1`, not an inverse of the curve embedding; the latter is not surjective in genus greater than one. Make the actual translation and curve-membership test explicit. The small genus-two experiment still needs its exact curve list and replayable certificates. These are conditional applications, not completed formal computations.

### F11. Modular rank over Q needs an exact supplier theorem

GZ.8 provides a Gross–Zagier trace formula over an admissible imaginary quadratic/CM field; HE.7 requires its real-multiplication, non-CM and Kolyvagin admissibility inputs. Neither statement alone is the shortcut "analytic rank one over Q implies the desired Q-rank" used in the application. Exhibit the quadratic field, twist factorization/nonvanishing, admissible trace and passage back to Q, or import a supplier theorem with exactly that conclusion. The existing request is a good description of a need, not its proof.

The X_s(13) interval theorem proves that the derivative is nonzero, but not that the L-value vanishes or that the analytic rank is exactly one. Actual analytic continuation, central vanishing and certified derivative evaluation must be connected to the correct newform. The conditional Stoll six-cycle application also needs a certified nonzero reported analytic derivative; weak BSD alone does not prove that numerical premise. Preserve both the analytic and arithmetic condition labels.

### F12. QC Hodge and precision statements need stronger interfaces

The signs, Laurent carriers, gauge calculation, block multiplication matrices and local-height formulas match the cited texts with E15/E16 and the source extraction's corrections. A general Hodge algorithm cannot bound gamma's poles or the required Laurent truncations solely by the largest individual differential pole order: products of primitives occur. The unsupported bound was removed and a precise finite truncation/pole-bound gap added. The X_s(13) `H⁰(X,O(2D))` space is a concrete separate case.

The root perturbation statement now specifies restrictedness of `F(pT)`, positive `n-k`, a positive root-count bound, multiplicities over `ℂ_p` and the zero-valuation convention. Source E21's tail correction remains confirmed. A necessary congruence for an existing root does not prove root-cluster existence, multiplicity or isolation. `Polynomial.roots` lists exact roots over its coefficient field; it is not a modulo-p^n root enumerator and it does not manufacture `ℂ_p` roots. Give the actual Newton-polygon/Weierstrass perturbation and isolation interface.

NC.2 and NC.5 do not yet provide the requested connection/height geometry at node level; SF.3 is unplanned and RP.1 remains partial. RD.7's existing certified Kedlaya interface does not supply Tuitman's general plane-curve algorithm automatically. Requests are retained, and none of the seven stages is declared closed. Every generic QC formula must be linked to those genuine carriers before its numerical certificate factory is considered complete.

### F13. The explicit examples need concrete certificates, not assumed results

The X_s(13) quartic substitution identity is correct; it was checked by exact integer evaluation, and the seven given points lie on the quartic. Its Lean theorem states only that algebraic identity, point membership and homogeneity. It omits the modular identification, good-reduction certificate, twenty-point special-fibre count and complete charts promised by the packet. These omitted targets need explicit signatures or precise gaps.

The generated cubic-field dimension theorem is not an identification of `End(J_s(13))⊗Q`, nor a Neron–Severi rank proof. A positive derivative interval is not an analytic-rank-one certificate. The first-chart Hodge test uses a polynomial ring while assuming the quartic relation: use the curve's coordinate-ring quotient or function field to avoid a vacuous example. Hensel uniqueness for a supplied Frobenius lift does not construct its actual Tuitman matrix. Chart-point theorems receive the output certificates instead of giving the missing certified matrices, functions and zero tables.

The packet's `E_1(P5)` is already a p-adic Abel–Jacobi/logarithm vector. It is **not** rejected merely because a nonzero global Abel–Jacobi class might be torsion. Here the unresolved obligation is to certify this actual vector and the determinant, identify the inert local coefficient field, and connect them to the actual height pairing. The final seven-point theorem also needs the ramified P0-disc certificate, an existing gap. The X_S4(13) and X_0^+(N) examples were checked against arXiv v4, not an unread Compositio version of record; their isogeny, rank and numerical certificate inputs remain explicit.
