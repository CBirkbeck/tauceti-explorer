E8 also required a correction to the earlier reviewer’s justification. `Spec C(t) → Spec C` does not retain finite underlying fibres after every geometric field extension, so its stated role as a geometric-fibre counterexample was too strong. Instead take the square-zero algebra `B = k ⊕ V`, with V an infinite-dimensional k-vector space. Every field extension K gives `K ⊕ (K ⊗ V)`, whose square-zero ideal is its unique prime, so every geometric fibre has one underlying point. The ideal V has an infinite strictly increasing chain of finite-dimensional subideals, hence B is not Noetherian or finite type. This supplies the unrestricted counterexample needed by the finding. It does not satisfy the roadmap’s proper finite-type hypotheses; those hypotheses remain in its target.
