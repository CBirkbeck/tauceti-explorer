# Review: AdditiveCombinatorics package

Reviewer: Codex, session `codex-MNxz9s`, independent of the package author. Date: 2026-10-14. Job: REV-PKG-AdditiveCombinatorics; issue #9199.

## Verdict

**needs_changes.** The fixes below are applied to the package. Its representative Lean file elaborates, but the package is not ready for an upstream draft: supplier closure is not established, and seven groups of mathematical proof obligations in the accepted plan still lack the intermediate contracts required by PROTOCOL. The README now identifies those obligations at the relevant targets. This is a completed review, not a claim that the unresolved mathematics has been supplied.

The accepted packet has 100 nodes, 286 API items, 131 tests and eight recorded gaps. I compared all nodes with the README's 101 numbered subsections: the package splits some combined nodes and adds the nilpotent exponential/Haar and layered-cycle suppliers. No target was deleted to reduce size. The layered-cycle target closes the packet's missing directed-cycle supplier at the level of its statement, source and reduction; the other seven gap groups remain. The packet is unchanged.

## Fixes applied

- Restrict interval-norm independence of the modulus to positive order. At order zero, `N = M = 1` satisfies the former size condition, but `[1]` has no representative modulo one. The comparison would assert `1 = 0/0 = 0`. The README and Lean now retain this negative control and explain positivity of the denominator in the permitted case.
- Separate cyclic and one-sided interval generalised von Neumann estimates. With `N = k = 2`, `f₀ = (1,−1)` and `f₁ = 1`, the interval average is `1/9` while `U¹[2](f₀) = 0`. The cyclic theorem remains linear for `k ≥ 2`, `p > k`; the interval target has `k ≥ 3` and the convex-cutoff bound `C_k(min U^{k−1})^{1/6}`. Its scalar proof belongs in AC.2.2; it does not depend on the later general module target. The density-increment tolerance is adjusted to a small multiple of `δ^{6k}`, preserving the final quasipolynomial scale. The convex-cutoff proof interfaces remain explicitly open.
- Fix a Lean generality defect in `FilteredNilmanifold`: unused section variables had disappeared from the structure's parameters. Explicit parameters now retain the finite-dimensional real model, Hausdorff topology and Lie-group hypothesis. The degree-zero test forces the ambient group to be trivial.
- Add positive dimension and positive number-of-forms hypotheses to the Lean prime-pattern endpoint, matching the prose.
- Require a positive witness for quotient periodicity; distinguish pointwise rationality, quotient periodicity and their conjunction. A constant third of a circle point has period one but is not one-rational.
- Replace an unscaled comparison of binomial and monomial smoothness norms by the denominator-cleared comparison of the polynomial-nilsequence erratum, Lemma 2.1, p. 4.
- Restrict Kai's Cramér comparison to the convex cutoff of Corollary 9.2, rather than silently adding the independent congruence cutoff from Theorem 9.1. Retain the latter for the Siegel model.
- Make the step-reduction quotient `G'/(G' ∩ ker ξ)` explicit, so the kernel has the correct domain also at step one. Specify the fractional ideal generated by the affine images in the localised local-factor construction, and require the same localisation when changing that ideal.
- Replace the planned fixed-modulus qualitative PNT supplier by the theorem in the current Tau Ceti library. Retain the stronger Siegel–Walfisz supplier for the arbitrary-log-power error in the one-form prime-pattern endpoint; qualitative asymptotics do not give that error. Explain the nonunit residue classes and their negligible prime-power contribution.
- Correct source locators: Kai's box inverse is Appendix A, pp. 59–60; D.2 is a proposition; the polynomial-orbit erratum corrects Theorem 8.6; Green–Tao's Gowers and weighted estimates use the v6 pagination; CFZ's linear forms, relative counting and relative Szemerédi results are Definition 4.2, Theorem 6.5 and Theorem 4.3.
- Strengthen individual definition tests for entropy and conditioning, minimiser setups, Bohr regularity, cubes and interval representatives, derivatives, metrics, equidistribution, rational/smooth factors, factor averages, original pseudorandomness, arithmetic linear forms and discrepancy, prime weights and both majorants, scalar/vector complexity and local factors. The README-only definition list is explicit in the closing Lean comment. The additions are statements and examples, not a development of theory.
- Restore the accepted plan's unresolved proof obligations, including Kai's two exponent-sign repairs in §9.4, p. 40. The final inverse/counting theorems are not substitutes for their missing intermediate interfaces.
