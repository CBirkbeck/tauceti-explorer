## 1. Shared Ribet construction — confirmed with a narrower import contract

Route 1's brief has no IHG import, while its own `global-basis` and
`cocycle-kappa` notes explicitly assign the general construction to IHG.1.
This is a real inconsistency in the design handoff even though those item
notes already recognize the right owner.

Published pp.856–857 identify the residue field of the height-one prime m
as E, a finite extension of Q_p. Thus chi(tau)-1 is nonzero in a field of
characteristic zero and becomes a unit in the m-local ring. Residual
multiplicity-freeness here is not the reduction modulo the uniformizer of
O_E. In particular p=2 does not make the characters coincide in this
residue field. This is the IHG.1 branch.

The common construction is visible directly in (65)–(69): Hensel supplies
an eigenbasis at tau, the two trace equations determine the diagonal
entries modulo m, and the off-diagonal multiplication equation gives

    kappa(s*t) = kappa(s) + chi(s)^(-1) kappa(t).

The finite module generated by those entries and this extension class
belong to IHG.1. DKV must still check the hypotheses for its product of
Hida representations and prove its arithmetic local statements.

The proposed density import needs care. IHG.4 supplies the general
Frobenius-density/continuity method. Published p.858 explicitly uses
closedness of Lambda_H and m_H in the Lambda-adic topology to derive
integrality and the congruence at every group element. A uniqueness theorem
for already-defined continuous determinants alone does not prove those
closedness hypotheses or construct an integral determinant. The existing
`IHG.0/continuous-determinant-dense` node has just that uniqueness scope.
Import or request the closed-subring version from the common owner and
keep the application-specific topology and finite-generation argument.

IHG.6 is a separate general theorem allowing residual coincidence, with
additional local conditions and Fitting conclusions. It gives a comparison
with the Brumer–Stark consumer I.7; it should not be advertised as an
unconditional replacement for DKV's distinct-character proof. Keep the
choice of tau in Lemmas 4.2–4.3, the refinement through the actual W_i in
Lemma 4.4, and the R/R-prime analysis in Lemmas 4.6–4.9 in the Part II.

Add the named imports to the brief and align the five item notes proposed
by the finding. Do not relabel the arithmetic specializations as already
built: IHG.1's GMA/extension-module work remains partial and IHG.4/.6 are
`not_read`. The elementary Hensel input is already in pinned Mathlib.
