## Corrections applied

- The Hahn–Banach statement now requires an ultrametric norm as well as spherical completeness. Schneider's author notes, Proposition 9.2, pp. 58–59, and Corollaries 9.3–9.4, p. 59, give the accessible source. The non-spherically-complete fallback is restricted to chosen countable orthogonal coordinates that separate points; it no longer claims extension of every functional. Ingleton remains historical attribution, not a paper that this review claims to have read.
- Colmez–Nizioł Appendix A locators now identify Propositions A.1, A.3 and A.4 and Remark A.2, pp. 58–59. The left-heart construction is explicitly identified as missing; the normed-space open-mapping theorem is not presented as its supplier. General closed coefficient subfields use the projective-limit topology; the strong-dual comparison is only claimed under its finite-extension hypotheses.
- The compact-type strong-duality source is Schneider–Teitelbaum's 1999 paper, Theorems 1.1 and 1.3 and Corollary 1.4, pp. 3–4. The later Fourier-transform paper was not a sufficient locator for that claim. Completed projective and inductive tensor foundations are now explicit gaps, including the coefficient-extension assertion in §0.1. Colmez I.1.8–I.1.9 are on pp. 6–7.
- The nonexistent Colmez Proposition II.3.5 became Proposition II.3.2. The critical Amice–Vélu–Vishik functional uses the positive derivative convention. The degree-two coefficient witness for `dδ_a` is `a−1/2`.
- The purported distribution `Σ p^(−n)δ_(p^n)` was removed as a counterexample: evaluation on the constant function already diverges. The coordinate-kernel counterexample is the genuine distribution `δ_0`, supported outside the units. Normalized additive Haar masses are separately identified as unbounded in the p-adic norm.
- Arithmetic branch evaluation now integrates `ω(x)^i⟨x⟩^k`. It equals the ordinary moment `x^k` only on the matching torsion branch. The dyadic torsion check distinguishes the trivial branch at `−1` from its odd ordinary moment.
- A clearing factor gives a ratio only on its nonvanishing character domain. A pole bound requires a denominator that is not identically zero and uses its vanishing multiplicity. In particular, a torsion clearing factor can vanish identically on an entire component. The integer-coefficient evaluation API is not claimed to evaluate the logarithm with its `1/n` coefficients.
- The evaluated Fredholm product identity is sourced to Serre §5, Corollary 1 to Proposition 7, p. 76, with Buzzard's zero extension on pp. 19–20. Buzzard Lemma 3.1 concerns a different resultant criterion. The identity is at `T=1`, without a commutation hypothesis, and is not a multiplicative identity of entire series.
- The C0 coefficient-sum functional in Suggested.lean now requires `IsUltrametricDist K`. It was false over the real field. A negative control records why the values on coordinate vectors cannot extend continuously to real c0. The finite-coordinate helper now has selected-coordinate, other-coordinate and norm tests; the README no longer attributes a nonexistent single-coordinate helper to Mathlib.
- The fixed-radius basis check computes the basis at `h=0`. The C^r check uses a sparse Mahler sequence that is continuous and fails C^1, instead of repeating a basis or coefficient characterization as its own test.
- The unsupported full solid-localization interface was cut to a classical finite-window comparison. A higher-tier QuasiAbelianFunctionalAnalysisPartII citation was removed. The full solid construction is outside the package's scope.
- The torus comparison now uses `+v_p` for slopes, opposite to the supplier's apartment translation. It distinguishes a maximal torus, its split part, the maximal compact subgroup and the valuation-image lattice. The positive-monoid spanning assertion is a missing comparison, not an imported ReductiveGroupsPartII fact.
- Layer comments in Suggested.lean now occur in README order, 0 through 4. Commented pseudo-declarations and process residue were removed. Mathematical declaration docstrings were added where absent. The C^r differentiation target moved from §1.1 to §2.1. Finite-coordinate projection targets moved to §4.1 before their operator-norm consumers. Compact analytic restriction cites the specialized finite-range approximation input rather than a later local target.

All original `example` declarations were retained. Suggested.lean now contains 194 examples, compared with 184 originally. The ten added examples cover the positive-degree resultant against zero, the real c0 obstruction, p-adic Haar growth, the derivative sign, torsion-component Mellin evaluation, an identically zero clearing denominator, the three single-coordinate computations, and the coefficient-field maximum-principle obstruction.

## Remaining work and handoff

1. **Locally convex and categorical foundations, §§0.1–0.3.** Give named constructions for the Hausdorff nonarchimedean locally convex category, the relevant inductive/projective limit topologies, strict morphisms, its quasi-abelian structure, left heart and embedding. State the exact strict-sequence/left-heart comparison before using it. The cited normed-space quotient theorem cannot construct these objects. Give the strong-topology comparison over general closed L⊆C_p separately, with its true hypotheses, or consistently restrict that claim to finite extensions. Provide genuine topology and strictness examples and non-examples. Merely listing the global carriers in Suggested.lean's closing comment does not close this mathematical gap.

2. **Completed tensors, §§0.1, 0.3 and 2.2.** Construct separated completed projective tensor products, their continuous bilinear universal property and coefficient/basis comparison. Specify the inductive tensor topology and the compact-type comparison where used. Give named APIs and at least three discriminating checks, including a pure tensor, the zero factor and a distinction from an inappropriate algebraic or simultaneous-difference topology. These prerequisites are presently replaced by the correct but insufficient statement that the c0 model is intended.

3. **Full Coleman spectral transform, §4.12.** The package supplies entire B with polynomial P at a chosen rank. It still lacks D(B,P) for an entire characteristic series P, B(0)=0, and the simultaneous truncation limit with unbounded truncation degree. Supply uniform all-radius estimates, independence of truncation choices and the infinite-operator comparison in Coleman Theorem A3.9, pp. 435–436. Fixed-degree spectral resultants and coefficientwise convergence do not prove this endpoint. Keep the finite/nonreduced and padding negative controls when extending it.

4. **Torus valuation and positive monoids, §4.15.** Supply named character-valuation, lattice-image, maximal-compact-kernel and positive-monoid spanning comparisons. State the opposite-sign comparison with ReductiveGroupsPartII RG2.1 explicitly. Give computed split one-dimensional and anisotropic examples, and a failure test that reverses the sign. The original supplier contract was false; the corrected paragraph marks the gap rather than importing a nonexistent theorem.

5. **Global analytic maps and family comparisons.** Finish the stage-to-limit analytic pullback/pushforward, multiplication and convolution constructions and the actual universal-character analytic action. Separate their names from the abstract supplied-map transposes. Construct specialization on completed coefficient stages and prove its compatibility. Replace the specialization check that simply evaluates an already assumed equality by a nontrivial family computation and a wrong-scalar/action non-example. Review the `Cr_mahler`, `measureToDist_injective` and digit-doubling checks: a repeated API theorem or an unspecified function is insufficient evidence for a plausible wrong global definition. Every new foundation definition needs its own three discriminating tests. No artificial Lean carrier or `Prop := sorry` placeholder is needed to do this work in the README.

6. **Finish dependency order and synchronize the catalogue.** The layer order and the easy projection/C^r forward references are fixed. §4.5 still states analytic resolvent entireness before its §4.6 proof, and states the final finite-projective Riesz theorem before the §4.7 kernel results it consumes. Split the formal recurrence from the analytic estimate, order finite adjugates and retraction estimates before evaluated resolvents, then topological projectors, root-kernel finite generation/projectivity, and the final Riesz/slope theorem. Preserve all tests and locators. In a separately authorized packet update, reconcile the duplicate-target removals and these explicit gaps with the 303-node input; this review did not edit the packet.
