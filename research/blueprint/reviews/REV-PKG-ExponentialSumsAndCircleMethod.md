## Fixes applied

- Strengthened `shortCharacterHypothesis` with a true case, `(n,δ)=(3,1/48)`, and a false case for every `δ>1`. The latter uses the nonprincipal real character modulo three on the singleton `(0,1]`: its sum is one, whereas the claimed bound would tend to zero as `Q` grows. For `n≥3`, choose `ε=(δ−1)/(2n)`; smaller `n` already violates the predicate. Both controls are in Lean.
- Replaced the two generic convergence/realness Checks for `waringSingularSeries` with actual values. At `(d,s,m)=(1,0,0)` its nonsummable coefficients are `φ(q)` for positive `q`, so native `tsum` is zero. At `(4,9,15)` the absolutely convergent series is zero because its two-adic factor vanishes. These distinguish totalization and local obstruction from an always-positive definition. Both controls are in Lean.
- Replaced generic Checks for `waringLocalFactor` with its value zero at `(d,s,m,p)=(4,9,15,2)`, retaining the independent values one and `4/3`. Fourth powers are zero or one modulo sixteen, so nine terms cannot represent fifteen. The finite-density/prime-power partial-sum identity gives zero from level four onward. The negative control is in Lean.
- Added `XY` as a non-example to both perfectness predicates. Every integer is admissible via `(1,k)`, but every nonzero fiber has only finitely many integral divisor pairs on a positive-dimensional geometric curve. Perfectness fails on infinitely many targets; the almost-perfect exceptional proportion tends to one. Lean already contained the corresponding negative controls.
- Made the denominator of the Gaussian determinant ratio explicit: finite Brascamp–Lieb constant implies trivial common kernel, and positive exponents and positive-definite target matrices then make the denominator matrix positive definite. The zero-dimensional determinant convention remains one.
- Added public primary-source links and author-version locators for Heath–Brown 1996, Theorem 4, pp.7–8 and Corollary 1, p.8, and Blomer–Granville, Theorem 2, p.5 and its proof in §5, pp.19–22. The latter's adapter now explicitly retains the primitive-form and automorphism-count conventions before passing to the Markoff sector lattice count. Added an anchor for the existing Bennett–Carbery–Tao reference. Updated the weighted quadratic theorem's Lean docstring to match the source locators.

No target, API or worked example was removed. No supplier packet or atlas data was edited.
