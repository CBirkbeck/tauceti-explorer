### Settled by the paper contradicting itself

| id | one place | the other |
|----|-----------|-----------|
| **E1** | p. 733: the densities of Theorem 1.2 "are equal to 1" | Theorem 1.2, p. 730: they converge to `∏(1−ℓ⁻ⁱ)/|Aut(A)|` < 1, and the events for distinct A are disjoint |
| **E2** | p. 739: the fibres are listed `Homᶜ, Sur, Surᶜ` | their descriptions are listed `cⁿ`, "elements of `cⁿ` that generate G", "elements of `Gⁿ` that generate G" — the last two interchanged |
| **E3** | p. 750: `ker(α) ↞ M ⊗_R R[U]` | (4.6.3), one line later: `R[U] ⊗_R M`, and M is a **left** module over a noncommutative R |
| **E5** | (6.1.6): `U = (|c|/|c∩Q|) U_Q` | Lemma 3.5 and p. 763 define `U_D = Σ_{g∈c} r_g^{D|g|}` and `U_Q = Σ_{g∈c∩Q} r_g^{D|g|}`, both unnormalized |
| **E9** | Theorem 8.8: "A an ℓ-group" | §8.6: "A a nontrivial finite **abelian** ℓ-group" |
| **E10** | p. 779: covers identified with `Sur(V,A)` | Proposition 8.7: "(L,α) and (L,β) are isomorphic … if and only if β = ±α" |
| **E12** | p. 782: "modify the `yᵢ`'s for **i > g**" | the basis is `y₁,…,y_g`, so nothing has index > g |
| **E14** | p. 777: `ν_n(A)` = fraction with `Cl(O_L) ≃ A` | §8.1: the space is "isomorphism classes of finite abelian ℓ-groups" |

E5 is worth a sentence more. The two definitions are unnormalized sums, so on a component of
monodromy exactly Q the terms with `g ∉ Q` enlarge the monodromy and die modulo `⊕_{Q′⊋Q}`, and each
`g ∈ c∩Q` contributes once: the surviving operator is `U_Q` with coefficient **1**. The spurious
scalar does not break the induction — Theorem 6.1 assumes `k` is a field in which `|G|` is
invertible, so it is invertible — but had it been genuine, the argument would have needed that
hypothesis for a reason the paper never gives.

### Recomputed rather than believed

- **E6.** Enumerating all 48 surjections `C₉ × C₃ ↠ C₃ × C₃`: exactly **36 have no lift at all** and
  12 have lifts — matching the finding, and matching the extraction's own
  `liftHistogram {0: 36, 9: 12}`. So the claim that the lift set "is a principal homogeneous space"
  fails, since it presupposes non-emptiness. Meanwhile
  `|Sur(X,A′)| = |Aut(C₉×C₃)| = 108 ≥ 96 = (ℓ−1)|Sur(X,A)|`, so the aggregate inequality (8.4.1)
  survives: this is a gap in the proof, not a counterexample to the lemma.
- **E15.** In `SL₂(Z/9)` the subgroup generated by `[[0,1],[8,0]]` and `[[1,7],[6,7]]` has order
  **24** and maps **bijectively** onto `SL₂(F₃)`, inside a group of order 648. Its preimage in
  `SL₂(Z₃)` is therefore a proper closed subgroup with full mod-3 image, and the unqualified
  "standard group theory argument" fails at `(g,ℓ) = (1,3)`.
- **E7.** With `ν_n = (1−ℓ⁻ⁿ)μ + ℓ⁻ⁿ δ_{(C_ℓ)ⁿ}` the total variation distance is `≤ 2ℓ⁻ⁿ` while the
  `C_ℓ`-moment is `2 − 2ℓ⁻ⁿ → 2`: convergence in distribution does not give moment convergence, so
  "equivalent" is an overstatement of what Proposition 8.3 proves.
- **E17.** Checked by hand: over `ℂ(t)` the twist `y² = x³ − t²x` of `y² = x³ − x` has
  `T_ℓ = T_ℓ(E₀) ⊗ χ`, so the arithmetic image is `{±I}` and the geometric image trivial, while
  `ℂ(t)` already contains every root of unity. The printed isomorphism would read `{±I} ≅ 1`.
- **E18.** `Ker(S(R[x]/x²) → S(R))` is the additive group of `Lie(S)`, hence abelian, so the group
  commutator taken inside it is **always trivial**: as printed, every Lie bracket would be zero. Over
  `R[ε,δ]/(ε²,δ²)` one gets `(1+εX)(1+δY)(1−εX)(1−δY) = 1 + εδ[X,Y]`, the standard repair.

### The one finding that reaches a stated result

**E11**, and it is correctly classified. Theorem 1.2 asserts its limit "as q → ∞ with q ≠ 1 (mod ℓ)",
and nothing before it restricts q — the footnote on that page defines only "imaginary" as "ramified
at ∞", and the abstract states the consequence with no condition on the characteristic. But §8.6
opens "Let ℓ be an odd prime **not dividing q**", and Theorem 8.8 — which, with Proposition 8.3, is
what the paper says implies Theorem 1.2 — assumes "ℓ an odd prime **not dividing q or q − 1**". Two
cases the printed hypothesis allows are uncovered:

- **q = ℓ^k**, excluded outright by §8.6, and where the ℓ-part of a class group in characteristic ℓ
  is not expected to follow this law at all;
- **q even**, where the whole §8 apparatus fails: quadratic extensions `K(√f)` with `f` squarefree of
  odd degree, tame G-covers for `G = A ⋊ Z/2` whose class `c` consists of involutions, and the count
  `|S_n| = 2(qⁿ − q^{n−1})` taken modulo squares.

The extraction records this as a supported-domain gap and not as a disproof, which is the right
register.

## Corrections made in place

**1. Every finding's `printed` field was replaced by a verbatim quotation.** This is the substantial
change. As recorded, ten of the nineteen were single words or bare phrases — *respectively*,
*equivalent*, *indivisible*, *ℓ-group*, *i>g*, *is an isomorphism*, *the Lie bracket*,
*a free R-module*, *principal homogeneous space*, *equal to 1* — and `scripts/errata.py` prints that
field straight into the public register as "The source says `…`". An entry reading

> The source says `respectively`; it should be `The three fibres are c^n, generating tuples in G^n, …`

cannot be checked by a reader who does not already have the paper open, and PROTOCOL §18 asks for
"what the source says, **quoted**". Each replacement was read in the published text — E5 and the
Lemma 4.6 display on rendered page images, since the text layer mangles both — and what the
extraction had recorded is preserved alongside in `printedAsRecorded`. I ran `scripts/errata.py`
once to confirm the entries now render correctly, and reverted its generated outputs.

**2.** E12's locator said *Proposition* 8.9; the paper says *Lemma* 8.9.

**3.** A `verification` block was added for this review. The extraction's own `validation`, `audit`
and `completion` blocks are untouched.

## Two things I did not change

- **E7's `affects`.** It is recorded as reaching the proof. The sentence is expository — it is about
  the Cohen-Lenstra conjecture over ℚ, not the function-field theorem — and no proof in the paper
  uses the converse implication, so I would have said it reaches no further than the sentence itself.
  I left the classification and recorded the qualification in the verdict.
- **The unit-test vocabulary.** Tests carry kinds `sanity`, `edge`, `negative` with an `expected`
  field, rather than PROTOCOL §12's `computation | degenerate | compatibility | characterisation |
  non-example` and its `statement`. §12 governs blueprint nodes rather than paper items, so this is a
  note for the design job, not a defect.
