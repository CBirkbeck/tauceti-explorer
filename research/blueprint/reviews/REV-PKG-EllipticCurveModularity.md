## Independent example checks

Exact rational arithmetic independently reproduced the discriminants and c₄
values of all nine displayed curve models. Addition gave order five for (5,5)
on 11a1 and order seven for (1,0) on 26b1. The good-reduction counts at 3 gave
the nonsquare discriminants modulo 7 used to exclude rational 7-isogenies in
11a1 and the valuation-only example. For the isogeny-only example I checked
that x³ − 3x² + 3 is irreducible, divides the seventh division polynomial, and
is preserved by the duplication rational function with denominator coprime
to the cubic. These computations distinguish deletion of each exceptional-set
clause and rational subgroups from rational generators.

Point counts at all odd good primes through 43 agreed for 11a1, 11a2 and 11a3,
and verified the indicated −1-twist character. Expanding the eta product
independently reproduced the good-prime coefficients through 43, including
a₂ = −2 and a₃ = −1. These are finite mathematical checks, not proofs of
the infinitely quantified Lean examples. The genus formula gives dimensions
1, 2 and 2 at levels 11, 22 and 23 respectively; the level-22 oldspace thus
requires two copies. I checked the quadratic coefficient-field example against
the accepted plan and the old/new and embedding argument, including why one
mismatched prime alone would not disprove almost-all-prime attachment.

Cremona's primary [ecdata documentation](https://johncremona.github.io/ecdata/),
Tables 5, 6 and 8, and data at commit
`25cec5ecfec8b9f016eb1631ac633194c2bed39f` confirm the optimal models, degrees
1 and 2 for 11a1 and 37a1, and the degree-five isogeny from 11a1 to 11a3.
The rank-one Hom degree formula gives the minimum five for the last curve.
The exact statements retain the chosen quotient/isogeny distinction.
