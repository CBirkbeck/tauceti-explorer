## Required changes

### 1. Finish the definition and construction interfaces

Protocol §13 requires each definition/construction, its named API and its tests
to occur as Lean signatures and examples; §20 carries this requirement into the
package. The standard nonexhaustive header does not turn comments into those
interfaces. After the corrections in this review, these eight required API
names still have no Lean declaration:

| Target | Missing API |
| --- | --- |
| O0/meromorphic-basis-monodromy | `MeromorphicBasisMonodromy.identityMonodromy` |
| U0/cover-complex-structure | `CoverComplexStructure.projection`, `.chart`, `.topologyComparison` |
| F0/power-stabilizer-group | `PowerStabilizer.discConjugate` |
| F0/index-two-supergroup | `TriangleSupergroup.triangle`, `.orientation` |
| G0/exceptional-cusp-set | `ExceptionalCuspSet.horoballContainment` |

The `CoverComplexStructure` construction itself is only a block comment.
Its three tests (`discCover`, `annulusCover`, `sheetTransition`) are comments,
and `TriangleSupergroup.twoAreas` has no Lean example. The file has 52 examples:
50 of the 54 planned cases and two additional group-index/rotation examples.
Extra examples do not supply the four missing cases.

For the cover charts, use the existing universal-cover carrier and quotient
topology. Current Tau Ceti already has the generic lifted atlas and manifold
projection, so this is a complex-plane specialization and compatibility task.
For the triangle and horoball interfaces, connect actual regions and native
hyperbolic measure to the supplier's polygon/cusp data. An arbitrary area
function or a record of desired propositions would not repair these omissions.
For identity monodromy, state continuation around an actual small punctured
loop on the genuine solution-germ space.

The eight missing names were checked against declarations after removing block
and line comments and resolving namespaces, then against the file itself.
Autogenerated `LinearEquation.mk` and `MeromorphicBasisMonodromy.basis` are
present and were counted as API; they are not missing declarations.

### 2. Replace the remaining theorem specifications with signatures

The file still replaces numerous named mathematical targets by `Specification`
comments. Concrete examples include O0/global-solution-dimension;
U0/hyperbolic-uniformization-input and U0/projective-deck-realization;
S0/quotient-ode, /hempel-rational and /accessory-constraints;
L0/blaschke-zero-removal, /radial-smoothing and /quotient-lower-bound;
H0/transformed-equation, /special-solutions, /zero-balanced-continuation,
/digamma-difference-bound and /pochhammer-remainder;
R0/log-gamma-taylor, /log-radius-series and /positive-radius-expansion;
F0/ideal-polygon, /primitive-cusp-width, /free-deck-presentation and
/quadrilateral-domain; and G0/uniform-ratio-asymptotic, /cusp-height and
/exceptional-set-exclusion.

Some other signatures express only part of a target: `LinearEquation.initial_value`
gives existence and uniqueness, without the promised linear isomorphism of jets
and germs; `PointedCover.schwarz` gives the derivative inequality without its
equality characterization or the full based-lifting assertion. Match the
README's whole assertion, preserving the existing complex, germ, covering and
projective carriers. Leave an unavailable condition honestly unstated rather
than adding an empty placeholder, but record that omission and obtain a revised
package review; successful elaboration of the remaining declarations does not
satisfy the omitted targets.

These are interface-design and target-completion changes, beyond the local
corrections made below. No proof of an admitted target is demanded by this
package review. The defect is the missing or weaker statement.

## Corrections made in place

1. **Punctured-domain hypothesis.** The submitted
   `LinearEquation.puncturedSolutionGerms E α` required a representative with
   `x ∈ Ω` eventually on the deleted neighbourhood of α, but did not assume
   that Ω contains such a neighbourhood. For example, Ω can be the unit disc
   and α=2; then no representative satisfies membership, including zero.
   Its admitted `zero_mem'` was consequently a false assertion for legitimate
   parameters. Added the concrete hypothesis `Ω ∈ nhdsWithin α {α}ᶜ` to the
   submodule constructor, carried it in `MeromorphicBasisMonodromy.puncturedDomain`,
   and passed it to the basis and pole-bound API. The README now states this
   domain condition explicitly. This is a genuine domain hypothesis, not an
   unspecified proposition standing in for a missing definition.
2. **Existing cover-manifold supplier.** Current Tau Ceti's
   `TauCeti.Geometry.Manifold.Instances.UniversalCover` supplies
   `TauCeti.UniversalCover.instChartedSpace`, `instIsManifold` and
   `isLocalDiffeomorph_proj`. Its model field is general, so it specializes to
   the complex model. The README now consumes these results and
   `IsLocalHomeomorph.chartAt_chartedSpaceComap_apply` in
   `TauCeti.Geometry.Manifold.Instances.Comap`. General compatibility of
   induced atlases belongs to DifferentialGeometry Layer 2.3. Clarified that
   uniqueness means atlas compatibility, not equality of chosen chart records.
   The suggested comment records the same ownership; the current supplier
   modules are absent from the pinned build and were not copied or reimplemented.
3. **Riemann-map comparison.** Added `conformalRadius.riemannMap` using the
   pinned native `TauCeti.IsNormalizedRiemannMapOn` and its individual import.
   A private replacement normalization is unnecessary.
4. **Companion equivalence.** The matrix definition and one-way jet lemma did
   not express the full correspondence. Added `LinearEquation.companion_equiv`
   for analytic vector solutions, with the actual unscaled initial jet.
5. **Examples and identification.** Added the planned names to the formerly
   unnamed ODE, germ, radius, roots-cover, descent and group examples.
   Strengthened the second-order test with the jet coordinates; the ordinary
   point test with analytic basis representatives; the roots-cover test with
   its radial cusp limit and omitted interior value; and the descent tests
   with a genuine local inverse and the specified `IsBigO` expansion.
   The group-order test now tests membership of powers modulo the deck
   subgroup, not just order in the ambient projective group. The reflection
   example now excludes the actual half-plane reflection from the PSL action.
6. **Rotated exceptional set.** Added `ExceptionalCuspSet.rotatedLargeValues`.
   Replaced its reciprocal-only threshold example with the required value at
   the inverse half-rotation. This retains the distinction between S and sS.
7. **Source locators.** The Gauss equation was attributed to (5.1.19), which
   defines a ratio rather than that differential equation. Its locator now
   points to the Gauss-equation verification in the proof of Lemma 5.1.16,
   p.672. The descended equation is (5.1.17), p.671, with its derivation on
   p.672; corrected that locator as well.

## Mathematical conventions and source checks

Read Calegari–Dimitrov–Tang,
[*The unbounded denominators conjecture*](https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~fcale/papers/UDC.pdf),
JAMS 38 (2025), 627–702, DOI
[10.1090/jams/1053](https://doi.org/10.1090/jams/1053), in the cited locations:
§1.1.6 (p.632), Corollary 2.0.5's proof (p.636), Lemma 2.3.1 and its proof
(pp.639–641), and §§5–6 (pp.667–684). The author-hosted published offprint
matches SHA-256 `867026fbcc5592728173e5c4d6a87f58d57a55b0d0d8b0109b9774e84290ee1e`.

Checked the package's phase and branch guards, N≥2, positive derivative
normalization, primitive-width/full-group conventions, centre pole term,
representative-continuity guard and both small- and large-radius ranges.
In particular:

- For the native left action and C(z)=i(1+z)/(1−z), the displayed rotation
  matrix has derivative at i equal to exp(−2πi/N). Thus it corresponds to
  multiplication by ζ_N⁻¹, as the package correctly states. Its inverse gives
  the positive rotation; reversing the complete family of conjugate generators
  leaves the subgroup unchanged. This is an accepted-packet convention
  correction, not a new claim about an error in CDT.
- The zero-balanced leading constant follows from ψ(1)=−γ, and the coefficient
  uses ψ(k+1)−ψ(k+a). The package retains both corrections, compatible with
  native digamma identities.
- The horoball estimate uses the reciprocal of D(a²+c²), and Shimizu is applied
  to the full power-map group. Its combination gives 4h_N²/D. Large values
  belong to the rotated exceptional set. The outer mean radius is (1+r)/2.
- The local characteristic retains the log r centre correction. A punctured
  removable germ is not automatically a pointwise analytic scalar function.
  The proved spike regression and native order/continuity interfaces retain
  this distinction.
- Effective constants, especially finite-level completion, remain explicit
  mathematical proof obligations. A bare fixed-level asymptotic does not
  supply the small-radius or effective conclusion in the README.

The original Hempel, Goluzin, Carathéodory and fixed-puncture growth proofs were
not independently acquired in this review. The package identifies their CDT
invocations and asks for their proofs; this review does not certify those
unread originals or claim their results are already formalized. The accepted
plan's 15 recorded proof gaps were read and retained as work targets. They are
not themselves a reason to reject a roadmap specification. The defects above
concern its interfaces and supplier ownership.
