## Mathematical corrections

1. **Completed differential comparison.** M99 Lemmas 11.1–11.2 and Proposition 11.3, author-copy pp. 57–62, construct the cyclotomic inertia quotient and its continuous dual using completed extension sums. After completed scalar extension to the completed algebraic integers and inversion of p, the comparison has both a logarithmic differential summand and `F_0 = H¹(𝒳,O_𝒳)` for the stable integral model. The projection to completed differentials kills the weight-zero part. The original claim that the entire `C_T` embeds was false; the corrected statement retains the kernel and embeds only the quotient after the stated scalar operations. The uniform bound must be independent of the finite puncture set. The suggested omission ledger now states this obligation correctly.
2. **Target splittability.** M99 Proposition 7.4, pp. 43–45, requires irreducible splittability of the target disk-cover family. The induced source family inherits it. The previous source-only hypothesis could not justify FI⇒F on the target. The corrected hypothesis and first proof step retain the common normal disk refinement, section, geometrically irreducible fibres and Definition 6.4 factorization.
3. **Degree normalization without a cycle.** M96 Lemma 9.1 and the independent cover reduction inside Theorem 9.2, pp. 613–619, allow singular reduction before the reconstruction part of Theorem 9.2 is used. After sturdy reduction, a tree graph can acquire a loop by choosing nontrivial prime-to-p component covers, splitting node fibres over a constant extension and gluing them with parallel edges. Trace normalization tracks the cover degrees. Added the sturdy-cover, node and geometric-duality inputs, making the route independent of `local-special-isom`.
4. **Canonical relations and effectivity.** M99 Lemmas 13.1–13.3, pp. 65–68, use geometrically regular disk completion to prove normality and a special nodal-chart divisor calculation to make the graph Q-Cartier. Normality alone does not make arbitrary Weil divisors Q-Cartier. Exposed that calculation and the completed target in the all-degree relation statement. Added centre inputs at effectivity and subsequent Hom descent so arithmetic inner conjugacy is refined at the correct point.
5. **Locators.** Corrected M96 Definitions 2.1/2.4/2.5/2.7, Lemma 2.6 and Corollary 2.8; Proposition 4.2; Lemmas 6.2–6.3 and Proposition 6.4. Corrected T97 Proposition 2.8, Lemma 2.9, Corollary 2.10, the division of §3 into propositions and Remark 3.6, the lemma/claim distinctions in §4, and Lemma 4.4/Corollary 4.5. Corrected M99 Definition 4.3. Exact pages are in each node and the table below.
6. **Relative pro-p API.** Added the continuous quotient map `mk`, the lift into a target with pro-p geometric kernel, its computation rule and uniqueness. Projection surjectivity requires surjectivity of the original projection. The continuous geometric-kernel identification uses compact Hausdorff source and Hausdorff arithmetic base. The native signatures and tests keep the full arithmetic quotient.
