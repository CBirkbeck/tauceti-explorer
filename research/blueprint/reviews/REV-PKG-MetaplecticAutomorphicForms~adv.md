Corrections with mathematical consequences:

- **Theta norm and projection (8.21, 8.27).** With ordinary Lebesgue measure, W=Zλ+ρ contributes detY. Orthogonality in ρ leaves R=ν+2ak, and completing the square leaves exp(−4πa Y[λ+R/(2a)]). The translated λ boxes tile R². The Gaussian integral is 1/(4a√detY); multiplying by detY gives √detY/(4a). Thus the norms at Y=I and a=1,2 are 1/4,1/8, and the projection multiplier is 4a/√detY. This differs from the displayed norm in BFH Proposition 2.1, printed p.551. The finite theta S-transform retains its separate 1/(2a) factor. Added concrete Gaussian checks and signatures. The full Fourier unfolding constants still need reconciliation; see blocker B4.
- **Jacobi covariance (6.17).** An arbitrary pair of representations is insufficient for the semidirect action. The retained signature requires ω(g)ρ(n)=ρ(act(g)n)ω(g). The two matrices diag(1,−1) and coordinate swap have opposite (0,1) entries after reversing multiplication. This negative control appears in the README and Lean.
- **Direction (0.7–0.8, 0.13).** Native `e.trans f` applies e first, while group multiplication `e*f` applies f first. At the zero form on Q², diag(2,1) and coordinate swap send (1,1) to (2,1) or (1,2), respectively. The README and a proved Lean example now pin this order.
- **Generality.** `lerayGraphForm`, `thetaCoinvariantRelation_eq_zero`, `centralSignFunctions`, `halfWeightEigenfunctions` and `genuineThetaLift_central` are labelled by what their algebraic signatures actually express. They do not masquerade as the full reduced Leray form, smooth theta module, automorphic space, Maass space or normalized induced comparison.
- **Unbound analytic conclusions.** Removed the 13 full-target signatures listed below that quantified over independently supplied coefficients, seeds, sections or transforms without tying them to the actual BFH arithmetic family. Their full README targets and explicit gaps remain. Added controls for the Euler ratio, polynomial bound and constant-term identity.
- **Discriminating examples.** Added explicit Gaussian translation/modulation examples for the real L² model, an explicit positive coefficient value for the ideal theta series in place of the equality-under-equal-data example, and the norm/covariance/independent-data controls above. Inherited tests for untyped full carriers remain obligations, not silently discharged tests.
- **Locators and constants.** Duke Theorem 5 is printed pp.85–86, PDF pp.16–17 in the linked scan, which contains three blank PDF pages; pp.13–14 was incorrect. The DL Siegel–Weil measure is §4.1(H9), PDF p.41; (H8) is the section map. Ichino–Prasanna Proposition A.1 is PDF p.88, with the construction through p.96; its see-saw compatibility is §A.8, PDF p.96, and Lemma A.13 is §A.9, PDF p.97. Corrected these locators. Corrected the negative-factor worked example to λ/2 and fixed legacy residue/Bessel cross-references. Weil compact continuity is explicitly separated from the unproved differentiated moderate-growth transfer.

## Unfixed defects and where to resume

**B1 — The actual carriers are still missing.** The packet already has 168 gap entries in MP.0–MP.7 and nine in MP.8; many record separate source/prototype aspects of the same obligation. Scalar/finite algebraic fragments are not the full claimed constructions. In particular:

- 0.14–0.18 need finite-dimensional local Schwartz–Bruhat spaces, self-dual vector measures, smooth vectors and archimedean globalization; the companion’s real-line L² formulas do not supply all local fields.
- 1.3–1.7 need the irreducible oscillator normalizer’s strong topology, its normalized square character, cocycle charts and μ₂-cover. Raw kernels and unitary covariance subgroups do not establish these.
- 2.1–2.4 need oscillatory distributions, the full reduced triple-Lagrangian form and its intertwiner composition proof.
- 3.3–3.44 need exact local Hermitian/quaternionic carriers, smooth dual/tensor categories, finite length and the stated local-parameter inputs. Algebraic coinvariants and arbitrary tower families do not supply these categories.
- 4.2–5.8 need genuine adelic restricted products, rational splittings, uniform differentiated lattice estimates and full smooth/cuspidal automorphic spaces. Finite sums, a central-sign subspace and a totalized integral are only algebraic fragments.
- 6.2–6.22 need actual normalized sections and quotient measures, justified see-saw/Fubini and the unavailable toric Shimizu comparison.
- 7.2–7.51 need actual three-cusp L² Maass spaces, Fourier coefficient maps, resolvent/Hecke actions and the source arithmetic trace objects. A supplied linear operator and formal coefficient lists do not furnish them.
- 8.29–8.85 need the same normalized conductor-M newform, BFH K-type, finite arithmetic vector, induced covariance and continued family in every coefficient and transform. The README keeps the common datum N,m>0, 8M∣N, N∣m, 4m∣N², k≥2 even and the appropriate global divisor φ=φ₁ψ. A raw function on matrices or a list of coefficients lacks these links.

Resume by supplying named definitions and theorem contracts in those categories; keep formal fragments separate and do not restore the deleted identities over arbitrary independent data.

**B2 — Supplier ownership/order is not closed.** MetaplecticAutomorphicForms is not assigned a tier in the 94-roadmap CaraianiNewton order. Consequently this review cannot certify that its atlas supplier references are lower-tier or in a declared bundle. AA and SR are tier 2, AF tier 11, AL tier 12, AS tier 13, GL2AutomorphicRepresentationsAndTransfer tier 15 and ML tier 26; these facts do not assign a missing tier to MP. The R16 prefix denotes a GL₂ supplier here, while current packet stage identifiers use R17: an exact declaration/stage contract is still required. The function-field Satake/shtuka inputs are also conditional supplier extensions, not established ordinary Satake results.

There is a concrete bootstrap loop: `ModularityAndLanglandsExtensions:ML.4/gan-takeda-llc-gsp4` consumes MP.3; MP 3.18, 3.40 and 3.41 consume ML.4. ML.5/local-descent-mp2n also consumes MP.3. Separate the local-parameter input from theta-dependent ML results and assign its owner or a justified bundle. Do not count this as a lower-tier edge merely because an ML packet exists. GL₂ R17.4/adjoint-lift also consumes MP.5; therefore an undifferentiated whole-GL₂-stage dependency is insufficient for the BFH seed. An earlier exact newform contract is needed.

**B4 — Normalization propagation.** The concrete Gaussian correction makes the old projection normalization wrong by a factor two. The finite S-transform is independently normalized and remains correct. The full H-kernel prefactor `1/(2mN³)` in 8.62, the full-cusp unfolding identities 8.63–8.69 and their link to the original Fourier coefficient require a fresh derivation with the corrected norm. The algebraic discriminant-shift identity does not certify that prefactor. This review has not certified the downstream unfolding constants after the correction.

**B5 — Genuine analytic continuation.** The BFH rank-two adelic/dyadic comparison, genuine induced topology, uniform convergence chamber, raw long-Weyl operator, rank-one cuspidal cancellation, ramified P(s,0,r) denominator calculation and joint pole-cleared two-variable topology remain unconstructed. At 8.81 BFH p.589 omits the ramified calculation; one must prove it independently before using it to establish Eisenstein regularity. The global compact transition and uniform Novodvorsky majorant remain open, including the negative chart branch. Original confluent-function, rapid-decay and degenerate Bessel input is listed below.

**B6 — Definition tests and signature scope.** The README retains at least three checks per definition/construction, but not every full check can be typed before B1 is resolved. The companion’s 272 examples are not a complete mirror of all README checks. For example, 1.5 has no actual Rao-cocycle signature, 3.24 lacks the local quaternionic splitting, and 6.22 lacks the toric pairing. Complete those actual carriers and mirror their concrete checks; a generic kernel, quotient or raw integral test cannot certify a wrong cover or splitting. Some inherited docstrings merely name the operation and point to its displayed domain; replacing them with precise mathematical descriptions of each remaining fragment is still needed for final upstream polish.
