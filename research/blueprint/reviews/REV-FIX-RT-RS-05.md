### Checked against the source

I downloaded Fargues–Scholze, *Geometrization of the local Langlands
correspondence*, from the author's copy at
<https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/scholze/Geometrization.pdf> on 7 October
2026. It has 356 pages and SHA-256
`9ab9efbd0df251bfa3b610d1d1d88a8dfb1bdf7c397bd04f4c277280d98ae905`, the same
file the fixer read. I read the following passages:

- **Theorem V.4.1** (printed p.177), with Λ killed by an integer prime to p.
  For locally closed U ⊂ Bun_G, D_ét(U, Λ) is compactly generated. "An object
  A ∈ D_ét(U, Λ) is compact if and only if for all b ∈ B(G) contained in U, the
  restriction i^{b*}A ∈ D_ét(Bun_G^b, Λ) ≅ D(G_b(E), Λ) … is compact, and zero
  for almost all b." Compactness in D(G_b(E), Λ) "is equivalent to lying in the
  thick triangulated subcategory generated by c-Ind_K^{G_b(E)} Λ as K runs over
  open pro-p-subgroups of G_b(E)".
- **The proof of V.4.1** (pp.179–180). The generators A^b_K satisfy
  RHom(A^b_K, B) = (i^{b*}B)^K. A compact object is concentrated on finitely
  many points. Compactness is shown by induction over strata.
- **Theorem V.7.1** (p.183). A is ULA if and only if, for every b, the complex
  M_b has M_b^K perfect over Λ for all open pro-p K. Nothing in it bounds the
  support.
- **The chapter VII coefficients** (§VII.6, p.268): "a discrete Z_ℓ-algebra Λ
  for some ℓ ≠ p", read as the condensed ring Z_ℓ ⊗_{Z_ℓ,disc} Λ_disc.
- **Proposition VII.7.4** (p.273). D_lis(Bun_G, Λ) is compactly generated. "An
  object A … is compact if and only if it has finite support and i^{b*}A … is
  compact for all b ∈ B(G), i.e. lies in the thick triangulated subcategory
  generated by c-Ind_K^{G_b(E)} Λ for open pro-p subgroups K ⊂ G_b(E)."
- **Definition VII.7.8 and Proposition VII.7.9** (p.275), the lisse ULA
  criterion: perfect M^K for all open pro-p K.

The new VS4 text states V.4.1 and VII.7.4 accurately. "Finite HN support" is
the source's "zero for almost all b" (V.4.1) and "finite support" (VII.7.4).
The c-Ind clause is the source's description of compactness on a stratum.
J_b(E) is the atlas's name for FS's G_b(E). The perfect-invariants condition,
which belongs to V.7.1 and VII.7.9, is gone from VS4.

The keeps text says "the stated torsion and lisse coefficient hypotheses" and
does not spell them out. The VS4 stage text states them: "prime-to-p torsion
Lambda", and D_lis "in the precise coefficient range of VII.7". The reader's
new note places V.4.1 and VII.7.4 "in their respective torsion and lisse
coefficient regimes". I therefore left the clause as the fixer wrote it.
Editing it in the JSON alone would also break the exact agreement with the
reader's VS4 row, and the reader is not a deliverable of this review.

### The counterexample

I re-derived the red team's counterexample. Take E = Q_5, G = GL_1,
H = Q_5^×, K = 1 + 5Z_5 (open, pro-5) and Λ = F_3.

- Hom_H(c-Ind_K^H Λ, M) = M^K is exact and commutes with sums. So
  V = c-Ind_K^H Λ is compact, and it is one of V.4.1's own generators.
- H is abelian, so K acts trivially on V. Hence V^K = V = F_3[H/K], and
  H/K ≅ Z × F_5^× is infinite. So V^K is not perfect over F_3.
- B(GL_1) = Z, and the degree-0 stratum is open and closed. Extending V from it
  gives a compact object with finite HN support that the old wording excluded.

Λ = F_3 is also a discrete Z_3-algebra with 3 ≠ 5. The same object therefore
refutes the old wording in the lisse regime of VII.7.4. The new wording admits
the object in both regimes.
