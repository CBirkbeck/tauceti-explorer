  systems, two connects lifting steps and untensoring/descent. NT II 2.1 keeps
  the projective-image sandwich over a field of cardinality p^a, both weight-two
  p-nonordinary inputs and exactly matching Steinberg sets away p. Mok retains
  finite packet multisets and nongeneric multiplicities. FS I.10.2 keeps the
  canonical Whittaker adjunction, compacts and precise coherent/nilpotent support
  target; Zou's integral torus subcase has its own exact scope.
- Patrikis–Taylor's pure regular theorem is not replaced by BLGGT's extremely
  regular endpoint. NT's residually reducible and symmetric/tensor lifting

  chains, MP.3's elementary theta inputs and ET.3's ordinary fundamental lemma

  weighted/twisted results.
- The reciprocal Euler polynomial, strict compatibility, real signs and
  optional cohomological duality dictionary are distinguished. The real
  odd-rank gamma correction remains a request to the R24.5 owner, with the
  rank-three symmetric-square positive control.
- Actual parent assignments remove the local stage cycles without renaming
  citation keys. The remaining PA/ML coarse edge needs the documented

- The reader was independently reviewed and synchronized. Its uncertainty
  statements, source issues, bibliography and layer assignments agree with the
  corrected packet; the suggested manifest retains all target/API/test names.
