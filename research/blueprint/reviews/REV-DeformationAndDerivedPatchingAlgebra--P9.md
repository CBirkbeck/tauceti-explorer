The proof iterates the one-variable quotient identification. The suggested file now also states the ambient support intersection with V(X).
- Removed perfect-only support-base-change dependencies from bounded finite-cohomology localization and rational concentration. Flat cohomology base change and the existing module theorem supply those statements without adding perfectness.
- Replaced the indirect `DerivedCategory.Q` citation in zero detection by `DerivedCategory.isIso_iff`. Added `DerivedCategory.HomologySequence.exact₁`, `exact₂` and `exact₃` for triangle support and Euler additivity. The short-exact module support lemma is applied through images, rather than pretending a three-term part of a long exact sequence is short exact.
- Removed the finite-length lemma from the argument establishing the dimension bound that lemma assumes. Removed the irreducible-divisor lemma from isolated-prime decomposition, which needs only the stated Noetherian ring facts.
- Strengthened the suggested `balancedDepth` and `balancedPatchedComplex` conclusions. Existence of a regular sequence of length n−l now comes with an upper bound for every maximal-ideal regular sequence, so the signature expresses exact depth.
- Expanded derived-action transport with explicit functor-image, scalar, identity and composition laws. Transport to D(A) is an S-compatible ring action; an A-compatible algebra action requires additional coefficient data. The Euler definition no longer unnecessarily assumes T is Noetherian.
- Expanded the split-idempotent API with the corner action, its action formula and selected-idempotent identity, cohomology inclusion equivariance, a canonical induced map, identity/composition laws, and a unique comparison between choices. The cohomology range equivalence is constrained to be the actual inclusion, avoiding an arbitrary S-linear equivalence with no action compatibility.
- Added `divisor-quotient-action`: derived reduction in D(S/(f)) kills f in the transported action and hence factors through T/(fT). This fact does not require f regular.
- Added `residual-divisor-localization`: when f is regular and the localized divisor has dimension zero, the residual prime is maximal and minimal in T_q/(f), so its isolated factor splits the reduced object. This justifies localization at a prime that need not be generic in T. Both added nodes are marked with this review's `addedBy`.
- Made the use of the existing Tau Ceti Euler theorem precise: apply it to a finite exact sequence of actual T-cohomology modules. A derived T-action is not asserted to lift to a strict T-chain action.
- Added the truncation locator for bounded ghost nilpotence. Removed P7's strict-chain spectral-action node as a sufficient prerequisite for the general derived-action right-edge comparison; the needed native comparison is explicitly requested.
- Made π avoidance of top components explicit before using the principal-divisor dimension formula.
- Renamed the weaker Lean intermediates to `derivedLengthIdentityFromModuleFormula` and `supportTransportFromEulerComparison`. The packet retains the complete mathematical theorem statements. The full excellence/ACC and finite-algebra localization forms remain explicitly omitted rather than receiving weaker signatures under their full theorem names.

The reviewed library audit for P9 and its P7/R03.3/R03.6 suppliers was checked. Existing module support, annihilators, length, projective dimension, regular sequences, native derived homology and generic Euler theory remain imports. Current Tau Ceti sources and current DGAInfinity/GrothendieckEulerForms roadmaps were read for overlap. DGAInfinity Layer 5 plans DG perfect envelopes; it does not by itself supply idempotent splitting for every ordinary bounded finite-cohomology derived object. The additional native bounded splitting contract is therefore assigned to P7, with DGAInfinity used only for its existing perfect-subcategory scope. The specialized P9 arguments remain algebraic suppliers to PA.3, R22.4 and R31.5, without replanning their automorphic applications.

The five examples were checked by computing their cohomology, supports and fibres: DVR torsion, the power-series Koszul divisor, two reducible-component cases, a free Taylor–Wiles stalk, and S/(x)⊕k over k[[x,y]]. The last has depth 0, projective dimension 2, support dimension 1 and fibre ranks 2,3,1, so it tests strict codimension versus amplitude. The Euler cancellation test prevents treating a zero signed length as acyclicity. The generic domain test is explicitly the unit-retract step after passing to the fraction field.

These are recorded dependencies, not contradictions in the proposed mathematical results.
