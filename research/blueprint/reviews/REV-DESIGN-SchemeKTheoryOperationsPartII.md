## Source findings

- **E1 rejected as a source mistake.** Zhang’s B.2(ii), pp. 974–975, is correct and compresses its argument. The coordinate-prime expansion is useful and retained, but the source never claims that a topological complement on the formal space is enough. No incorrect assertion or nonroutine missing theorem was established.
- **E2 confirmed with limited scope.** Zhang B.1, p. 972, gives no global resolution argument for the strict-complex comparison. This roadmap explicitly assumes the resolution property and finite dimension. The finding records a hypothesis/closure gap in that strict interpretation; it supplies no counterexample to every possible definition of formal K-theory.
- **E3 added and confirmed for Li–Zhang arXiv v3, §1.8, p. 9.** A proper reduced morphism alone does not ensure coherent formal pushforward. The map Spf k⟦t⟧ → Spec k has a point as its reduction, while its structure sheaf has infinite-dimensional sections over k. EGA’s adic finite-type properness excludes it. The finite flat application is unaffected. The publisher edition was inaccessible, so this is not an allegation about an unread version of record.

Existing PAPER-ZHANG-21/E26 (non-affine multiplicativity) and E60 (inverse limits of cokernels) remain respected. Every source finding in this packet has an independent verdict and a reason.
