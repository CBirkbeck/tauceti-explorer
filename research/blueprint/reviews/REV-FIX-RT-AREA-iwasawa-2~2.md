## /1: Gamma and Gauss-sum conventions

Read Morita §1, Lemma 1 and Theorem 1, printed pp.255–256; Gross–Koblitz
§1, (1.2), (1.5), (1.6) and Theorem 1.7, pp.570–571; and Robert (2001)
§4, pp.164–168, and Appendix 2, p.169. The scanned formula pages were
inspected as images. The packet's signed finite product gives Gamma_p(0)=1
and Gamma_p(1)=-1, followed by a continuous lift into native units. The
translation factor is -x on units and -1 on nonunits. Its finite congruence
excludes p=2 at exponent two, while the buffered construction uses enough
precision for every prime.

The chosen root satisfies pi^(p-1)=-p together with the second-order
congruence at zeta-1 in the integer ring. That ring matters: divisibility in
the fraction field would impose no normalization. The source-negative Gauss
sum and inverse Teichmüller convention are retained. Gross–Koblitz's original
odd-prime, nonzero-character range is distinguished from Robert's
0≤a<q-1 statement, including the negative trivial Gauss sum equal to one.
The packet requests the actual Dwork coefficient estimate and splitting/trace
identity from RD.6. Neither follows merely from naming the Gross–Koblitz
formula. The correction is accepted within these boundaries.
