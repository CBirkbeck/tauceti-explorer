## Duplication against upstream roadmaps newer than the atlas snapshot, and the current library

Read at the TauCetiRoadmap checkout: ProfiniteArithmetic (ẑ as `Additive TauCeti.zHat`, its limit property 0.2, units and the assembly of characters into ẑˣ in 0.4, profinite powers, continuous automorphisms), RestrictedProducts (restricted products, adeles, Tamagawa measures), and keyword scans of AlgebraicVectorBundles, DifferentialGeometry ("distribution" hits are tangent distributions), IntegralLattices, LocalGaloisGroups, OperatorTheory, OrthogonalSpinGroups, PeripheralActions, RealAlgebraicGeometry, ContourIntegration, EffectiveBounds, OrthogonalL2Bases. None states a target of this package: the measures, Amice theory, pseudo-measures, Λ-modules and coefficient orders occur in none of them, and the only neighbours (ẑ, ẑˣ, character assembly into ẑˣ) are the opposite direction from L0a's characters out of ℤ_pˣ. The README boundaries now say so (ProfiniteArithmetic layer 0, RestrictedProducts).

Spot-check of definitions against the current Tau Ceti library (a91d3aaf, grep with positive control `unitFiltration`): implemented since the pin f790474 are (1) `TauCeti.completedGroupAlgebra R Γ` for any commutative ring R, with `proj`, `of`, `mk`, `lift`, `ext`, topology, compactness, `isMulCommutative_iff`, `Map`, `Prod` (R⟦C × Γ⟧ ≅ R⟦Γ⟧[C]), `Discrete`, `powerSeriesCoordinate`, `Filtration`, `ProductCoordinate`, `DyadicCoordinate`, `CharacterDivision`; (2) `TauCeti.IsCompactModule`; (3) `TauCeti.fittingIdeal R M k` for every k with independence, monotonicity, `fittingIdeal_quotient_zero`, `fittingIdeal_congr`, free-summand and finrank lemmas and `fittingIdeal_baseChange`; (4) `PadicInt.unitsToZModPow`. Not implemented: pseudo-null and pseudo-isomorphism, characteristic ideals and divisors, μ/λ invariants, the Mahler derivation and the measure operators (weight, φ, ψ, unit restriction), `PowerSeries.cartier`, pseudo-measures, character group rings, compound matrices and higher adjugates, determinant lines (`AbstractMeasure` is Mathlib's, unchanged). Consequences applied: the L6 targets `higher-fitting-ideal`, `higher-fitting-independence` and the lemma `higher-fitting-base-change` are removed and cited as `TauCeti.fittingIdeal`; `L4/initial-fitting-ideal` is reduced to the API the library lacks (annihilator bound, direct-sum product) on the library's declaration; `L1/unit-reduction` and the L1 comparison name the library declarations as their carriers; the scope table has a row listing these. Suggested.lean stays pinned at f790474 (the swarm's build), where none of these declarations exists, and its stand-ins now say which library declaration they stand for.
