## Corrections that must survive the fix

**Levels (/5).** The counterexample uses a neat level L′ and its enlargement by the central involution. The coarse variety stays the same, while volume doubles and the even Schwartz-function orbit sum halves. The normalized quadratic pairing therefore changes by a factor two. Repair the cycle colimit and level compatibility, fine model representability and projection/degree normalizations together, using a cofinal neat tower. This is materially stronger than E19's smoothness-only diagnosis. It does not change the claimed arithmetic inner product constant after restricting the convention correctly.

**Archimedean terms (/7).** The published-copy proof invokes harmonicity of the full averaged KM theta form after discarding coefficients outside the totally positive cone. The analogous replacement of an Eisenstein derivative by that positive subseries is unsupported for an archimedean singleton Diff. Liu's primary formulas include nonzero indefinite/empty-cycle contributions. The proposed all-index formula requires a Green-form extension and convergence/averaging proof. Record that as unfinished mathematical work. The degeneracy issue is separate: joint regular support does not give individual regular support, but the second definite real place excludes the globally degenerate tuples in this setup.

**Hecke actions (/8, /47–/48).** The analytic change of variables in Lemma 11.1 acts by inverse translates on the conjugated form. It gives `chi_pi(t)` under the displayed test-function convention. DL's cycle correction still gives `chi_pi-dual` in Proposition 6.10. A blanket eigencharacter replacement does not reconcile them, and the red team's further suggestion to undo the cycle correction is not proved. The fix needs an explicit transpose/adjoint/coefficient-conjugation dictionary, nonzero normalized annihilators in the correct ideal, the conjugate-ideal version of Lemma 7.3 and generic-fibre vanishing over K. The class to annihilate in Theorem 1.5 is the tempered class, not the raw special cycle.

**Coefficient prime and base change (/24, /29).** B.13 cannot be used on a p=l special fibre. An auxiliary prime requires the actual cycles to be null-homologous and the correspondences tempered for that prime. For Lemma B.3 the correct ramified-extension factor is e. DL Remark A.6 sends a uniformizer to −1, so the red team's +1 toy value needs a sign correction; the failure of invariant ramified base change persists. Identified applications use unramified extensions, so this overgeneral statement alone does not invalidate the main theorem.

**Shared suppliers.** The fixes must import the accepted doubling, Rallis, compact-Igusa, absolute-purity, Selmer-height and formal-intersection constructions. Preserve AIPF-specific comparison and normalization lemmas. Multiple source papers for one construction are allowed; repeated instructions to build that construction under different owners are the defect. MC.7 can export the conjectural statement before the AIPF theorem, with any proof-bearing theorem-register suffix placed downstream.

**Status/version qualifications (/23, /26, /30, /34).** Matsushima is already planned; an EHLS holomorphic-only cohomology statement does not supply all the cohomological discrete series here. Ramakrishnan's degree-one condition is relative to the base field. LTXZZ v2 C.2.3 becomes v3 C.2.2, not C.2.4; the latter concerns the inner form. S.7's node includes a noetherian-scheme degree-zero clause, although it does not give the required arithmetic supported-product theorem. P-adic and finite fields are fields: the Hochschild–Serre gap is the continuous-Galois/étale bridge, not a field-scope exclusion. Reconcile the exact statements rather than adopting the red team's conflicting missing/planned instructions mechanically.

Finding /40 is rejected because pp.16–17 includes the whole Proposition 3.6 proof. Finding /50 is confirmed because p.53 alone excludes the substantial opening display on p.52. The rendered checks confirm the separate misprints in /49, but those repairs do not settle the proof gaps above.
