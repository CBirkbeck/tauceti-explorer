## What I checked

For each of the twelve findings I read the printed text at its locator and checked the reason and
the correction. All twelve quotations are printed as recorded. Three are settled without leaving the
paper:

- **E2.** Corollary 1.1.15 (p.11) concludes "there exists `w ∈ W(G,M) := N_G(M)/M`", while its proof
  reduces to the quasi-split case and invokes Proposition 1.1.13, whose conclusion (p.10) is "there
  exists `w ∈ W(G,T)`", the absolute Weyl group of a maximal torus. The proof supplies an element of
  `W(G*,T*)`; the corollary claims one in the smaller `N_{G*}(M*)/M*`.
- **E11.** Page 19 defines `V_{m,n} := V^{⊗n} ⊗ V^{*⊗m}` and then prints
  `ω_L(V_{m,n}) = D^{⊗m} ⊗ D^{*⊗n}`, with the exponents interchanged.
- **E1.** (1.1.2.2) on p.6 prints `c b σ(b) σ²(b) ··· σ^r(b) σ^r(c)^{−1} = c(rν_b)(p)c^{−1}`. The
  `r`-th power of `bσ` has `r` factors, not `r+1`, and the `G_m` case decides it: for `b = p` the
  printed left side has valuation `r+1` for every `c`, since `v(c) = v(σ^r(c))`, while the right side
  has valuation `r`. The paper's own (1.1.4.1) on p.7 uses the corrected form.

The others:

- **E3** (p.14): Proposition 1.2.5 names `[b_bas(μ_x)]` where its own hypothesis and parenthetical
  are about `μ_x^{−1}`.
- **E4** (p.26): "we say ι is accommodating if there is a finite collection of accommodating
  embeddings" — circular; the collection is of *strictly* accommodating embeddings, defined just
  above.
- **E5** (p.28) and **E13** (p.27): the same defect twice. A product group acting block-diagonally on
  `V ⊕ V′` lands in `GSp(V ⊕ V′)` only where the two similitude characters agree, so `G × T` and
  `G′ ×_{G^ad} G` have to be cut down to that locus — which is `G ×_{G^ab} T` in the first case and
  the identity component of `{c′(g′) = c(g)}` in the second.
- **E6** (p.29): Lemma 2.2.11 is stated for an irreducible finite-type `S` with no reducedness or
  separatedness hypothesis; agreement of two morphisms on a dense set of `k`-points gives equality
  only on a reduced source, and the equalizer is closed only when `S` is separated. Both hold in
  every use, so the correction records them rather than narrowing the lemma's reach.
- **E7** (p.37): `U = D − S` for the complement `S − D`, and `j_*O_U` for the divisorial log
  structure `O_S ∩ j_*O_U^× → O_S`.
- **E9** (p.19): the proof of Corollary 1.3.13 asserts `C_{D,L} ≃ Rep_L G_{s_0}`, where the
  construction gives the subcategory generated by `D` and so the subgroup `H ⊂ G_{s_0}`.
- **E12** (p.34): the proof of Proposition 2.3.11 names `{μ_X}_p` where the special pair needs the
  Hodge cocharacter attached to `λ_T ∈ {μ_X^{−1}}_p` with `Nλ_T = ν_δ`.
- **E8** (p.20): §1.3.15 asserts a unique `[b_μ]` whose Newton point is the Hodge average and then
  that the corresponding stratum is non-empty. Existence needs a hypothesis — quasi-split `G`
  suffices — and the maximal admissible class need not attain that average. Here I state the limit of
  my own reading: I checked the printed assertion and its (1.3.10)/(1.3.14) context, and did **not**
  collate Kisin–Zhou, whose Definition 2.2.4 and Remark 2.2.5 the record cites for the same
  restriction. That corroboration therefore stands as recorded, not as my reading.

## What this review did not do

I verified the recorded defects, not the repairs: the corrections are recorded as the right shape and
each is consistent with the paper's own later use, but the checkpoints' outstanding work — collating
the journal text, finishing the Noot §2.3 comparison, and auditing E13's repaired lifting,
centralizers and quotients — remains open. E10 stays a rejected candidate with its identifier
reserved. I add no finding of my own; I read §§1.1–1.3, 2.2–2.3 and Appendix A.2 around the twelve
locators, not all 41 pages line by line.
