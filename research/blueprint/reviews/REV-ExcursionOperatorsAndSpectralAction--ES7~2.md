## Corrections in this round

1. **Restriction along strata.** Full faithfulness identifies a central action along a fixed embedding; it does not by itself identify actions from different embeddings. The proof now invokes ES5's enhanced comparison/retraction supplier for embedding independence. The reader agrees.
2. **Abelian versus enhanced Bernstein centre.** The SR.1 request and structural reconciliation formerly assigned the enhanced degree-zero centre to SR.1. The verified RT/9 fix assigns SR.1 the abelian smooth-category centre, pro-p corner limit and integral separatedness; ES0 owns the enhanced centre and comparison. Both files now make this distinction. Added the direct residual ES6 equal-characteristic stage request and the direct SR.3 Drinfeld–Carayol coefficient comparison dependency, matching their already recorded consumers. Arbitrary coefficient-action base change remains its own gap.
3. **Two-leg Schur argument.** Evaluation and coevaluation intertwine the diagonal Weil action; they are not invariant under independently varying Weil elements. The source argument needs scalar endomorphism rings for both the smooth irreducible σ and finite-dimensional ρ. Added the direct SR.3 prerequisite/consumer and the precise scalar-ring request. A rank-one ratio check detects the false product-equivariance assumption. The Lean algebraic lemma assumes the scalar normalization explicitly and does not pretend to prove the geometric Schur step.
4. **Maximal orders.** The packet permits central simple algebras, including split matrix algebras; the suggested carrier only permitted division rings. Generalized it to finite-dimensional central simple rings and added the matrix-algebra instance. Order existence and gluing remain imported or requested, rather than being asserted from the lattice type alone.
5. **Algebraic automorphic coefficients.** Complex Hilbert decomposition does not prove restricted-tensor-product factorization or descent to Q̄_ℓ. The discrete-spectrum proof and AS.0/FA.6 requests now assign functional analysis to AS.0 and coefficient/factorization comparisons to FA.6/SR.3. The corresponding order/spectrum gap is explicit.
6. **Local rank.** `LocalInvDivAlg.finrank` formerly included d=0, incompatible with a division ring's positive dimension. Its signature now requires positive rank through `NeZero d`.
7. **Globalization independence and functional equations.** Introduced one chosen fixed-global-setup carrier for the local construction. The equality signature now varies selected Π and auxiliary choices inside that setup, as LRS 15.13–15.14 prove; it cannot silently vary the curve, fixed places or D. The global functional equation requires compact-support cohomology of nonconstant lisse sheaves on open curves beyond WC.2's present proper constant-coefficient theorem. This and Laumon's local ε-factor product formula are separately recorded in the request/gap. Cross-setup independence is not claimed.
8. **Degeneration bridge and source links.** The proof now uses selected concentration, a simple JL(π)⊗σ_d(π) constituent and multiplicity one to pass from the degenerate spectral sequence to the semisimplified isomorphism. Equal multiplicities alone were insufficient. Corrected stale suggested-file docstring links after the preceding review's layer moves. E10/E11 are retained with the semisimple-block and finite-index repair obligations.
9. **Selector API.** LRS 15.10's matrix-coefficient selector kills every other irreducible admissible representation. Its API and Lean trace signature only mentioned supercuspidals. Broadened both to match the target, with the same-central-character domain; added compact-open assumptions to the value and central-translation examples. The normalized selector remains distinct from the subgroup indicator.
10. **Stage-graph claim.** The structural note claimed all three proposed same-roadmap edges were acyclic against every packet. The combined stage graph has existing return paths and cycles, although this packet's internal node graph is acyclic. Removed the global claim, retained the semantically needed edges, and recorded the reconciliation question below. No other roadmap or atlas file was changed.

All packet corrections are reflected in the reader, including the added SR.3 consumer, acceptance notice and the current independent verdict on every source issue. The suggested file retains honest carrier/condition omissions in docstrings and gap `prototypes`.
