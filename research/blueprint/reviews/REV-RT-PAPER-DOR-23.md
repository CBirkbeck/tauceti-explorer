## Finding decisions

### 1. Conductor lattice versus additive-character kernel — confirmed

Verified in the published Remark 3.47, p. 33 (including the page image), Construction 3.44, pp. 31–32, and arXiv v2, pp. 38–39. Items 027, 050 and E7.correction contain the quoted condition. The intersection hKh^{-1} ∩ U is compact. For F=F_q((t)), the nontrivial character e(x)=exp(2πi Tr_{F_q/F_p}(coeff_{t^{-1}} x)/p) has t^{-n} in its kernel for every n≥2, so its kernel is not compact. For h=diag(1,-π^ν), direct conjugation gives the intersection u(π^{-ν}O), the conductor lattice defined in Construction 3.44, rather than the entire kernel. Confirm the missing source issue and correct those three occurrences to the conductor lattice. Qualify the fix: the corrected intersection alone does not establish the global counit or its match to the self-dual lattice. Retain E7's explicit distribution-matching, counit and restricted-product compatibility obligations; do not mark them proved. Describe the general-h modification as requiring those checks. Record the version of record when adding E8 and refresh the erratum search; a Crossref metadata search alone does not prove that no correction exists.

### 7. The central quotient prevents the proposed specialization — rejected

The suggested identification is outside the cited supplier's hypotheses. Dor §4.1, pp. 38–39, defines compactly supported smooth functions on GL_2(F)\GL_2(A) with no central-lattice quotient. The cited GS.0/cuspidal-automorphic-forms node instead constructs C_c(Bun_{G,N}(F_q)/Xi,E) and depends explicitly on GS.0/automorphisms-and-the-central-lattice. That prerequisite fixes Xi of finite index in Bun_Z(F_q), with trivial Xi offered only for finite centre. For GL_2, Z=G_m and the degree direction makes Bun_Z(F_q) infinite, so Xi={1} is not an allowed specialization. Taking a colimit over levels does not remove Xi-invariance or change compact support modulo Xi into compact support on the unquotiented space. Thus the cited node does not already provide Dor's finite-level carriers, and the claimed duplicate is not established. GS.0/weil-uniformization and its level structures are useful imports, but an unquotiented function-space and constant-term extension/comparison must be requested explicitly. Keep item 036 missing; do not say only its smooth colimit, p, I and smooth dual are missing on this evidence.

For GL_2, multiplying the matrices gives wgw^{-1}=det(g)g^{-T}. Confirm an explicit R16.2 input for irreducible smooth complex V, with admissibility supplied by SR.3. Record an isomorphism for each eligible V, not a natural identification of the duality functor on arbitrary modules.
