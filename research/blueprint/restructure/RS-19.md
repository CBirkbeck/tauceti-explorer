## Decision

Keep **Endoscopic transfer and unitary trace comparison** (ET) and **Étale duality, cycle classes and perverse sheaves** (EDC), with their existing titles and identifiers. EDC is the common scheme foundation; ET adds genuinely different endoscopic geometry, harmonic analysis and trace applications. ET is not simply a second part of EDC, since substantial representation-theoretic and automorphic prerequisites enter independently. EDC is not absorbed into an endoscopy roadmap used by only some of its consumers.

Remove four redundant *planning vertices*: `ET.2`, `ET.2a`, `ET.2a:duality-perversity`, and `ET.2a:pure-decomposition`. Preserve their historical identifiers and anchors as supplier redirects. All their mathematical obligations remain explicit at EDC.0–7, LPV.6 and ET.2b. Twenty-four other family stage records remain unchanged. Four direct supplier links replace the removed import paths.

The family has no listed Tau Ceti anchors. This does not license replacing the pre-existing finite-coefficient, enhancement or representation-theoretic foundations: their existing owners are imported unchanged.

### Two overlaps that must not become deletions

**EDC.7 versus ET.2b.** The pure-perverse decomposition theorem is not Ngô's support theorem. ### Reachability and cycle check

For any two retained vertices, a path through removed vertices is replaced by the corresponding boundary link. Conversely each new link expands to the old two-edge path. Thus attaching the unchanged remainder of the atlas preserves exactly the old reachability relation between retained vertices. Any cycle after the change would expand to an old cycle. This proves that the change cannot create a new dependency cycle; it does **not** claim to have verified global acyclicity of the entire original atlas.

In particular this cannot add a late decomposition dependency to the early EDC.5/LPV.6/IG.4 branch. ET.5's complete trace-formula branch already reaches later geometric inputs through ET.4/ET.3/ET.2b; that fact must not be confused with the independence of its early perverse import interface.
