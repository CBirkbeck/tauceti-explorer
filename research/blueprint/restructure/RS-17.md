## Dependency structure and rejected shortcuts

```text
early EDC duality/cycles/weak Lefschetz
    → LPV.0–5 → DWP.2–4 → DWP.5–8
DWP.1 → FA.5 → DWP.3             │
                                   → LPV.7:invariant-cycles → DWP.9 → EDC.7

LPV.0–2 + nodal/Neron suppliers
    → LPV.7:semistable-curves → R34.3 → its arithmetic consumers

PR196 trace/zeta → WC.1–2 → WC.3 (imports DWP.4)
                                   → WC.5 → DWP.10 → R34.6
DWP.7 + WC.3 → RD.7 → WC.6
SF.5 + WC.1–2 + WC.5:power-sum-converse → WC.5:surface-alternative
```

This diagram is phase-level; the complete links retain their contract qualifications.

1. **Curve seed stays early.** DWP.1 uses Rosati positivity and the geometric Tate/H1 comparison, not general RH, Faltings' isogeny theorem or WC.5. FA.5's finite-cover Chebotarev can therefore supply DWP.3 without a cycle. The compact l-adic Haar-null exceptional-set passage remains DWP.3 work.
2. **Geometry is not purity.** LPV.4 constructs the radical quotient without hard Lefschetz; LPV.5 proves open symplectic monodromy over the original Q_l model. DWP.3 verifies these inputs and proves rationality. Zero quotients, delta=0 and characteristic-2/parity branches remain mandatory.
3. **Similar positivity is not the same theorem.** ADS.8 owns Dirichlet Landau at the actual finite abscissa and its finite 3-4-1 combination. DWP.2 retains the positive *power-series* coefficient/radius lemmas; DWP.5 retains the sheaf-specific generalized-majoration argument. SW.9's complex Sp commutant does not by itself prove Q_l coinvariants: descent/base-change of invariant equations and the pairing contractions are explicit DWP.2 bridge tasks. An open Q_l image need not stay open in Sp(E).
4. **Only late invariant cycles use weights.** LPV.7 is an aggregate, not a second proof. Its semistable child remains independent of DWP.7–9; the invariant child uses DWP.7–8 and supplies DWP.9. EDC.7's relative theorem cannot be used to prove its own absolute input.
5. **A strengthened theorem does not re-own an early construction.** H1:valuation-nearby-cycles extends beyond traits but LPV.0 remains the trait object. H1's formal comparison must identify the map and inertia action; Huber's nearby object is not the cone Rphi. R01.2's local-field representation theorem does not prove quasi-unipotence for arbitrary geometric representations.
6. **Proper is not projective.** DWP.4 is smooth projective. DWP.7 retains the stronger proper-smooth theorem and its mixed/real/integral distinctions. RD.7's proper nonprojective branch therefore gets a direct DWP.7 input; its existing DWP.4 input remains valid for the projective branch. WC.3's algebraic factor-separation lemma is shared, not a second crystalline-comparison proof.
7. **Independent proof routes survive.** SF.5 owns the Hodge-index/Riemann–Roch/adjunction and graph-intersection argument; the surface child retains its zeta/spectral comparison using the all-power converse. There is deliberately no DWP.1→surface-child prerequisite. The shared public curve-bound contract has one owner; recording that agreement does not make the two proofs depend on each other.
8. **Examples compare APIs.** DWP.10 owns weight-facing Jordan/Tate/ordinary-versus-compact tests. WC.7 keeps zeta-facing sign/factor/recurrence assertions on the same examples. Neither duplicates a cohomology calculation or deletes a test merely because the example is shared.
