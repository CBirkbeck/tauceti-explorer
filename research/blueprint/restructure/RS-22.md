Fresh primary-source checks substantiate the last distinction. Vincent Lafforgue's **Lemma 8.2**, printed pp. 107–108 of [arXiv:1209.5352v10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1209.5352v10), concerns constructible lisse O_E-sheaves on U^I with commuting partial Frobenius, whose composite is total Frobenius, and continuous product-fundamental-group representations on finite-type O_E-modules. The diagonal-fibre and external-product conditions are part of the statement. The statement and following inverse-functor discussion were read; the full subsequent proof was not audited.

[Fargues–Scholze, IV.7](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/scholze/Geometrization.pdf), printed pp. 164–166, expressly distinguishes its local Weil-group formulation. Corollary IV.7.2 gives full faithfulness for the divisor-to-Weil-classifying-stack pullback; Proposition IV.7.3 gives equivalence on v-locally constant perfect objects. This section starts with coefficients killed by an integer prime to p. Its statements and proof were read, including the failure of unrestricted essential surjectivity. Extending the action to HS1's coefficient category still uses the separate solid/lisse inputs; this check does not certify those proofs.

Even equal-characteristic local E does not make its Fargues–Fontaine curve the global curve C. No equivalence between these constructions is inferred from their analogous notation.
