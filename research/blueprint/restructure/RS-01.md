## Exact ownership changes

### CP.0: retain the diagram, import its coefficients

The construction owners are: `AI.0:integral` for the specialized integral theta/epsilon/twist package and the normalization of the Witt reduction; `CR.0` for the PD envelope, built on the pinned `PreTilt`, `WittVector`, `WittVector.fontaineTheta` and `WittVector.map` carriers, with the maps given by its universal property (θ_cris, the Witt reduction A_inf → W(k) and the induced A_cris → W(k)); `R06.1` for rational period rings, their structure maps and period-ring-level arithmetic-model comparison; `AI.0:period-comparison` for the compatibility of AI.0's integral twist and Frobenius-twist normalizations with R06.1's rings; and `AI.0` for the generic coefficient algebra over an arbitrary perfectoid field that the decomposition recorded at CP.0, namely the coherence of W_r(O) (BMS1 Proposition 3.24, Lemmas 3.25–3.28, Corollary 3.29) and the §4.2 specialization objects (x, W̃ with kernel Q, the discrete valuation ring A_inf,(p) with A_inf → W(K♭) flat, μ a unit in W(C♭)). CR.0 is upstream of AI.0, so it builds the abstract envelope and states its regular-principal description conditionally on a generator; AI.0:integral applies it to O_C.

R06.1 and AI.0:period-comparison are not two owners of the same theorem. R06.1 proves its ring-level comparison, the structure maps A_inf → A_crys → B_crys^+ → B_dR^+, t with t/ξ a unit and I^[r]A_inf = μ^r A_inf, and it imports ε, μ, ξ and the kernel-generator criterion from AI.0:integral. The later AI stage compares only the Breuil–Kisin twist, the θ̃-specializations and the Frobenius-twisted scalar extensions with R06.1's rings. It does not send a prerequisite back to R06.1 or reconstruct the rational carriers.

CP.0 still constructs its geometric/site comparison functors, matches formal, special and generic fibres, fixes compatible geometric points, proves the actual cohomological/base-change commutativities, and transports twists and semilinear scalar extension on the compared cohomology and complexes (the coefficient-level comparisons are AI.0:integral's and AI.0:period-comparison's). None of the imported packages supplies the Breuil–Kisin uniformizer normalization, so CP.0 keeps constructing it: π, π♭, E(T), the map S = W(k)[[T]] → A_inf sending T to [π♭]^p and acting as Frobenius on W(k) (BMS1 §4.4), and S → W(k) sending T to 0 and acting as Frobenius on W(k). The dictionary is a collection of proved identifications among concrete maps, not an arbitrary record asserting that desired diagrams commute. Re-homing a coefficient constructor does not discharge any of these geometric proofs.
