# RS-14 — one rational construction, explicit automorphic extensions

Author: Codex, session `codex-a71f92`; issue #828. This is a §15 restructuring
proposal, not an applied atlas edit, a completed source decomposition, or a
claim of Lean formalization.

## Decisions

Keep **DirichletPadicLFunctions** (D) as the explicit arithmetic construction
over Q. Make **AutomorphicPadicLFunctions** (A) its extension, titled
**Dirichlet p-adic L-functions, special values, and Eisenstein measures, Part II:
Ray-class and automorphic interpolation**. D is its first roadmap prerequisite.
The boundary is D's constructed pseudomeasures, all tame-character measures,
branches and Eisenstein measure, not merely the desired interpolation formula.
A starts beyond that boundary and compares its rational specializations with
those objects. It still imports modular-symbol, analytic and geometric owners;
the title does not assert that D alone proves GL2, CM or unitary geometry.
Stage-level prerequisites remain precise: an early ray-class construction does
not acquire an artificial dependency on every later rational comparison.

Keep all fifteen stage IDs and both source-unit checkpoints. Narrow twelve
stages; keep D `KU-zeta`, A `L3h` and A `L4e` unchanged. No layer is moved or
dropped. The JSON gives exact retained contracts, 33 single-owner records and
161 explicit links, of which 144 are new to the inspected graph. It changes no
Tau Ceti roadmap, live campaign document, atlas extraction or decomposition.

Before restructuring, A L3's general Deligne–Ribet construction overlapped
D L1/L2/L4, while several stage descriptions mixed canonical foundations with
their arithmetic instantiations. After restructuring, the shared construction
has one owner, and the general theorem retains a comparison obligation:

| Shared object or theorem | Owner | Import/comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rational Kubota–Leopoldt pseudomeasure | D L1 | A L3's F=Q partial-zeta comparison; Coleman and integral Iwasawa uses |
| Rational tame-character integral measures | D L2 | A L3's rational ray-character specializations |
| Rational Eisenstein measure and specialization | D L4 | A L3's Hilbert-to-rational comparison |
| General Hilbert Eisenstein/Deligne–Ribet construction | A L3 | IntegralIwasawa I.3's Stickelberger dictionary; A checkpoint |
| BSW general-field evaluation/control | A L1/L2 | Rational comparison imports ModularSymbols L1/L2 |
| Analytic CPR/Panchishkin existence proposition | A L5 | ModularIwasawaMainConjectures L5 imports the statement, not its truth |

The six family evidence records are three reciprocal pairs, not six independent
collisions. All three are resolved by D -> A, never by replacing the early
rational construction with a later general-field theorem.

## Classical and analytic boundaries

D L0 keeps the actual RJW 2.4 Mellin proof, Bernoulli smoothing, algebraic-value
comparison through explicit embeddings, and the Dedekind-series/meromorphic-germ
and class-number-residue comparison. General Dirichlet continuation and
conductor/Euler-factor interfaces import AnalyticNumberTheory AN.1; general
adelic continuation and the trivial-character pole import AL.1. This distinction
does not discard the specific Mellin proof merely because another continuation
route exists. A real one-sided class-number limit alone does not prove a
meromorphic simple pole.

The existing ModularForms Layer 0 owns generalized Bernoulli quantities and the
finite Fourier/Gauss translation required for its character Eisenstein series.
D L0 imports those quantities and proves its L-value/algebraicity comparisons;
D L4 imports the actual modular forms and builds the measure. Existing Gauss
inversion, conductor and Euler-deletion declarations are reused. Missing
composite-conductor Gauss calculations needed for the classical translation
remain obligations of that existing owner, not evidence of implementation.
Generalized Bernoulli numbers do not get a second carrier in D.

GlobalNumberFields Layers 9–10 own complex Hecke/ray characters, infinity types,
connected archimedean factors and the rational idele-character dictionary. D L0
and A L0 prove their normalization/embedding comparisons on those carriers.
GlobalNumberFields Layer 2 supplies finite ray groups and transition maps;
ClassFieldTheory Layer 11 supplies the normalized global Artin map. A L0 still
constructs the p-power inverse limit and quotient descent, keeps global-unit
closure and torsion, and proves the dimension statement with the Leopoldt
defect. Finite ray classes or reciprocity do not automatically give that result.

PadicMeasures L0a owns character-space representability and universal
characters; LocallyAnalyticDistributions L3 supplies the analytic Mellin and
coordinate operations on that space. Their imported overlap is recorded with
one representability owner and a direct link, not two interchangeable spaces.
PadicMeasures L1 supplies completed measure algebras; L2 supplies
Mahler–Amice/restriction/twist/phi/psi operators; L3 supplies generic localized
pseudomeasures. D L1 still proves cancellation in the integral power-series
ring, actual moments and smoothing regularity. D L2 still constructs the
arithmetic tame measures and proves their Gauss computations. A generic
localization theorem is not the arithmetic existence proof.

Evaluation of a pseudomeasure is only defined where its character denominator
is nonzero. The reviewed EXT-16 corrections remain operative: the full total
quotient ring does not admit evaluation at every character; the cyclic argument
in RJW 3.38 is odd-prime; the auxiliary element in 3.36(iii) excludes both ±1;
the multiplication-by-z^x Amice substitution is `(1+T)z-1`. This proposal does
not close that supplier's remaining source gaps.

ColemanIntegration L0 owns the compatible analytic logarithm with log_p(p)=0.
D L3 owns the complex cyclotomic-log value, Leopoldt's p-adic formula and the
specific residue theorem. The safe order is
`D L0 -> Coleman L0 -> D L3 -> Coleman L3`.
The last stage compares the k=1 case of its higher positive-value theorem with
the earlier Leopoldt formula; it must not supply the earlier logarithm by a
backward edge. IntegralIwasawa L0 imports D L3's complex formula for its unit
regulator; it does not construct a second one.

All D tests survive: zeta(0)=-1/2 with a zero Euler factor at k=1; primitive
character evaluation at p rather than evaluation at an arbitrary modulus; pure
p-power as well as tame conductor; the trivial-character pole and its
coordinate-dependent residue; scalar extension/descent; and an integral
dyadic branch using ±1 times (1+4Z_2). D L4 retains actual p-stabilization and
coefficientwise specialization for even k>=4. Its measure-valued q-expansion
does not claim full geometric Hida/Coleman family control. Exceptional classical
weights remain governed by ModularForms, not silently imported into RJW 8.2.

## Automorphic constructions remain substantial

A L1/L2 retain the BSW cycles in degree r1+r2, rational periods, actual
distribution coefficients/lifts, unit descent, growth and Theorems 5.7, 8.7 and
11.1. The source weight condition k+2v parallel and bound
`h_q < (min k_sigma + v_q(lambda) + 1)/e_q` remain, together with p-level,
coefficient-field and operator-normalization hypotheses. Every interpolation
factor is retained. ModularSymbols L1/L2 own the Q critical-value and
small-slope theorems; A proves comparison of constructed objects/maps.
LocallyAnalyticDistributions L4's Fredholm machinery does not prove BSW's
arithmetic estimates. Hilbert/Bianchi and admissible nonparallel-weight tests
remain, and general-field family-control/Leopoldt hypotheses are not inferred
from a single-form theorem.

A L3 owns the actual general Hilbert Eisenstein series, integral congruences,
bounded denominators and Deligne–Ribet measure, using canonical geometric
expansion principles from AutomorphicBundles B5. Its optional Shintani route
requires equality in the same normalization. IntegralIwasawa I.3 owns the
Stickelberger dictionary, not a duplicate measure. The checkpoint's phrase
about owning missing modular/q-expansion prerequisites is narrowed to verifying
their completion at L3 or its named suppliers. All original checkpoint edges
and the no-weight-two-shortcut obligation remain.

Katz's branch imports ShimuraVarieties V5's CM/reflex data. Katz's differential
operators, CM periods, ordinary deformation and critical-value evaluation remain
new. A L4 similarly imports PELModuli M5, ShimuraCompactifications C2/C5 and
AutomorphicBundles B2/B3/B5, then proves EHLS's particular ordinary modules,
pairings, operators, measure, doubling identity and local factors. Igusa IG.1 is
used only for its special-fiber torsor/actions and stated finite-level models.
It is not a blanket supplier of the mixed-characteristic ordinary lifts needed
by Katz or EHLS: missing lifts and integral/coherent comparison hypotheses stay
explicit in A L3/L4. A generic coherent pushforward is not automatically a vector
bundle, and good-base PEL compactifications are not all-prime canonical models.

EHLS Main 9.2.2 retains the anti-holomorphic cuspidal ordinary representation,
tame type, coefficient modules/bases and Hypotheses 7.3.2/7.3.3 and
Proposition–Hypothesis 7.3.5. Its Iwasawa–Hecke/period-module tensor target and
congruence/local factors are unchanged. None of these arithmetic conditions is
discharged by naming an existing geometry roadmap.

A L3h and L4e are not duplicates. Hsieh's published A–C/§6 retains the actual
square-root distribution and its square's interpolation, the ordinary CM-type
condition (not ordinariness of pi), squarefree n-minus/root-number condition and
all additional hypotheses for mu=0/nonvanishing. Finite-exception and
higher-dimensional density conclusions remain distinct. Eischen–Wan v2 retains
its finite-slope vector Klingen construction and exact odd split-prime,
principal-series, auxiliary-character and refinement inputs. Its r=2
constant-term divisibility is not promoted to all r. AutomorphicCongruences
L2/L2s and BSD.6a still receive their exact inputs; the later congruence and
Galois-class arguments are not supplied by interpolation alone.

A L5 imports Selmer L4's realization/criticality and local-condition comparison.
It owns the analytic CPR/Panchishkin existence proposition once and exports it
to ModularIwasawa L5. Selmer's RJW 13.21 arithmetic statement and the latter's
Greenberg/main-conjecture examples retain their distinct conclusions. No
universal motive-level existence constructor or noncommutative theorem appears.
