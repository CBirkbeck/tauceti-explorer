## Pair-by-pair decision

### AL.1 ↔ PS.1: import the analytic theorem, retain the motivic proof

AL.1 keeps local Tate integrals, finite-place rationality, archimedean meromorphy, local L/epsilon/gamma factors, conductor and ramified-character computations, and the global functional equation with its trivial-character poles. PS.1 does not construct another version of this package.

That import alone does not prove the Dirichlet/Tate Deligne case. The existing L0 owner separately constructs generalized Bernoulli numbers and identifies their algebraic values with complex L-values. Add `DirichletPadicLFunctions:L0 → PS.1`. PS.1 still proves the comparison with its actual motive and PS.0's period lines, with coefficient embeddings and signs. This is a nonempty mathematical adapter, not a relabeling of an arbitrary tuple of realizations.

L0's character-conductor/Bernoulli specializations are not the unrestricted GL_n/Rankin normalization interface. Generic existing finite-product and Euler-product lemmas remain library/Tau Ceti imports; this proposal does not require L0 to build Godement–Jacquet or Rankin–Selberg before proving Dirichlet algebraicity.

### AL.4 ↔ PS.1: keep both sides of a comparison

AL.4 starts with an unramified Satake class and an algebraic representation of an L-group. PS.1 starts with admitted motivic realization data and includes completion and ramified information. These inputs are not interchangeable.

Keep AL.4 and its existing edge to PS.1. Its output is used only where an actual local-compatibility theorem applies. Do not move the construction of a motivic L-function, ramified Weil–Deligne factors or the archimedean Hodge factor into AL.4. Do not infer analytic continuation from a convergent partial Euler product. Consequently this pair creates **no duplicate-owner entry**: recording one would falsely identify distinct objects.

### AL.5 ↔ PS.1: one automorphic normalization, a separate period identification

AL.5 keeps the scoped complex/formal GL2 and Rankin normalization interface, local corrections, conductor/test-vector/twist comparisons and zero-factor limits or derivatives. PS.1 imports that interface, rather than reproving its formulas under new names.

PS.1 nevertheless retains the separately sourced modular-form motivic comparison. An automorphic rational structure or Whittaker/cohomological period from AL.3/AL.5 is not definitionally a Betti/de Rham period from PS.0. A proved comparison map, its determinant, coefficient embeddings and normalization factors must connect them. No reverse dependency `PS.1 → AL.5` is introduced; it would contradict the existing analytic-to-motivic direction and form a cycle with `AL.5 → PS.1`.

The source-specific p-adic distributions, evaluation cycles, growth/control and interpolation identities of AutomorphicPadicLFunctions remain there. No generic p-adic existence theorem is added to AL.5 or PS.1.

## Required mathematical regression cases

These are acceptance obligations for the subsequent blueprint, **not Lean tests executed in this restructuring run**.

1. For the Tate/trivial-character specialization, retain the pole conventions and the value `zeta(0) = -1/2`; a removed Euler factor at the relevant boundary can vanish without the original value vanishing. Never cancel that factor without a separate limit/derivative theorem.
2. For a nontrivial primitive Dirichlet character, first obtain the generalized-Bernoulli/Gauss identity in its coefficient field through L0, then compare its complex embedding to the same analytic L-function and PS.0 period convention. Vary the inducing modulus and retain every local correction.
3. Criticality checks **both** the infinity factor and the dual factor at the transformed argument. Comparing only one gamma factor must fail a boundary/non-example test. Keep finite L-values distinct from completed values.
4. Change a period basis by a nontrivial coefficient-field unit. The period class remains unchanged but its chosen scalar representative changes. The numerical formula must transform with it; an up-to-unit identity is not an integral normalization.
5. Keep an algebraic zero normalized value admissible wherever the statement allows it. A nonzero period does not make the L-value nonzero; a theorem formulated in units must have the requisite nonvanishing hypothesis, as in Deligne's coefficient-valued formulation.
6. Compare an unramified standard GL2 Satake polynomial with an admitted Galois factor using a real compatibility theorem. A ramified WD datum or an arbitrary reductive-group parameter must not instantiate the unramified comparison automatically.
7. A modular motivic specialization must exhibit the map between its automorphic period and its Betti/de Rham period. A matching label, numerical approximation or rescaling chosen after seeing the value is not a proof of that map.
8. Retain separate finite-place rational/gcd arguments and archimedean meromorphic-integral arguments. None of this ownership change permits an archimedean gcd shortcut or arbitrary-group analytic continuation.
