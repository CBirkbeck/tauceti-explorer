# RS-29: inverse Galois theory after Belyi maps

**Proposal:** make `InverseGaloisAndArithmeticFundamentalGroups` an extension titled
**Belyi maps, dessins d'enfants, and three-point covers, Part II: inverse Galois theory and arithmetic fundamental groups**.
Keep its identifier and all seven stage identifiers. Narrow IG.0, IG.1, IG.3,
IG.4 and IG.6 to imports plus their additional mathematics; keep IG.2 and IG.5.
The accompanying JSON records 24 ownership decisions and 29 directed supplier
links. It changes no Tau Ceti roadmap and deletes no target.

This is a restructuring proposal for independent review, not a blueprint,
implementation, or claim that its theorem dependencies are proved. The
maintainer applies an accepted proposal. The extension's introduction must name
the Belyi roadmap as its **first prerequisite** and state the boundary below.

## Decisions by layer

### IG.0: instantiate the existing classification, then compare carriers

Mathlib already supplies the abstract classification used here. At the
programme pin, the statement of
[`CategoryTheory.PreGaloisCategory.functorToContAction`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/082e2d37e8b0463410cdb532e111cd43d5a66174/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Galois/Equivalence.lean#L40)
was read with its ambient `GaloisCategory` and `FiberFunctor` assumptions and
its `IsEquivalence` instance. Likewise,
[`autEmbedding_isClosedEmbedding`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/082e2d37e8b0463410cdb532e111cd43d5a66174/Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Galois/Topology.lean#L107)
and the following instances give compactness, Hausdorffness, total
disconnectedness and the topological group structure on fibre-functor
automorphisms. IG.0 must instantiate these, not plan another proof of them.

The actual finite-étale category for schemes, its geometric fibre functor and
the necessary category axioms remain substantive targets. Components and
ordinary geometric-basepoint comparison remain, including the existence of
the fibre-functor isomorphisms needed for the comparison. The audit specifically
distinguishes these missing instantiations from the existing abstract theory.

[ModularCurves 0D](../../../content/tau-ceti/ModularCurves/README.md#0d-finite-étale-schemes-and-galois-actions)
already owns the field-case equivalence with finite continuous absolute-Galois
sets, including its group-object compatibility. IG.0 imports it to recover
the fundamental group of Spec K. The G_m computation over an algebraically
closed characteristic-zero field stays in IG.0, with the Tate twist and any
choice of compatible roots of unity explicit.

Belyi 12.5 defines a group by a maximal function-field extension; 12.6 compares
it to a free profinite group through fibre functors. Its opening to Layer 12
and its scope exclusions expressly do not build a scheme-theoretic étale
fundamental group or the comparison to one. Accordingly, the audit's IG.0/12
pair is a special-case import and a new comparison obligation, not grounds
for dropping IG.0. The comparison must be a continuous group isomorphism
arising from compatible fibre functors, not a bijection of cover classes.

### IG.1: retain general sequences and specialization

Belyi 12.8–12.9 supplies the arithmetic exact sequence and peripheral inertia
for the three-punctured line in its field-theoretic presentation. IG.1
imports them and proves agreement through IG.0. It retains the general
scheme sequence in the selected SGA range, decomposition and inertia as
subgroups of that group, tame quotients and wild inertia, and the
smooth-proper specialization theorem with its characteristic restrictions.

The extra audit leads identify arithmetic inputs, not replacements for this
scheme theory. [AlgebraicCurves Layer 8](../../../content/tau-ceti/AlgebraicCurves/README.md#layer-8-constant-field-extensions-galois-ramification-and-inseparability-)
owns function-field decomposition/inertia and lower ramification, including
the residue-inseparability qualifications. LocalFieldsRamification Layers
2–4 owns its unramified correspondence, ramification filtration and tame
absolute-Galois quotient. Those local-field imports require their actual
finite-residue-field hypotheses; they do not justify substituting local-field
tame inertia for geometric inertia over an algebraically closed residue field.

The finite-field Frobenius and punctured-curve tests remain. Finite-level
Frobenius cyclicity alone is not the profinite comparison; IG.1 retains the
required comparison with the supplier's inverse-limit calculation. Wild
inertia remains visible before taking the tame quotient.

### IG.2: retain the full Hilbert-specialization programme

Belyi's existence of maps over number fields does not provide a regular
realization of each prescribed finite group over Q, or infinitely many
linearly disjoint specializations. Retain every IG.2 target and its worked
certificates. A new direct IG.2 → IG.6 link ensures that the final polynomial
exports carry the full-group specialization and avoidance hypotheses, not
just a polynomial expression.

### IG.3: import three-point geometry; retain rigidity and the obstruction example

Belyi Layers 0–3 supply triples, passports, dessins and triple-counting;
Layers 5–8 supply the three-point topological/analytic realization; Layers
9–11 supply the algebraic comparison, Belyi's theorem and positive descent
theory; Layer 12 supplies the cyclotomic branch-cycle constraint. IG.3's text
already says to consume this material. The proposal makes that instruction
explicit in ownership entries and prerequisite links.

The pinned
[`TauCeti.PermutationTriple`](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/f790474821cf4256814db967cb154e7af3d0c369/TauCeti/Combinatorics/PermutationTriple/Basic.lean#L71)
and its two-permutation constructor were read directly. The relation is
σ∞σ₁σ₀ = 1 with multiplication by function composition. The implementation
stage must use this existing carrier and check each further API declaration
at the programme pin; neither the roadmap's prototype nor the library audit
is permission to invent a second triple object.

General finite branch sets, braid actions on Nielsen classes, the additional
descent theory and rational rigidity stay in IG.3. Belyi's six branch-point
operations do not replace a general braid action. Likewise, the product-one
character count does not establish generation or rationality; a passport
orbit under the normalizer is not an inner Nielsen class. Those distinctions
are obligations of the rigidity theorem and its worked realization.

Most importantly, Belyi 11.6 proves the positive trivial-automorphism descent
case but explicitly excludes formalized negative examples. IG.3 asks for
an actual example where the field of moduli does not supply a model, so
that construction stays here. Sending it to 11.6 would lose a target.
The dessin/triple comparison, simultaneous-conjugacy convention, actual
ramification check and passport nonuniqueness remain acceptance tests using
the imported objects, rather than duplicate constructions.

### IG.4: import weak lifts; prove proper arithmetic solutions

[ProfiniteProPGroups 5.1](../../../content/tau-ceti/ProfiniteProPGroups/README.md#continuous-extensions-and-finite-p-embedding-problems)
owns the generic finite embedding problem and its continuous weak solution.
It explicitly warns that a solution need not be surjective. IG.4 uses that
carrier and adds properness and arithmetic local constraints. The selected
Shafarevich proof, abelian-kernel reductions, auxiliary ramification, successive
proper solutions and cyclic/dihedral examples all remain. Generic weak
solvability does not discharge the properness induction. This additional
import follows an AUDIT-09 lead outside the supplied family's Belyi pairs;
it changes no outside roadmap.

### IG.5: retain Hurwitz spaces and the characteristic-p branches

The anchor expressly excludes Hurwitz spaces, moduli stacks of covers and
general branch sets. Retain the whole layer, including Nielsen/braid
components, descent, the regular-extension interface, tame reduction and
the separate wild patching/Abhyankar realization subtasks. An Abhyankar
ramification lemma and an Abhyankar realization theorem are different inputs.

The LPV.5 audit lead does not supply this full theory: its text constructs
a tame-specialization argument for the dual locus of a Lefschetz pencil.
It gives no general Hurwitz moduli or wild realization theorem. No speculative
LPV.5 → IG.5 edge is added. Any shared general theorem must be resolved at
declaration granularity during the subsequent blueprints. IG.5's existing
AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry R09.4 dependency remains intact.

### IG.6: export applications through their owners

Belyi 11 supplies its conjugation action and moduli/definition distinction;
12 supplies peripheral inertia and the branch-cycle convention; 13 supplies
the stated faithfulness theorem, including its genus-zero route. IG.6
transports and applies them, without asserting stronger reconstruction.
The exponent convention and the conjugacy-class statement must survive:
componentwise powers of a tuple need not satisfy the product relation.

[PolynomialGaloisGroups Layer 9](../../../content/tau-ceti/PolynomialGaloisGroups/README.md#layer-9-sₙ-as-a-galois-group-over-ℚ)
owns its explicit symmetric-group polynomial family. IG.6 imports that family
into the solved-family index. Other checked polynomials, extensions, cover
maps and group isomorphisms remain outputs of IG.2–IG.5 with their hypotheses.

NC.0 imports the IG fibre functor and arithmetic sequence and constructs
arithmetic path torsors, rational-point sections and tangential-basepoint
comparisons. IG.0 retains ordinary geometric-basepoint comparison; NC.0 uses
that interface for its stronger arithmetic/tangential statements. The final
IG.6 interface references NC.0's torsors instead of constructing them again.
Thus IG.0/IG.1 → NC.0 → IG.6 is allowed; IG.6 → NC.0 would reverse the
supplier direction. The unrestricted inverse Galois and generic-polynomial
frontiers stay identified as such, never imported as proved hypotheses.

## Remaining source obligations and validation

The restructuring decision is complete; the later blueprint is not. Its source
register still requires complete SGA and rigidity proofs, the chosen solvable
realization proof and exact local prescriptions, the constructed descent
obstruction, and separate tame/wild realization sources. This job has not
read those external proof texts or certified their theorem leaves. They remain
explicit obligations of the retained layers, not assertions justified by the
ownership graph. The broad IG.1 SGA range must be pinned by that blueprint.

The audit's LPV.1, LPV.5, FA.3 and R01.2 leads concern neighboring geometric
monodromy, function-field ramification and arithmetic representations. Their
distinct settings were inspected; this proposal neither resolves all overlaps
among those outside roadmaps nor uses them to erase general IG targets.
Prose-only Belyi successor names and their generic profinite supplier require
exact declaration contracts at implementation time. Their absence as separate
atlas stages is recorded here, not repaired by creating phantom IDs.

Validation uses a scratch checker against the snapshot: JSON structure and
actions; existence and ownership of every stage endpoint; unique owner targets
and links; direct suppliers for every narrowed layer; preservation of all
nine recorded consumer edges; and rejection of any new edge whose addition
would close a directed cycle. It also checks the exact 15 family-evidence
pairs are represented by supplier edges and checks for private filesystem
paths. The report records source-level checks at both pinned library commits.

`scripts/check_blueprint.py` accepts `blueprint-v1` packets with declaration
nodes, not the section-15 restructuring format; it is not a valid checker for
this JSON. No blueprint packet or suggested Lean file belongs to RS-29, so
