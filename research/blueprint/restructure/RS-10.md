## Before and after

Before, HR.1 owns Lambda theory, HR.4 owns degree-zero q-Witt theory and HQ.4 owns the positive
degrees. HB.6 and HR.5 each describe a twisted Taylor ring. HQ.3 requires HQ.4, while the reviewed
plan gives HQ.4 an application of HQ.3. The ordinary and derived completion descriptions also
repeat elementary coefficient machinery. A cohomology test stage currently waits for an open
analytic comparison.

q-Witt theory. HR.1 constructs the early Taylor ring, HB.6 specializes it arithmetically, and
HR.5 identifies it with the independently glued relative ring. HR.5-number-field-comparison

input of the coefficient construction. HQ.3 owns the descent theorem, HQ.4 applies it to framed
complexes, and the trace-dependent existence case lives in HQ.5-trace.

by HR.2. At declaration granularity its ordinary construction still follows HC.4 alone. The

a derived construction. Likewise HC.6's late rational-number-field comparison test moves to
