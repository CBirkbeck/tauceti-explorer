## Decision and extension boundary

Keep **Smooth representations of local groups** as the general foundation. Make
**GL₂ Automorphic Representations And Transfer** an extension titled
**Modular forms — Hecke theory, newforms, and L-functions, Part II: GL₂ automorphic
representations and transfer**. Put `tauceti:TauCetiRoadmap/ModularForms` first in
its whole-roadmap prerequisite list, retaining the other prerequisites. Its
introduction should state that classical forms, their Hecke algebra, primitive
forms, classical multiplicity, coefficient fields and classical L-functions are
imported. Its new mathematics begins with the number-field automorphic realization,
explicit local representation formulas and global transfers.

No roadmap is retired or merged. All 23 stage IDs survive; 15 scopes narrow and
8 remain. The JSON has 38 ownership entries and 161 directed links, of which 133
are absent from the current stage-edge union. No existing edge is removed.
Smooth theory is not an extension of classical modular forms: its locally
profinite groups and coefficient regimes serve many independent consumers.
Nevertheless its general Hecke theory must import the anchor's special cases and
prove compatibility with them. No Tau Ceti roadmap or anchor stage changes.

The three extracted SR.0 entries contain the same source paragraph. They denote
an early compatibility alias, an abelian construction and a separately imported
late derived extension. They must not generate three copies of the category, or
make completed cohomology's early smooth modules depend on the entire derived
and spectral-action programme.

### Source checks and limits

Read the closed-subgroup induction and Jacquet statements in Casselman's
[author draft, 1 May 1995](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~cass/research/pdf/p-adic-book.pdf),
§2.4 (PDF pp.25–27) and §3.2 (PDF pp.33–35). Its smooth induction has the
closed-subgroup right adjunction `Res ⊣ Ind`; compact induction must not be
assigned that direction. The parabolic first adjunction is `r_P ⊣ i_P` in the
specified normalization. The PDF SHA-256 is
`78b921fc6331ad2b75389f37d15bd5ac3399e6cfc2d7555e1a4d666e0d42612a`.
The downloaded draft is not a checked source for all conductor/newvector targets.

Read DHKM [arXiv:2203.04929v2, §1](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.04929v2).
Finite generation over a finitely generated center has the noetherian
Z_ell-algebra, ell≠p hypothesis (Theorems 1.1–1.2). Second adjointness holds over
Z[1/p]-algebras (Corollary 1.3); its unnormalized right adjoint includes the
modulus twist. Theorem 1.7 and Corollary 1.8 retain integral finite-wild parameter
quotients and reduction in the excursion statement. These are late targets;
the excursion-center action is a substantive input. This check supports the
coefficient and ownership boundary, not a verification of the entire proof.

Bernstein's early second-adjointness/uniform-admissibility proof, Helm's integral
family hypotheses, the full Casselman newvector proof, the global converse
proof, and the wild/dyadic transfer and Tunnell/Rohrlich proof chains still need
versioned theorem-by-theorem source closure in the blueprint jobs. No missing
source has been replaced by an assertion that the corresponding theorem is built.
