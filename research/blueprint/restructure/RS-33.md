## The five family leads

1. **H.1 / AT2: genuine duplicated foundation, retained comparison.** Local
   systems and twisted chains belong to AT2; H.1 constructs the comparison
   between category/group coefficients and that singular theory. BG and its
   bar comparison remain new. UniversalCovers owns the covering machinery
   and K(G,1) recognition, not a simplicial construction of BG for every G.
2. **H.2 / AT5: shared carrier, not the same theorem.** AT5 owns disc-lifting
   Serre fibrations, their pullbacks/maps, and the homology Serre spectral
   sequence. H.2 still owes path replacement, homotopy pullback/fibre and the
   homotopy LES. Require compatibility with AT5 where that carrier is used.
   A quasi-fibration in Quillen's argument is not automatically a Serre
   fibration; no such instance is proposed.
3. **H.2 / AT8: exact import.** Kan homotopy groups, the singular Kan result
   and the cubical comparison are AT8. H.2 proves its fibre-sequence
   application using this interface. The based-pair LES does not by itself
   supply the fibre LES. Keep points, transport and nonabelian/pointed-set
   low degrees visible, including the anchor's degree-two warning.
4. **H.3 / AT8: theorem versus application.** Import Hurewicz and Whitehead;
   retain their use in the plus tests and in the generic central-extension
   consequences needed by T.1:plus and V.1. Those arithmetic applications
   also receive direct AT8 links.
5. **H.6 / AT4: special case versus extension.** AT4 keeps its skeletal
   exact couple and cellular/singular comparison. H.6 keeps filtered spectra
   and their convergence, and proves agreement in the ordinary
   Eilenberg--Mac Lane/CW case. This is a Part II compatibility obligation,
   not an assertion that AT4 already proves spectral convergence. The audit
   distinguishes existing spectral-object machinery from missing
   exact-couple and convergence work; the anchor's inventory alone is not
   declaration-level evidence of a built general exact-couple package.

## Additional overlaps and the ordering correction

### Group completion and Q

The reviewed packet already links
`H.4/cofinality-projective-modules` to `GeneralAlgebraicKTheory:K.2:plus`.
Keep that direction. H.4 proves cofinal **homotopy** stabilization and the
projective group-completion-to-plus comparison. Z.1 supplies actual finite
projective complements, U.1 the stable elementary/perfect subgroup, and
GrothendieckEulerForms supplies the algebraic K0 carrier. K.2:plus proves
the separate Q-to-completion comparison and assembles ring K-theory.

This does not create a reverse Q-to-H.4 edge. It also preserves the distinction
between the maximal subgroupoid and the contractible category of all
projective maps; free-module and projective components; block-sum naturality
and chosen translations between components; and the packet's countability
and phantom-map qualifications on uniqueness. Existing coherence theorems
do not discharge the remaining strictification-independence proof.

### Abstract categories, concrete spectra, and products

EDS E0 owns abstract stability; E5:abstract owns monoidal/operadic
enhancement. H.5:spectra constructs an actual model and verifies its
properties, rather than creating a second abstract axiom package. Its
Eilenberg--Mac Lane construction is distinct from the later EDS comparison
of spectral modules, tensor products and actions.

Place the **generic concrete smash and homotopy pairing** in H.5:spectra,
reexported by the later child. RefinedTraceMethods already requests concrete
spectra and smash products from this early supplier. K.7 owns actual
biexact-functor K-theory products and their application-specific coherences,
not the ambient smash operation. Add early-spectrum and S-assembly inputs
to K.7, never a K.7 input to H.5. The EDS comparison remains a late return.
These are different theorems with explicit handoffs, not a reason to merge
the category, homotopy and K-theory roadmaps.
