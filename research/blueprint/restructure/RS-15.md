## Mathematical regression obligations

These are implementation acceptance requirements, **not executed Lean tests**.

**Coefficients and signs.** Test p-typical, ramified mixed-characteristic and equal-characteristic coefficients, with the uniformizer inverted in the isocrystal field. For a block whose r-th Frobenius is pi^s sigma^r, track the isocrystal slope s/r separately from the associated bundle slope. For general G, compare bundle HN via `w0(-nu)` and the degree class via `-kappa`; never infer the full Kottwitz class solely from the rational Newton point.

**Morphisms and descent.** Unequal slopes and a nontrivial extension test that the geometric iso-class classification has not become full faithfulness of the whole isocrystal functor. A rank-one local system with nontrivial monodromy tests the distinction between a constant zero HN polygon, pro-etale local triviality and global triviality. A geometric-point bijection alone must fail the relative-stack acceptance test.

**Ordinary versus general properness.** The early ordinary-bundle theorem must elaborate without classification or VB4. Its properness and geometric surjectivity, followed by the scalar torsor, produce the VB4 v-cover on the specified locus. The two-term theorem remains separate: test `[0 → O(1)]` and a genuine negative-source complex, preserving the stated slope and cover hypotheses. Do not infer necessity of every sufficient hypothesis merely because the source assumes it.

**Nonbasic automorphisms.** For the bundle `O ⊕ O(1)`, retain the off-diagonal section group; discarding it would replace the full automorphism object by just the two scalar factors. Test the positive graded contribution, the automorphism dimension and the opposite sign for its classifying stack. The later sheaf-category comparison does not permit erasing this geometric kernel.

**Dependency negative controls.** Adding VB4 → general-BC while retaining the old reverse edge must be rejected. Routing general-BC or projectivized properness into geometric classification must also be rejected. Completed Satake and late Bun_G coefficient categories must not become inputs of the early smooth-Artin atlas.

The integration requires stage-link correction and import annotations, not deletion of the stronger general-BC nodes. The relevant review follow-ups are:

1. The first isocrystal node's title inverts pi, but its statement omits that inversion when naming the coefficient field. Correct the carrier spelling before implementation, preserving the intended field and scalar Frobenius normalization.
2. The slope-zero local-system node asks for a bundle with all fibre slopes zero but nonconstant polygon. In the fixed-rank setting that is not a valid negative example. Use a genuinely nonzero HN polygon or nontrivial monodromy instead. Its global-section shorthand also needs the appropriate section sheaf/local-system interpretation on a disconnected base, not an unconditional identification with the scalar field.
3. Maintain node-level early/late distinctions: VB1's bundle carrier precedes the basic Lubin–Tate calculation, whereas later cohomology statements can use that calculation. The decomposition records such a return. A coarse all-of-VB1 prerequisite is not permission to assume the later cohomology theorem in its own explicit input proof. Similarly the associated-bundle normalization is checked after the actual functor exists.

These are precise re-review/implementation obligations, not extra claimed formalization or a second audit of the whole decomposition.
