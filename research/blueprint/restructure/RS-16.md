## The ownership cuts

1. **Completed algebras and measures.** ProfiniteProPGroups Layer 9 prerequisites own ℤₚ[[G]] for arbitrary profinite G, not just pro-p groups. PM L1 extends coefficients and proves the measure comparison, retaining noncommutative multiplication order and the joint inverse limit. Bounded K-valued measures are O[[G]][1/p]; exchanging coefficient inversion and inverse limit would change the object. Coleman L0 and arithmetic I.1 instantiate the action theorem. NE.0's p-adic Lie finiteness is an additional theorem.
2. **Bounded versus locally analytic transforms.** PM L0/L2 own the bounded interface; LAD L0/L1 construct the different test-function topology and its strong dual, and prove the commuting inclusion. CPL L1 constructs a norm and proves that its normalized trace matches the bounded ψ. PG.4 must separately compare its period-ring ψ. Shared formulas do not identify these three carriers.
3. **Early weights, late actions.** PM L0a owns the scalar character functor. LAD L3 imports it and proves Mellin evaluation. Move only PM L0a's action on affinoid locally analytic/distribution coefficient modules, its uniform radii and completed-tensor action comparisons to LAD L4, which already builds those modules. O0, PadicFamilies L2a and PG.7 receive direct L4 edges. LAD L3 needs the retained scalar character and does not import the later family action.
4. **Module algebra and arithmetic.** PM L4 owns the elementary-module and characteristic-invariant extensions to pinned Weierstrass. PM L5 owns compact-module exactness/Nakayama and determinant comparisons. I.1, I.4 and L2 prove the particular arithmetic hypotheses and resulting control/growth statements. A finite error invisible at height one can survive specialization.
5. **Finite units, local closures, global theorem.** Iwasawa L0 owns global cyclotomic-unit subgroups, conductor generators and the finite index calculation. CPL L4 owns their local principal-unit closure, compatible generators and local quotient. Iwasawa L1 owns the classical local/global sequence on I.2's general arithmetic carriers. L3 keeps Vandiver explicit; the Euler-system proof and historical Hecke proof are separate mathematical methods and are not deleted as duplicate conclusions.
6. **General missing algebra.** P7 supplies the earlier complete-Noetherian-local perfect-module construction and derived specialization; S.1 extends to the general-affine comparison, local perfectness and scheme interfaces. PM L5 builds the determinant functor on that carrier, and PS.4/M.8/Selmer/automorphic consumers apply it. StableReduction Layer 1 owns the shared finite-presentation Fitting carrier; PM L4/L6 add the required Iwasawa/order comparisons. PM L6 owns the generic exterior bidual and its order-duality/base-change API; ES.6–8 retain the actual system contractions, transition maps and arithmetic bounds.

`suppliedBy` in PM L0a is a **relocation record**. It must not be automatically turned into an L4 → L0a prerequisite: the retained early stage no longer needs that late theorem. The explicit links are normative: L0a → L4 and L4 → the three affected consumers. All other `suppliedBy` rows in this proposal are imports into the retained layer. No original graph edge needs removal.
