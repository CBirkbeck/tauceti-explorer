### Distinctions which prevent invalid consolidation

**Ext is not Tor.** T6's cohomological universal-coefficient theorem uses Ext from homology. ALS.1 also requires derived *coefficient change*, with Tor terms for tensoring a coefficient complex. The latter is not discharged by citing the former. For example the perfect cochain complex `[O --varpi--> O]` in degrees 0 and 1 has zero H⁰, but its derived reduction to the residue field has nonzero H⁰. A comparison that simply tensors H⁰ misses precisely the correction. ALS.1 retains this proof obligation and its coefficient hypotheses.

**Finite cells are not a completed equivariant model.** T4 constructs cellular chains; ALS.1 supplies actual finite-level arithmetic models; ALS.2 supplies the Borel–Serre geometry; CC.4 lifts the chosen cells coherently along the tower and forms the completed module complex. It must prove finite-level recovery by derived coinvariants. Neither finite-level projectivity nor an abstract perfect-complex predicate proves this construction. Bounded projective terms also do not imply projective homology, as the same two-term complex demonstrates.

**Generic duality is not arithmetic corner duality.** The anchor's manifold-with-boundary statement does not by itself supply a corner reduction, a theorem for arbitrary orbifolds with torsion stabilizers, or its perfect-coefficient/derived formulation. Those bridges remain at the early ALS.5 prefix. Orientation twists, coefficient hypotheses and the adjoint Hecke involution remain in the target.

**Finite-cover and completed descent are different applications.** T5 supplies chain transfer and the appropriate generic finite-cover descent. ALS.4 constructs the unipotent/Levi applications on boundary strata, ALS.6 the arithmetic finite-cover application, and CC.6 descent from the completed object with its convergence and edge-map qualifications. They are not three copies of one theorem. The existing R02/D7 continuous-cohomology suppliers remain unchanged. In particular no CC.6 → ALS.6 edge is introduced by conflating these statements.

**Supports and degrees do not disappear in the limit.** Ordinary, compactly supported, boundary and Borel–Moore variants retain their maps and direction conventions. The completed interior object is a specified image requiring its own comparison, not a formal interchange of image and inverse limit. CC.7 is not an arithmetic vanishing theorem.
