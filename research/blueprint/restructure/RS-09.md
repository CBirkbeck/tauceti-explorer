## Decision and extension frontier

Keep **Arithmetic locally symmetric spaces and their cohomology** (ALS). Keep the stable identifier `CompletedCohomologyPartII` (CC), and name its extension relation explicitly:

> Arithmetic locally symmetric spaces and their cohomology, Part II: completed cohomology and homology

Its first prerequisite is the existing finite-level ALS construction, not the GL₂/Qₚ local–global compatibility roadmap. CC starts with the actual spaces, coefficient complexes and level maps of ALS.0–4; its support/duality suffix also uses ALS.5:finite-level-duality. It adds the completed objects and their topology, equivariant completed chain complexes, finiteness, descent and named Shimura adapters. The construction can be stated for towers satisfying the source hypotheses, with ALS providing the arithmetic instantiation; the title does not add an arithmetic assumption to an abstract theorem.

Round 1 called this a "stage-scoped extension" and let ALS.6 re-export CC.2/CC.4/CC.7 on top of CC; REV-RS-09 rejected that, and round 2 narrows ALS.6 to its finite-level content (finite-cover descent), gives its tower assembly to CC.0/CC.1 and drops its re-exports, whose objects CC.2, CC.4 and CC.7 own. The existing Tau Ceti **Algebraic topology of spaces and manifolds** roadmap is unchanged. Generic topology is imported from its actual stages. ALS is an arithmetic application of several foundations, not a second generic topology roadmap.

### Distinctions which prevent invalid consolidation

**Ext is not Tor.** T6's cohomological universal-coefficient theorem uses Ext from homology. ALS.1 also requires derived *coefficient change*, with Tor terms for tensoring a coefficient complex. The latter is not discharged by citing the former. For example the perfect cochain complex `[O --varpi--> O]` in degrees 0 and 1 has zero H⁰, but its derived reduction to the residue field has nonzero H⁰. A comparison that simply tensors H⁰ misses precisely the correction. ALS.1 retains this proof obligation and its coefficient hypotheses.

**Finite cells are not a completed equivariant model.** T4 constructs cellular chains; ALS.1 supplies actual finite-level arithmetic models; ALS.2 supplies the Borel–Serre geometry; CC.4 lifts the chosen cells coherently along the tower and forms the completed module complex. It must prove finite-level recovery by derived coinvariants. Neither finite-level projectivity nor an abstract perfect-complex predicate proves this construction. Bounded projective terms also do not imply projective homology, as the same two-term complex demonstrates.

**Generic duality is not arithmetic corner duality.** The anchor's manifold-with-boundary statement does not by itself supply a corner reduction, a theorem for arbitrary orbifolds with torsion stabilizers, or its perfect-coefficient/derived formulation. Those bridges remain at the early ALS.5 prefix. Orientation twists, coefficient hypotheses and the adjoint Hecke involution remain in the target.

**Finite-cover and completed descent are different applications.** T5 supplies chain transfer and the appropriate generic finite-cover descent. ALS.4 constructs the unipotent/Levi applications on boundary strata, ALS.6 the arithmetic finite-cover application, and CC.6 descent from the completed object with its convergence and edge-map qualifications. They are not three copies of one theorem. The existing R02/D7 continuous-cohomology suppliers remain unchanged. Round 2 adds ALS.6 → CC.6: the completed descent restricts at each finite stage to the finite-cover spectral sequence, and imports it rather than restating it. No CC.6 → ALS.6 edge exists or is added.

**Supports and degrees do not disappear in the limit.** Ordinary, compactly supported, boundary and Borel–Moore variants retain their maps and direction conventions. The completed interior object is a specified image requiring its own comparison, not a formal interchange of image and inverse limit. CC.7 is not an arithmetic vanishing theorem.
