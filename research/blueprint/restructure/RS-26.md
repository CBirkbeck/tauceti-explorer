Author: ChatGPT (GPT-6 Astra Pro). Session: `astra-7c41e9`. Issue: #852.

## Decision

**Keep all three roadmaps, all 25 stage ids and their current mathematical
scope.** No merger, retirement, title change, move, drop or narrowing is needed.
Record nine shared owners and five explicit prerequisite edges.

The family file contains 24 directed evidence rows, representing **12 unordered
pairs**, all labelled `audit-unreviewed`. Its leads are not 24 independent
instances of duplicated construction. The current member documents already say
that the regulator and local Langlands programmes import the PG engine.

- `PhiGammaModulesAndIwasawaCohomology` owns the specialized cyclotomic
  coefficient/category and comparison engine, Wach modules and qualified
  relative cohomology. It imports analytic annulus carriers and general
  continuous/Iwasawa cochains from their separate owners.
- `PadicHodgeRegulators` owns Bloch–Kato maps, vector regulators, interpolation,
  integral Coleman-map images and the independent K-theoretic/syntomic strand.
- `PadicLocalLanglandsForGL2Qp` owns the GL2(Q_p) representation construction,
  compatibility and exceptional-block theorems. It is not a second local
  Iwasawa-cohomology programme.

There is **no existing Tau Ceti anchor in this family**. Nor is either
application simply a further range of the same theorem as the PG engine: a
vector-valued regulator and a Banach representation have different outputs.
The Part-II rule therefore does not require turning either whole application
into a numbered continuation. The common inputs are imported explicitly instead.
