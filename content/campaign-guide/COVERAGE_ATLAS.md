# Modern number theory coverage atlas

This is a subject-ownership map. All detailed number-theory codes in the supplied official [MSC2020 classification](https://mathscinet.ams.org/mathscinet/msc/pdfs/classifications2020.pdf) are assigned to a roadmap. The classification is a coverage scaffold; individual theorem and proof completeness requires the [execution gates](AI_EXECUTION.md).

## Additional connected strands

The classification rows connect to scheme/stack/intersection foundations; etale, crystalline, prismatic and rigid cohomology; perfectoids and diamonds; Fargues-Fontaine and geometric local Langlands; global shtukas; motives and periods; ordinary and hermitian K-theory; abelian and noncommutative Iwasawa theory; anabelian and Chabauty methods; tropical/Berkovich geometry; automorphy, torsion cohomology and trace formulas; Habiro and arithmetic quantum topology. See the complete area index.
