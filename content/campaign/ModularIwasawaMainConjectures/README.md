# main conjectures for modular forms and their families

Comparison and source-precise ordinary, signed and family equalities.

## Canonical ownership and interfaces

- Retain FW v3 and BCS v2 as explicitly versioned proof targets. A theorem's current publication status is not inferred from its presence in the package.
- Use one lattice, one period line and one primitive/imprimitive convention on both divisibilities. Derived determinant specialization precedes any ordinary or signed characteristic-ideal simplification.
- Generic Euler/Kolyvagin-system descent is supplied by [EulerSystemsAndKolyvaginSystems](../EulerSystemsAndKolyvaginSystems/README.md); KatoEulerSystems verifies its hypotheses for the constructed modular classes. This roadmap combines the resulting bound with an independently proved reverse divisibility, and does not construct another generic Euler-system theory.

**Campaign dependencies:** [AutomorphicCongruences](../AutomorphicCongruences/README.md), [KatoEulerSystems](../KatoEulerSystems/README.md), [PadicHodgeRegulators](../PadicHodgeRegulators/README.md), [SelmerIwasawaCohomology](../SelmerIwasawaCohomology/README.md), [PadicFamilies](../PadicFamilies/README.md).

The source-label dictionary and audit resolves H/K/Z/U/T/V/S/M/L/N/R/P/D/I/B/E/ER/HB and R01–R16 references below. The shared conventions are part of this specification. Suggested.lean contains non-exhaustive prototypes; the complete targets are here.

## Scope

Formulate the cyclotomic main conjecture in ordinary Selmer, cohomological/determinant and signed forms, prove their precise comparisons, and establish the theorem families below. This gives an explicit replacement for RJW Theorem B.4's unspecified 'mild additional technical hypotheses'. The source hypotheses are part of the theorem, not a hidden assumption that a main conjecture is already true.

For ordinary forms of arbitrary nebentypus and for more general refinements the definitions and comparison theorems are retained. The unconditional equality targets are the explicitly listed source families; neither R14's analytic construction nor a generic regulator proves all other main conjectures.

## L0. The three formulations

Fix the actual representation and stable lattice T from R11, V=T[1/p] and A=V/T. Define the ordinary Greenberg Selmer group using the selected saturated local submodule and unramified conditions away from p. Its dual X is compact, with the contragredient Iwasawa action. Prove finite generation and torsion under the hypotheses of the relevant theorem; neither is part of the definition of X.

State the ordinary integral equality

    char_Lambda X = (L_p(f,alpha))

with canonical integral periods and matching primitive/imprimitive convention. Character components use the involution and Tate twist determined by R07. A rational equality is explicitly over Lambda[1/p].

Construct Kato's cohomological complex with the exact extension-by-zero/local conditions away from p used in the source; do not replace it by unrestricted Galois cohomology for an arbitrary larger set S without correction. State its equality as

    char_Lambda H^2_Iw = char_Lambda(H^1_Iw / Lambda z_Kato)

on the appropriate components, equivalently generation of its fundamental line by the zeta element. Prove the comparison with the ordinary statement via Poitou–Tate, local regulators and all Euler/Tamagawa corrections. This is not the stronger statement of every special-value/leading-term conjecture at points where the L-value vanishes.

For crystalline nonordinary representations define signed local conditions as the orthogonal complements of kernels of the chosen integral Coleman maps. Initially the analytic term is

    char_Lambda(Im(Col_i) / Lambda L_(p,i)),

not automatically the principal ideal generated by L_(p,i). Compute the image using R09 and LLZ Theorem 5.10; simplify to a principal-ideal formula only after the image correction is proved trivial up to pseudo-isomorphism. Record dependence on the Wach basis and the logarithm-matrix comparison with the unbounded refined functions.

## L1. Ordinary theorem with explicit ramification hypotheses (T1)

Prove the ordinary modular theorem in the form recorded as FW Theorem 1.6. FW explicitly modifies the cited SU statement by lifting a weight restriction and using a different period argument; the proof route is SU together with those specified refinements, not SU alone. Let f be a normalized cuspidal eigenform of weight w≥2 on Gamma0(N), with trivial nebentypus and p not dividing N. Assume the standing odd-prime/coefficient conditions of that theorem, absolute irreducibility of the residual representation, and a prime ell exactly dividing N such that

    dim(bar V_f)^(I_ell) = 1,       dim(bar V_f)^(G_Qell) = 0.

At p require the ordinary filtration by characters and distinct residual characters. Keep the determinant, lattice and cohomological normalization fixed by L0. R12 gives one divisibility and R15.L1–L2, with FW §4.8’s period refinement, gives the opposite divisibility; their combination proves the equality. Provide both the cohomological formulation and the ordinary Selmer/p-adic-L-function formulation after proving the integral-period comparison.

The older author manuscript of SU has different theorem numbering and a differently packaged hypothesis set. Do not substitute its 'up to a power of p' version for the integral theorem or identify those statements merely by title. The preserved source-theorem catalogue records the versions separately.

## L2. Broader cohomological theorem (T2)

Prove FW Theorem 1.7 in the following form. Let p>2, let the coefficient residue field be finite of characteristic p, and let bar rho be an absolutely irreducible odd representation. Require:

1. For every residual character chi, the semisimplification of bar rho restricted to G_Qp is not chi ⊕ chi_cyc chi.
2. There exists ell≠p such that the local representation is a ramified extension

       0 → mu chi_cyc^(1−w/2) → bar rho|G_Qell
         → mu chi_cyc^(−w/2) → 0,

   with mu the nontrivial unramified quadratic character.

Let f be a normalized cuspidal eigenform of even weight w≥2 on Gamma0(N), with residual representation isomorphic to bar rho and ell exactly dividing N. In FW’s representation convention, prove the integral cohomological main conjecture, FW Conjecture 1.5. Prove the translation from this normalization to R11; the half-weight twists here are legitimate because the trivial-nebentypus form has even weight.

This theorem allows the reductions at p covered in FW, not only good ordinary reduction. Its local residual exclusion and the auxiliary ramified prime remain genuine restrictions. Derive the ordinary and, at crystalline nonordinary points, signed consequences via L0. Do not assert a globally bounded scalar L-function at a supercuspidal/infinite-slope point merely because the cohomological main conjecture holds.

## L3. Ordinary elliptic curves with irreducible residual representation (T3)

Prove BCS Theorem 1.1.2: for an elliptic curve E/Q, p>3 a prime of good ordinary reduction, and E[p] irreducible, the ordinary Selmer dual is torsion and the cyclotomic main-conjecture equality holds over Lambda[1/p]. For the integral strengthening, additionally assume that there exists

    sigma ∈ G_(Q(mu_(p^infinity)))
    with T_p(E)/(sigma−1)T_p(E) ≅ Z_p.

Then prove the equality over Lambda itself. Construct the analytic L-function using R10 and the curve's modular form, and prove the comparison of canonical periods and lattices. When using modularity of E, consume the actual modularity theorem from its owning roadmap; alternatively state and prove the comparison for a specified modular parametrization, with modularity added as a separate dependency rather than silently assumed.

Surjectivity of the residual representation is a useful sufficient condition in appropriate source corollaries but is not interchangeable with bare irreducibility or with the displayed rank-one quotient without proof. R15.L5 provides the anticyclotomic/two-variable proof ingredients.

## L4. Families and signed consequences (T4–T5)

Prove FW Theorem 1.8 for the specified universal family with the same residual/local restrictions as its statement. In that theorem the universal representation is viewed as a finite free module over the specified regular subring Lambda ≃ O[[X1,X2,X3]], and the zeta morphism trivializes the corresponding integral fundamental line **over Lambda**. Do not silently strengthen this to a generator statement over every possibly singular deformation ring. R15.L3–L4 owns its deformation and descent proof. Derive specializations by derived base change, recording Euler and local-condition corrections. Do not claim that every deformation ring is regular or every specialization preserves a characteristic ideal without Tor terms.

Prove the signed main-conjecture comparison of LLZ for the crystalline forms to which its local theory applies. Combine it with T2 only on the intersection of both hypothesis sets. First retain the image-of-Coleman-map term. Then use LLZ Theorem 5.13, for the bases it constructs, to deduce the simplified characteristic-ideal equality up to the proven pseudo-null correction. This basis-adjusted statement is not automatically Pollack's or another author's standard plus/minus convention; prove that comparison separately in its range.

## L5. Consequences and integrity tests

Prove the local Euler-factor change formulas, invariance under coefficient extension, and the relation between mu invariants of the two sides. Give ordinary weight-two, higher-weight, and crystalline nonordinary examples satisfying the stated hypotheses; verify the hypotheses explicitly rather than naming a familiar form without a ramification calculation.

Define the Greenberg and Coates–Perrin-Riou conjectural generalizations from RJW as mathematical propositions with all their inputs. This package does not claim to prove Greenberg's totally-real mu=lambda=0 conjecture or BSD in unrestricted rank.

**Acceptance:** the integral cohomological theorem T2, the ordinary theorem T1, the correctly distinguished rational/integral T3, the universal-family theorem T4, and the signed comparisons T5; each has a traceable analytic construction, arithmetic class, Selmer complex and independently proved reverse divisibility.

## Shared conventions and sources

The programme conventions, dependency contracts, and source register are part of this specification. References such as RJW, Rubin, SU, and FW denote the precise sources and versions listed there. The mathematical milestones above, not a source-code inventory, define completion.

## Public API and review contract

Every construction supplies extensionality, constructor/eliminator lemmas, identity/composition, equivalence invariance and scalar-extension or base-change compatibility wherever defined. Quotients have universal properties and map-level comparison theorems. Finiteness, flatness, admissibility, convergence, perfectness and integral-lattice hypotheses are stated and proved in each unconditional application.

A completed target includes the intrinsic construction, its working API, the stated comparisons and the worked tests above. Matching abstract groups with different maps, rational substitutes for integral statements, or an expected equality stored in an unconstrained structure do not discharge the contract. Check current Mathlib/Tau Ceti APIs before selecting names. Suggested signatures involving unavailable higher objects remain explicit comments; no stand-in theorem asserts their conclusions.

## Sources and evidence

The source register and machine-readable references fix source versions and download checks. Source theorem numbers refer to those versions. A bibliographic citation does not claim the result is formalized.
