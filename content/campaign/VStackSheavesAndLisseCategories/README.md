# Roadmap: Artin v-stacks, solid and lisse coefficient categories

Construct the sheaf-theoretic extension from ECD diamonds to the categories used by
Fargues–Scholze. Dependencies: [DiamondsAndVStacks](../DiamondsAndVStacks/README.md),
[EnhancedDerivedSheaves](../EnhancedDerivedSheaves/README.md),
[DiamondSixOperations](../DiamondSixOperations/README.md),
[AdicCoefficientsAndComparisons](../AdicCoefficientsAndComparisons/README.md),
[RelativeFarguesFontaine](../RelativeFarguesFontaine/README.md), and
[VectorBundlesAndIsocrystals](../VectorBundlesAndIsocrystals/README.md).
VS4–VS5 also use [BunGAndNewtonStrata](../BunGAndNewtonStrata/README.md) BG0–BG4 and
[SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups](../SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups/README.md).
VS0–VS3 do not consume Bun_G charts, avoiding the BG4/VS1 dependency cycle.

## VS0. Artin v-stacks and eligible operations

Define Artin v-stacks by representable diagonals and the cohomologically smooth atlas
condition of FS IV.1; prove atlas independence and stability under fibre products.
Construct smooth-lisse descent of the enhanced coefficient category and extend the
allowed operations with specified representability, separation and finite-dimensional
hypotheses. Prove pullback, exceptional pullback and their normalization for smooth
atlases, and the comparison with existing small-v-stack ECD categories.

Construct partial compact-support functors of FS IV.5 for stacks whose structural map
is not in the original representable ECD class. Prove independence of presentation,
adjunctions and base-change formulas in their actual scope. In particular notation
pi-sharp for an Artin stack is not an unqualified ordinary ECD Rpi!.

## VS1. ULA, Jacobian criterion and localization

Define relative local acyclicity and universal local acyclicity with the overconvergence,
geometric-fibre and constructibility conditions of FS IV.2. Prove base change, composition,
descent, tensor and relative-dual properties with all compactness hypotheses. Construct
formal smoothness and the Jacobian criterion of IV.3–IV.4 for the specified relative
section functors on Fargues–Fontaine curves. Prove the tangent/cohomology calculation,
the positive-slope condition and the resulting cohomological smoothness. This general
criterion uses already established diamond geometry; it cannot justify the earlier
AdicEtaleGeometry A3 input to constructing diamonds.

Construct attractor/repeller correspondences, hyperbolic localization and the comparison
map of FS IV.6, including equivariance and boundedness hypotheses. Prove its compatibility
with base change and duality. Establish Drinfeld's lemma IV.7 for the divisor/Weil-action
applications, separating partial Frobenius descent from an arbitrary fundamental-group
product claim. Satake uses these results as named inputs.

## VS2. Condensed and solid foundations

Use Mathlib's `CondensedMod`, its free/forgetful adjunction, and its profinite free-module
and solidification maps. The current `CondensedMod.IsSolid` documentation explicitly
restricts its naive general-ring definition: first prove the finite-type Z-algebra
construction, then extend to general coefficient rings by the prescribed restriction
of scalars. Do not silently use that predicate as already correct for every ring.
Construct the missing solidification universal property, derived solid
tensor/Hom and the generators/exactness needed for FS VII.1. Build the finite/cofiltered
limit and completion comparisons with actual topological modules. These are new module
constructions on EnhancedDerivedSheaves' common enhancement, not a second infinity model.

Construct D_solid(X,Lambda) over the appropriate cutoffs on a small v-stack, four
operations and relative homology f-sharp in the FS VII.2–VII.3 range. Prove the solid
projection/base-change identities, comparison with torsion D_et, and dualizability theorem
of VII.4–VII.5. The unrestricted large solid category need not be presentable at one
fixed universe; construct compatible adjoints at adequate cutoffs.

## VS3. Lisse categories and coefficients

Fix ell != p and a discrete Z_ell-algebra Lambda, interpreted as the condensed extension
Z_ell tensor_(Z_ell,disc) Lambda_disc. Define D_lis of an Artin v-stack as the stable
subcategory closed under sums generated by f-sharp Lambda for separated maps f
representable in locally spatial diamonds and ell-cohomologically smooth. Here lisse refers to the Artin-stack lisse site, not to
the assertion that every object is a locally constant local system.

Prove the inclusion's right adjoint, tensor closure, pullback and lisse internal Hom/
pushforward constructions. Compare ordinary, adic, solid and lisse coefficients through
the actual fully faithful functors of VII.6. Prove reduction and scalar extension in
their established range. Rational coefficients require the constructed solid/lisse
localization; replacing every integral complex by its tensor with Q_ell is not the
definition of a completed coefficient system.

## VS4. Sheaves on Bun_G strata and compact generation

Identify D_et([*/H],Lambda) with the enhanced derived category of smooth H-representations
for H locally pro-p and prime-to-p torsion Lambda. Construct both functors using continuous
torsor descent; identify pullback with forgetting the action and invariants with the
derived appropriate pushforward. Apply this to J_b(E), and prove the connected-kernel
invariance giving the category for nonbasic Bun_G^b. Extend to D_lis in the precise
coefficient range of VII.7.

Prove localization triangles and the infinite semiorthogonal decomposition along the
HN strata using justified exhaustions. Characterize compact objects by compact restrictions
with finite stratum support, and by the thick closure of compact induction from pro-p
open subgroups. These are the same compact induction objects as the local-group roadmap.

## VS5. Duality and admissible objects

Construct Bernstein–Zelevinsky duality on compact Bun_G objects using the partial-support
functor; prove biduality and its basic-stratum comparison. Separately construct Verdier
duality on ULA objects and prove that ULA is equivalent to perfection of K-invariants on
every stratum for every appropriate pro-p open K. Prove scalar-extension and Hecke-use
compatibility; admissibility of complexes means perfect invariants, not just finite
dimensions of separate cohomology groups.

## Acceptance and sources

Tests: point, [*/Z_p], a basic Bun_G stratum, a nonbasic stratum, a compactly induced
representation, a noncompact-support stratum family and a perfect Z_ell complex. Distinguish
D_solid(point,F_ell) from the ordinary derived category of discrete vector spaces.
All geometric sheaf tests retain ell != p. The integral spectral-action restriction
ell not dividing |pi_1(dual G)_tors| belongs to its consumer and is never removed by the
existence of these categories.

## Completion contracts added on 2026-09-15

**Applies to:** `VS0`, `VS1`, `VS2`, `VS3`, `VS4`, `VS5`. Existing stage identifiers remain stable. These are required construction and validation tasks; they do not assert that the mathematics has been formalized.

### Intermediate constructions

Construct atlas descent for the exact enhanced category, then show refinement invariance of the partial-support functor. VS2 proves solidification on the correct coefficient category before passing to cutoffs; VS3 constructs lisse generators and their right-adjoint projection. At a nonbasic stratum prove the connected-kernel invariance separately from the classifying-stack equivalence for J_b(E).

### Producer–consumer contract

Return compact generators as compact inductions and ULA as perfection of pro-p invariants, with coefficient hypotheses. Admissibility of each cohomology group is weaker than perfection of the invariant complex. Infinite stratum support and finite wild cutoffs are not automatically properties of arbitrary objects.

### Acceptance and source route

On a point solid F_ℓ-modules differ from discrete ones; [*/Z_p] tests derived invariants; a nonbasic GL_2 stratum tests the connected kernel; an infinite direct sum tests compactness.

**Source route:** FS VII.6.1–VII.6.3, VII.7 and IX.1; ECD §23 for eligible atlas operations. The revision source ledger distinguishes freshly inspected passages from inherited locators and pending proof-interior checks.
