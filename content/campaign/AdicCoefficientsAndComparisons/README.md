# Roadmap: adic coefficients and comparison with schemes

This specification uses the shared [diamond conventions](../../campaign-guide/DIAMONDS_CONVENTIONS.md),
[source contracts](../../campaign-guide/DIAMONDS_SOURCE_CONTRACTS.md), and
[65-milestone source graph](../../campaign-guide/DIAMONDS_DEPENDENCY_ORDER.md). The original ECD source
coverage is retained in the 328-item index.
Canonical campaign ownership takes precedence over historical supplier paths in the source index.

This component implements ECD §§26–27. It also owns two missing classical inputs exposed by
the portfolio audit: the extension of the existing Noetherian scheme compact-support package
to the qcqs setting used in 27.4, and the alteration theorems used in 27.6. Neither input is
silently attributed to a narrower neighboring roadmap.

The milestones are dependency ordered, not a demand to prove their labels consecutively.
In particular the purely schematic work L2 and L5 can proceed independently of the diamond
six-operation construction.

## L0. Adic coefficients

Fix a commutative ring `Λ` complete for the `I`-adic topology, with `I` finitely generated by
a regular sequence and containing an integer prime to `p`. Using E4, define `D_ét(X,Λ)`
inside the v-derived category by derived `I`-completeness and étaleness after derived reduction
modulo `I`. Distinguish this from `D_ét` for the same ring regarded as discrete.

Prove Proposition 26.2 at the enhanced level: presentability/stability and equivalence with
the inverse limit of the categories for `Λ/I^n`. Construct the completed tensor product and
completed colimits, and show that the uncompleted inclusion into the v-derived category need
not preserve them. The ordinary tensor product of complete objects is not by definition
complete.

Construct the six operations for the entire coefficient system, then pass to the limit. Prove
the finite-level compatibility of all six operations, the regular-sequence/devissage argument
for right adjoints and Hom, and the detection of equivalences modulo `I`. Derive the adic
forms of the introductory theorems with unchanged geometric restrictions. Include `Λ=ℤ_ℓ`,
`I=(ℓ)`, `ℓ≠p` as the principal test. This construction does not assert the same theory for
`ℤ_p`, and rational `ℓ`-adic coefficients require a separately specified localization rather
than being smuggled into a completion theorem.

## L1. The scheme-to-v-sheaf constructions

For a scheme in characteristic `p`, construct the small v-sheaf of ECD §27 using its adic
realization with the integral closure of `𝔽_p` as the plus subring in the affine case. Prove
independence of affine cover, functoriality and factorization through perfection. Construct
the étale/pro-étale comparison morphisms of sites. The target is a small v-sheaf in general;
do not assert an equivalence of étale topoi merely from the existence of this functor.

Separately construct the mixed-characteristic version over a complete discrete valuation ring
`O` with perfect residue field: marked untilts over `Spa(O,O)` with a morphism of locally
ringed spaces to the scheme over `Spec O`. Prove the relative representability statements
used in 27.5. These constructions have different integral conventions: for a residue field `k`,
one uses `(k,k)`, whereas the characteristic-`p` construction uses the integral closure of
`𝔽_p`. Provide comparison maps in the cases where there are such maps; never identify the
two by notation alone.

## L2. General qcqs scheme compactification and coefficient extensions

Consume the existing scheme compact-support construction in its **Noetherian**, separated
finite-type range, together with its canonical comparison maps. Construct its extension to
separated finite-type morphisms of qcqs schemes needed by ECD 27.4. The same constructions
must extend the existing carrier, not define a second scheme `Rf!`.

First develop noetherian approximation: limits of schemes with affine transition maps,
finite-presentation descent of morphisms and diagrams, eventual recognition of the relevant
properties, and étale/cohomological continuity. Treat finite type versus finite presentation
separately. Prove the reduction of a finite-type separated morphism to a closed immersion
followed by an appropriate finitely presented morphism, and the compactification of the
latter at a noetherian stage. Prove the passage back to the original morphism, including
closure and plus-free scheme-theoretic constructions. This gives the nonnoetherian Nagata
statement, not merely its finite-presentation special case.

Construct common refinements and the larger-coefficient/bounded-below scheme lower shriek
using the same open extension by zero and proper pushforward. Prove agreement with the
supplier on overlap, composition, base change and projection formula. Use finite relative
dimension/cohomological bounds and E1–E3 to pass to the unbounded/left-completed ranges
needed for comparison. Extend from finite coefficient rings by the relevant colimit and
perfect-complex arguments, not by asserting that every module over an infinite coefficient
ring is a finite local system. Pass to perfections through the proved invariance theorem.

**Sources:** the scheme compactification/limit results of the Stacks Project, ECD 27.4, and
the supplier's Noetherian compactification proof. Missing approximation, schematic closure,
compactification and derived-coefficient lemmas are owned here. This prefix has no dependency
on diamond `Rf!` and can supply classical uses of the same qcqs extension.

## L3. Characteristic-`p` cohomological comparison

Construct the comparison pullback and right adjoint, and prove 27.1–27.3. Full faithfulness of
pullback in 27.2 is a theorem about the specified derived categories, **not** essential
surjectivity or an equivalence of all sheaves. Follow the reduction to perfected affine
space, extension to an algebraically closed nonarchimedean field, exhaustion by balls,
finite-stage constructibility and the classical analytic comparison H5. Prove that the
restriction maps used in the radius argument are the actual cohomology maps; abstract
isomorphisms of finite vector spaces are insufficient.

Use L2 and S0–S3 to prove 27.4 for a separated finite-type map of qcqs characteristic-`p`
schemes, or their perfections. Prove that its associated morphism is compactifiable,
representable in locally spatial diamonds and of finite `dim.trg`. Construct and prove the
`Rf!` comparison, then obtain the stated right-adjoint identity for exceptional pullback.
The proposition does not license every possible interchange of `c*`, `Rf*`, `Rf^!` and
internal Hom without checking which identity is actually stated.

## L4. Mixed-characteristic proper-support comparison

For separated maps of schemes of finite type over the complete DVR `O` with perfect residue
field, prove the geometric eligibility and `Rf!` comparison of 27.5, with its right-adjoint
consequence. Follow Nagata compactification, reduction to proper maps and the analytic test
space given by the locus `x ≠ 0` in `Spa O[[x]]`.

Construct that test space, prove its stated analytic smoothness and the conservativity after
passing to diamonds using S5, and compare both sides through H5's algebraic/analytic proper
base-change theorem. Record the maps and their base-change compatibility. Do not extend this
theorem to arbitrary non-finite-type schemes over an arbitrary complete ring.

## L5. Alterations and the local normal-crossing calculations

This is a required proof branch for 27.6, not an optional geometric application. The curve
stable-reduction theorem alone does not supply alterations in arbitrary dimension.

Prove the forms of de Jong, *Smoothness, semi-stability and alterations*, Theorems 4.1 and
6.5 used by the comparison proof: alteration to a smooth situation with normal-crossing
boundary in the field case and the semistable alteration situation over the valuation base
after the permitted finite base extension. Preserve the source's properness, dominance,
generic-finiteness, integrality and base-field hypotheses; apply each to the reduction for
which it is stated, not as resolution of singularities without extension.

The internal proof tasks are:

1. Establish the required finite-type scheme geometry: normalization under the applicable
   excellence hypotheses, flattening by blowup, strict transforms, generic smoothness,
   compactification of pairs and the relevant induction on dimension.
2. Establish the curve-fibration construction, extension of the generic curve and marked
   boundary, finite base extension, and stable/semistable reduction of that family. Consume
   a proved stable-curve supplier where its hypotheses agree; otherwise the needed
   curve-family theorem is a task of this stage, not an unexplained import.
3. Carry out de Jong's inductive alteration construction, including the modifications and
   boundary control. Prove the normal-crossing and strictly semistable conclusions in the
   form used in ECD. Do not require a characteristic-zero resolution route for the
   positive-characteristic case.
4. Develop proper hypercovers by the resulting alterations and their cohomological descent,
   using scheme proper base change and E2's enhanced descent. An alteration may have
   degree divisible by `ℓ`; do not divide its degree to manufacture a trace splitting.
5. Compute the local étale purity/Kummer and nearby-cycle descriptions for the smooth
   strata and normal-crossing boundaries, with their maps, Galois actions and compatible
   analytic versions from H1/H5. Prove the gluing and dimension-induction statements used
   to reduce a comparison to the remaining closed strata.

These tasks have schematic proofs independent of the conclusion of L6. The bibliography
references the primary alteration paper; the implementation must prove the required forms,
not register them as opaque facts from algebraic geometry.

## L6. Constructible direct-image comparison and full faithfulness

Prove 27.6 for a separated map of schemes of finite type over `O` and a **finite** coefficient
ring killed by an integer prime to `p`, on constructible complexes. Factor into an open
immersion and a proper map. Use L5's alterations and local calculations for the
characteristic-`p` and generic-fibre parts, then the strictly semistable reduction, the
boundary-stratum induction and proper pushforward. Prove the remaining comparison for
`Spec K → Spec O` by the explicit Galois-cohomology calculation with inertia.

Prove the constructible full-faithfulness statement 27.7 in this standing prime-to-`p`
coefficient setting and for schemes locally of finite type over `O`, by the base-DVR case,
27.6 and the appropriate local-to-global argument. The isolated wording “finite ring” in
27.7 is not used to claim a new `p`-torsion comparison outside the coefficient assumptions
of the proof. Any such stronger coefficient theorem would require its own justification.

## Completion

Tests compare an actual scheme finite étale cover, the two residue-field integral conventions,
a proper finite-type scheme map, a normal-crossing open immersion, the trait generic-point
calculation, and compatible `ℤ/ℓ^n` coefficient systems. Every comparison refers to the
previously defined scheme and diamond functors and is compatible with their units, counits
and coefficient maps.

## Completion contracts added on 2026-09-15

**Applies to:** `L0`, `L2`, `L3`, `L5`, `L6`. Existing stage identifiers remain stable. These are required construction and validation tasks; they do not assert that the mathematics has been formalized.

### Intermediate constructions

For a compatible Λ/I^n system, construct its transition equivalences as derived base changes and prove reconstruction after reduction, before defining an operation by its levels. At the schematic comparison seam retain the support immersion, compactification and its refinement maps. In the alteration branch use proper hypercover descent when the alteration degree is divisible by ℓ; there is then no degree-inverse splitting.

### Producer–consumer contract

Export a natural transformation for each permitted operation comparison, with the finite-coefficient theorem and adic limit theorem separately named. The rational localization has its own colimit/compactness proof. L6's constructibility hypotheses must accompany every downstream use.

### Acceptance and source route

A tower with nonzero lim¹, an alteration of ℓ-divisible degree, the generic point of a trait, and the two different plus-ring conventions at a residue field must be explicit tests.

**Source route:** ECD §§26–27, especially 26.2 and 27.4–27.7; Stacks qcqs compactification. The revision source ledger distinguishes freshly inspected passages from inherited locators and pending proof-interior checks. Before implementing a leaf, record its exact source statement, proof dependencies and comparison with the current Tau Ceti/Mathlib types; do not replace a missing producer by an assumed theorem field.
